# Seven suppliers sell speed that factories ration

As of 14 November 2026, all seven companies sell commercially mature core hardware for powering data centres (gas turbines and engines, solid-oxide fuel cells, transformers, switchgear, HVDC and UPS), and all seven report record orders, rising margins and multi-year backlogs. The evidence shows, however, that how fast that power arrives is now set by factory slots, castings and forgings, transformer test bays, gas pipelines and air permits rather than by the physics of the machines. Sources the companies do not control confirm the scarcity they describe: EPRI puts order-to-delivery for large gas turbines at **more than five years** ([Utility Dive, 23 Mar 2026](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/5-year-waits-and-rising-costs-how-demand-is-redefining-the-gas-turbine-mar/813385/)), Wood Mackenzie counted about **110 GW of 2025 turbine orders against 60–70 GW a year of manufacturing capacity** ([Power Engineering, 1 Apr 2026](https://www.power-eng.com/gas/turbines/gas-turbine-prices-climb-195-as-supply-crunch-reshapes-power-development/)), and the US producer price index for transformers stood **87.5% above January 2020** in October 2026 ([FRED series WPU1174, accessed 14 Nov 2026](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/WPU1174)). The same sources show how little of the announced build exists: Cleanview counted about **90 GW of behind-the-meter data-centre generation announced by mid-2026 but only about 2 GW operating** ([Cleanview, mid-2026](https://cleanview.co/reports/behind-the-meter-data-centers)). Outside verification is solid for the mature products: fleet operating hours, a Guinness efficiency record, CIGRE failure statistics, multi-billion-euro HVDC awards by transmission operators, and customers' SEC filings. It is absent for the data-centre-specific extensions the companies now market: following AI load swings without storage, 800 VDC and solid-state transformers, gigawatt-scale fuel-cell delivery in 90 days, and high-hydrogen firing in large turbines. Cross-examination turns up concrete conflicts. **58 GW of GE Vernova's 122 GW "under contract" are reservations outside its backlog** ([GE Vernova Q3 2026 release, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000162/gevpressrelease3q26.htm)). SoftBank has taken a loss on about $2.9bn of non-refundable prepayments for power equipment it will not take, after cancelling more than 13 GW of turbine reservations that the Wall Street Journal reported were mostly GE Vernova's ([24/7 Wall St., 12 Nov 2026](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/12/softbank-cfo-says-there-is-no-such-default-after-musks-turbine-claim-but-a-3-7b-charge-on-scrapped-turbine-orders-suggests-elon-was-onto-something/)). **Bloom's 65% efficiency headline sits beside the 53% average measured for California's electric-only fuel cells** ([Verdant Associates for the CPUC, 14 Jan 2021](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/-/media/cpuc-website/divisions/energy-division/documents/self-generation-incentive-program/sgip_impact_report_2018-2019_finaldraft.pdf)) and an adversarial dataset showing faster decline. Siemens Energy's estimate of a 110–120 GW-a-year turbine market sits against Mitsubishi's ~70 GW ([POWER, 1 Sep 2026](https://www.powermag.com/ansaldo-us-gas-turbine-market-equipment-crunch/)). HD Hyundai Electric and Hyosung each claim US transformer leadership. Several headline data-centre deals have not been confirmed in the counterparties' own disclosures: GE Vernova's with Chevron and NRG, Siemens Energy's with Williams, Schneider's with Digital Realty, and every HD Hyundai Electric sale to a named US buyer.

## Seven suppliers occupy three layers of the power chain

The seven companies do not compete in a single market. Four sell on-site generation that can bypass a slow grid connection: GE Vernova (aeroderivative and heavy-duty gas turbines), Siemens Energy (heavy-duty, industrial and aeroderivative turbines), Caterpillar (diesel and gas reciprocating gensets, plus Solar Turbines industrial turbines) and Bloom Energy (solid-oxide fuel cells). Four sell the high-voltage equipment that connects a campus to the grid: Hitachi Energy, HD Hyundai Electric, Siemens Energy's Grid Technologies segment, and GE Vernova's Electrification segment, which has owned transformer maker Prolec GE outright since 2 February 2026 ([GE Vernova Q3 2026 10-Q, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000163/gev-20260930.htm)).

Schneider Electric sells mainly the in-building chain: medium- and low-voltage switchgear, UPS, prefabricated power modules and cooling. GE Vernova (medium-voltage UPS, shipping from mid-2027) and Bloom (800 V DC-ready output) are entering that layer. Each company defines its "data-centre" exposure differently, so the shares in Table 1 cannot be compared with one another.

**Behind-the-meter build.** Behind-the-meter generation is mostly still on paper. In its tracker, Caterpillar equipment appears in 33% of projects and Bloom in 14% ([Cleanview, mid-2026](https://cleanview.co/reports/behind-the-meter-data-centers)).

## Company fact files: what each makes, earns, owes, has signed and faces

Each file below runs in the same order: products and stage; roadmap and dated milestones, met or missed; finances; customers and capacity; and ownership, people, and legal and regulatory matters.

### GE Vernova: half of a 122 GW gas book is reservations

GE Vernova sells the full gas-turbine range used for data-centre power. At the fast end are the trailer-mounted TM2500 (up to 37 MW, 5-minute start, power "within 30 days of contract signature") and the LM2500XPRESS (~35 MW, "cold iron to base load within 5 minutes"); above them sit the LM6000 (~57 MW) and LMS100 (~116 MW) aeroderivatives and heavy-duty HA units from the 7HA.02 (384 MW simple cycle) to the 9HA.02 (571 MW), rated at "63–64%" (7HA) and ">64%" (9HA) combined-cycle efficiency at ISO conditions ([TM2500 page](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/gas-turbines/tm2500), [LM2500 page](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/gas-turbines/lm2500), [7HA page](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/gas-turbines/7ha), [9HA page](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/gas-turbines/9ha), all accessed 14 Nov 2026). The company's own investor deck puts ship-to-commercial-operation at ~1.5 months for an LM2500, ~18 months for a 7F 1x1 and **~24 months for a 7HA.03 2x1 combined cycle** ([Investor Update deck, 9 Dec 2025](https://www.gevernova.com/sites/default/files/gev_webcast_presentation_12092025.pdf)). On the grid side it sells switchgear, breakers, HVDC and synchronous condensers, and it owns Prolec GE outright, with ~10,000 staff and seven plants, five of them in the US ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000163/gev-20260930.htm)). The data-centre-specific products are pre-commercial. A medium-voltage UPS launched on 24 August 2026 is expected to ship mid-2027 and first energise in late 2027, with no published ratings or customer ([release, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.gevernova.com/news/press-releases/ge-vernova-introduces-medium-voltage-ups-help-accelerate-buildout-ai-factories)). For the solid-state transformer, management said in January that the first unit would reach a hyperscaler "in the autumn of this year", but the October call mentioned only a "6 MW solid-state transformer award" and no delivery ([Q4 2025 call, 28 Jan 2026](https://gevernova.com/sites/default/files/gev_webcast_transcript_01282026.pdf); [Q3 2026 call, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/28/ge-vernova-gev-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)). The BWRX-300 small modular reactor received the first US construction permit, for TVA's Clinch River site, on 28 September 2026 ([GE Vernova news listing, accessed 14 Nov 2026](https://www.gevernova.com/news/media-hub?type%5Bpress_release%5D=press_release&page=1)).

The roadmap is a manufacturing schedule. Heavy-duty output was to reach 20 GW a year from Q3 2026, ~24 GW in 2028 (90–100 units) and 30 GW in 2030 ([Q2 2026 release, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000147/gevpressrelease2q26.htm)); the 20 GW goal was first set in January 2025 for 2027 and then pulled forward ([Q4 2024 call, 22 Jan 2025](https://gevernova.com/sites/default/files/gev_webcast_transcript_01222025.pdf)), and Q3 2026 shipments of 32 turbines totalling **5.3 GW** met the first step on time ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000163/gev-20260930.htm)). Transformer and switchgear output is to double between 2024 and 2028 ([Q4 2025 call](https://gevernova.com/sites/default/files/gev_webcast_transcript_01282026.pdf)) within a 2025–2028 envelope of $6bn of capex and $5bn of R&D ([Q3 2026 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000162/gevpressrelease3q26.htm)). Against its own targets since the April 2024 spin-off, the company met or beat on revenue, margin and cash: FY2024 revenue of $34,935M, adj. EBITDA margin of 5.8% and FCF of $1,701M against a $1.3–1.7bn FCF guide ([FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000015/gev-20251231.htm); [Q2 2024 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681024000065/gev2q2024earningsrelease.htm)); FY2025 revenue of $38.1bn, margin of 8.4% and FCF of $3.7bn against a raised $3.0–3.5bn guide ([Q4 2025 release, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.gevernova.com/sites/default/files/gev_webcast_pressrelease_01282026.pdf)); and the Prolec purchase closed on 2 February 2026 against a "by mid-2026" target ([Q1 2026 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000063/gevpressrelease1q26.htm)). ### Caterpillar: power generation becomes the largest line in its largest segment

Caterpillar sells data-centre power through its Power & Energy segment, called Energy & Transportation until Q3 2025, from which rail moved out on 1 January 2026 ([8-K, 26 Mar 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/18230/000001823026000013/cat-20260326.htm)). The segment offers three mature families. Diesel standby gensets remain, in CEO Joe Creed's words, "the growing base of our business" ([Q3 2026 call, 29 Oct 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/29/caterpillar-cat-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)). Natural-gas reciprocating gensets are increasingly sold for prime or bridge power. They include the G3516 fast-response unit, which a joint release with customer American Intelligence & Power says "can ramp from zero to full load in approximately seven seconds" and which carries selective catalytic reduction ([AIP/Caterpillar/Boyd CAT release, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/american-intelligence--power-forms-strategic-alliance-with-caterpillar-and-boyd-cat-to-deploy-2-gigawatts-of-dedicated-power-for-hyperscale-ai-infrastructure-302673060.html)); the G3520K ([Joule/Caterpillar/Wheeler release, 7 Aug 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/joule-caterpillar-and-wheeler-announce-an-agreement-to-power-americas-growing-data-center-energy-needs-302524328.html)); a 10-MW MaK gas engine being restarted, with first units shipping in Q4 2026 ([Q3 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/29/caterpillar-cat-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)); the 3600 series; and MWM TCG engines of 400–4,500 kW. Among the MWM engines, the TCG 4170 claims up to 46% electrical efficiency "at optimal CHP output", full power in under five minutes and a 60% maximum load step ([MWM TCG 4170 page, accessed 14 Nov 2026](https://www.mwm.net/en/gas-engines-gensets/gas-engine-tcg-4170/)). Solar Turbines supplies industrial gas turbines including the Titan 350 and the mobile PGM 130 ([Solaris 10-Q for Q2 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1697500/000155837025009911/0001558370-25-009911.txt)). ### Hitachi Energy: an HVDC-led backlog, with data-centre deals mostly memoranda

### Gas turbines: verified records, much lower fleet averages, and castings as the limit

The current independently certified efficiency record belongs to Siemens Energy. Guinness World Records lists Keadby 2, the SSE Thermal plant with a Siemens HL turbine, as the most efficient combined-cycle plant at **64.18%**, verified on 21 May 2024. The page does not say whether that figure is net or gross, LHV or HHV, or who measured it ([Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/431420-most-efficient-combined-cycle-power-plant)). The operator describes the plant as "around 63 per cent" efficient ([SSE Thermal](https://www.ssethermal.com/flexible-generation/operational/keadby-2/)). GE's earlier records, Bouchain at 62.22% (2016) and Nishi-Nagoya at 63.08% gross (2018), are superseded ([Power Engineering, 27 Mar 2018](https://www.power-eng.com/2018/03/27/ge-powered-plant-awarded-world-record-efficiency-by-guinness/)).

Fleet reality is lower. EIA's 2025 tested full-load heat rates are 7,535 Btu/kWh for US combined cycle and 10,975 Btu/kWh for simple-cycle turbines ([EIA Electric Power Annual Table 8.2](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/annual/html/epa_08_02.html)), equal to roughly 45% and 31% HHV (derived). EIA's 2025 net summer gas capacity was 6.6% below net winter capacity ([EIA Table 4.3](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/annual/html/epa_04_03.html)). That is consistent with hot-weather derating, although no unit-level derating curves were found.

On reliability, GE took a $0.2bn charge in Q3 2018 for an "oxidation issue within the HA and 9FB Stage 1 turbine blades" ([GE FY2018 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40545/000004054519000014/ge10-k2018.htm)). The same issue shut a unit at Exelon's Colorado Bend plant that September ([Utility Dive, 21 Sep 2018](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/trouble-in-texas-causes-shutdown-of-advanced-ge-turbine/532901/)). No independent data were found on whether the fix has proven durable.

Hydrogen claims rest mostly on the manufacturers' own tests. The demonstrations with an outside party present are Mitsubishi's: a 20% hydrogen blend by volume at Georgia Power's McDonough plant in 2022, with EPRI on site, cut CO2 by only ~7% ([POWER, 16 Jun 2022](https://www.powermag.com/southern-co-gas-fired-demonstration-validates-20-hydrogen-fuel-blend/)). GE Vernova's results come from its own Greenville facility with no third-party body named and no NOx data: a 50% blend in HA units, a 50% blend "validated" in the LM2500XPRESS, and 100% hydrogen tested in the LM6000 ([GE Vernova, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.gevernova.com/news/press-releases/ge-vernova-advances-hydrogen-capabilities-aeroderivative-gas-turbines-new-milestones)). Siemens Energy reports its 100% hydrogen run on a small SGT-400 ([SGT-400 page](https://www.siemens-energy.com/global/en/home/products-services/product/sgt-400.html)). The run took place in HYFLEXPOWER, an EU project with a €10.5M EU contribution that Siemens coordinated and that included DLR and Lund University ([CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/884229)); no independent report of the run's results was found. Its HL hydrogen rating rests on rig tests ([HL-class page](https://www.siemens-energy.com/global/en/home/products-services/product-offerings/gas-turbines/hl-class.html)).

The binding limit is manufacturing, not combustion physics. EPRI experts said in January 2026 that rotor forgings and hot-section blades are "the primary bottlenecks", and that some large frames have shipped without rotors or blades, to be fitted on site ([POWER via Yahoo Finance, 15 Jan 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/gas-turbine-supply-chain-bottlenecks-130656088.html)). Wood Mackenzie names single-crystal blade production as the main constraint ([Utility Dive, 9 Apr 2026](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/gas-turbine-supply-crunch-set-to-raise-prices-195-by-2027-woodmac/816904/)). Supplier data agree. Howmet's gas-turbine revenue rose 45% year on year in Q3 2026 ([Howmet release, 29 Oct 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4281/000110465926119857/tm2628456d1_ex99-1.htm)), and GE Aerospace agreed on 8 September 2026 to buy castings maker CPP for $11.75bn, citing castings bottlenecks ([GE Aerospace, 8 Sep 2026](https://www.geaerospace.com/news/press-releases/ge-aerospace-acquire-consolidated-precision-products-cpp-expanding-mission-critical)).

AI training loads are the open physics question. NERC says power-electronic loads "can shift their consumption in seconds, much quicker than conventional generators can ramp" ([NERC white paper, Jul 2025](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/who-we-are/standing-committees/rstc/whitepaper-characteristics-and-risks-of-emerging-large-loads.pdf)). A Microsoft, OpenAI and NVIDIA paper finds the energy of production training swings concentrated at 0.2–3 Hz, and lists shaft torsional modes at about 7 Hz and above as a resonance risk ([Choukse et al., arXiv:2508.14318, Aug 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14318)). GE Vernova's own launch material for its medium-voltage UPS says the product "buffers sudden shifts in facility demand so they aren't passed back to the grid or onsite generation" and protects gas turbines "from sharp load fluctuations" ([GE Vernova, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.gevernova.com/news/press-releases/ge-vernova-introduces-medium-voltage-ups-help-accelerate-buildout-ai-factories)). That is an acknowledgement that turbines alone are not meant to absorb those swings.

### Reciprocating engines: mature, but gas accepts load more slowly than diesel

ISO 8528-5:2022 sets the transient classes for gensets. In the tightest standard class, G3, frequency may dip by up to −7% for diesel but −15% for gas per load step, with recovery within 3 seconds. Kohler, a rival genset maker, states that "Gaseous engines respond slower than diesel due to fuel delivery differences" ([Kohler white paper on ISO 8528-5:2022](https://techcomm.kohler.com/techcomm/pdf/ISO%208528-5%20and%20Generator%20Transient%20Performance_WP.pdf)). Caterpillar's own white paper, seen only in a search-result summary, concedes that gas units "would typically have lower load acceptance capabilities than diesel units". It suggests running gas at base load while diesel follows the load ([Caterpillar white paper](https://www.cat.com/en_IN/by-industry/electric-power/Articles/White-papers/converting-data-centers-from-diesel-to-gas-power-generation.html)). Against that background, the G3516 claim of zero to full load "in approximately seven seconds" rests only on a joint release with a customer, and no independent test of it was found.

Fleet efficiency of US gas reciprocating engines is 8,853 Btu/kWh, about 38.5% HHV (derived from [EIA Table 8.2](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/annual/html/epa_08_02.html)). Response time is the physical gap for AI loads. Rehlko, a competitor, says AI clusters swing within 10–200 ms, that diesel gensets take 200–450 ms to respond to a step, and that UPS or battery buffering acts in 2–4 ms ([Rehlko, accessed 14 Nov 2026](https://www.rehlko.com/why-ai-clusters-move-faster-than-your-power-train-can-react)). Caterpillar's large deals bundle batteries designed "to handle the extreme load swings of AI data centers" ([AIP release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/american-intelligence--power-forms-strategic-alliance-with-caterpillar-and-boyd-cat-to-deploy-2-gigawatts-of-dedicated-power-for-hyperscale-ai-infrastructure-302673060.html)).

Scale is the other limit. At customer-stated unit sizes of 2.6–10 MW, a 1 GW engine plant needs roughly 100–385 gensets (derived from the [ProPetro 8-K, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1680247/000168024726000122/pump-20261028.htm)). No multi-year availability data for such plants in continuous prime duty were found.

### Solid-oxide fuel cells: sound electrochemistry, contested durability

Laboratory evidence on stack life is strong. A short stack ran 100,000 hours at 700 °C with an overall voltage degradation of 0.5% per 1,000 hours, according to a search-result summary of a 2020 paper ([peeref record](https://peeref.com/works/21460552)). Jülich reports ~0.1% per 1,000 hours in late-life testing ([J. Mater. Chem. A, 2025, search-result summary](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2025/ta/d5ta03917g)).

Field evidence is weaker and contested. California's programme evaluation measured electric-only fuel cells at **53% LHV electrical efficiency** in 2019, with 0.36 t CO2/MWh against 0.25–0.26 t/MWh for the grid generation they displaced. That is a net increase in emissions ([Verdant Associates for the CPUC, 14 Jan 2021](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/-/media/cpuc-website/divisions/energy-division/documents/self-generation-incentive-program/sgip_impact_report_2018-2019_finaldraft.pdf)).

A data analysis of public records published on 30 July 2026 by Hunterbrook, whose affiliate was short Bloom, found that 37 Bloom systems in New York started at a 52.0% average (HHV), lost 2.4 percentage points a year and all fell below the contractual guarantee line at a median of 20 months; that Delaware's fleet averaged ~86% of nameplate output; and that 110 of 111 reliably metered plants in EIA data averaged below 95% output ([Hunterbrook, 30 Jul 2026](https://newsletter.hntrbrk.com/p/do-blooms-fuel-cells-work-as-claimed)). Hunterbrook itself notes that the New York sample skews to older ES-5 hardware. Bloom's own Q2 2026 10-Q attributes higher guarantee costs to "fleet degradation" and shows accrued warranty rising to $77.8M at 30 June 2026, as reported by [Energy News Beat, 22 Oct 2026](https://energynewsbeat.co/data-center/bloom-energy-fuel-cells-natural-gas-and-the-ai-power-rush/).

On CO2, Energy News Beat's comparison using EIA and Lazard heat rates sets Bloom's datasheet range of 679–833 lb/MWh against ~881 lb/MWh for the US combined-cycle fleet, ~757–766 lb/MWh for a new best-in-class combined cycle and ~1,284 lb/MWh for simple-cycle turbines (same source): **below the fleet average, roughly level with the best new combined cycle, and well below peakers**, with the advantage shrinking as stacks degrade.

On load following, Bloom's own test simulated an AI load with a resistor bank stepping from 40% to 100%, with supercapacitors carrying the step ([Bloom whitepaper, Feb 2024](https://www.bloomenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/load-following-solid-oxide-fuel-cell.pdf)). Its sponsored material, by contrast, describes AI swings from 20% to more than 150% ([Latitude Studios, Bloom-sponsored, 11 Sep 2025](https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/how-fuel-cells-are-meeting-the-power-needs-of-data-centers-at-speed-and-scale/)). Academic work says fuel cells alone match a load step only after "several seconds or even minutes" ([arXiv:1806.04506, 2018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.04506)).

Scandium supply is disputed. Thunder Said Energy estimated scandia loading at ~30 t per GW ([Thunder Said Energy, 2 Aug 2023](https://thundersaidenergy.com/downloads/bloom-energy-solid-oxide-fuel-cell-technology/)). Hunterbrook, as reported by Barchart, put 5 GW a year at ~220 t of scandium oxide, against ~240 t of projected global supply ([Barchart, Jul 2026](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3226408/be-stock-alert-bloom-energy-stock-remains-in-focus-following-short-report-rebuttal)). Bloom cites supply visibility for 25 GW a year ([Bloom 8-K, 9 Jul 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001664703/000162828026047734/be-20260709.htm)).

Federal statistics cannot settle any of this. EIA-860M lists only 373.8 MW of operating fuel cells nationally, 108.6 MW of them under an entity named Bloom Energy, and 8 MW added in 2025 (research-team tabulation of [EIA-860M, Sep 2026](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/xls/september_generator2026.xlsx)).

### Grid equipment: transformer and HVDC physics are settled; supply and the gas transition are not

CIGRE surveys covering 964 major failures from 58 utilities put annual major-failure rates for substation and step-up transformers "within 1%". A follow-up covering more than 425,000 unit-years gives ~0.3% (per search-result summaries of [CIGRE TB 642](https://e-cigre.org/publication/642-transformer-reliability-survey)). For HVDC, CIGRE data summarised by KU Leuven and Elia put LCC converter stations at ~98–99% energy availability and radial VSC links at ~96.5%, though the VSC figure rests on only 3–4 systems ([Bastianel et al., arXiv:2512.13496, 23 Jul 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13496)). The same paper notes that "only a handful of manufacturers can currently build HVDC cables and (VSC-MMC) converters", and that a DC circuit breaker occupies about 400 m². NERC judged grid-forming battery inverters "commercially available and field-proven for transmission-connected applications" in September 2023. It cited Kauai (since 2018) and Hornsdale (since 2022), and found in hardware-in-the-loop tests of Maui's grid that ~30% of online capacity needs to be grid-forming ([NERC white paper, Sep 2023](https://www.nerc.com/comm/RSTC_Reliability_Guidelines/White_Paper_GFM_Functional_Specification.pdf)). Hitachi Energy describes its own grid-forming validation as simulation plus in-house software-in-the-loop testing ([Hitachi Energy blog, 10 Apr 2026](https://www.hitachienergy.com/news-and-events/blogs/2026/04/applying-grid-forming-converters-to-enhance-stability-in-current-and-future-power-systems)).

SF6-free switchgear is proven at lower voltages but not yet at transmission level. SINTEF's December 2025 review found that air or vacuum designs at ≤24 kV, the class of Schneider's AirSeT, have been "standard for decades"; that at 145 kV Siemens Energy has "hundreds of breakers in Norway and Europe" using vacuum with N2/O2, while Hitachi Energy and GE Vernova CO2/O2 breakers and HD Hyundai's C4F7N–CO2 GIS are available; and that at 420 kV commercial SF6-free GIS uses C4F7N gas mixtures, with vacuum plus N2/O2 "not yet ready" ([SINTEF, 8 Dec 2025](https://blog.sintef.com/energy/what-is-the-status-of-phasing-out-sf6-gas-in-switchgear-and-circuit-breakers/)). C4F7N mixtures have a global-warming potential of ~300–700, fall under the EU's PFAS definition and face a proposed restriction (SINTEF; [Umweltbundesamt, 13 Nov 2025](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/en/topics/climate-energy/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases-fully-halogenated-cfcs/application-domains-emission-reduction/switchgear)). No independent type-test certificate for any of the seven companies' SF6-free or 765 kV products was found.

Supply, not physics, is the binding limit. Wood Mackenzie put Q2 2025 lead times at **128 weeks for power transformers, 143 weeks for generator step-up units and 44 weeks for switchgear** ([Wood Mackenzie, 15 Oct 2025](https://www.woodmac.com/news/opinion/mind-the-gap-tackling-supply-chain-challenges-in-the-electric-td-sector/)). On 31 August 2026 it described Executive Order 14420 as banning bulk-power equipment imports from certain nations, especially China, affecting more than $22bn of imports, and estimated 2026 shortages of 15% for power transformers and 8% for substations ([Wood Mackenzie, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.woodmac.com/press-releases/us-executive-order-banning-chinese-bulk-power-equipment-set-to-worsen-critical-transformer-shortage/)). Cleveland-Cliffs describes itself as the sole North American producer of grain-oriented electrical steel for distribution transformers, and DOE reopened its 2024 efficiency rule for comment in June 2026 ([Utility Dive, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/doe-mulls-changes-to-biden-era-transformer-rule-raising-utility-concerns/826440/)). HD Hyundai Electric cites bushings as a long-lead component ([Finance Scope, 23 Feb 2026](https://www.finance-scope.com/article/view/scp202602200024)), the same component GE Vernova's pending Trench purchase covers.

### In-facility power electronics: UPS logic is the proven risk, 800 VDC the unproven fix

The best-documented in-facility hazard is control logic, not hardware. On 10 July 2024 in Virginia, a failed lightning arrester caused six voltage depressions, and about **1,500 MW** of data-centre load disconnected. About 1,260 MW dropped at the third reclose because UPS protection schemes counted three dips within a minute and moved load to backup, where it stayed for hours ([NERC Incident Review, 8 Jan 2025](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/our-work/reports/event-reports/incident_review_large_load_loss.pdf)). ERCOT now prohibits such count-based transfer schemes for large computational loads under NOGRR 282, effective 1 August 2026 ([ERCOT NOGRR 282](https://www.ercot.com/mktrules/issues/NOGRR282); [ERCOT presentation, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/03/02/04_LEL-RT-Requirements_SPWG_Feb2026.pdf)). After settings were changed, a similar fault on 18 April 2026 caused only 500 MW of sustained loss. Then in July 2026 a "nearly 4 GW" customer-initiated load reduction occurred in Virginia ([NERC Reliability Insights, Sep 2026](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/who-we-are/news/2026/09/reliability-insights_large-computational-load-risks-due-to-voltage-sensitivity.pdf)).

Rack-level smoothing helps, but evidence that it works at facility scale is lacking. NVIDIA's GB300 power shelves store 65 J per GPU and cut peak grid demand by 30% in NVIDIA's own test ([NVIDIA, 28 Jul 2025](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-new-gb300-nvl72-features-provide-steady-power-for-ai)). That is ~4.7 kJ per 72-GPU rack, enough to carry a 72 kW step for about 65 ms (derived). The Microsoft, OpenAI and NVIDIA paper rates rack-level storage high on cost. In simulation, holding GPUs at a 90% power floor cost 10.5% extra energy ([arXiv:2508.14318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14318)).

Facility-level 800 VDC remains a vendor programme. NVIDIA, which lists Schneider Electric, GE Vernova and Hitachi Energy among its 800 VDC partners ([NVIDIA, 13 Oct 2025](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/building-the-800-vdc-ecosystem-for-efficient-scalable-ai-factories/)), concedes "new challenges in safety, standards, and workforce training" and calls DC fault detection "a key area for innovation" ([NVIDIA, 20 May 2025](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-800-v-hvdc-architecture-will-power-the-next-generation-of-ai-factories/)). Dell'Oro reported from the October 2026 OCP summit that the sidecar "is here" but that upstream 800 VDC is "an object of engineering ambition and speculation", and that for solid-state transformers "field reliability is unproven at scale". The draft OCP solid-state transformer specification targets ≥98% efficiency at up to 10 MW ([Dell'Oro, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.delloro.com/inside-ocp-global-summit-2026-cold-plates-800-vdc-sidecars-and-a-buildout-at-full-steam/)). Hitachi Energy's CEO concedes that the solid-state transformer's "cost is slightly higher" for "a slight improvement in efficiency" ([Q&A, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/content/dam/hitachi/global/en/press/files/2026/06/260610/20260610_06_qa_session_en.pdf)). Bloom's modelled claim that 800 VDC saves $3.6bn of non-compute capex per GW ([Bloom/Business Wire, 16 Sep 2026](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260916878071/en/Bloom-Energys-800V-DC-Native-Power-Can-Cut-Billions-from-AI-Data-Center-Costs-Reduce-Power-Use-and-Eliminate-Need-for-Transformers)) appears in an independent write-up as ~27% lower non-compute capex and ~9% lower five-year total cost of ownership. That write-up notes some modelled equipment is not yet in volume production ([Environment+Energy Leader](https://environmentenergyleader.com/stories/why-800v-dc-is-reshaping-ai-data-center-power,140942)).

For lithium-ion batteries in UPS systems and prefabricated modules, UL 9540A is "the only consensus standard explicitly cited in NFPA 855 for large-scale fire testing", and NFPA 855's 2026 edition adds an annex on large-scale fire testing ([UL Solutions](https://www.ul.com/services/ul-9540a-test-method)). No UL 9540A report for any of the seven companies' products was found.

### AI load dynamics: a test none of the seven has independently passed

NERC has measured how fast these loads move: a 50 MW block of a 200 MW AI training site changed demand at **1.9 per-unit per second** (1.9 times the block's rating each second) for ~250 ms, a data centre fell from ~450 MW to ~40 MW in 36 seconds, and xAI's Colossus swings 35–70 MW within a minute ([NERC white paper, Jul 2025](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/who-we-are/standing-committees/rstc/whitepaper-characteristics-and-risks-of-emerging-large-loads.pdf)). In February 2025, 1,800 MW of data-centre load dropped after a transmission fault ([NERC State of Reliability, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/programs/rapa/pa/nerc_sor_2026_overview.pdf)). NERC issued a rare Level 3 alert on 4 May 2026 ([Utility Dive, 5 May 2026](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/nerc-issues-rare-level-3-alert-over-data-center-load-losses/819295/)). FERC has directed NERC to finish registry criteria and foundational standards for large computational loads by the end of 2026 ([NERC, Sep 2026](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/who-we-are/news/2026/09/reliability-insights_large-computational-load-risks-due-to-voltage-sensitivity.pdf)). ERCOT's draft ride-through rules require these loads to stay connected for 0.5 s at 0.5–0.8 per-unit voltage, and to return to at least 90% of pre-disturbance consumption within 2 s of recovery ([ERCOT, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/03/02/04_LEL-RT-Requirements_SPWG_Feb2026.pdf)).

Demand flexibility has been shown only at small scale. EPRI's London test with National Grid, Nebius and Emerald AI achieved reductions of up to 40% with sub-minute response, on a cluster "taken out of active production"; EPRI calls it a "small-scale demonstration" ([EPRI DCFlex summary, 2026](https://restservice.epri.com/publicattachment/98143)).

No source documents a turbine, engine or fuel cell from any of the seven following AI-training swings without electrical buffering. The deal structures reflect this. Caterpillar bundles batteries, Siemens Energy pairs SGT-800s with storage, Bloom relies on supercapacitors, GE Vernova is launching a medium-voltage UPS, and Hitachi Energy sells grid-forming converters.

The first pattern is that **every company's headline book contains a layer that is not a firm order**, and that layer is financed by customers. At 30 September 2026, reservations made up 58 of GE Vernova's 122 GW and 39 of Siemens Energy's 104 GW ([GE Vernova Q3 2026 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000162/gevpressrelease3q26.htm); [Siemens Energy Q4 FY26 deck](https://assets.siemens-energy.com/dam/991522b0-f8bd-4c08-bc2a-b4ff00538589/2026-11-11_Q4_Analyst_presentation_final_-pdf_Original%20file.pdf)). Hitachi Energy reports ~US$10bn of frameworks and capacity reservations outside its US$66.9bn backlog, yet counts them in its 2030 goal of "around 100 billion" ([CEO deck](https://www.hitachi.com/content/dam/hitachi/global/ja_jp/press/files/2026/11/1103/20261103_01_ceo.pdf); [Q&A, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/content/dam/hitachi/global/en/press/files/2026/06/260610/20260610_06_qa_session_en.pdf)). Elsewhere the same layer appears in other forms: Bloom's $20bn headline against $717M of GAAP obligations, Caterpillar's "up to 2.1 GW" against a 1.5 GW minimum, HD Hyundai Electric's KRW 1.12tn framework released in tranche purchase orders ([AutoDaily/M Today, search-result summary](https://www.autodaily.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=545415)), and Schneider's supply-capacity agreements with no disclosed binding volumes.

The reservation layer carries real money. GE Vernova's contract liabilities grew by $19.9bn in nine months ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000163/gev-20260930.htm)). Siemens Energy's reservation fees run at 10–20% ([Q3 FY26 call](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-siemens-energy-posts-stronger-q3-2026-profit-shares-rise-93CH-4836509)), and PROPWR owes 75% of the price on any shortfall. The only large public test so far, SoftBank, ended with the buyer forfeiting deposits rather than the supplier taking a loss ([24/7 Wall St., 12 Nov 2026](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/12/softbank-cfo-says-there-is-no-such-default-after-musks-turbine-claim-but-a-3-7b-charge-on-scrapped-turbine-orders-suggests-elon-was-onto-something/)). Two sources disagree on SoftBank's settlement cost: $0.8bn according to 24/7 Wall St. and $0.9bn according to [Yahoo Finance, 11 Nov 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ge-vernova-stocks-rise-nearly-183214486.html).

The second pattern is that **companies quote speed on the shortest available clock**, while customers and independents quote order-to-power. Federal data show 5.26 GW of gas capacity entering US grid service in 2025 against 43–51 GW of US orders that year (EIA-860M tabulation; [Utility Dive, 23 Mar 2026](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/5-year-waits-and-rising-costs-how-demand-is-redefining-the-gas-turbine-mar/813385/); [ESG News, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.esgnews.earth/latest-news/global-gas-turbine-orders-exceed-100-gw/21572.html)), and only about 2 GW of announced behind-the-meter capacity was operating in mid-2026 ([Cleanview](https://cleanview.co/reports/behind-the-meter-data-centers)). Customers say the same. NextEra's CEO said gas "won't be available at scale until 2030" ([Latitude Media, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/nexteras-ai-bet-google-meta-and-more-gas/)), and Google's Lucia Tian that "Slots for new gas turbines are now going out to 2030" ([Modern Power Systems, 9 Feb 2026](https://www.modernpowersystems.com/data-centre-power/could-a-delivery-backlog-derail-a-gt-boom/)). ## Conclusion

The evidence changes what "fast power" means. On paper it is a property of machines: five-minute aeroderivative starts, 85 MW-per-minute ramps, 90-day fuel-cell sites, six-month prefabricated blocks. In practice it is a property of contracts and permits. The seven companies have turned delivery slots into customer-funded assets through reservation deposits, minimum-purchase obligations and supply-capacity agreements. GE Vernova alone took in $19.9bn of customer prepayments in nine months ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000163/gev-20260930.htm)). The one large public test, SoftBank, ended with the buyer forfeiting about $2.9bn of prepayments, not with any supplier recording a loss ([24/7 Wall St., 12 Nov 2026](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/12/softbank-cfo-says-there-is-no-such-default-after-musks-turbine-claim-but-a-3-7b-charge-on-scrapped-turbine-orders-suggests-elon-was-onto-something/)). Headline commitment figures therefore include volume that is not yet firm, even where the reported backlog excludes it. Only Siemens Energy gives even a fee range (10–20%) and a typical conversion period (about six months) for its reservations ([Q3 FY26 call](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-siemens-energy-posts-stronger-q3-2026-profit-shares-rise-93CH-4836509); [pre-close call, 30 Sep 2026](https://assets.siemens-energy.com/dam/e8ced7ab-8dcc-48e5-b0b5-b4d500fee9a5/2026-09-30---Q4-FY26-Pre-Close-Call_transcript-pdf_Original%20file.pdf)). None of the seven discloses refund terms or historical conversion and lapse rates, so the only independent window into contract firmness is customers' own filings, such as ProPetro's, Solaris's and AEP's.

The second shift is that regulators are defining data-centre power performance before any vendor's data-centre-specific claim has been independently tested. FERC has directed NERC to finish registry criteria and foundational standards for large computational loads by end-2026 ([NERC, Sep 2026](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/who-we-are/news/2026/09/reliability-insights_large-computational-load-risks-due-to-voltage-sensitivity.pdf)). ERCOT's ride-through rule took effect on 1 August 2026 and bans count-based UPS transfer schemes ([ERCOT NOGRR 282](https://www.ercot.com/mktrules/issues/NOGRR282)). On 18 June 2026 FERC ordered six grid operators to justify or reform their large-load rules ([McGuireWoods, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.mcguirewoods.com/client-resources/alerts/2026/6/ferc-issues-section-206-show-cause-orders-directing-all-six-rtos-isos-to-justify-or-reform-large-load-integration-rules/)). Meanwhile, no independent source documents a turbine, engine or fuel cell from these companies following AI-training swings without batteries or power electronics. The behind-the-meter fleet is also largely invisible in federal statistics: EIA-860M shows 373.8 MW of operating fuel cells nationally ([EIA-860M, Sep 2026](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/xls/september_generator2026.xlsx)). With ERCOT's rule in force and NERC's standards due by end-2026, on-site and in-building equipment will from 2027 be assessed against ride-through and oscillation requirements as well as datasheet speed. Verification will continue to depend on customer filings, regulator incident reports and private trackers rather than on company disclosures.
