# Japan: Hitachi High-Tech, Lasertec and other Japanese inspection/metrology suppliers

Research date: 12 November 2026. Final version after one interruption; coverage of the smaller Japanese suppliers (Part 4) is thin and is labelled as such.

Conventions. Lasertec's fiscal year ends June (FY2026 = Jul 2025-Jun 2026; Q1 FY2027 = Jul-Sep 2026). Hitachi's fiscal year ends March (FY2025 = Apr 2025-Mar 2026; FY2026 = Apr 2026-Mar 2027; Q2 FY2026 = Jul-Sep 2026). "CC" = company claim, unconfirmed by any outside party. "My calc" = my arithmetic on cited figures. Quality evidence (Parts 1-4) is kept separate from entry-point data (Part 5). Sources with dates: inline and in Part 7.

## Part 2. Hitachi High-Tech: supporting detail

### 2.1 Corporate status (as of 12 Nov 2026)
Takeaway: still 100% owned; no merger-into-parent, sale or relisting plan found. It sits in Hitachi's Connective Industries (CI) sector, Industrial Solutions business unit, reported as "Measurement & Analysis Systems".
Cited findings:
- Hitachi describes "Hitachi High-Tech Corporation, part of the Industrial Solutions Business Unit of Hitachi, Ltd.'s Connective Industries Sector"; FY2025 consolidated revenue ≈ ¥821.7bn. — [Hitachi PR, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/en/press/articles/2026/09/0930/). FY2024 revenue ¥756.5bn. — [Hitachi PR, 24 Apr 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/en/press/articles/2026/04/0424a/)
- Businesses moved in, not out: Hitachi's healthcare unit transferred to Hitachi High-Tech, described as "日立の100％子会社", on 1 Apr 2024 (search snippet) — [Hitachi notice, Jan 2024](https://www.hitachi.co.jp/New/cnews/month/2024/01/f_0126a.pdf); railway-inspection business absorbed 1 Apr 2025 — [Hitachi High-Tech, 7 Feb 2025](https://www.hitachi-hightech.com/jp/ja/media/hsl-20250207_tcm26-252496.pdf); board changes 1 Apr 2026 — [Hitachi High-Tech, 25 Mar 2026](https://www.hitachi-hightech.com/jp/ja/media/nr20260325_tcm26-282661.pdf).
- CI strategy lists "Expansion of the measurement portfolio" as a focused investment; CI CEO Noriharu Amiya: "we have defined a specific roadmap and will steadily execute the moves we should make, including acquisitions". — [Investor Day Q&A, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/content/dam/hitachi/global/en/press/files/2026/06/260610/20260610_06_qa_session_en.pdf)
Inferences: M&AS FY2026 forecast ¥900bn at +10% implies FY2025 ≈ ¥818bn, matching Hitachi High-Tech's ¥821.7bn, so M&AS ≈ Hitachi High-Tech consolidated.
Gaps: no hit for 吸収合併/株式交換/上場 involving Hitachi High-Tech in 2025-26; absence of evidence only.

### 2.3 Products for high-NA EUV, GAA and 3D structures
Cited findings:
- GT2000 (Dec 2023): CD-SEM with functions for high-NA EUV; first CD-SEM with a 100 V ultra-low condition plus high-speed scan for low-damage resist measurement; high-sensitivity backscattered-electron detection for GAA, CFET and 3D memory. — [Hitachi PR, 12 Dec 2023](https://www.hitachi.com/ja-jp/press/articles/2023/12/1212/); [MONOist, 15 Jan 2024](https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/2401/15/news065.html)
- CV7300 high-voltage CD-SEM: maximum acceleration voltage raised to 60 kV for taller 3D NAND (search snippet of 2025 Hitachi material; primary not read).
- CS5000 (2025) CD-SEM for 75-200 mm and non-silicon wafers; metrology SEMs since 1984; >6,000 shipped by end-Nov 2025 (CC); SU9600 FE-SEM (2025), 0.4 nm BSE resolution at 30 kV (CC). — [Hitachi Technology 2026, 28 May 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/en/insights/hitachihyoron/hitachi-technology/2026/13/)
- Multi-beam inspection with ZEISS SMT (91-beam MultiSEM): ~10 nm defects for 2 nm HVM and high-NA EUV, "several dozen times faster" (CC); prototypes found small defects existing optical tools miss (CC); next: evaluation on mass-production wafers and "Process of Record (POR) acquisition". — [Hitachi PR, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/en/press/articles/2026/09/0930/)
- R&D: defect-free reference reconstruction for ≤10 nm defects in a multi-beam SEM; electron-holography dopant/potential mapping with Nagoya University. — [Hitachi Technology 2026 R&D, 28 May 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/en/insights/hitachihyoron/hitachi-technology/2026/20/)
- A semiconductor metrology/inspection development base opened in the Netherlands in July 2026 (search snippet; PDF returned 403). — [Hitachi High-Tech, 30 Jun 2026](https://www.hitachi-hightech.com/jp/ja/media/nr20260630_JP_tcm26-289701.pdf)
Gaps: status of dark-field wafer inspection, review SEM, nano-probing and TEM/FIB lines; any hybrid-bond, HBM or backside-power product; any 2026 CD-SEM launch; Rapidus and TSMC relationships (not found).

### 2.4 AI, software and data (transitions 1, 2, 4, 5)
Cited findings:
- Edge-AI chip (24 Apr 2026): CNN+transformer model, ">10 times" more power-efficient than leading GPUs (CC, GPU figures from catalogue specs); in a semiconductor metrology test, one AI-processed image replaced multi-image overlay metrology "while maintaining the required accuracy" (CC); deployment "will begin", no dates. — [Hitachi PR](https://www.hitachi.com/en/press/articles/2026/04/0424a/)
- Slide 10: ExTOPE, Support AI, Recipe AI for deposition-litho-etch ("80% of the front-end process"); "Development Time approx. 50% Reduction", "Utilization Rate approx. 90%" (CC); target "Semiconductor Manufacturing Market (AI and Software Area)" CAGR 22% to ¥3tn by 2030 (Hitachi citing DATAINTELO, MarketsandMarkets, Technavio et al.). — [CI deck](https://www.hitachi.com/content/dam/hitachi/global/en/press/files/2026/06/260610/20260610_03_connective_en.pdf)
- HMAX qualifies only if it uses data from "Digitalized Assets", uses AI with domain knowledge and "consists of recurring digital services" (Deputy CFO Hatakeyama). — [Q2 FY2026 Q&A, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.hitachi.com/content/dam/hitachi/global/en/press/files/2026/10/261028/261028_summary.pdf)
- Amiya: "the main axis of business has completely shifted from simple 'product delivery' to 'advanced solutions and services' centered on data"; CD-SEM data "is extremely precious" but needs X-ray and other measurements. Tokunaga: discussions with Intel's CEO now extend to a "digital solution utilizing data obtained from that manufacturing equipment". — [Investor Day Q&A](https://www.hitachi.com/content/dam/hitachi/global/en/press/files/2026/06/260610/20260610_06_qa_session_en.pdf)
Inferences: "said" far exceeds "done": done = named partnerships, ZEISS programme, a chip prototype and test case; no software revenue disclosed. Recipe AI targets process-tool recipes, which helps Hitachi's etch side more than its metrology side.
