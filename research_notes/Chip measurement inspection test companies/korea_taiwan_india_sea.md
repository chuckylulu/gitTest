## 1. Summary tables

### Takeaway
Park Systems is the only company in these regions with primary-filing evidence of a global #1 position (AFM, roughly 22-24% of a US$540-590M market by company-cited third-party estimates). It is a small, high-gross-margin, mostly hardware business that is moving from front-end CMP/roughness AFM into advanced-packaging metrology. The other Korean inspection suppliers are cyclical, single-digit-percent-margin or loss-making in 2025-26 and heavily exposed to one customer group: China for Nextin and Auros, substrate makers for Gigavis and Intekplus.

## 3. Park Systems: path through the six transitions

### Takeaway
Park's concrete actions concentrate on transition 3 (packaging): products shipped, the imec JDP and the CEO's packaging statements. It has done little visible on transitions 2, 4, 5 and 6, and none of its statements address measurement by inference with stated uncertainty (transition 1).

### Cited Findings
- SAID: the company aims to be a "comprehensive metrology solutions company" combining AFM with optical techniques (ISE, DHM, WLI) — H1 2026 report. The roadmap lists an "FX Project — the next standard in in-line AFM (higher throughput and detectability)", an "Interferom Project (Hybrid WLI platform)", and advanced packaging/hybrid bonding applications — Value-up plan, May 2026. Industrial tools will migrate from NX to the FX line — [ET News, 12 Jul 2026](https://n.news.naver.com/mnews/article/030/0003446789).
- DONE (packaging): NX-Hybrid WLI orders and shipments, NX-TSH300 Overlay for advanced-package RDL developed, NX-Wafer Plus for wafer bevel commercialised, FX300 with a sliding stage for Cu-pad flatness in back-end; planned: next-generation advanced-package metrology and "Cu pad inspection/metrology technology" — H1 2026 report. The CEO expected 2.5D-packaging mass-production orders in 2026 (company statement) — ZDNet Korea, 29 May 2026. Packaging metrology "below 2 µm can't be handled by existing optical tools" (Executive VP Lee Dong-chun, company claim) — same source.
- Acquisitions: Accurion GmbH (imaging spectroscopic ellipsometry, active vibration isolation), 100% on 4 Oct 2022; Lyncée Tec SA (digital holographic microscopy), announced 6 Jan 2025, plan to build a fully automated DHM for advanced packaging — [Park release, 6 Jan 2025](https://www.parksystems.com/en/news-events/news/news-details.pr250106009). The H1 report dates the deal March 2025 and the value-up plan says 2024 (inconsistent dating). Rocky Mountain Nanotechnology LLC (solid Pt/Pt-Ir AFM probes; tip radius ~10 nm), acquired April 2026 for "parts internalisation" and next-generation nanoprobing — [FN News, 6 May 2026](https://n.news.naver.com/mnews/article/014/0005517677). The CEO said 5 targets (Korea, US, Germany, France) were under review, with ~2 to be disclosed in 2026, all metrology tools or components — [FN News, 1 Jun 2026](https://n.news.naver.com/mnews/article/014/0005529223).
- New research products: NX1 (atomic resolution at room temperature, with Prof. Franz Giessibl; launched 30 Apr 2026); FX40 IR (2 Jun 2026) — IR deck; Naver news list.
- AI/automation: FX40 uses "AI and robotics" to recognise and mount probes and automate experiment set-up (company description) — H1 2026 report. SmartScan/SmartAnalysis software — IR deck.

### Inferences
- T1: Park sells the physical measurement, not the inference. If fabs move to inferred metrology with stated uncertainty, AFM's best role is as the non-destructive reference that calibrates fast optical tools. Hybrid WLI is a step in that direction, but no uncertainty-reporting product was found.
- T4/T5: with service at ~7% and no software line, Park has no data business that would capture value if reference data stays with chipmakers.
- T6: a fall in tools per wafer would hit unit sales. The offset is AFM's possible role as the reference that is hardest to replace by inference.

### Gaps
- No public evidence on Park's on-premises AI, data products or recipe automation for industrial tools. Status of the "2 acquisitions in 2026" after June: could not verify.
