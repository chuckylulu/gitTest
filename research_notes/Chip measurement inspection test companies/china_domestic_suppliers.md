## Q1. Products, nodes verified, customers and results, by company (quality evidence)

### Takeaway
Domestic process-control vendors (Skyverse, Jingce, Dongfang Jingyuan) grow 25-35% a year. They are loss-making or barely profitable on a recurring basis, spend 24-100% of revenue on R&D, and are piling up inventory and customer prepayments. Their volume products sit in unpatterned, packaging, film and mature-node inspection; advanced-node bright-field, OCD, overlay and CD-SEM are mostly "in validation". Test, probe-card and X-ray makers (Changchuan, Huafeng, Maxone, Unicomp) grow 44-63% with high margins and positive cash flow, but sell to very concentrated customers. Every node claim is the company's own.

## Q3. Export controls from the Chinese side

### Takeaway
The EL now covers most of China's front-end metrology makers and Huafeng in test. US controls since Dec 2024 explicitly include metrology and inspection tools. The affiliates rule is suspended only to 10 Jan 2027. None of the companies' own reports names the EL.

### Cited Findings
- **The December 2024 package** (2 Dec 2024):
  - New controls on 24 SME types, "including certain etch, deposition, lithography, ion implantation, annealing, metrology and inspection, and cleaning tools", plus three software types and HBM.
  - An SME foreign-direct-product rule covering China, a footnote-5 FDP rule, and a de minimis rule for any US content.
  - 140 EL additions "spanning PRC tool manufacturers".
  - Compliance date 31 Dec 2024.
  - — [BIS press release](https://media.bis.gov/sites/default/files/documents/FINAL%20DOC%20Nat%20Sec%20Action%20Rls%20Dec%202%2024.pdf)
- **EL entries** (effective 2 Dec 2024; FR 2024-28267), all under a presumption of denial:
  - Skyverse: Beijing, Chengdu, Guangzhou, Shanghai, Shenzhen Qianhai, Wuhan, Xiamen and Zhuhai entities; "Skyverse a.k.a. Nanolighting / Shenzhen Nanolighting Technology"; Skyverse Limited ("Hong Kong Zhongke Feice"); Skyverse Pte. Ltd. (Singapore).
  - Dongfang Jingyuan Electron, plus Beijing Jingyuan, Shenzhen Jingyuan and Oriental Crystal (Qingdao and Shanghai).
  - **Shenzhen SiCarrier Technologies, aliases including "Huawei Starlight Engineering Department"**, and Xinkailai Industrial Machinery.
  - Shanghai Jingce Semiconductor ("Shanghai Precision Measurement", "PMISH"), not the Wuhan parent.
  - Raintree/RSIC and Ruili Microelectronics Equipment.
  - Shanghai Yuwei ("Yuweitek").
  - Beijing Huafeng Test & Control (AccoTEST) and three Huafeng affiliates.
  - SwaySure is the only footnote-5 match.
  - Not matched in a keyword check of the rule text: Changchuan, Jingzhida, Maxone, Semishare, Unicomp, Semitronix, Wintech, TZTEK, Angstrom. The last ~17,000 characters of the page were not read, and later EL rules were not checked.
  - — [Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-12-05/html/2024-28267.htm)
- **Affiliates (50%) rule:**
  - In effect from 29 Sep 2025, then suspended from 10 Nov 2025.
  - On 5 Nov 2026 the suspension was extended to 10 Jan 2027 (FR doc 2026-22793, published 9 Nov 2026), alongside a US-China truce to the same date.
  - Squire Patton Boggs: it "moves the cliff rather than removing it", with pressure from the new Congress.
  - — [Thompson Hine, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/11/bis-extends-affiliates-rule-until-january-2027/); [Squire Patton Boggs, Nov 2026](https://www.squirepattonboggs.com/insights/publications/bis-extends-suspension-of-affiliates-rule/)
- **Netherlands:** licence requirement for specific measurement and inspection equipment from 1 Apr 2025. — [NL Times, 15 Jan 2025](https://nltimes.nl/2025/01/15/restrictions-chip-machine-exportations-dutch-companies-asml-asmi)
- **Japan:** 23 equipment categories, including inspection, controlled from 23 Jul 2023. — [Baker McKenzie](https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/japan-to-strengthen-its-export-control-regulations-to-control-various-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment/). A further 21 items added in 2025 (single consultancy source, unverified). — [Timewell](https://timewell.jp/en/columns/japan-semiconductor-21-items-control)
- **China's domestic-tool rule:** new capacity must use ≥50% domestic tools, relaxed for advanced lines (unpublished; sources). — [Reuters via Yahoo, 30 Dec 2025](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/exclusive-china-mandates-50-domestic-090850539.html)
- **What the companies say:**
  - "Trade frictions" could cut supply of "some core components". — [Skyverse FY2025 annual report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-25/1225182753.PDF)
  - "Some core components are single-source", with export controls a risk. — [Huafeng H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-31/1225534170.PDF)
  - A word search of all 24 FY2025 and H1 2026 reports found no use of "实体清单" (Entity List).
- **January 2026 easing:** licence review for advanced chips to China moved to case-by-case; this covers chips, not tools. — [FR 2026-00789](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-15/html/2026-00789.htm) (search summary)

### Inferences
- **Component dependence:** a footnote-5 or FDP designation applied to these companies' suppliers would hit lasers, DUV optics and electron sources hardest. SiCarrier's "self-developed DUV sources" claims are aimed at exactly that.
- **Easing risk:** an easing would help KLA more than the domestic firms, since about 30% of KLA's FY2026 revenue already came from China.

### Gaps
- Part-level import dependence. Japan's 2025-26 list text. China's countermeasures (rare-earth and other export controls). Reuters (June 2026) reported that about 75 more chip and SME entities were awaiting EL listing. — [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/06/18/deepseek-cxmt-among-chinese-firms-awaiting-us-entity-list-action-as-washington-weighs-trade-risks/224127) (page blocked; snippet only)
