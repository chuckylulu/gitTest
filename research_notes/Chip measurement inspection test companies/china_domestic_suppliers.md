## Q1. What each company ships, the nodes, the customers and the results (quality evidence)

### Takeaway
Domestic process-control suppliers (Skyverse, Jingce, Dongfang Jingyuan) are growing 25-35% a year. They spend 24-40% of revenue on R&D (Dongfang Jingyuan about 100%), have negative operating cash flow, and their inventories and contract liabilities are swelling. Volume products sit mainly at mature nodes and in packaging; advanced-node tools are still mostly "in validation". Test, probe-card and X-ray makers (Changchuan, Huafeng, Maxone, Unicomp) are growing 45-65% with high margins and positive cash flow.
