# Valuation cross-section: chip measurement, inspection and test companies (latest close on or before 12 Nov 2026)

**Research date:** 12 November 2026 (data pulled 12:00-12:30 UTC). **Snapshot convention:** Asian listings (Tokyo, KOSDAQ, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Taipei/TPEx) = close of **12-Nov-2026**; US and European listings = close of **11-Nov-2026**, because the US session of 12 Nov had not opened and the European sessions were still trading when the data were pulled. **One basis for all companies:** company figures come from a single provider, stockanalysis.com (SA). SA's fundamentals and consensus come from S&P Global Market Intelligence, and its analyst-rating detail from TipRanks. Using one provider lets companies in different countries be compared on one method. Figures are as reported by the source unless marked "my calc".

**Definitions.** TTM = last four reported quarters (the period end is shown in each row).
- **P/E TTM** = price / TTM GAAP or IFRS EPS.
- **Fwd P/E NTM** = SA's "Forward PE". SA does not state the basis on the page, but the arithmetic points to next twelve months. KLA: $161.01 / 25.91 gives an implied EPS of $6.21, which lies between the FY6/27 consensus ($5.76) and FY6/28 ($7.09). AMAT: $395.08 / 22.27 gives $17.74, below the FY10/27 consensus of $18.66. That fits a twelve-month window that still includes the unreported Oct-26 quarter. For US-style reporters SA states that EPS and forward P/E are non-GAAP adjusted.
- **P/E FY1 / FY2** = my calc: price / SA consensus EPS for the first and second unreported fiscal years. The fiscal year-ends are shown.
- **EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, dividend yield** = SA TTM values. **52-week range** = SA intraday range.
- **YTD / 1-yr change** = my calc from SA daily closes, which are split-adjusted but not dividend-adjusted. YTD runs from the last 2025 close; 1-yr from the close on or before the same date in 2025.
- **\* = European listing.** SA's statistics pages were captured intraday on 12 Nov, so I rescaled market cap, EV and price-based multiples to the 11-Nov close. The factor is close / intraday price: Technoprobe 23.70/22.80, Comet 317.20/318.60, PVA TePla 26.38/27.44. EV moves by the same amount as market cap.
- **Analysts** = number in the SA/S&P rating panel / number contributing to the FY1 EPS estimate.
- **Business quality is not assessed and nothing here is a recommendation.**

## 1. Master snapshot: 20 priority tickers

### Takeaway
Most process-control, tester and probe-card names have fallen 40-60% from their late-June 2026 highs. They now trade at 18-27x consensus earnings for the next twelve months (NTM). ## 3. What the price assumes (my calculations; inputs shown)

### Takeaway
Suppose forward P/Es return to each company's own 5-year average. A 10% annual share-price return would then need only about 0-5% annual NTM EPS growth for ASML, Advantest, Teradyne, Lasertec, Onto, Camtek, FormFactor, JEOL and Technoprobe, and about 10-13% for KLA, AMAT, Hitachi, Bruker and TMO. Both are well below the 20-50% FY2 consensus EPS growth for the semiconductor names. The exposure is in the multiple. With NTM EPS unchanged, a fall to each name's 5-year minimum forward P/E would take KLA down 52%, AMAT 43%, Advantest 57% and Camtek 41%.

### Method (reader can redo)
1. NTM EPS now = price / SA fwd P/E.
2. EPS needed in n years (NTM basis) = price x 1.10^n / 5-yr average fwd P/E.
   - Required EPS CAGR = 1.10 x (fwd P/E now / 5-yr avg)^(1/n) - 1. This does not depend on the price.
   - Dividends are ignored. Yields are 0-2%, so this slightly overstates the hurdle.
   - **Worked example, KLA:** NTM EPS = 161.01 / 25.91 = $6.21. EPS needed in 3 years = 161.01 x 1.331 / 26.17 = $8.19, which is 1.10 x (25.91/26.17)^(1/3) - 1 = 9.6% a year. EPS needed in 5 years = 161.01 x 1.6105 / 26.17 = $9.91 (9.8% a year). For comparison, consensus FY6/28 EPS is $7.09.
3. **Stress case:** the same calculation with the 5-year minimum fwd P/E. "Downside on unchanged EPS" = 5-yr min / current - 1.
4. **Margin check.**
   - EBIT needed in year 3 = (market cap x 1.331 + net debt) / 5-yr average EV/EBIT (from SA quarterly data). Names are excluded where negative values make the average meaningless.
   - Revenue in year 3 = last reported fiscal-year revenue x (1 + S&P 3-year consensus revenue CAGR, SA "Revenue Growth Forecast (3Y)")^3.
   - Required EBIT margin = EBIT / revenue, compared with the TTM operating margin.
   - **Worked example, KLA:** (209.79 x 1.331 + 1.25) / 25.2 = $11.13bn; 13.58 x 1.2405^3 = $25.92bn; required margin 43% against 42.6% TTM.
   - **Assumptions:** net debt and share count stay constant. Year-3 revenue is the FY+3 level implied by consensus. For calendar-year companies FY+3 ends before the 3-year horizon, which biases the required margin upward.
