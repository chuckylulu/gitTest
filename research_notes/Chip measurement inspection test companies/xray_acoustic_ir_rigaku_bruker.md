## 3. Supporting detail

### 3.1 Methods actually used in production today

**Takeaway:** Different methods have reached production to different degrees:
- **X-ray film metrology** (XRF, XRR, XRD/HR-XRD) is a production technique at leading logic and memory fabs.
- **Transmission CD-SAXS** is entering memory mass production in 2026.
- **3D X-ray** (CT, laminography) is used for alignment, overlay, warpage and coarse voids. X-ray TSV-void inspection for HBM is only at evaluation stage.
- **Hybrid bonding at 4-9 µm pitch:** independent 2025-2026 reporting says production control relies on pre-bond optical, interferometric and AFM metrology, infrared alignment checks and electrical test structures. Acoustic, infrared and X-ray imaging "typically lack the resolution or contrast" for nanoscale bond voids. Acoustic inspection runs after assembly at ~12 µm resolution.

**(1) Void and delamination detection after hybrid bonding**
- **Process window** [SE1] (10 Nov 2026):
  - Production hybrid-bond pitches are "4 to 9 µm", replacing 35 µm micro-bumps.
  - Defect size requirements are "as low as 50 nm" (Onto).
  - Cu pad recess is 2-5 nm and must be held within 2 nm; CMP roughness target is 0.5 nm RMS; anneal is 350°C for 2 h.
- **Pre-bond control** [SE1]:
  - Macro inspection on 100% of wafers; micro-inspection samples only "two to five wafers per lot" because it is slow (Microtronic).
  - Bright-field and dark-field automated optical inspection (Onto).
  - White-light or phase-shift interferometry (Bruker, KLA, Onto, Zygo).
  - OCD and AFM for nm-scale dishing; Bruker AFP for large areas.
- **Post-bond control** [SE1]:
  - "IR metrology verifies alignment" (IR penetrates silicon).
  - ZEISS CT and laminography, but ZEISS "does not typically detect voids in hybrid bonds".
  - Bruker XRDI full wafer at ~20 wph (company-quoted).
  - X-ray at 150 nm resolution "has been demonstrated" (2026 ICEP-HBS paper).
  - Nordson acoustic micro-imaging: 165-200 MHz, 12 µm resolution, after assembly.
  - "No single technique" covers resolution, sensitivity, coverage and throughput.
- **Aug 2025 view** [SE2]:
  - IR, SAM and X-ray "typically lack the resolution or contrast" for nanoscale hybrid-bond voids.
  - Micron uses daisy chains "to evaluate thousands of connections at once" for HBM.
  - Modus Test does resistance mapping at "sub-milliohm accuracy".
  - "The industry still lacks standard test structures or qualification protocols for hybrid bonding" (Micron).
  - Onto and PDF Solutions train AI models on metrology data; adaptive sampling is "key to scaling inspection".
- **Acoustic limits:** water coupling (PVA TePla uses deionised water) [P2]; resolution trades against penetration depth [SE1]. Gaps under ~0.1 µm thickness pass ultrasound [SE1].

**(2) TSV and micro-bump inspection in HBM and 2.5D**
- Rigaku AX-3000 beta for TSV fill, voids and bond integrity, with evaluation units in 4Q 2026 [R3][R9].
- Comet CA20 has "successful qualifications" (unnamed customers) [C1].
- Excillum NanoTube N3 + SFA in-fab system, demo in 2026 [E2].
- PVA TePla SAM markets HBM stacks and CoWoS/SoIC applications [P2].
- HBM yield also leans on design-for-test (BIST, boundary scan) for TSVs and micro-bumps [SE3, 11 Aug 2026 article summary].
- I found no production adoption figures for CT or laminography at named HBM makers.

**(3) High-aspect-ratio holes and channels (3D NAND/DRAM)**
- Rigaku T-SAXS (CD-3200T): adoption in a memory mass-production line has begun; next generation (3300T) in development. Rigaku aims to reach its projected 2030 revenue for this product in 2028 [R1] (co. claim).
- Bruker lists "advanced memory high-aspect-ratio 3D metrology" as an application of its X-ray metrology line [B11] (co. claim).
- Throughput is limited by source brightness: Rigaku is working "to improve measurement speed through the development of high-intensity X-ray sources" [R9].

**(4) Film composition, thickness and strain at GAA**
- Rigaku: HR-XRD (XD-3300) is POR at "a majority of customers"; MF-3400 GEN4 XRF/XRD replaces GEN3 from 2027; ONYX XRF for Al-O dipole ultrathin films is in evaluation at 3 logic and 2 memory makers [R1][R3][R9].
- Bruker: QCVelox-HR (HRXRD+XRR) and SIRIUS-RF (µXRF+XRR), with "nanosheet thickness and composition metrology" listed [B11].
- All co. claims.

**Market size and share**
- Yole, via Rigaku: Rigaku ~40% of X-ray metrology in 2025 [R3][R4]. The chart labels "532" and "498" have no legible units (could not verify).
- Rigaku's 2030 SAMs for its new products sum to ~US$1.05bn (calc.), and it targets >50% overall [R1]. The parts are: GI-SAXS $100m, HR-XRD $100m, XRF/XRD $200m, light-element XRF $100m, T-SAXS $100m, XRT $50m, packaging AXI $300m, hybrid metrology $100m. Co. estimates, unconfirmed.
- No independent market size found for SAM, IR or X-ray package inspection.

**Inferences:**
- For hybrid bonding, the measurement that decides whether a stack moves on is currently electrical (daisy chains, resistance maps) plus pre-bond surface metrology. Buried-interface imaging below ~10 µm pitch is an unfilled gap rather than an owned position.
- In front-end X-ray film metrology, Rigaku is the nearest to a tool-of-record owner.

- **Regions, FY2025** [R1]: Japan ¥27.4bn, Americas ¥23.4bn, EMEA ¥13.8bn, Asia ex-China ¥16.8bn, China ¥12.5bn. On China, management says its share has "historically been relatively low, but we have nevertheless shipped a considerable number of systems there"; RTC-China opens in 2026 [R4].

- **Onto alliance** [R10][R22]:
  - Joint hybrid-metrology work since May 2025; joint new-market SAM of ≥US$300m by 2030.

  - R&D ¥7.3bn (FY2025) → ¥8.5bn guided (FY2026); capex ¥7.7bn guided (FY2026).

**Inference:** the Onto stake makes Onto both Rigaku's sales channel into fabs and a possible future bidder once the standstill ends. That is an option, not a disclosed intention.

### 3.3 Bruker: further detail

**Takeaway:**
- Semiconductor metrology is ~$300m with ~30% EBIT margin. These are Citi's numbers, which the CEO called "directionally correct" but did not confirm [B7].
- It is now fast-booking, but it sits inside a ~$3.5bn group dominated by life science.
- If ~$300m is right, it is ~8.5% of revenue and ~28% of Nano (calc.). Even 30% growth adds only ~2.5 points to group growth (calc.).
