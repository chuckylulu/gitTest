# X-ray, acoustic and infrared inspection of buried features and bonded interfaces: Rigaku (268A.T), Bruker (BRKR) and field scan

Research date: 12 November 2026. "Latest" means latest published by this date. VERSION 1, written after an interruption: sections marked [INCOMPLETE] are still being researched. The web-search budget ran out part-way through, so later additions come from direct reads of primary documents.

Conventions: "co. claim, unconfirmed" = a company statement that none of my outside sources confirms. "calc." = my own arithmetic on the cited figures. SPC = Rigaku's Semiconductor Process Control Instruments category. JEP = Rigaku Joint Evaluation Program (tool installed at the customer, revenue booked when the evaluation completes). POR = process of record.

## 3. Supporting detail

### 3.1 Methods in production today [INCOMPLETE: independent conference/press evidence still being gathered]

Takeaway: the only production evidence in hand comes from supplier disclosures. They say X-ray film metrology (XRF/XRD/XRR, HR-XRD) is POR at leading logic and memory fabs, that transmission CD-SAXS is entering memory mass production in 2026, and that X-ray TSV-void inspection for HBM is only at beta/evaluation stage in 4Q 2026. I have not yet verified anything independent on acoustic or infrared inspection of hybrid-bond interfaces.

- Film composition, thickness and strain at GAA: Rigaku XTRAIA XD (HR-XRD, Si/SiGe superlattice) "POR with a majority of customers", aiming to reach its projected 2030 revenue in 2028. XTRAIA MF GEN4 (XRF/XRD) to replace GEN3 from 2027. ONYX 3200 (XRF, light elements, Al ultrathin film) "steadily penetrating the market" (Rigaku, 11 Nov 2026; co. claims).
- High-aspect-ratio holes: XTRAIA CD-3200T (T-SAXS) "memory mass-production line adoption begins", 3300T in development. Shipped with Onto's model-based software (Rigaku, 7 Aug and 11 Nov 2026; co. claim).
- GAA/CFET 3D shape: low-energy GI-SAXS (CD-3200G) still in development, "orders secured for Logic (plan to ship in FY27 1Q)". Proof of concept at the NanoTerasu soft-X-ray beamline (7 Aug 2026; co. claim).
- 3D DRAM SiGe multilayer stress and dislocations: X-ray topography XRTmicron in 300 mm in-line fab version (XTRAIA XT-3000) (7 Aug 2026; co. claim).
- TSV/micro-bump: Rigaku AXI beta for TSV voids (4Q 2026). Comet CA20 X-ray for advanced packaging, with "successful qualifications" (20 Oct 2026; co. claim). Bruker automated AFM "used in the production of HBM" (10-K, 27 Feb 2026).
- Acoustic: PVA TePla metrology orders +76% in 9M 2026 (acoustic, optical and wet-chemical; split not disclosed).
- Market size and share: Yole (via Rigaku) has Rigaku at ~40% of X-ray metrology in 2025. Rigaku's own SAM for its 2030 new-product roadmap is ~US$1.0bn (sum of 8 SAMs: $100m GI-SAXS, $100m HR-XRD, $200m XRF/XRD multifunction, $100m light-element XRF, $100m T-SAXS, $50m XRT, $300m AXI packaging, $100m hybrid metrology). Co. estimate, unconfirmed.

### 3.2 Rigaku Holdings: detail

Takeaway: a profitable X-ray specialist in which SPC (~30% of revenue, ~29% operating margin in FY2025) is growing fast on memory and new GAA products and is guided to give >50% of FY2026 operating profit. Group earnings in 2026 are depressed by the academic instrument business, special charges and a heavy back-end load on Q4. Ownership changed in 2026 from Carlyle to Onto (27%) plus free float.

Cited findings:
- 3Q FY2026 (11 Nov 2026): orders +66% YoY, revenue ¥21.1bn (+12.7%), OP ¥1.5bn (7.4% margin; 9.3pt better excluding special factors). Special factors: ¥0.6bn US doubtful-account allowance and ¥0.75bn impairment at MILabs (EMEA). SPC 3Q revenue ¥5.5bn (+29.4%), OP margin 14.0%, orders +189%. 1-3Q SPC revenue ¥18.3bn (+21.4%), orders +117%, OP margin 15.1%. Multipurpose 1-3Q revenue ¥23.7bn (-10.3%), OP margin 3.7%. Components & services ¥18.6bn (+4.0%), services +15% (3Q +20%).
- FY2026 guidance unchanged: revenue ¥101.0bn (+7.2%), OP ¥19.4bn (+16.1%), profit ¥12.5bn, EBITDA ¥25.3bn (25.1%). Full-year orders guided around +30%, SPC orders +70%. "In 4Q we expect to reach our guidance target excluding special factors" (11 Nov 2026).
- FY2027 direction: backlog at end-December 2026 >+30% YoY. SPC: "Turning strong orders into >30% growth". SPC described as a ">¥30bn business ... growing by 30% annually" (co. claim). Mid-term plan FY12/27: revenue ¥115-120bn, adj. EBITDA ¥31.5-32.5bn, adj. OP ¥26.5-27.5bn, SPC ¥35.0-36.5bn. Aug 2026 slide: SPC 2030 revenue target "approx. JPY 100 billion including existing products" (co. claim).
- SPC history: ¥18.6bn (FY2023), ¥23.3bn (FY2024), ¥27.8bn (FY2025, +19%). FY2025 logic declined (US IDMs held back), memory "became very large", and the third pillar is sales to wafer-fab-equipment makers (FY2025 transcript, 13 Feb 2026).
- JEPs: 14 in 2025 (5 completed); 14 under way in mid-2026, of which 8 complete in 2026. New products were planned at ~¥14bn of FY2026 revenue (¥4bn in 1H, ~¥10bn in 2H): mainly XTRAIA XD, MF-3400, ONYX and CD-3200T (7 Aug 2026).
- Regions FY2025: Japan ¥27.4bn, Americas ¥23.4bn, EMEA ¥13.8bn, Asia ex-China ¥16.8bn, China ¥12.5bn. End markets FY2025: semiconductor/electronic components ¥33.3bn, academia/government ¥24.8bn, other industries ¥15.7bn, services ¥15.1bn, life science ¥5.0bn (11 Nov 2026 appendix). Management on China: share "historically been relatively low, but we have nevertheless shipped a considerable number of systems there". RTC-China (Shanghai) to open in 2026 (7 Aug 2026).
- EUV multilayer mirrors: FY2025 sales fell ~¥1bn ("really a single supply chain"). Customer EUV inventory "should be depleted during next year" (11 Nov 2026).
- Onto alliance (21 Apr 2026): joint hybrid-metrology development since May 2025 (CD-SAXS + Onto analytical software). The scope extends to R&D, go-to-market, cross-selling, after-sales services and Asian manufacturing. Joint new market SAM of at least US$300m by 2030. Onto: standstill, transfer restriction, pre-emptive right to keep its ratio, one non-executive director (Michael Plisinski). Onto paid ~US$720m and uses fair-value accounting with no consolidation (Onto 8-K, Aug 2026). Onto moved the shares to Onto Innovation Japan on 1 Oct 2026.
- Research and capital committed in 2026: new 3-year imec programme signed 9 Apr 2026 (CFET metrology, EUV mask degradation, advanced-packaging non-destructive inspection, 3D DRAM). Tohoku University co-creation lab from 1 Aug 2026 (3 years, soft X-ray). R&D ¥7.3bn in FY2025 → ¥8.5bn guided for FY2026. Capex ¥7.7bn guided for FY2026.

Inferences:
- At ~¥28bn, SPC is about a third of the size of KLA's or Onto's semiconductor metrology lines [comparison not sourced here; other researchers cover KLA and Onto]. The Onto stake makes Onto both a channel and a potential acquirer once the standstill ends. This is a strategic option, not a fact.
- Guided Q4 OP of ¥15.3bn would be about 79% of the year. That is the main near-term earnings risk and also the main near-term test.

### 3.3 Bruker: detail [INCOMPLETE: product-level semiconductor detail]

Takeaway: semiconductor metrology is a small (~$300m, per a Citi analyst whose figure management called directionally correct but did not confirm), higher-margin, now fast-booking part of a ~$3.5bn life-science-heavy group. Cited findings:

Q3 2026 Nano revenue $263.1m (vs $266.5m). 9M Nano $770.4m, a low-single-digit decline in constant currency.

Inferences: if semiconductor metrology is ~$300m, it is ~8-9% of group revenue and ~28% of Nano (calc.). Even 30% semiconductor growth would add only ~2-3 points to group growth.
