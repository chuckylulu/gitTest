# Cadence Design Systems: business quality and forward strategy (as of 12 Nov 2026)

Scope: business facts only (share moves appear only to date the July 2026 sell-off). "Company claim, unconfirmed" = no outside confirmation found. Assessments sit under "Inferences"; "my calc" = derived from cited figures. The shared WebSearch budget ran out mid-session.

## Summary for the report writer

### Takeaway
Cadence (FY2025 revenue US$5.30bn, +14%; 2026 guide US$6.39-6.42bn, ~21%, of which ~US$160-170m is Hexagon D&E, so ~18% organic, my calc) is the No. 2 EDA vendor; the Big-3 hold >85% of a ~US$18bn EDA+IP market (SemiAnalysis, May 2026). Provisional scores (inferences): (a) Position: strong but uneven. It owns judges in custom/analog (Virtuoso/Spectre) and emulation (Palladium ~55-60% share). In digital sign-off its tools are foundry-certified second sources behind golden incumbents (PrimeTime 90%+, Calibre 85%+). SD&A mixes certified solvers (Clarity, Sigrity, Celsius; MSC Nastran, used for FAA/EASA certification loads) with pre/post-processing (BETA CAE ANSA/META) that agents may commoditise. (b) Lead: real in analog, emulation, digital implementation and IP. No independent benchmarks of its AI or GPU claims. (c) Revenue model: ~78-80% recurring, on 2-3-year time-based licences (84% in 2023); ~20-22% up-front (hardware, IP, SD&A term licences); ~9% services. Seat counts are undisclosed. Agent pricing is moving to "Coins" and virtual-engineer subscriptions. (d) Financials: non-GAAP operating margin 44.6% (GAAP 28.2%), FCF 30% of revenue (2025). ROIC was ~23% in 2025 and ~16% trailing after the D&E goodwill (my calc). Net debt ~US$1.2bn. No customer ≥10%. China was 13% of 2025 revenue and 16% in Q3 2026. (e) Resilience: good in steady growth. In a 2028 financing pause, the record-high hardware and up-front IP are most exposed; the RPO of US$8.2bn (40% due in months 13-36) cushions it. Export-control exposure is material and recurring.
Transitions (said vs done): (1) Pricing: management says subscriptions stay the anchor, with "virtual engineer" agent pricing on top. Done so far: Coin-based Super Agent terms (assigned per user); no agentic revenue disclosed; consumption excluded from 2026 guidance. (2) GPU: Millennium M2000 shipped (2025) and 14 GPU tools are slated for 2026, but sign-off judges Tempus/Pegasus are not on the list and all speed-ups are vendor claims. (3) Agents/APIs: four Super Agents are in early access or production and Maestro models enter early access in Q4 2026; the strategy is to be the engine that any agent calls. (4) Open source: Kimi K3 (17 Jul 2026; 45 nm; simulation only) cut the stock ~9.5% in a day. The moat is foundry PDK access and certification at advanced nodes; Cadence has no open-source strategy. (5) Learned models are positioned as explorers, with deterministic solvers as judges. (6) Acceptance: certified at TSMC A16/A14, Samsung 2nm and Intel 18A-P/14A. Its public ISO 26262 tool certificates date from 2017. The prior that vendors keep growing through 2029 is supported by the backlog, industry growth (+12%) and forecasts; the main contrary signals are low-end pricing pressure from open tools and hardware cyclicality.

## 1. Position (test a): which products are sign-off judges, against which incumbents

### Takeaway
Cadence's sign-off tools are accepted at every leading foundry. Acceptance is necessary but not exclusive: in digital timing and physical verification the golden references remain Synopsys PrimeTime and Siemens Calibre. Cadence's owned judges are custom/analog (Virtuoso/Spectre) and hardware emulation (Palladium).

### Inferences
- Provisional (a): Cadence owns judges in analog/custom (Virtuoso/Spectre) and emulation (Palladium, a hardware constraint). It is a certified challenger in digital sign-off (Tempus/Quantus/Voltus/Pegasus against PrimeTime/StarRC/RedHawk/Calibre), where certification is shared by all three vendors and decides nothing on its own.
- MSC Nastran adds an aerospace certification-loads judge with a 50-year track record. It is not unique: Simcenter Nastran shares the lineage.
- By standard description, BETA CAE ANSA/META, Pointwise-type meshing and Optimality are pre/post-processing or optimisation layers, i.e. "gap fillers" in the brief's framework (product descriptions not re-verified this session). Reality DT and OpenEye are decision support, not regulatory judges.

### Gaps
- No third-party share for Tempus, Voltus, Quantus, Pegasus, Spectre, Clarity, Celsius, Sigrity or Fidelity.
- No named customer confirms Cadence-only digital sign-off. No TSMC OIP 2026 award list was retrieved.

## 4. Revenue model (test c)

### Takeaway
About four-fifths of revenue recurs, through 2-3-year time-based licences, maintenance, royalties and leases. The rest is up-front: hardware, IP and multi-year SD&A licences. Services (labour hours) are ~9%. Revenue scales with engineers and design projects rather than metered compute, and agent pricing is just beginning to change that.

### Cited Findings
- Mix: FY2025 Core EDA 70% / IP 14% / SD&A 16%; Q3 2026 69/15/16. Three-year CAGRs to FY2025: Core EDA 11%, IP 19%, SD&A 25%. Q3 2026 y/y growth: Core EDA +20%, IP +24%, SD&A +30%. — [CFO commentary Q4'25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000013/cfocommentary02172026ex9902.htm); [Q3'26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000116/cfocommentary10262026ex9902.htm); [Motley Fool Q3'26](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/26/cadence-cdns-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Recurring share: 80% (2025) vs 83% (2024) and 84% (2023); Q3 2026 79%; trailing-twelve-month 78%. Up-front revenue comes "primarily" from hardware, individual IP licences and software licences longer than one year. SD&A revenue is "recognized at a point in time and cash is typically collected over the term". — [10-K](https://s206.q4cdn.com/597110084/files/doc_downloads/2026/Mar/CDNS-FY-2025-Form-10-K.pdf); [Q3'26 CFO](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000116/cfocommentary10262026ex9902.htm)
- Licences: mainly time-based, "generally two to three years", with remix rights and some perpetual. Cloud arrangements "may include usage-based terms". IP carries royalties. Hardware is sold or leased (leasing is immaterial). — [10-K](https://s206.q4cdn.com/597110084/files/doc_downloads/2026/Mar/CDNS-FY-2025-Form-10-K.pdf)
- Services: US$475.2m in 2025 (9.0% of revenue; 6.3% in 2023), with cost of services at 43% of services revenue. 9M 2026: US$425.1m (9.1%). — [10-K](https://s206.q4cdn.com/597110084/files/doc_downloads/2026/Mar/CDNS-FY-2025-Form-10-K.pdf); [Q3'26 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000116/cdns10262026ex9901.htm)
- Hardware: revenue is not disclosed. Record years in 2025 (30+ new customers) and 2026 (12 new logos in Q2). Devgan: "every year is a record for hardware". Palladium software fees are put at US$2-5m per system per year (SemiAnalysis; its own figures conflict). — [Motley Fool Q4'25](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/17/cadence-cdns-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/); [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/eda-market-primer)
- Backlog/RPO: US$5.8bn (2022), 6.0 (2023), 6.8 (2024), 7.8 (2025), 8.2 (Q3 2026). The RPO includes US$0.9bn of non-cancellable commitments with products not yet selected; of the remainder 58% falls due within 12 months and 40% in months 13-36. About 64% of 2026 revenue was expected from opening backlog. — [Q3'26 CFO](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000116/cfocommentary10262026ex9902.htm); [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/813672/000081367226000121/cdns-20260930.htm)
- Seats: no counts disclosed. Devgan (17 Feb 2026): "this is a misnomer that we are like seat count limited"; AI tools "are increasing the usage of our tools". Spend is US$80-150k per engineer per year at fabless firms (SemiAnalysis); EDA is ~11-12% of chip R&D budgets (Cadence IR, 9 Jun 2026, via TIKR). — [Motley Fool Q4'25](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/17/cadence-cdns-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/); [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/cadence-design-systems-just-unveiled-its-agentic-ai-revenue-model-heres-why-it-could-reset-the-stocks-growth-ceiling)

### Inferences
- Provisional (c): good. Revenue is mostly recurring, with multi-year, prepaid-style visibility and only ~9% labour-hour services, but its unit of value is still the engineer-project. The ~20-22% up-front slice (hardware, IP, SD&A) adds cyclicality. The falling recurring share (84% to 78%) reflects faster growth in hardware, IP and SD&A, not weaker software.

### Gaps
- Hardware revenue share; seat or user counts; share of revenue under usage-based cloud terms.

## 5. Financial strength (test d)

### Takeaway
Elite margins and cash conversion, moderate leverage after D&E. GAAP margins are falling because of amortisation, stock compensation and deal costs, and the D&E goodwill has diluted ROIC.

### Cited Findings
- FY2025: revenue US$5,296.8m (+14%); GAAP operating income US$1,492.0m (28.2%; it was 29.1% in 2024, the dip driven by the US$128.5m BIS/DOJ charge); non-GAAP operating margin 44.6%; net income US$1,108.9m; non-GAAP gross margin 88.0%; operating cash flow US$1,728.8m; capex US$141.9m; FCF US$1,587m (30.0%, my calc); stock compensation US$455.2m (8.6% of revenue); buybacks US$925m; ~13,800 employees. — [10-K](https://s206.q4cdn.com/597110084/files/doc_downloads/2026/Mar/CDNS-FY-2025-Form-10-K.pdf); [CFO Q4'25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000013/cfocommentary02172026ex9902.htm)

- Balance sheet (30 Sep 2026): cash US$1,452m (~15% in the US vs 71% at YE2025). Debt principal US$2,650m: notes due 2027 (US$500m, 4.2%), 2029 (US$1bn, 4.3%) and 2034 (US$1bn, 4.7%), plus US$150m on a US$1.25bn revolver (covenant ≤3.5x debt/EBITDA). On a trailing basis to Q3 2026, measured on Q3 invested capital of ~US$8.06bn, ROIC is ≈16% GAAP and ≈29% non-GAAP. Net debt ~US$1.2bn ≈ 0.4x trailing non-GAAP operating income.
- Concentration: no customer ≥10% in 2023-2025. "45% of our business is coming from the system companies" (Devgan, Q3'26). 2025 geography: US 44%, other Americas 3%, China 13% (US$680.0m, +18.7%), other Asia 19%, EMEA 15%, Japan 6%. ### Inferences
- Provisional (d): very strong. Margins, FCF and low customer concentration are top-tier for software. Watch items: the ~16-point GAAP/non-GAAP gap, rising DSO, D&E-driven ROIC dilution, and China at 13-16% of revenue.

## 6. Resilience (test e): cycle, AI scenarios, export controls

### Takeaway
Demand tracks design starts and R&D budgets more than capex, and contracts run for years, which dampens shocks. The cyclical part of the business (hardware, IP, SD&A up-front) is at record highs, and China is a repeat policy risk.

### Cited Findings
- Cyclicality (10-K risk factors): demand "dependent upon the commencement of new design projects"; hardware and IP revenue is "inherently difficult to predict". Wall: hardware "could be lumpy"; the 2026 guide was "prudent" for second-half hardware; inventory is being built against backlog with "no capacity or supply chain constraints". — [10-K](https://s206.q4cdn.com/597110084/files/doc_downloads/2026/Mar/CDNS-FY-2025-Form-10-K.pdf); [Webull Q3'26](https://www.webull.com/news/15818016766808064); [Motley Fool Q4'25](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/17/cadence-cdns-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/)
- AI exposure (company): hyperscalers "Google and Meta and Microsoft and Amazon and Tesla" are doing more designs. Devgan cites a TSMC roadmap implying 48x larger chips in five years. — [Webull Q3'26](https://www.webull.com/news/15818016766808064); [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/transcripts/262240586-tradingkey)
- History (flag: old): SemiAnalysis notes revenue fell 36% in one year under CEO Fister (2004-08 era), alongside a licensing-model change. — [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/eda-market-primer)
- China and export controls: a BIS licence requirement for EDA to China ran from 23 May to 2 Jul 2025; China fell to 9% of Q2 2025 revenue. On 28 Jul 2025 Cadence pleaded guilty to conspiracy over 2015-21 sales to NUDT (Entity List); penalties were criminal ~US$118m and BIS >US$95m, net >US$140m. It paid US$140.6m in 2025 and has ongoing audit/compliance duties (three-year probation per Wikipedia, unverified). BIS's affiliates (50%) rule of 29 Sep 2025 was suspended to 9 Nov 2026, then extended on 5 Nov 2026 to 10 Jan 2027. The 2026 outlook assumes export rules stay "substantially similar". The 10-K warns that restrictions push Chinese customers to domestic and open-source substitutes. — [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/cadence-design-systems-agrees-plead-guilty-and-pay-over-140-million-unlawfully-exporting); [10-K](https://s206.q4cdn.com/597110084/files/doc_downloads/2026/Mar/CDNS-FY-2025-Form-10-K.pdf); [Thompson Hine, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/11/bis-extends-affiliates-rule-until-january-2027/); [CNBC, 28 May 2025](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/business/money-report/chip-software-stocks-sink-on-report-trump-ordered-halt-to-china-sales/3835436/)
- China's domestic rival Empyrean reported 2025 revenue of RMB1.325bn (+8.4%), well below Cadence's US$680m of China revenue (reported figure, secondary). The Big-3 are said to hold 70-80% of China's EDA market. — [cnstock, 28 Apr 2026](https://paper.cnstock.com/html/2026-04/28/content_2208030.htm); [Caixin, 3 Jul 2025](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-07-03/us-lifts-export-ban-on-chip-design-software-to-china-amid-trade-thaw-102337708.html)

### Inferences
- Steady growth (~55%): good fit. Expect organic growth in the low-to-mid teens (management's "normalized" frame) with stable ~45% non-GAAP margins and optional agent upside.
- Financing-led pause around 2028 (~25%): the up-front ~20-22% (hardware at a multi-year peak, IP, SD&A) and AI-startup/hyperscaler new logos would fall first. Time-based software (2-3-year terms; 40% of RPO in months 13-36) would slow at renewal rather than collapse. My estimate: growth could fall to low single digits for 1-2 years, with margin compression from the operating leverage of a high-R&D cost base. This is an inference with no company data behind it.
- Faster acceleration (~20%): capacity is not the constraint; upside comes from hardware, IP and agent usage (Goldman models mid-20s growth).
- Industry-cycle position: late-upcycle markers include record hardware three years running, record backlog, IP +40% (Q2) and ESD +12%; 2026 was a "lighter renewal year".
- Export controls: material. China is 13-16% of revenue, the rules changed twice in 2025, a criminal compliance regime is in force and the affiliates rule expires on 10 Jan 2027.

## 7. Path forward: six transitions (said vs done)

### Takeaway
The plan is coherent and self-funded: become the physics engine and data layer that agents call, and charge for agents as virtual engineers on top of subscriptions. Shipped evidence is early (early access, company-reported results), and none of it yet shows in disclosed revenue.

### Cited Findings
- (1) Seats to use/result pricing.

IR (9 Jun 2026, via TIKR): a virtual engineer has a base rate plus usage overage, value "commensurate with what a physical human engineer can do", "definitely not priced like an LLM token"; 2026 guidance excludes consumption revenue. ### Inferences
- (1) Pricing: plausible but early. The Coin-per-user design keeps a seat-like anchor while adding metering, which hedges both ways. What must go right: agents must multiply engine calls faster than they shrink human users, and customers must accept value-based virtual-engineer pricing over token-cost pricing.
- (2) GPU: largely done in SD&A and simulation. Digital sign-off judges are not yet GPU-native, a potential opening for rivals; Palladium's custom silicon is its own moat.
- (3) Agents/APIs: being the API agents call protects the engines but erodes GUI/ease-of-use rents. Deliberate openness to any agent is the right hedge.
- (4) Open source: a low risk at advanced nodes while foundry PDK licensing and certification gate access. It is a real price ceiling at mature nodes, in education and in China. Cadence's answer is rhetorical (essential for real designs) rather than a product.
- (5) Learned models: consistent with the judge thesis; Cadence sells AI as an explorer and keeps solvers as arbiters.
- (6) Acceptance: strong with foundries; thinner and older public evidence for automotive and aerospace tool qualification.
- Funding: all of this is self-funded from ~US$1.8bn+ FCF and R&D of ~33% of revenue. Timing: early access through 2H 2026; revenue visibility from 2027 at the earliest.

### Gaps
- Agentic revenue to date; Coin pricing levels; share of the portfolio GPU-accelerated; Cadence's position on OpenFOAM against Fidelity; current ISO 26262 and DO-330 certificates.
