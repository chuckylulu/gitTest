# Entry-point valuation: SNPS, CDNS, NVDA, KEYS, SVCO, SDGR (prices = close Wed 11 Nov 2026; research date 12 Nov 2026)

**Summary for the report writer.** Valuation facts only, kept apart from business quality; no buy/sell advice. "My calc" = my arithmetic, inputs shown in the sections.
- **Price / EV (11 Nov close):** SNPS $462.12, EV ~$95-96bn (net debt ~$6.4-7.2bn after Ansys); CDNS $329.26, EV ~$91.5bn; NVDA $173.73, EV ~$4.17tn (net cash ~$24bn), **~$161 pre-market on 12 Nov** with the board's review of CEO Jensen Huang unresolved; KEYS $305.64, EV ~$52bn; SVCO $7.66, EV ~$0.25bn; SDGR $31.52, EV ~$2.0-2.1bn.
- **Forward adjusted P/E:** SNPS ~24x FY27 guide; CDNS ~33x FY27 consensus; NVDA ~19x FY27 / ~11x FY28 consensus; KEYS ~22x FY27; SVCO ~38x FY27 ($0.20 EPS, thin coverage); SDGR loss-making.
- **Own history (daily TTM multiples, my calc from SEC XBRL + Yahoo prices):** SNPS P/S 9.4x is at the 8th percentile of 5 years (median 12.3x); CDNS 14.7x at the 22nd (median 16.7x); NVDA trailing P/E 21.7x is at the bottom of its 5- and 10-year ranges, with the highest FCF yield in that time; KEYS P/S 7.9x is at the 86th (5y) and 93rd (10y) percentile; SDGR EV/S 7.4x at the 64th; SVCO EV/S 3.4x at the 69th since its 2024 IPO.
- **Peers:** design-software median EV/S 6.5x, forward P/E 22x, FCF yield 5.1%. SNPS and CDNS trade at a premium; Chinese EDA is far richer (Empyrean 33x sales). AI-hardware median forward P/E ~30x vs NVDA ~14x. KEYS's forward P/E (22.6x) is at the test-and-measurement median but its EV/S is above it. SDGR is ~2x Certara and Simulations Plus on EV/S.
- **What the price implies (reverse DCF, 9% discount, 3% terminal growth):** 10-year FCF CAGR SNPS ~12% (~18% after deducting stock pay), CDNS ~18% (~23%), KEYS ~12.5%, NVDA ~11.5%. On a two-stage view, NVDA needs only ~1%/yr FCF growth after FY28 if FY28 FCF reaches ~$290bn. SNPS's own FY26-30 target is ~15% revenue CAGR, a ~50% margin, and mid-20s FCF growth.
- **Cycle:** EDA industry revenue +12% y/y (Q2 2026), record backlogs; AI capex still rising (Alphabet 2026 capex $195-205bn; NVIDIA supply commitments $279bn); KEYS in an order upcycle (book-to-bill ~1.13); SDGR mid-way through its hosted transition, with biotech stress "largely eased".
- **Seat exposure:** SNPS and CDNS earn most revenue from time-based software priced by licensed copies or network seats, sold in multi-year agreements. IP (~19% / ~15%) and hardware (not disclosed) are not seat-based. NVDA ~0. KEYS: software and services 37% (seat share not disclosed). SDGR: throughput-tiered, not seat-based. SVCO: mostly licence-count based.
- **Provisional labels (12 Nov 2026):** SNPS fair; CDNS stretched; NVDA fair (provisional: governance shock, results 17 Nov); KEYS stretched; SVCO cannot assess; SDGR stretched. Conditions for each are in section 9.
- **Caveats:** the Nasdaq/Zacks EPS basis is unclear for SNPS and CDNS; forward-P/E history exists only at fiscal year-ends; the WebSearch budget ran out, so some causes of price moves are unverified.
