# Physical-simulation software in mainland China, as of 12 November 2026 (v1, research continuing)

Summary for the report writer (v1; to be revised). China has no vendor that ranks with Synopsys/Ansys, Cadence, Siemens or Dassault on breadth or sign-off acceptance. The strongest Chinese candidates are chip-design simulation vendors: Empyrean (华大九天, 301269, state-controlled via CEC; FY2025 revenue RMB1.325bn), Primarius (概伦电子, 688206; RMB484m) and Semitronix (广立微, 301095; RMB735m, much of it test hardware). General CAE is small and loss-making: Suochen (索辰科技, 688507; RMB466m in 2025, engineering-simulation software only RMB198m and falling) and ZWSOFT (中望软件, 688083; CAD-led). Paratera (并行科技, 920493.BJ) has become an AI-compute reseller (95% of revenue). Positions rest heavily on import substitution, state customers and subsidies: Empyrean's 2025 government support (~RMB150m incl. VAT refunds) exceeded its net profit (RMB61m). Chinese AI-for-science has two independently verified strengths: DP Technology/AISI's DPA-4.1 leads the Matbench Discovery leaderboard, and CMA's AI weather models (Fengqing etc.) are in operational deployment. The July 2026 Kimi K3 "chip in 48 hours" episode was a simulation-only, Nangate-45nm, open-source-EDA demo by Moonshot AI. Details and gaps below.
