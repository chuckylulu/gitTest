# Mid-size and specialist Western physical-simulation vendors (status 12 Nov 2026)

Business facts only (no share prices or valuation multiples). Method note: the shared web-search budget ran out mid-run and Firecrawl had no credits; later items come from fetching known URLs (filings, company pages) only. "Company claim" = not confirmed by an outside party.

## Summary for the report writer

- **Keysight**: simulation (ADS/RFPro, ESI ~$141M FY24, ex-Synopsys optics + PowerArtist ~$110M/yr, OpenFOAM steward) is small inside a hardware-led AI-test boom; software+services fell from 37% of revenue (FY25) to ~33% (Q3 FY26), ARR 29%→24%. Judge elements: foundry PDK support (Tower RF >10 yrs; ADS Photonic Designer certified for Tower PH18, 9 Nov 2026). Shipped MCP servers in ADS 2027 and GPU OpenFOAM; no pricing change.
- **Autodesk**: simulation (Fusion CAE, Inventor simulation, Moldflow) is an undisclosed, gap-filling slice of MFG ($1.38B, 19% of FY26 revenue). Clearest case of a seat vendor building meters above the seat: consumption ~17% of revenue; machine API metering since Oct 2026; "every automation machine requires a licensed named-user seat"; NRR at/above the top of 100-110%.
- **Hexagon**: sold D&E/MSC to Cadence (closed 23 Feb 2026, ~EUR2.7B) but agreed on 9 Oct 2026 to buy Rocscience (geotechnical simulation, ~$50M revenue). **Octave** (listed May 2026, FY25 revenue $1.64B) owns analysis tools (CAESAR II, PV Elite, GT Strudl). Neither belongs in a global top 8-15.
- **COMSOL**: private, very profitable (2024 group revenue EUR159.5M, pre-tax EUR91.9M; 2025 SEK1,534M, -13% in SEK, likely partly FX — inference), seat/concurrent licences; fast on GPU (cuDSS) and agents (v2027 with official MCP server announced 16 Sep 2026; shipping unconfirmed); no regulatory sign-off role found.
- **MathWorks**: $1.5B revenue (same figure in 2025 and 2026 factsheets); quasi-judge via DO-178C/DO-330 and ISO 26262 qualification kits with TÜV SÜD certificates; most complete agent tooling shipped (MCP server, MATLAB/Simulink agentic toolkits).
- **Bentley**: infrastructure analysis (STAAD with built-in design-code checks, SACS, PLAXIS, OpenFlows) in a $1.58B-ARR, 110%-NRR business; E365 (46% of ARR) is the main use-pricing precedent but meters "application days", a proxy for engineer time; AI-in-design monetisation starts 2027, not material.
- **Prior test** (seat tools/mid-market solvers lose pricing power after 2029): no 2025-26 evidence of lost pricing power in the in-depth names (NRR 105-110%, discount removal). Support is structural: E365 usage follows labour (Europe moderating as work moves to India); Octave warns AI may "reduce or eliminate the need for software licenses"; MSC grew ~3%/yr pre-sale (Schnitger estimate); DNV software flat. Vendors are pre-empting by pricing machine use; acceptance after 2029 is untested.
- **Sweep**: grid tools are hard judges (PJM, Oct 2026: PSCAD-compatible EMT and PSS/E-format models required); Studsvik codes in NRC review for SMRs; DNV certifies and sells solvers. Leave out of top 8-15: Hexagon, Octave, Autodesk-as-simulation, AVL, DNV, Studsvik, Gamma, Convergent, Flow Science, ESTECO, DIgSILENT, MHI (too small or simulation not core). Candidates: MathWorks, Bentley; Keysight and COMSOL borderline.

### Inferences

- (d) Financial strength: strong (high margins, FCF, diversified customers) but 17.5% China.
- (e) Resilience: highly exposed to AI-capex cycle (steady-growth: good; 2028 pause: hardware orders fall, as in FY24's -9%; acceleration: best). Export controls already bite.
- Transitions: T1 no plan visible; T2 done only for open-source CFD; T3 done (Oct 2026); T4 hedged (it owns an OpenFOAM line); T5 said ("simulation validates agent output") but no ML-in-solver product found; T6 foundry PDK certification is the live channel. Funded easily; what must go right: RF/photonic co-design for AI optics (CPO) must pull ADS into sign-off-adjacent flows.

## 8. Cross-group evidence: seat pricing power and mid-market displacement

### Takeaway
No 2025-26 sign of lost pricing power; the mechanism behind the prior (usage tied to engineer hours) is visible, and vendors are already pricing machine use to get ahead of it.

### Cited Findings
- **Against loss of pricing power (2025-26)**: Autodesk NRR at/above the top of 100-110% and multi-year discount removal for "price realization" — [Autodesk 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000769397/000076939726000059/q227pressrelease.htm); machine seats plus API meters; AEC Magazine's author expects design software to get "more expensive, not cheaper" — [AEC Magazine](https://aecmag.com/ai/autodesk-beyond-the-seat-the-meter/); Bentley NRR 110%, retention 99%, ARR growth accelerating; "The transition to fixed price ... there's no pushback" (G. Bentley) — [Q3 transcript](https://www.earningswhispers.com/transcript/BSY/Q32026); Nemetschek Design +10.4%; Octave NRR 105%; Emerson software ACV +10% (sources above); Synopsys prices GPU CFD "on the speedup delivered" — [Schnitger, 16 Sep 2026](https://schnitgercorp.com/2026/09/16/cae-earnings-recap-no-hallucinations-here/); Moldflow compute priced per result — [Flex rate sheet](https://www.autodesk.com/buying/flex/flex-rate-sheet)
- **For the prior**: Bentley usage tracks engineer time and European consumption is moderating as work moves to India — [Q3 transcript](https://www.earningswhispers.com/transcript/BSY/Q32026); Octave's AI risk factor — [Form 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2083632/000162828026027570/exhibit991-form10x12ba3.htm); Anagnost expects customers to need "fewer people per project" — [Q4 FY26 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/26/autodesk-adsk-q4-2026-earnings-call-transcript); MSC ~3% CAGR, sold — [Schnitger](https://schnitgercorp.com/2025/09/05/cadence-expands-cae-portfolio-with-3-25b-msc-software-deal/); DNV Digital Solutions flat, COMSOL down in SEK, Studsvik Scandpower -4.3% (sources above); unofficial MCP servers make incumbents' tools agent-drivable — [Glama](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/wjc9011/COMSOL_Multiphysics_MCP)

### Inferences
- Through Nov 2026 the evidence contradicts near-term pricing erosion; the prior concerns post-2029. The decisive variable is whether customers accept "machine seats" and per-machine-speed prices (Autodesk, Bentley) once agents cut engineer hours; E365's labour link and Octave's disclosure show the mechanism is real. Mid-market displacement so far is by consolidation (MSC to Cadence, Altair to Siemens) rather than by open source.

### Gaps
- No seat counts, renewal-price data or third-party discounting evidence was found for any in-depth vendor; no data on open-source codes displacing a named commercial solver.
