## 1. Keysight Technologies

### Takeaway
Keysight's simulation business (PathWave ADS/RFPro/EMPro/SystemVue, ESI, ex-Synopsys optics, PowerArtist, OpenFOAM via OpenCFD) is a small, undisclosed slice of a hardware-led test company whose FY26 boom is AI-infrastructure test hardware; software and services fell from ~37-40% to ~33% of revenue during FY26.

### Cited Findings
- FY25 (year to 31 Oct 2025): revenue $5,375M (+8%); software and services 37% of revenue, recurring revenue 29%; record FCF $1.3B; R&D $1,007M; ~16,800 staff (5,800 Americas, 3,600 Europe, 7,400 APAC) — [Keysight 2025 Annual Report/10-K, letter dated 27 Jan 2026](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report.pdf)
- FY25 revenue by country: US $1,869M, China $938M (17.5%), rest $2,568M; no customer >10%; Keysight says it has "been prevented from selling large orders to certain key customers due to trade restrictions" — [same 10-K](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report.pdf)
- FY25 point-in-time revenue $4,337M (81%) vs over-time $1,038M; perpetual software and the licence portion of term subscriptions are recognised up front — [same](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report.pdf)
- Software-and-services share of revenue / ARR share: FY25 37%/29%; Q1 FY26 ~40%/29%; Q2 FY26 ~36%/27%; Q3 FY26 (to 31 Jul 2026, call 18 Aug 2026) ~33%/24%, "software and services both grew double digits" — [Q4 FY25](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_earnings/2025/q4/transcript/Q4-FY2025-Prepared-Remarks.pdf), [Q1 FY26](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_earnings/2026/q1/transcript/Q1-FY2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf), [Q2 FY26](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_earnings/2026/q2/transcript/Q2-FY2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf), [Q3 FY26 prepared remarks](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_earnings/2026/q3/transcript/Q3-FY2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Q3 FY26: revenue $1,846M (+36% reported, +31% core), orders $2,091M (+56%); non-GAAP operating margin 33.2%; EISG (which contains ESI) $501M (+21%); FCF $403M; cash $2.6B; debt ~$2.5B; Q4 guide $1.93-1.95B, FY26 growth ~32% — [8-K Ex.99.1, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001601046/000160104626000029/exhibit991-q326pressrelease.htm); [Q3 remarks](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_earnings/2026/q3/transcript/Q3-FY2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- ESI Group: 50.6% bought 3 Nov 2023 ($477M net of cash), rest Jan 2024 ($458M), EUR155/share; ESI revenue $141M and net loss attributable $68M in FY24 — [10-K](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report.pdf); about EUR913M total — [Schnitger, 16 Sep 2026](https://schnitgercorp.com/2026/09/16/cae-earnings-recap-no-hallucinations-here/)
- Synopsys Optical Solutions Group (CODE V, LightTools, LucidShape, ImSym, RSoft) bought 17 Oct 2025 for $581M; Ansys PowerArtist $26M; Spirent $1,564M (15 Oct 2025). Three deals expected to add ~$375M FY26 revenue; >$100M cost synergies, 80-90% run-rate exiting FY26 — [10-K](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report.pdf); [Q3 remarks](https://s22.q4cdn.com/444849635/files/doc_earnings/2026/q3/transcript/Q3-FY2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)

- 6 Oct 2026: ADS 2027 and RF Circuit Simulation Professional ship Keysight-built MCP servers, macro recording and Python script generation so customers' own LLM agents drive circuit generation/optimisation, with "Keysight simulation" validating agent output; no pricing change announced — [Keysight press release](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/about/newsroom/catalog/news-release.2026.1006_pr26-095-keysight-advances-its-design-software-with-agentic-ai.html)
