# Simulation for chip manufacturing and packaging (ex-mainland China): computational lithography, TCAD, virtual fabrication, package thermal/stress; foundry sign-off; GPU and learned-model tests; specialists (as of 12 Nov 2026)

**Summary for the report writer (v2, being updated).** (1) Map: merchant computational lithography (OPC/ILT/verification) is a three-vendor oligopoly (Synopsys Proteus, Siemens Calibre, ASML Brion/Tachyon) after Cadence sold its OPC line to Silvaco (Mar 2025, $11.5m). The only free third-party sizing (QYResearch, Oct 2025) is ~$1.27bn (2024), top three >80%; low reliability. No public third-party TCAD share data; Synopsys leads, Silvaco is the listed number two (TCAD revenue $30.0m in 2025). Virtual fabrication is captive to equipment makers (Lam SEMulator3D, Applied ACE+/Ginestra). Package thermal/stress is a Synopsys(Ansys)/Siemens/Cadence contest. (2) Judge: TSMC's public certification tables (as of 1 Oct 2026) list only Cadence, Synopsys and Siemens EDA and now include a Thermal row (A16) and a 3DFabric 'static and transient' thermal row; Ansys RedHawk-SC Electrothermal (Apr 2025), Cadence Celsius (Apr 2026, per search summary) and Siemens Calibre 3DThermal (Sep 2026) are certified for A16. No stress/warpage certification found. Mask synthesis is not on those tables: the foundry runs OPC itself and the wafer is the judge. Thermal sign-off for wafer-scale packages is unfinished (Futurum, Sep 2026). (3) GPUs: kernel speed-ups of 10-60x (company claims) shrink to a '20-50% improvement in cost effectiveness or cycle time' at TSMC in NVIDIA's own June 2026 release. Siemens' Oct 2026 '10-17x' has no stated baseline, module or customer: company claim, unconfirmed. (4) Learned models: primary evidence supports 'learned proposes, physics certifies' (TSMC/Synopsys/NVIDIA 2024: final mask 'derived by traditional and physically rigorous methods'); neural-network-assisted OPC is claimed inside Calibre, but no public evidence was found of a learned model as the final OPC check in volume. Counter to 'accuracy comes last': curvilinear masks are viable but low-volume because qualification, inspection and metrology lag (Micron, May 2026). (5) Silvaco: 2025 revenue $63.1m (+6%), net loss $41.2m, China 20%; Q3 2026 revenue $16.3m (-13%), TCAD bookings +89%, cash $22.0m after a $10m Micron convertible; Q4 guide $22m; Pesic family >59%. PDF Solutions Q3 2026 revenue $68.3m (+20%). ASML does not disclose computational-lithography revenue. (6) Export controls: EUV computational-lithography software controlled since 2020 (3D003); DUV computational-lithography software (3D993.c, Dec 2024) only towards listed entities; the May-July 2025 EDA licence episode (3D991/3E991) cut vendors' China sales for ~6 weeks.

## 1. Map and size of each niche; leaders and third-party share data

## 2. The judge: how foundries certify these tools

### Takeaway
There are two judges. For design-side sign-off (extraction, EM/IR, timing, now thermal), the foundry certifies named vendor tools node by node, and only three vendors appear on TSMC's tables. For manufacturing-side tools (OPC/ILT, TCAD, virtual fab) the foundry is the customer and qualifies them internally against wafers; there is no public certification.

### Cited Findings
- TSMC EDA Alliance tables 'As of 10/01/2026' (A14, A16, N2P, N3C) list only Cadence, Synopsys, Siemens EDA; rows APR, library characterisation, timing, power, EMIR, DRC, dummy fill, LVS/LPE, RC, custom design, Tx EMIR, Tx timing, simulators; A16 adds Thermal; the 3DFabric table includes 'Thermal - Static and Transient Analysis', IR drop, RCX, 'Hierarchical 3Dblox', 'Certified for Technology Requirement'. ### Inferences
- Thermal analysis became a certified sign-off item at A16 (2025-2026) with three certified engines; stress/warpage has 'validated' or 'enabled' status only, so the judge for mechanical sign-off of 3D stacks is not yet set.
- For OPC/ILT the moat is calibrated, wafer-proven recipes at each fab rather than a public certificate, which keeps the three incumbents entrenched and makes a newcomer (Silvaco OPC, start-ups) slow to qualify.

### Gaps
- TSMC table check marks per vendor/category not machine-readable; Samsung and Intel do not publish certification tables; no evidence on how foundries formally qualify OPC software.
