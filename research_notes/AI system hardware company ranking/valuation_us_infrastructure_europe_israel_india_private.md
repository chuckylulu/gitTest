# Entry-point evidence: US interconnect/power/cooling names, Europe/Israel/India-listed names, and private companies (as of 12 November 2026)

Status: COMPLETE (written 12 Nov 2026). Scope: valuation snapshot, cycle position, what the price assumes, dated near-term events and investor access. No ranking, no technology or business-quality judgement, no buy or sell instruction. This is exploratory analysis, not personal financial advice.

How the data was gathered (read before using the numbers):
- Prices and performance: Yahoo Finance public daily price history, retrieved 12 Nov 2026 about 12:40 UTC. US prices are the 11 Nov 2026 close. European and Tel Aviv prices are given as the 11 Nov 2026 close; their 12 Nov intraday quotes (about 13:25 CET) are also shown where Stock Analysis multiples use them. Indian prices are the 12 Nov 2026 close. Returns are price returns (dividends excluded) on split-adjusted prices. Bases: year-to-date = 31 Dec 2025 close (30 Dec 2025 for Xetra, Stockholm, SIX and Vienna); 12 months = 11 Nov 2025; "since Jan 2023" = 30 Dec 2022 close. The 52-week range uses closing prices unless marked intraday.
- Multiples: Stock Analysis statistics pages (US: 11 Nov 2026 close; Europe: 12 Nov 2026 13:25 CET, delayed; India: 12 Nov 2026 15:29 IST). Trailing P/E is on reported (GAAP/IFRS) trailing-twelve-month EPS. Forward P/E is Stock Analysis's own forward figure. "P/E FY+1 / FY+2" is computed here as price divided by consensus adjusted EPS. Consensus figures are S&P Global data shown on Stock Analysis forecast pages, with each page's update date. Third-year (FY+3) consensus is paywalled and was not captured.
- Own-history multiples: Stock Analysis annual ratio pages give fiscal-year-end values for five years only (FY2021-FY2025, or FY2022-FY2026 for September year ends). The "5-year range and median" below is the range and median of those five year-end values, not a continuous daily range. No ten-year history was obtainable, because GuruFocus blocked access (HTTP 403).
- Results and events: primary sources wherever possible (SEC EDGAR 8-K/6-K/10-Q exhibits, company press releases).
- Tool limits: the shared WebSearch budget ran out early, and the Firecrawl and Exa tools returned out-of-credit errors (Firecrawl at the first call, Exa with HTTP 402). Everything below therefore comes from pages fetched directly at known addresses. As a result, the reasons for the mid-2026 sell-off, ETF weights, index changes, export-control or tariff decisions, and several private funding rounds could not be checked. They are listed under "What could not be verified".
- FX (Yahoo, 12 Nov 2026 about 12:39 UTC): EURUSD 1.1088; USDSEK 9.9722; USDCHF 0.8325; USDINR 94.04; USDILS 3.1851.
- "Implied-growth reading" (method, used for every Tier 1 name): the EPS a company must earn in FY2030 for a buyer at today's price to earn 9% a year, if the stock then trades on 20x (or 25x) earnings. n = 4 years and dividends are ignored. The required EPS is P x 1.09^4 / exit P/E. The resulting EPS growth rate per year is shown from the FY2026 base and from the FY2027 consensus. This is a deliberately simple, transparent reverse-DCF proxy, not a valuation model.

Cross-cutting observation (inference from the price data): most names in this set made their 52-week closing high between 11 May and 30 Jun 2026 and then fell 40-65%. These include VRT, GLW, TSEM, CAMT, BESI, IFX, NVT, FLEX, MOD, NVMI, SMHN, AIXA, SOI, ATS and HUBN. The Q3 2026 results released in late October and early November 2026 nevertheless show orders and backlogs still accelerating. Examples are VRT organic orders up more than 100%, ETN Electrical backlog up 46%, APH book-to-bill of 1.19, TEL orders up 29% and BESI orders up 96%. APH, MPWR, TEL, ETN, SU and LR fell less, by 10-22% from their highs. Prices therefore appear to discount a slowdown that reported orders do not yet show. The cause of the sell-off (for example AI capex worries, rates or positioning) could not be verified because no news search was available.
