## Market backdrop and answers to the key questions

### Takeaway
- **What prices imply.** The memory makers trade at 2.5–4.6 times earnings and 2–3 times book. That prices a sharp fall from record margins (SK hynix's operating margin was 80% in 3Q26, against roughly 53% at the 2018 peak), even though consensus has margins near the peak through 2027. The substrate, bonder and equipment names (SEMCO, Ibiden, Hanmi, TEL, Disco) still trade at 21–43 times forward earnings and well above their own 5-year P/B and EV/EBITDA medians, so their prices assume the upcycle extends. At a 9% discount rate, the implied perpetual growth from already-elevated FY2026/27 earnings is about 5–8%. LG Innotek and SKC trade at or below the bottom of their 5-year P/B ranges.
- **Cycle position.** Reported fundamentals are still at or near records for 3Q26. Share prices peaked around 22 Jun 2026, fell hard in July, and fell again from early October to 6 Nov. Early demand-side cracks (PC memory inventories) and accelerating capacity additions are visible.
- **Events in the next 3–6 months.** Q4 results come in late January to early February 2027. Samsung's board decides the size of KRW 55–75T of remaining 2026 returns in late January. SK hynix runs quarterly buybacks. Absolics' customer qualification is described as a "1H 2027 watershed". The details of US AI regulation remain unverified.
- **Access.** Korean names are reached mainly through KRX. SK hynix now has a Nasdaq ADS (SKHY), but it trades at a large premium (median +40%). Samsung has London GDRs near parity. Japanese names trade in 100-share units, with unsponsored ADRs for TEL, Advantest and Disco (liquid) and Ibiden (thin).

### Inferences
- The pattern is a classic one: equities peaked about four months before the reported earnings peak. Prices are falling while 3Q26 results are records and Q4 consensus is rising (Yuanta's 4Q26E operating profit for SK hynix is KRW 118.2T). The market is discounting a turn in the cycle in 2027–28 rather than reacting to weaker current numbers.
- The macro triggers in November (US AI regulation, the US election "blue wave", rate fears, Nvidia headlines) are verified at headline level only. Their substance is a gap.

### Gaps
- The content of the US "AI regulation tightening" of 5–6 Nov 2026, the cause of the Nvidia and "Jensen Huang" headlines of 11–12 Nov, and the Fed "big step" context could not be read. No article bodies were accessible.

**3. What the price assumes**

- Reading of the price (my arithmetic):
  - Market cap equals 3.6 years of FY2026E net income.
  - FY2026E net income plus the implied FY2027E figure covers about 68% of market cap.
  - At a 10x through-cycle P/E, the price implies normalised EPS of about KRW 125,400. That is 35% of FY2026E EPS, though still 2.1 times FY2025 EPS of 58,955. At 8x it is 44%; at 12x, 29%.

- Bridge and successor: consensus already assumes the successor arrives on schedule. The company says HBM4 will overtake HBM3E in both shipments and revenue in 4Q26. Consensus also assumes conventional DRAM and NAND pricing stays strong.
- Testing the prior view (memory supply-limited through 2028 at 70%; memory is the first area to turn in a spending pause):
  - 2027 is supported by the LTAs and by Samsung's statement that 2027 demand "will far exceed the industry supply".
  - The equity market is already behaving as if memory turns first. The stock is down 57% while earnings are rising; foreign investors sold about KRW 3T of memory stocks on 5 Nov; PC OEMs are cutting 2027 memory procurement (TrendForce); and Morgan Stanley's headline reads "2028 uncertain".

  - For US buyers, the ADS premium narrowing towards parity.
