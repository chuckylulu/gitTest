# Multimodal AI, world models, generative media and robotics / embodied AI: capability advances, Oct 12 – Nov 11, 2026

> Method and caveats (read first). Notes compiled on Nov 11, 2026 from 33 extended web searches plus Hugging Face Hub and GitHub search lookups. Page fetching was blocked in this session (deepmind.google failed to resolve), so no web page was read in full: web facts come from search-result excerpts of the linked pages, and primary posts are linked but were not opened. Facts marked "repository-verified" were read directly from Hugging Face or GitHub (creation dates, model cards, pull requests, paper abstracts). Evidence status uses four labels: vendor-reported, independently measured, peer-reviewed, disputed. Items dated before Oct 12 are labelled "outside window". Abbreviations: VLA = vision-language-action model (camera and instruction in, robot actions out); WAM = world action model (a robot policy built on a video world model that predicts future frames and actions together); HF = Hugging Face.

## 1. World models and simulation: what did Genie 4 add over Genie 3, what else appeared, and how are world models used to train robots and agents?

### Takeaway
Genie 4 (Google DeepMind, Oct 27, research preview) is the main in-window release: it adds natively generated audio, natural-language steering while the simulation runs, and a reported two minutes of scene consistency, but DeepMind published no resolution, frame-rate, latency or benchmark figures and no sim-to-real evidence. The other in-window activity is mostly world models wired into robot pipelines (NVIDIA's surgical Cosmos3-H models on Oct 20, four Cosmos 3 Nano checkpoints on Nov 5, GR00T 2 as a Cosmos-based world action model on Nov 9, AgiBot's GO-3) and one open interactive world model from Tencent (Hy-Nomas); I found no in-window world-model launch from xAI, Meta, World Labs or Runway.

### Cited Findings

**Genie 4 (Google DeepMind, announced Oct 27, 2026). Evidence status: vendor-reported; no independent benchmarks found.**
- Announced Oct 27, 2026 as a research preview for trusted testers; described as a real-time interactive world model that "builds on Gemini-family capabilities", with Gemini Omni anchoring the appearance and behaviour of simulations — [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/); [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-genie-4/); [TechCrunch, Oct 27](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/); [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/google-deepmind-rolls-out-genie-4-world-model-in-research-preview/)
- New versus Genie 3, item 1 (audio): each world generates ambient sound, music and interactive dialogue on the fly; DeepMind calls it its first real-time world model to generate audio natively — [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/)
- New versus Genie 3, item 2 (steering): users steer a running scene in natural language, for example moving the camera, changing the weather or adding characters — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-genie-4/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/)
- New versus Genie 3, item 3 (consistency): secondary pages attribute to TechCrunch that Genie 4 holds a consistent scene for up to two minutes, and one says this is double Genie 3's limit; Google's own model page says "a few minutes". Genie 3 (August 2025, outside window) was itself described as having "multiple minutes of visual consistency", so the size of the gain is unclear — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/); [DeepMind Genie model page](https://deepmind.google/models/genie/); [DataNorth](https://datanorth.ai/news/google-deepmind-genie-4-world-model); [hokai.io](https://hokai.io/hub/models/genie-4) (secondary, low quality)
- No quantitative evidence: the posts give no resolution, frame rate, latency or test scores, and no comparison with Genie 3 beyond demo clips — [markgrid.ai](https://markgrid.ai/resources/google-genie-4-world-model-explained-access) (secondary); [DataNorth](https://datanorth.ai/news/google-deepmind-genie-4-world-model)
- Disputed detail: Wikipedia lists 11 billion parameters for Genie 4 with no primary DeepMind source; 11B is the figure in the original 2024 Genie paper, so it is probably a carry-over — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genie_%28world_model%29); [HF paper page for Genie, 2402.15391](https://huggingface.co/papers/2402.15391)
- Limits acknowledged by DeepMind: details and objects can drift over longer simulations; real-time audio accuracy and legible in-world text are unsolved; complex physics, spatial accuracy and long-term consistency remain open; Google's Diego Rivas reportedly called the work "still early days" — [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/); [markgrid.ai](https://markgrid.ai/resources/google-genie-4-world-model-explained-access)
- Access: trusted testers now; Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early-access partners of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio "in the coming weeks"; no launch date or pricing — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-genie-4/); [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/google-deepmind-rolls-out-genie-4-world-model-in-research-preview/)
- Use for robots and agents: DeepMind's Genie 4 page describes a robotics experiment in which "Gemini 4 Argon" writes a multi-step plan that is passed as text captions to Genie 4 while the simulation renders; one report says the demo showed Apptronik's Apollo humanoid playing checkers; the page says Genie 4 worlds "could be" where agents like SIMA learn new skills. No sim-to-real or agent-training numbers were published — [DeepMind Genie model page](https://deepmind.google/models/genie/); [Google on X](https://x.com/Google/status/2115114426827501782); [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/genie-4) (secondary)
- Precedent (outside window): the SIMA 2 paper (Dec 2025) reported SIMA 2 self-improving in environments generated by Genie 3 — [arXiv 2512.04797](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.04797)
- Skeptical views: "until there is published evidence of sim-to-real transfer from Genie-generated environments, the robotics claims should be read as direction, not proof"; Genie 4 "is not a commercial robotics or enterprise simulation platform" — [i-SCOOP](https://www.i-scoop.eu/genie-4-world-model/); [i10x.ai](https://i10x.ai/news/google-deepmind-genie-4-world-model); [folderit.net](https://folderit.net/what-is-google-genie-4-and-how-do-ai-world-models-work/) (secondary commentary)

**NVIDIA Cosmos (lead corrected: Cosmos 3 itself is from June; the Oct 19–20 release was two surgical derivative models).**
- Outside window: Cosmos 3 was announced June 1, 2026 at GTC Taipei as an open world foundation model combining vision reasoning, world generation and action prediction; Cosmos3-Super has 64B parameters — [NVIDIA newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-cosmos-3-the-open-frontier-foundation-model-for-physical-ai); [Cosmos 3 technical report](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/cosmos-lab/cosmos3/technical-report.pdf)

- Nov 5, 2026: NVIDIA released four open-weight Cosmos 3 Nano post-trained checkpoints for interactive robot and autonomous-driving simulation. Evidence status: vendor-reported via secondary outlets — [TUN](https://www.tun.com/home/nvidia-cosmos-3-adds-4-open-models-for-robot-and-av-simulation/); [mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/nvidia-opens-four-cosmos-3-models-for-interactive-robot-and-driving-simulation/)
- Nov 9, 2026: GR00T 2 is described by NVIDIA as "an open frontier world action model built on NVIDIA Cosmos", so the robot policy itself sits on a world-model backbone (details in section 4) — [NVIDIA blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/robotics-simulation-advancements-corl/)

**Tencent Hy-Nomas (released about Oct 23, 2026; technical report Oct 28). Evidence status: open weights repository-verified; quality claims vendor-reported.**
- Open interactive video world model with an 8B diffusion backbone: multi-turn free-form text instructions and continuous camera control, generated one video segment at a time with a persistent key/value cache; distilled to 8 denoising steps; 720×1280 output at 24 frames per second; a quantized configuration runs on a single RTX 4090; Apache-2.0 — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-Nomas); [arXiv 2610.36729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36729)
- Ships Hy-Nomas-Bench (280 test cases in four benchmarks: multi-turn text interaction, camera navigation, interleaved control, spatial memory); the paper claims superior human-evaluation results over existing world models, without independent confirmation — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-Nomas); [arXiv 2610.36729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36729)
- Date: aggregator trackers give Oct 23; the arXiv report was published Oct 28 — [af.net tracker](https://af.net/realtime/ai-model-releases-october-2026/) (aggregator); [arXiv 2610.36729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36729)

**Independent evaluation of world models and agents.**
- "Dual Exam" (arXiv 2610.31066, published Oct 22–23, 2026; about 80 authors across HKUST, NUS, CMU, Berkeley and others) evaluates multimodal agents and world models separately: 10 agent configurations on 1,088 items; 16 world-model configurations on 400 cases in eight scenarios. Best agent index: GPT 6 Astra at 31.3; best world-model overall score: GPT-Image-2 at 21.7. The authors state that whether these capabilities "predict coupled performance or sustained reciprocal improvement remains to be tested". Evidence status: independently measured, preprint — [arXiv 2610.31066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31066); [HF paper page](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31066)

**Other world-model-for-robots items in window.**
- AgiBot GO-3 Preview (Oct 29) is built on a "World Reasoning Action Model" that unifies task understanding, future-state prediction and action generation (section 4) — [AgiBot press release](https://www.agibot.com/article/231/detail/133.html); [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330152/20261106/agibot-unveils-go-3-preview-robot-model-predicts-outcomes-before-acting-rankings-self-reported.htm)
- SWAM (arXiv 2611.12870, Nov 9–10; Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Noematrix): studies scaling world action models with about 47,000 hours of multisource data and reports generalization to unseen scene conditions in RoboTwin and on real robots. Evidence status: preprint, author-reported — [arXiv 2611.12870](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12870)

**Not in window (context only).**
- Outside window: Meta FAIR's RoboJEPA (arXiv dated Oct 7, 2026; code repo created Oct 8): an 8B-parameter JEPA latent world model trained on 12 robot embodiments; imagination error follows a power law in compute and correlates with planning success; zero-shot planning toward a single goal image on real hardware; checkpoints and code released — [arXiv 2610.10515](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10515); [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/robo_jepa)
- Outside window: Runway GWM Worlds 2 (Sept 3) and Praxis-1 world action model for robots (announced Sept 30, rolling out to partners as of Oct 4); World Labs Atlas preview (Sept 1) — [post on X about Praxis-1](https://x.com/CyberRobooo/status/2106721324148351444); [worldsimulator.ai roundup](https://worldsimulator.ai/blog/articles/best-ai-world-models) (aggregator, low quality)
- No in-window world model from xAI or Meta was found; aggregators list only xAI's Grok Voice 3.0 (Oct 14, a speech model) — [cleus.ai October roundup](https://cleus.ai/blog/new-ai-models-october-2026) (aggregator, low quality)

### Inferences
- The in-window world-model gains are in interaction modalities (sound, language steering, multi-turn control) and in coupling to planners and policies, not in measured physical fidelity: no lab published a quantitative physics or consistency benchmark, and the one independent benchmark (Dual Exam) reports low absolute scores.
- World models are being used for robots in four ways visible this month: as the policy backbone (GR00T 2, GO-3, SWAM), as a simulator for policy evaluation and synthetic data (Cosmos3-H-Dreams, the Nov 5 Cosmos checkpoints), as a latent planner (RoboJEPA, just before the window), and as a prospective training ground for agents (Genie 4 with SIMA, stated as an aspiration).
- Genie 4's pairing with a Gemini planner ("Gemini 4 Argon" writing plans that drive the simulation) suggests DeepMind is positioning the world model as an imagination or rehearsal component for embodied reasoning models, but nothing published shows it improves real-robot success.

### Gaps
- Genie 4 resolution, frame rate, latency, parameter count and any head-to-head numbers against Genie 3 are unpublished; the "two minutes" figure was seen only through secondary pages citing TechCrunch.
- I could not confirm which Hugging Face repositories correspond to the four Nov 5 Cosmos 3 checkpoints (the Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Auto, Sim-Transfer, Transfer-Auto and Sim-Bimanual repos were last modified Nov 9 and are the likely candidates).
- One aggregator says World Labs is being acquired by AMD; I found no reliable source and did not verify it.
- Hy-Nomas's exact release day and any third-party quality comparison are unverified.

## 2. Multimodal and real-time models: what shipped and what capability change does each represent?

### Takeaway
The in-window real-time changes are incremental rather than new flagship models: Google added a `proactive_video` switch to the Gemini Live API (SDK commits Nov 9), OpenAI expanded "Live Responses delegation" for its GPT-Live voice model (SDK changes Nov 2–10), ElevenLabs launched a reasoning-capable realtime voice-agent family (Oct 19), and Tencent open-sourced a simultaneous interpreter with about 3 seconds of lag (Oct 26). Two leads need correcting: Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (Nov 5) is a video generation and editing model, not a real-time model, and Microsoft's Mage-VL-Cowork (Oct 30) is a small open computer-use agent.

### Cited Findings

**Google Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (Nov 5, 2026). Evidence status: vendor documentation; third-party pricing reports.**
- Listed as generally available with release date Nov 5, 2026 (model ID `gemini-omni-1.2-flash`); generates or edits video with synchronized audio up to 15 seconds at 360p, 720p, 1080p or 4K — [Google Cloud model page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/gemini/omni-1-2-flash); [TUN](https://www.tun.com/home/google-deepmind-releases-gemini-omni-1-2-flash-with-15-second-video/)
- Takes text, images and video as input; clips run 3–15 seconds in one-second steps (default 10); follow-up edits chain off a previous interaction ID through the Interactions API, not the Live API — [Gemini API docs](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/omni); [apidog](https://apidog.com/blog/what-is-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/) (secondary)
- Change versus version 1.1: third-party coverage reports about $0.07 per second at 720p, down from about $0.10, and faster processing; from Dec 3, 2026 requests to `gemini-omni-1.1-flash` route to 1.2. One outlet instead cites $1.05 per clip — [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/gemini-omni-1-2-flash); [emergent.sh](https://emergent.sh/news/gemini-omni-1-2-flash-launches-faster-video); [TUN](https://www.tun.com/home/google-deepmind-releases-gemini-omni-1-2-flash-with-15-second-video/)
- Repository-verified: Google's Python SDK changelog adds `gemini-omni-1.2-flash`, makes it the Gemini API video default, and adds `gemini-4-carbon` to the model list — [python-genai CHANGELOG](https://github.com/googleapis/python-genai/blob/f1670003f5eaf9d367ab414b8c5d757c991d8cc0/CHANGELOG.md)

**Google Gemini Live API: `proactive_video` (SDK commits Nov 9, 2026). Evidence status: repository-verified existence; behaviour undocumented in what I found.**
- Commits titled "Add `proactive_video` to `ProactivityConfig` for the Live API" landed in Google's Python and JavaScript SDKs on Nov 9, 2026; the field is an optional boolean next to `proactive_audio` — [python-genai commit](https://github.com/googleapis/python-genai/commit/97d0678602c95caa268a819a1dffd01ce52c1580); [js-genai commit](https://github.com/googleapis/js-genai/commit/2164fe72de77aea191c1e43e7d8f346426752c0d); [java-genai ProactivityConfig](https://github.com/googleapis/java-genai/blob/1b014c070ae72805d1f3244290556ba55793b7fd/src/main/java/com/google/genai/types/ProactivityConfig.java)
- Existing documented behaviour for the audio flag: with `proactiveAudio` enabled "the model can reject responding to the last prompt" (for example, staying silent on off-topic speech). Gemini 3.8 Live documentation says proactivity is on by default, video is sampled at 1 frame per second, and turns include all video by default — [Live API reference](https://ai.google.dev/api/live); [Gemini 3.8 Live developer guide](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/guides/gemini-3-8-live); [Gemini 3.8 Live model page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-3.8-live)

**OpenAI "Live Responses" (in-window SDK changes Nov 2–10, 2026). Evidence status: repository-verified; no launch post found under this name.**
- "Live Responses delegation" is the mechanism by which OpenAI's full-duplex voice model GPT-Live hands reasoning and tool use to a Responses API backend. In window, OpenAI's SDKs merged "expand Live Responses delegation tools [and tool choices]" on Nov 2–3 (Python, Node, Ruby, Java, Go) and "update Live Responses delegation tool choices" on Nov 10; the Python pull request says it exposes additional delegation tools and tool choices, including MCP tool choice, at session creation and update — [openai-python PR 4233](https://github.com/openai/openai-python/pull/4233); [openai-node PR 3101](https://github.com/openai/openai-node/pull/3101); [openai-go PR 1235](https://github.com/openai/openai-go/pull/1235)
- Outside window (context): GPT-Live was announced July 8, 2026; `gpt-live-1` became generally available in the API on Sept 10, 2026 with a `v1/live/sessions` endpoint, $0.05 per minute and a 300-concurrent-session launch tier; it can listen and speak at the same time and delegate reasoning and tool use to a backend agent — [OpenAI model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-live-1); [tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/openai-realtime-api-gpt-live-1-setup-2026/) (secondary); [Rime](https://www.rime.ai/resources/openai-gpt-live-api-capabilities-limits-and-alternatives)

**ElevenLabs Eleven Realtime (Oct 19–20, 2026). Evidence status: vendor-reported.**
- New model family for voice agents: Eleven Realtime v1 Mini (default, fastest and cheapest) and v1 Max (reasons before answering, aimed at complex instructions and multi-step tool calls), released with live speech translation and video-to-music generation; the changelog says Oct 19 with gradual rollout, one tracker says Oct 20 — [ElevenLabs changelog, Oct 19](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/19); [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/eleven-labs); [TechPilot](https://techpilot.ai/tools/elevenlabs/)
- Outside window: the Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo text-to-speech models launched Sept 28 — [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/eleven-v4)

**Tencent Hy-SimulMT (Oct 26, 2026). Evidence status: open weights repository-verified; performance vendor-reported.**
- Open-source (Apache-2.0) real-time Chinese–English simultaneous speech translation model, a 30B mixture-of-experts with 3B active parameters, released Oct 26 with weights, an inference service and training tools; voice cloning needs a separate speech-synthesis service — [TechNode, Oct 27](https://technode.com/2026/10/27/tencent-open-sources-ai-model-for-real-time-chinese-english-interpretation/); [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-SimulMT-30B-A3B); [Tencent Hunyuan on X](https://x.com/TencentHunyuan/status/2114701815807947159)
- Reported numbers: streaming lag (StreamLAAL) of about 3 seconds on the RealSI long-form benchmark, the lowest of the systems compared; translation-quality (GEMBA) score above the open models compared and above four of five commercial APIs; a small test against 6 certified interpreters on 18 recordings is claimed to show professional-level quality — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-SimulMT-30B-A3B); [post on X summarizing the release](https://x.com/0xLogicrw/status/2115031013374218729)
- Limit: the 3-second figure excludes speech synthesis and playback time — [post on X](https://x.com/0xLogicrw/status/2115031013374218729)

**Microsoft Mage-VL-Cowork (published on HF Oct 30, 2026). Evidence status: open weights repository-verified; benchmark vendor-reported.**
- A 4B-parameter (4.74B per Hub metadata) open-weight (Apache-2.0) computer-use agent that chooses at each step between clicking through the screen and running code in a terminal, inside one policy with no external orchestrator — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Mage-VL-Cowork); [project page](https://microsoft.github.io/Mage/cowork/)
- Reported numbers: 66.0% on OSWorld-Verified under a 50-step budget (Microsoft's own evaluation), 87.5% on operating-system tasks and 62.7% on multi-application workflows; trained on 293,806 synthesized tasks across 6,915 atomic skills; online reinforcement learning adds 10.16 points over supervised training while cutting model calls 13.0% and output tokens 25.4% — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Mage-VL-Cowork)
- One secondary write-up says this is ahead of Claude Sonnet 4.5 (62.9%, with twice the step budget); not independently verified — [i-SCOOP](https://www.i-scoop.eu/mage-vl-cowork/)

**Unverified lead.**
- xAI "Grok Voice 3.0" (Oct 14) appears only in an aggregator roundup; I found no primary source — [cleus.ai](https://cleus.ai/blog/new-ai-models-october-2026) (aggregator, low quality)

### Inferences
- The common direction is real-time voice and video sessions that hand work to a slower reasoning or tool-using backend (OpenAI delegation, ElevenLabs Realtime Max) and that decide for themselves when to respond to what they see (Gemini `proactive_video`). These are plumbing changes; no vendor published latency or task-success measurements for them in the window.
- Open-weight releases narrowed specific gaps with small models: simultaneous interpretation at about 3 seconds of text lag (Hy-SimulMT) and desktop computer use at 4B parameters (Mage-VL-Cowork). Both rest on the authors' own evaluations.

### Gaps
- What `proactive_video` does at run time (for example, whether the model speaks unprompted when the video changes) is not documented in anything I could read; only the field's existence and date are verified.
- I found no OpenAI announcement or changelog entry named "Live Responses"; the feature name comes from SDK pull-request titles.
- No independent latency or quality measurements were found for Eleven Realtime, GPT-Live delegation or Gemini Live.

## 3. Generative media: which image, video, audio, music and speech models were released or updated, and what improved?

### Takeaway
The clearest in-window changes are about output control and cost rather than a new quality tier: OpenAI's GPT-Image-2.6 added animated GIF and SVG output (Nov 4), Google's Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash made 15-second video generation and multi-turn editing with audio cheaper (Nov 5), and Kling 4.0's full model (30-second clips, 10 keyframes) reportedly launched Oct 15, though its 4K availability is disputed. Several leads fall just outside the window (Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo on Oct 9, YuE2 in September, Kling 4.0's announcement on Sept 27–28), and I found no new Sora, Veo or Runway video model in the window.

### Cited Findings

**Image.**
- Nov 4, 2026 (date from third-party coverage), OpenAI GPT-Image-2.6 with two API models, "Flare" and "Sunburst": adds animated GIF output (`output_format: "gif"`, `frame_count` 2–16 with default 9, `frame_duration_ms`) and SVG vector output (`output_format: "svg"`, one image per request; the API first makes a raster reference image, then an "authoring model" writes the SVG, and both are billed). Evidence status: vendor documentation — [OpenAI image generation guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation); [OpenAI API reference](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/images/methods/generate); [MarkTechPost, Nov 4](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/04/openai-gpt-image-2-6-flare-sunburst/)
- Headline speed claims ("6-second API generation", "50% faster") and saved reference images in ChatGPT Images 2.6 come from secondary coverage — [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/04/openai-gpt-image-2-6-flare-sunburst/); [Atlas Cloud](https://www.atlascloud.ai/models/gpt-image-2.6); [felloai](https://felloai.com/chatgpt-images-2-6/)
- Limits: GIF and SVG output do not support streaming, partial images or custom compression; GIF requires access to a "GIF preview"; official docs say GIF works on both the generations and edits endpoints while two third-party guides say the edits endpoint does not return GIF or SVG — [OpenAI API reference](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/images/methods/generate); [mihata.jp](https://mihata.jp/en/column/gpt-image-2-6-gif-svg); [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/chatgpt-images-2-6)

### Inferences
- The two headline robot models report few-shot or task-suite success rates without trial counts, named baselines or intervention rates, and neither can be tested by outsiders yet (partner-only for Gemini Robotics 2.5; no weights located for GR00T 2). They should be treated as claims.
- Several separate efforts converge on the same recipe: pretraining mostly on human first-person video (GR00T 2, GO-3, REKI), a world-model backbone that predicts outcomes with actions, and adaptation from one to five demonstrations as the headline metric.
- A two-level design is becoming standard: a general reasoning model plans and checks while a smaller VLA moves the robot (Gemini Robotics-ER with the 2.5 action model; GPT-6-Astra with π0.5). The SenseNova paper suggests the planner level is now strong in simulation and that the remaining gap is real-world cost, speed and failure recovery.
- Infrastructure matured alongside models: a hosted fine-tuning and inference API (Physical Intelligence), an open data-labelling engine and data-collection hardware (NVIDIA Flora and GR00T hardware designs), a faster simulator (Isaac Lab 3.0) and new benchmarks (DexBench, VABAR, G1-Factory).
- Factory demonstrations (Mercedes-Benz kitting, Xiaomi sorting) remain single-station proofs of concept.

## 6. What limits and failures were documented?

### Takeaway
The best-documented failure in the window is regulatory: on Nov 6 the US road-safety regulator NHTSA opened a recall query covering 2,895 Waymo vehicles because robotaxis kept passing stopped school buses after a software fix. Elsewhere the limit is evidential: almost every robot and world-model result is vendor-reported without trial counts, baselines or human-intervention rates, and the independent studies that exist (the GPT-6-Astra robotics preprint, the Dual Exam benchmark) emphasize cost, slow execution, weak failure recovery and low absolute scores.

### Cited Findings

**Safety investigations.**
- NHTSA's Office of Defects Investigation opened recall query RQ26006 on Nov 6, 2026 covering 2,895 Waymo vehicles: robotaxis continued to illegally pass stopped school buses after a software update that Waymo says was completed for all affected vehicles on Nov 17, 2025; NHTSA says most incidents came after that update. Austin's school district documented 19 incidents; Waymo says it has received no citations since March 2026. A recall query is an investigation, not a defect finding. Evidence status: independently documented by a regulator — [TechCrunch, Nov 9](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/); [Technology.org, Nov 10](https://www.technology.org/2026/11/10/waymo-robotaxis-school-bus-nhtsa-recall-query-2895-vehicles/); [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/private-markets/26/11/63071430/waymo-faces-fresh-federal-probe-as-robotaxis-keep-blowing-past-stopped-school-buses)

### Inferences
- Verification is thinnest where the claims are largest. Of the window's items, only the TU Delft paper is peer-reviewed, only the Waymo problem is documented by a regulator, and only the GPT-6-Astra and Dual Exam preprints are third-party measurements; Gemini Robotics 2.5, GR00T 2, Genie 4, GO-3, Neuron AI, Optimus and the Xiaomi shift are all company statements.
- Human-intervention and failure-recovery data are the consistent omission. No robot or robotaxi announcement in the window disclosed intervention rates, and the one independent robotics study names failure recovery and physical-state verification as the open problems.
- The Waymo case shows a deployed system failing a specific, legally defined behaviour even after a targeted software fix, which is a caution against reading aggregate success rates as evidence that rare-case behaviour is solved.

### Gaps
- No in-window incident or disengagement statistics were found for Tesla, Aurora or Kodiak.
- No documented failures of humanoid deployments (injuries, stoppages) dated in the window were found; this reflects what was searched, not confirmed absence.
- I did not find independent tests of Genie 4, Gemini Robotics 2.5 or GR00T 2; all three were at most two weeks old at the time of writing.
