## 1. Products and stage

### Takeaway
HD Hyundai Electric is a mature, commercial-stage maker of grid and in-plant power equipment:
- Power transformers up to 800 kV / 1,500 MVA, and 765 kV shunt reactors.
- GIS up to 800 kV.
- Medium- and low-voltage switchgear, breakers and distribution transformers up to 36 kV.
- Motors and generators, and battery energy storage (ESS/BESS) as an EPC provider.

Its data-centre exposure comes through three routes:
- Grid-connection equipment: 765 kV transformers for US backbone transmission projects.
- Packaged "power transformer + distribution equipment" supply to North American hyperscale data centres, the main example being a KRW 1.12 trillion framework deal disclosed in July 2026.
- A new group-level push to sell generators alongside HD Hyundai engines for on-site data-centre generation.

It sells no UPS or busway products that I could find. Its DC products are LV DC breakers and 1 MW-class LVDC distribution equipment for buildings.

### Inferences

## 2. Technology and roadmap with dates

### Takeaway
The technology is incumbent and long proven:
- 765 kV transformers in commercial operation in Korea since 2001.
- An 800 kV transformer/GIS factory and test facility since 1999.
- 800 kV GIS developed in 2002.

The roadmap is incremental:
- SF6-free GIS: 170 kV certified in 2023, 145 kV developed in 2024.
- A 362 kV dead-tank breaker for the Americas, targeted for 2028.
- 500 kV VSC-HVDC converter transformers under a Korean government R&D programme to 2027, with HVDC cooperation with Hitachi Energy.
- UL-listed and DC breakers.

## 6. Capacity and lead times

### Takeaway
- **Ulsan**: power-transformer capacity rose from 380 to 420 units a year (+40–50) with the plant completed Sep/Oct 2026. Production starts in November 2026, targeting full utilisation by November 2027.

- 765 kV transformers in Korean commercial operation since 2001.
- More than 160 765 kV contracts worldwide since 1999, according to the company.
