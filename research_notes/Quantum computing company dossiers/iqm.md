## 1. Corporate facts, founders, key people and ownership

### Takeaway
IQM was registered in May 2018 in Espoo as an Aalto University spin-out and became a listed company when its SPAC merger with Real Asset Acquisition Corp. (RAAQ) closed on 1 Jul 2026 (Nasdaq: IQMX from 2 Jul 2026; also Nasdaq Helsinki). Jan Goetz has been sole CEO since 1 Jan 2026, and the seven-member board is chaired by Sierk Pötting. No named post-listing shareholder stakes were found. Juha Vartiainen is confirmed as a co-founder. Kuan Yen Tan and Mikko Möttönen appear only as co-authors of IQM papers in the sources retrieved.

## 2. Funding rounds, investors, valuation, debt, grants and listing

### Takeaway
IQM raised about $600M of private capital by September 2025:
- about €11.5M seed (2019)
- €39M Series A1 (2020)
- €128M Series A2 led by World Fund (Jul 2022)
- $320M (≈€275M) Series B led by Ten Eleven Ventures at about $1B valuation (Sep 2025)

It also took €35M of EIB venture debt (Apr 2022). The July 2026 SPAC and PIPE delivered about €198.7M net, at a $1.8B deemed equity value (about $1.9B at listing). IQMX closed at $6.74 on 13 Nov 2026, against the $10.00 PIPE/reference price.

## 4. Products and stage; system-sale claims versus customer confirmations

### Takeaway

- **Spark**: 5 qubits.
- **Radiance**: NISQ line of about 20–150 qubits, including a 24-qubit "Star" variant.

## 9. Science: what is demonstrated, independently checked, unproven, and the physical/engineering limits

### Takeaway
IQM's peer-reviewed results are device-level:
- The unimon qubit (Nature Communications 2022: 99.8–99.9% single-qubit gates).
- A 99.81% CZ gate across a long-distance tunable coupler (PRX Quantum 2023).

System-level numbers are company-measured: median CZ about 99.5% on 20- and 54-qubit chips, QV 32/64, and production T1 of about 40 µs (2024). The only substantive independent study found, a Jülich preprint from Oct 2026, ranks Emerald above Garnet. It also finds IQM performance degrading faster with depth than IBM's and IQM feed-forward architecturally restricted.

IQM has published [[4,2,2]] error *detection* on a 6-qubit star chip. No IQM hardware demonstration of error-*corrected* logical qubits was found. Its qLDPC "barbell" codes are simulation-only at 10⁻⁴ noise; first QEC demonstrators are targeted for 2027.

### Cited Findings
**Demonstrated: IQM-authored results**
- **Unimon** (Hyyppä et al., Nature Communications 13, 6895, Nov 2022; DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-34614-w) (older) — [C · DOAJ record (search excerpt)](https://doaj.org/article/e0bb45b795b84a6ebad20baf79b40a28); [C · VTT CRIS record](https://cris.vtt.fi/en/publications/unimon-qubit/):
  - Single Josephson junction with no superinductor.
  - 1-qubit gate fidelities of 99.8–99.9% with 13 ns gates on three unimon qubits.
  - Two-qubit gates were listed as a next step.
- **Long-distance coupler** (Marxer et al., PRX Quantum 4, 010314, 2023; arXiv:2208.09460): CZ (99.81 ± 0.02)%; qubits at least 2 mm apart; more than 50 MHz qubit–coupler coupling (older) — [C · arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09460).
- **July 2024 lab records**, announced by press release (older) — [C-relay · TQI, 2024-07-15 (search excerpt)](https://thequantuminsider.com/2024/07/15/iqm-quantum-computers-achieves-technological-milestones-with-99-9-2-qubit-gate-fidelity-and-1-millisecond-coherence-time/):
  - CZ (99.91 ± 0.02)% by interleaved randomized benchmarking on an isolated test device.
  - T1 0.964 ± 0.092 ms and T2-echo 1.155 ± 0.188 ms on a planar transmon test device.
- **Garnet (20 qubits)** (older):
  - Median CZ 99.5%; all 20 qubits genuinely entangled in a GHZ state. Preprint only, 113 authors, no journal reference listed — [C · Abdurakhimov et al., arXiv:2408.12433, 2024-08-22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12433).
  - Production T1 "approximately 40 μs", with a target of more than 100 µs "already achieved in test devices" — [C · Garnet white paper](https://www.iqm.tech/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/IQM-Garnet-20Q-Whitepaper-2024-sm.pdf).
- **Emerald / Crystal 54**:
  - Medians 1-qubit 99.93% and 2-qubit 99.5%, as "early characterisation" (older) — [I, relays IQM · QCR, 2025-07-22](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/amazon-braket-launches-iqm-emerald-54-qubit-superconducting-quantum-processor/).
  - Max CZ 99.85%, QV 64, 25-qubit GHZ >0.5 — [C · Resonance page](https://iqm.tech/iqm-resonance/).
- **Star / central-resonator ("MOVE") architecture** (Renger et al., arXiv:2503.10903; v1 13 Mar 2025, v3 8 Dec 2025):
  - 6 transmons around a transmission-line resonator.
  - 6-qubit GHZ fidelity of 0.86 after readout-error mitigation — [C · arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10903).
  - A secondary summary reports MOVE fidelity 98.8 ± 0.6%, CZ 98.3 ± 0.9% and resonator T1 5.53 ± 0.32 µs — [AlphaXiv overview (search excerpt)](https://alphaxiv.org/overview/2503.10903v2).
  - The architecture is deployed in VLQ (24-qubit star) — [I · EuroHPC](https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/vlq-europes-next-leap-towards-quantum-powered-research-and-innovation-2025-09-23_en).
- **Error detection** (Vigneau et al., arXiv:2503.12869; APS DOI 10.1103/8m33-wn4g, journal not identified on the page) — [C · arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12869):
  - [[4,2,2]] code giving **2 logical qubits** on a 6-qubit star QPU.
  - Logical state fidelities above 96%; logical error per cycle 0.25(2)%–0.91(3)%; logical lifetimes above the best physical element.
  - Post-selection rates are not given in the abstract.
- **Barbell qLDPC codes / Constellation topology**:
  - IQM release (9 Jun 2026) claims up to 1,000× lower logical error and up to 8× fewer physical qubits than the surface code. In Constellation each qubit couples natively to up to 12 neighbours, versus 4 on a square grid — [C · IQM release](https://iqm.tech/?p=18199).
  - Paper (Choe et al., arXiv:2606.06062, 4 Jun 2026) — [C · arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06062):
    - Circuit-level simulations at **physical noise strength 10⁻⁴**.
    - Fewer than 30 data qubits per logical qubit; information preserved for "several trillion" QEC cycles.
    - Hardware complexity constant with code distance.
    - **No hardware experiments.**
- **Halocene targets**: 99.7% physical 2-qubit fidelity at 150 qubits; up to 5 logical qubits for research (older) — [C-relay · TQI, 2025-11-13](https://thequantuminsider.com/2025/11/13/iqm-launches-a-new-quantum-computer-product-line-for-error-correction/).

  - Tested 10 QPUs from IBM, IQM and Quantinuum, including iqm_garnet and iqm_emerald.
  - Two-qubit LR-QAOA at p=3:
