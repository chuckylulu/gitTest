## 1. What TerraPower makes and at what stage (snapshot, 14 Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
TerraPower (Bellevue, WA; founded 2006; chaired by Bill Gates) has three lines of business:
- **Natrium.** A 345 MWe sodium-cooled fast reactor with molten-salt storage, able to run at 100–500 MWe for up to about 5.5 hours. The first unit, Kemmerer Unit 1 (KU1) in Wyoming, received NRC construction permit CPAR-1 on 9 March 2026. Formal construction started on 23 April 2026. By mid-October 2026 the nuclear-island excavation was about half dug. The lead EPC contractor, Bechtel, left in September 2026, and no replacement had been named in the sources found.
- **Molten Chloride Fast Reactor (MCFR).** Still at the experiment stage. The Molten Chloride Reactor Experiment (MCRE) at INL is expected to run around 2028.
- **TerraPower Isotopes.** An actinium-225 (Ac-225) supplier building a $450M plant in Philadelphia, with cGMP output targeted for 2029.

### Cited Findings
- **KU1 design.** A 345 MW sodium-cooled fast reactor coupled with molten-salt integrated energy storage. It can run flexibly from 100 to 500 MW for up to 5.5 hours, and is sited next to PacifiCorp's Naughton plant in Kemmerer, WY. — [PacifiCorp, "Application for Approval of Water Use and Conveyance Agreement and Request for Waiver of Competitive Bidding Rules" (Oregon PUC), 21 Oct 2025](https://edocs.puc.state.or.us/efdocs/HAA/haa340991027.pdf) [CP]
- **Rated thermal power.** The construction permit application is for 840 MWt / 345 MWe. — [POWER, "TerraPower's Kemmerer 1 Enters Construction: Timeline…", S. Patel, 23 Apr 2026](https://www.powermag.com/terrapowers-kemmerer-1-enters-construction-timeline-of-the-natrium-projects-road-to-first-power/) [IND]
- **Storage and output.** The NRC describes KU1 as varying its supply of energy to the grid "up to a net 500 megawatts electric (MWe)—while maintaining constant reactor power." The plant will serve PacifiCorp's service area. — [NRC Commission, CLI-26-5, Memorandum and Order, Docket 50-613-CP, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2606/ML26063A399.pdf) [REG]
- **Technology owners.** Natrium is a TerraPower and GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy technology. — [World Nuclear News, "TerraPower starts construction of first US utility-scale advanced nuclear plant", 23 Apr 2026](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/terrapower-starts-construction-of-first-us-utility-scale-advanced-nuclear-plant) [IND]
- **Construction permit.** NRC staff issued CPAR-1 on 9 March 2026 to US SFR Owner, LLC (USO), a wholly owned TerraPower subsidiary. The permit does not authorize operation. — [NRC, "NRC Issues Construction Permit to US SFR Owner" (page updated 10 Mar 2026)](https://www.nrc.gov/node/2156776) [REG]
- **Site status, 15 Oct 2026.** During a site tour, the Sodium Test and Fill Facility was standing and the reactor excavation was "about halfway to its 120-foot depth." The project was "moving from groundwork toward vertical construction." The 60-acre site splits into a 30-acre Energy Island and a 30-acre Nuclear Island. — [Cowboy State Daily, "Bill Gates Tours Kemmerer's Natrium Nuclear Facility…", 16 Oct 2026](https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/10/16/bill-gates-tours-kemmerers-natrium-nuclear-facility-and-sees-something-profound/) [IND]
- **Sodium test facility.** The facility is 30,000 sq ft and 110 ft tall, will hold more than 300 tonnes of liquid sodium, and will test first-of-a-kind components before installation. — [SVI News, "TerraPower provides update on Kemmerer Unit 1 Construction", 15 Oct 2026](https://svinews.com/2026/regional-news/124129/terrapower-provides-update-on-kemmerer-unit-1-construction/) [IND (relaying company figures)]
- **MCRE schedule.** MCRE is a six-month subscale test "expected to run in 2028." Commercial MCFR deployments "could occur during the 2030s." — [ANS Nuclear Newswire, "INL makes first fuel for Molten Chloride Reactor Experiment", 8 Dec 2025](https://www.ans.org/news/2025-12-08/article-7602/inl-makes-first-fuel-for-molten-chloride-reactor-experiment/) [IND]
- **Isotopes plant.** TerraPower Isotopes broke ground on a 250,000 sq ft Ac-225 plant ("Bellwether Laboratory") in Philadelphia, with cGMP production beginning in 2029. — [TerraPower, "TerraPower Isotopes Breaks Ground on World's Largest Actinium-225 Manufacturing Facility in Philadelphia", May 2026](https://www.terrapower.com/TerraPower-Isotopes-Breaks-Ground-on-Worlds-Largest-Actinium-225-Manufacturing-Facility-in-Philadelphia) [CO]
- **Corporate basics.** Founded 2006 by Bill Gates (chairman); headquartered in Bellevue, WA. — [Wikipedia, "TerraPower"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TerraPower) [SEC]

### Inferences
- As of 14 Nov 2026 Natrium is in early nuclear-island civil works: excavation, not yet reported safety-related concrete. That is about 4–4.5 years before TerraPower's stated 2030 construction-completion target.
- TerraPower has no commercial revenue from power generation. The isotopes business is its only operating product line, and it does not disclose revenue for it.

### Gaps
- I found no report of the first safety-related (nuclear-island basemat) concrete pour as of 14 Nov 2026.
- I found no public percent-complete figure for KU1.
- TerraPower's total headcount was not found in any reliable source.

## 2. Natrium licensing and construction: NRC, state permits, construction start, EPC

### Takeaway
The NRC review ran from submission in March 2024 to permit issuance on 9 March 2026, in an uncontested proceeding with no intervenors. The permit came about three months later than the NRC's accelerated end-2025 target, but about five months ahead of its original 27-month schedule. Four exemptions were granted. TerraPower has told the NRC it expects to finish construction by 28 February 2031 and then apply for a 40-year operating license (Class 103). TerraPower's public target is construction completion in 2030. Non-nuclear works began in June 2024 and "official" construction started on 23 April 2026. Bechtel, EPC contractor since 2020, left in September 2026, and TerraPower is rebidding the EPC contract.

## 3. Schedule and cost: target dates over time, cost estimates, DOE ARDP cost share and disbursements, GAO/OIG

### Takeaway
Target operation has moved from 2028 (announced 2021) to about 2030 (after the HALEU problem in December 2022), and then to a 2030 construction finish with commercial operation in 2031. PacifiCorp's filing gives "fall 2031," and its economic model uses 2032. The project cost has been stated as about $4 billion since 2021, and I found no public update. Sources give the DOE ARDP share variously as "up to $2B," "nearly $2B" (GAO), or "up to $1.6B" (POWER, 2024). I could not find the amount actually disbursed, and USAspending lists no ARDP award under TerraPower's or USO's name. GAO's 2022 oversight recommendation on DOE's large demonstration projects was still open at GAO's last update.

## 4. Fuel: HALEU sources and contracts, DOE allocations, fuel fabrication, effect of losing Russian supply

### Takeaway
Natrium's first core was originally to use Russian HALEU. That plan ended after the February 2022 invasion of Ukraine, and in December 2022 TerraPower announced at least a two-year delay. Current arrangements:
- **DOE.** A conditional HALEU allocation from April 2025, quantity undisclosed.
- **ASP Isotopes / Quantum Leap Energy (QLE).** A May 2025 loan agreement (up to $22M, conditional) and two supply agreements. One covers the **initial core**; the other covers up to 150 tU of HALEU in 2028–2037. Both depend on a South African enrichment plant at Pelindaba that is not yet licensed or built, using a technology ASP says it has not yet applied to U-235.
- **Fabrication.** Framatome (metallization) and a GNF-A Natrium Fuel Facility at Wilmington, NC.

Centrus's latest 10-K does not mention TerraPower. The first-core HALEU quantity was not found.

### Inferences
- On NRC licensing, TerraPower is the documented front-runner among commercial advanced reactors. Its permit (March 2026) came about 8–15 months before X-energy's Long Mott permit (expected H1 2027, possibly approved November 2026 per an unverified headline).
- Compared with Oklo, TerraPower has a full NRC construction permit for a commercial unit. Oklo's near-term milestones rely on DOE authorization paths and a stated 2028 Aurora target that this research could not verify against NRC records.
- Each of TerraPower's announced schedule slips (2028 to 2030 to 2031) traces to an external constraint the company named: HALEU availability, and later the licensing and construction sequence. Independent sources have not yet tested the new 2030/2031 dates against a contractor-backed schedule, because the EPC contract is being rebid.
