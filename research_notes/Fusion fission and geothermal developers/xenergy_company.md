Source labels used throughout: **[CC]** = company-controlled (X-energy press releases, website, investor decks, and X-energy's own SEC filings; filings carry securities-law liability but remain company statements); **[IND]** = not controlled by X-energy (NRC and other regulators, counterparties' own statements/filings, journalism). ## Q1. What X-energy makes, at what stage, and how far along the overall programme is

### Takeaway
X-energy is a pre-revenue-scale, pre-commercial reactor-technology licensor and TRISO fuel fabricator: no Xe-100 has been built and no customer has taken a final investment decision (FID), but its first plant (Dow's 4-unit Long Mott station) received an NRC construction permit on 9 Nov 2026 and its TX-1 fuel plant (NRC-licensed 13 Feb 2026) finished vertical construction in Q3 2026, targeting operations in 1H 2028. The company guides to first commercial reactor delivery in the "early 2030s."

### Cited Findings
- Corporate identity: X-Energy, Inc. (Delaware) is an Up-C holding company whose principal asset is ~69.7% of the common units of X-Energy Reactor Company, LLC ("XERC"), which runs the business; HQ 530 Gaither Road, Rockville, MD; founded 2009 by Dr. Kamal "Kam" Ghaffarian — [X-energy Form 424B4 prospectus, dated 23 Apr 2026, filed 27 Apr 2026 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Xe-100: helium-cooled pebble-bed high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR) designed for 80 MWe or 200 MWt per module; standard "Xe-100 plant" is four modules = 320 MWe / 800 MWt; designed to deliver 565°C steam and to ramp from 100% to 40% power "in minutes"; 60-year anticipated life; online refuelling — [424B4, 23 Apr 2026 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Design maturity (Apr 2026): "The Xe-100 is in the second of three engineering design phases"; the final design phase (phase 3) is expected "in 2027" and "would culminate in a final investment decision" — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Company risk-factor admission: "We have not yet delivered a commercial Xe-100 or achieved final investment decisions (FIDs) for any deployments; our FOAK schedule, cost, and performance are uncertain" — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Fuel: TRISO-X pebble fuel using HALEU enriched to 15.5%; made by wholly owned subsidiary TRISO-X, LLC; a pilot line at Oak Ridge National Laboratory has operated since 2016 and produces "kilogram batch quantities ... for development purposes" — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Fuel qualification status (company claim, Apr 2026): NRC has approved X-energy's TRISO-X pebble fuel qualification methodology (topical report, ADAMS ML21246A289); company says it is "in the irradiation test phase and in the final stages of qualifying" the fuel — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm); [X-energy Regulatory Milestones web page, accessed Nov 2026 [CC]](https://x-energy.com/regulatory-milestones/)
- Independent framing of fuel testing (Feb 2026): fuel qualification testing at Idaho National Laboratory is "a 13-month irradiation campaign still underway" — [POWER magazine, "TRISO-X Secures First-Ever NRC Category II License...", 13 Feb 2026 [IND]](https://www.powermag.com/triso-x-secures-first-ever-nrc-category-ii-license-for-commercial-advanced-nuclear-fuel-fabrication/). On 12 Nov 2026 CEO Clay Sell said interim ATR irradiation results "have met all of our expectations," with a full report to follow (not independently verified) — [Motley Fool transcript of X-energy Q3 2026 call, 12 Nov 2026 [CC via transcript]](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/12/x-energy-xe-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript)
- NRC pre-licensing record (company claim): X-energy says it "secured safety evaluations on 14 topical reports" before the March 2025 construction permit application; its website lists 12 topical reports (QA programme, fuel qualification, control-room staffing, NEI 18-04 risk-informed licensing, principal design criteria, atmospheric dispersion, operator training, core design method, transient/safety analysis methods, source term, EPZ sizing, graphite qualification) without approval dates — [X-energy Regulatory Milestones page [CC]](https://x-energy.com/regulatory-milestones/)
- XENITH microreactor: HTGR on TRISO compacts, 750°C, 3–10 MWe (likely 5 MWe single or 10 MWe dual), 20-year life without refuelling; "not yet operational"; grew out of DoD Project Pele work (~$60 million across three DoD awards: 2020 $14.3M, 2021 $28.7M, 2023 $17.5M); "the period of performance for the XENITH microreactor has expired, but the period of performance for Project Pele remains active" — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Space: a DOE/NASA Fission Surface Power design award (June 2022); TRISO-X performs fuel fabrication work for others "including space applications" — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Business model: technology fees, engineering/lifecycle services and fuel fabrication; X-energy says it does "not construct the power plants and do[es] not plan to own or operate" them and does not provide EPC services or take construction risk — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm); [X-energy investor presentation, 8-K Ex. 99.1, 19 May 2026 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000208889626000005/xe-ex99_1.htm)
- Illustrative unit economics (management estimates, May 2026) per 320 MWe plant over 60 years: technology fee $50–300M (100% margin); services $800–1,300M revenue (20–30% margin); fuel $1,560–3,125M (30–60% margin); total $2.41–4.725B revenue and $750–2,545M gross margin (30–55%); "~15–35% of Reactor Revenue Received Prior to and Including COD" — [X-energy investor presentation, 19 May 2026 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000208889626000005/xe-ex99_1.htm)
- Pipeline claim: "144 reactors with three announced commercial Xe-100 projects in the U.S. and the fourth announced in the U.K. for approximately 11.5 gigawatts electric, assuming each customer exercises its contingent rights in full" (Nov 2026); in April 2026 it was "more than 11 GWe, 144 reactor pipeline" across Dow, Amazon and Centrica — [X-energy Q3 2026 earnings release, 8-K Ex. 99.1, 12 Nov 2026 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000119312526456633/xe-ex99_1.htm); [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm)
- Facilities: Frederick, MD operator training facility (opened March 2023, 10,447 sq ft, Xe-100 simulator; one control room designed to manage up to 12 reactors); planned 90,000 sq ft XTAC non-nuclear test facility (helium test loop) in Frederick with 50% ARDP cost share — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm); on 12 Nov 2026 Sell said a helium test loop is expected to become operational "over the next couple of years" — [Q3 2026 call transcript [CC via transcript]](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/12/x-energy-xe-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript)
- Headcount: 916 employees as of 17 Mar 2026 (317 with master's degrees, 104 PhDs) — [424B4 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000110465926049515/tm2527015-22_424b4.htm); ">900 employees" in May 2026 — [investor presentation, 19 May 2026 [CC]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2088896/000208889626000005/xe-ex99_1.htm)

### Inferences
- Of the ~11.5 GWe "pipeline," only the 320 MWe Long Mott plant holds an NRC permit and none has an FID; the pipeline figure depends on counterparties exercising options/contingent rights in full (inference from the company's own qualifying language).
- The company's revenue model is back-end loaded: by its own illustrative economics most lifetime revenue (fuel and post-COD services) arrives after commercial operation, which it guides to the early 2030s.

### Gaps

- Independent confirmation of ATR/INL irradiation results was not found; the INL/DOE status of the TRISO-X pebble irradiation was not retrieved.
- Headcount after March 2026: one transcript summary showed "889," but it was not in the spoken remarks and was not verified.

## Q2. Long Mott Generating Station (Dow, Seadrift, Texas; four Xe-100): licensing record, schedule, and how construction/operation dates moved

### Takeaway
The NRC docketed Dow subsidiary Long Mott Energy's construction permit application on 12 May 2025 and issued the permit on 9 Nov 2026 (the NRC dashboard lists 8 Nov), after an Environmental Assessment/FONSI (not an EIS), a final safety evaluation in late October and a hearing on 22 Sep 2026. That was earlier than X-energy's own April and August 2026 guidance (Q1 or 1H 2027). Construction timing has not been firmed up: Dow says the permit "does not represent a final decision" to build, X-energy now speaks of Dow considering a "preliminary investment decision in 2027," and ARDP reimbursement cannot extend past about 2030 without a contract change.

- NRC milestone dashboard (actual dates): application tendered 31 Mar 2025 (ADAMS ML25090A063); acceptance review began 1 Apr 2025; docketing decision 12 May 2025; review schedule letter issued 10 Jun 2025; draft safety evaluation Feb 2026; advanced safety evaluation (all sections available for ACRS) Aug 2026; final safety evaluation Oct 2026; hearing 22 Sep 2026; "Construction Permit Decision: 11/08/2026"; page last updated 12 Nov 2026 — [NRC, "Long Mott Energy, LLC" applicant project page, updated 12 Nov 2026 [IND]](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/advanced/who-were-working-with/applicant-projects/long-mott)
- Environmental review was an Environmental Assessment with a Finding of No Significant Impact, not an EIS: initial EA inputs 26 Sep 2025; revised EA 19 Dec 2025; EA finalized 14 Apr 2026; EA and FONSI issued 18 May 2026; the EA title refers to "Environmental Review Exemptions" (which exemptions is not stated on the page) — [NRC Long Mott page [IND]](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/advanced/who-were-working-with/applicant-projects/long-mott)
- The NRC released its Final Safety Evaluation Report on 30 Oct 2026, concluding the plant and site provide the safety basis to begin construction — [World Nuclear News, "Texas SMR plant moves to final permitting stage," 2 Nov 2026 [IND]](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/texas-smr-plant-moves-to-final-permitting-stage)

### Inferences
- Timing arithmetic: docketing (12 May 2025) to permit (9 Nov 2026) is 546 days (~17.9 months); schedule letter (10 Jun 2025) to permit is 517 days (~17 months). Sell's "over a month ahead of schedule" and Nieh's "delivered on the 18-month schedule" are compatible only if the official target date fell in December 2026 (consistent with an 18-month clock starting at the June 2025 schedule letter); the NRC dashboard shows no target dates to settle this.
- The language around construction has softened over 2026: from "construction is expected to begin in 2027" / "first half of 2027" (April 2026 prospectus) to Dow "consider[ing] a preliminary investment decision in 2027" (Nov 2026) and Dow's own "later this decade." No construction start date is currently committed by Dow.
- Because ARDP reimbursement effectively ends around 2030, and the early-2030s COD means most nuclear construction would fall after that, Dow's share of construction costs could rise materially unless DOE modifies the award; X-energy itself flags that this "could affect Dow's final investment decision."

## Q10. Roadmap promised vs actual; documented contradictions; comparison with TerraPower and Oklo

### Takeaway
Against its own 2026 guidance, X-energy beat its licensing schedule (Long Mott permit in Nov 2026 vs guided Q1/1H 2027) and says TX-1 vertical construction finished on schedule, but its reactor deployment dates have drifted: the ARDP demonstration originally framed "through 2027" is now an early-2030s COD with no FID, Long Mott construction-start language softened, and the Cascade CPA target slipped from end-2026 to 1H 2027. Press figures on ARDP money "received" overstate cash actually reimbursed. TerraPower's Natrium received its construction permit about eight months before Long Mott (March 2026).

### Inferences
- Licensing milestones have recently been met early relative to company guidance, while customer-decision milestones (FID, construction start) and second-project licensing (Cascade CPA) have moved later; the binding constraint has shifted from NRC review to customer investment decisions and supply chain, as Sell himself states.
- Relative to TerraPower, X-energy's ARDP demonstration is about eight months behind on the construction permit and, unlike Natrium, has no announced construction start for nuclear work or EPC price guarantees in the sources reviewed (X-energy's model leaves EPC to customers).
