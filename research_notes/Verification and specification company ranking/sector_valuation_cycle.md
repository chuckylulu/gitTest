## 3. Drawdowns and price episodes (dates, causes, magnitudes)

### Takeaway
The EDA shares have had five distinct shocks since May 2025: the China EDA export-licence episode (about -10% in a day, reversed in July 2025), Synopsys's own results shock (-35.8% on 10 Sep 2025), the early-2026 software sell-off on AI-agent fears (Synopsys -15% and Cadence -11% from 27 Jan to 6 Feb 2026), the Kimi K3 open-tools chip demo (-7.9% and -9.5% on 17 Jul 2026), and the October-November 2026 AI-infrastructure sell-off, in which the chip ETF fell about 40% from its June peak while the EDA names fell much less. DeepMind's AlphaProof Code announcement (10 Nov 2026) moved both EDA shares only -1.5%.
