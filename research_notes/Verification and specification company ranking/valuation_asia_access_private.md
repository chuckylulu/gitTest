## 1. Valuation table: latest close on or before 12 Nov 2026

### Takeaway
On one consistent source, the two large Chinese EDA vendors, Empyrean and Primarius, trade at 26–33x EV/sales on near-zero profits. Global Unichip (GUC) trades at about 20x EV/sales and 159x trailing earnings after rising about 312% in a year. Most other Asian design-service, IP and engineering names trade at 1–9x EV/sales, and most sit 25–77% below their 52-week highs. The exceptions on multiples are eMemory (about 36x) and VeriSilicon (about 15x).

## 4. Legal limits on US persons (not legal advice; uncertain where marked)

### Takeaway
- **Listed shares.** Under the Outbound Investment Security Program (31 CFR 850, in force since 2 Jan 2025) and the COINS Act rule proposed on 26 Oct 2026, buying listed shares or fund units is an "excepted transaction". The exception is lost if the investment gives governance rights beyond standard minority protections.
- **Private rounds.** Private equity in Chinese EDA developers falls under "prohibited technology".
- **NS-CMIC list.** None of the Asian names here is on the Non-SDN Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Companies list. However, OFAC bars buying funds that hold any NS-CMIC security, and the main domestic Chinese chip ETFs hold SMIC.
- **Entity List.** Empyrean's listing restricts exports to it. I found nothing in that rule that bars buying its shares.

### Cited Findings
- **COINS Act and the proposed rule.** "On December 18, 2025, Congress enacted the Comprehensive Outbound Investment National Security (COINS) Act as part of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026". It adds "high-performance computing and supercomputing" and "hypersonic systems". Countries of concern now include Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia and Venezuela (Maduro regime) alongside the PRC, which includes Hong Kong and Macau. Source: Treasury NPRM, 91 FR 67396, 26 Oct 2026; proposed 31 CFR Part 851; comments due 7 Dec 2026 [PRIMARY] (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-26/pdf/2026-21786.pdf).
- **EDA coverage.** "Consistent with the Part 850 Regulations, the Treasury Department proposes including within the definition of prohibited technology the development or production of any electronic design automation software for the design of integrated circuits or advanced packaging" [PRIMARY, same NPRM].
- **Exceptions for listed securities and funds** [PRIMARY, same NPRM]:
  - excepted: "investment in a security ... that trades on a securities exchange or the 'over-the-counter' market in any jurisdiction", which Treasury calls "substantively equivalent" to the Part 850 exception;
  - also excepted: securities of "an investment company, such as an index fund, mutual fund, or exchange traded fund"; limited-partner stakes of $2 million or less, or with binding assurances; and derivatives of these;
  - carve-out: "an investment is not an excepted transaction if it affords the U.S. person governance rights beyond standard minority shareholder protections".
- **Transition.** Part 850 "will continue to apply to activities occurring between January 2, 2025, and the effective date of the final regulations" [PRIMARY].
- **Listed-entity escalation.** Proposed § 851.226(v) would turn a notifiable technology into a prohibited one where the foreign person, or any affiliate or subsidiary, is on one of these lists [PRIMARY]:
  - the Entity List;
  - the Military End-User List;
  - the SDN list, including the 50% rule;
  - the NS-CMIC list;
  - the section 1260H list;
  - the BIOSECURE list.
- **NS-CMIC list (EO 13959 as amended by EO 14032).**
  - OFAC's consolidated non-SDN file (fetched 12 Nov 2026) shows no match for Empyrean, Huada, Primarius, Semitronix, VeriSilicon, Brite, SMIT, Guowei, Xpeedic, Alchip, Global Unichip, Socionext or Zuken. "CHINA ELECTRONICS CORPORATION" and "SEMICONDUCTOR MANUFACTURING INTERNATIONAL CORPORATION" are listed under CMIC-EO13959 [PRIMARY] (https://www.treasury.gov/ofac/downloads/consolidated/cons_prim.csv).
  - FAQ 857: the prohibitions reach a subsidiary "only if such subsidiary itself is publicly listed on the NS-CMIC List", and "OFAC's 50 percent rule does not apply" [PRIMARY] (https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/topic/5671).
  - FAQ 861: buying US or foreign funds that hold such securities "is prohibited, regardless of such securities' share of the underlying index fund, ETF, or derivative thereof" (3 Jun 2021) [PRIMARY] (https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/861).
  - Empyrean's largest holder, China Electronics Co. (中国电子有限公司, 21.03% at 30 Sep 2026), belongs to the listed CEC group [PRIMARY via china.md]. Under FAQ 857 that listing does not reach Empyrean [INFERENCE].
- **Entity List.**
  - Effective 2 Dec 2024 (89 FR 96830), BIS added these entities as entities that "aid in the development or production of ICs for military end use" (§ 744.11). "A license will be required for all items subject to the EAR", with "a presumption of denial" [PRIMARY] (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-12-05/pdf/2024-28267.pdf):
    - Beijing Huada Jiutian Technology Co., Ltd., a.k.a. "Empyrean", "Empyrean Technology" and "Huada Jiutian";
    - its Chengdu, Guangzhou, Nanjing, Shanghai, Shenzhen and Xi'an companies, and Empyrean Korea;
    - S2C Limited, Guowei Group (Shenzhen) (SMIT Group) and Shenzhen Guoweichip (GWX).
  - The rule governs items subject to the EAR. I found no investment prohibition in it [INFERENCE].
- **BIS "affiliates" rule.** The rule of 30 Sep 2025 (90 FR 47201) would extend Entity List restrictions to affiliates. It was stayed on 10 Nov 2025 (90 FR 50857). On 9 Nov 2026 BIS said it "is extending the suspension" and that the rule "extends the suspension through January 10, 2027" (91 FR 70789) [PRIMARY] (https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/09/2026-22793/extension-of-the-suspension-of-expansion-of-end-user-controls-for-affiliates-of-certain-listed).

Implications by route [INFERENCE; legal reading, not advice]:
