## ASM International (full data sheet)

### Takeaway
ASM is the largest supplier of single-wafer atomic layer deposition (its own figure: >55% share) and a challenger in epitaxy; in the nine months to September 2026 it earned a 52.4% gross margin and 33.2% adjusted operating margin with €1.34bn cash, no debt, and now guides 2027 revenue above €5.7bn — its former 2030 target. The share closed at €694.60 on 13 Nov 2026, 36% below its 22 June 2026 high, at about 34× trailing and about 20× consensus 2027 earnings; no net short position of 0.5% or more is on the AFM register.

### Cited Findings

#### (a) Position
- **Products.** Deposition equipment: single-wafer ALD, epitaxy (silicon; silicon carbide via LPE, bought 2022), PECVD, vertical furnaces; CMP for compound/More-than-Moore devices via Axus Technology (bought Dec 2025); spares and services. [FILING] — [ASM FY25 PR](https://www.asm.com/media/yvxbavwe/20260303-asm-reports-q4-and-full-year-2025-results.pdf)
- **ALD share.** ">55% market share in ALD" (Oct 2026); "mid-50s %", with ALD "more than half of our total equipment revenue" (Jul 2025). The market data behind it is ASM's own ("Historical market data: ASM"). [COMPANY CLAIM] — [ASM Q3-26 deck](https://www.asm.com/downloads/presentations-other/2026-q3-investor-presentation); [ASM Q2-25 deck](https://www.asm.com/media/spnjlce3/q225_investor_presentation.pdf)
- **Epitaxy share.** 16% of the silicon-epitaxy market in 2022, source cited as TechInsights; target then was >30% by 2025. [CONFIRMED: TechInsights as cited by ASM] — [ASM Q2-25 deck](https://www.asm.com/media/spnjlce3/q225_investor_presentation.pdf). No later epitaxy share figure appears in the 2026 materials; the 2030 aim is worded "further expand leading-edge market share". [COMPANY CLAIM] — [ASM Q4-25 deck](https://www.asm.com/media/o0el4c3i/q425_investor_presentation.pdf)
- **Market sizes (ASM data, Investor Day Sept 2025).** Single-wafer ALD $3.0bn (2024) → $5.1–6.1bn (2030); epitaxy $1.5bn (2024) → $2.5–3.2bn (2030); both 9–13% a year.
