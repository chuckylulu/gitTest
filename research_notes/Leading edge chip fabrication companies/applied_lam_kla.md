## 1. Cross-company snapshot: latest numbers, the 2026 share-price round trip, consensus

### Takeaway
All three reported record quarters in Oct–Nov 2026 and guided higher, and Lam and KLA put 2027 wafer-fab-equipment spending above $200B against the mid-$150B range for 2026; yet all three shares closed on 13 Nov 2026 about 48–50% below their 30 June 2026 record closes, at 19–26x current-fiscal-year consensus earnings, after rising roughly 150–180% in the first half of 2026.

### Inferences (mine)
- On vendor data all three trade near or below their five-year average forward multiples, but on earnings estimates that are 1.5–1.7x the fiscal year just ended; the comparison with "own history" therefore depends on whether FY2027–28 estimates are treated as mid-cycle or peak.
- Short interest of 1.7–2.2% of float and zero sell ratings on any of the three indicate that neither a crowded short nor published sell-side scepticism exists; a short position would be contrarian against sell-side positioning, though not against the price action since June.

## 2. Applied Materials — data sheet

### Takeaway
Applied closed fiscal 2026 with record revenue of $34.4B, a 35% operating margin in the fourth quarter and guidance for an $11.1B first quarter, with China down to 25–27% of revenue and its export-control case settled for $252.5M; the weaker points on the record are cash conversion (free cash flow 61% of net income, capex 7.8% of revenue) and market-position claims that rest largely on the company's own word.
