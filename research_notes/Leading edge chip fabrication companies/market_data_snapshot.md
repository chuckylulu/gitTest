## 8. What could not be found or verified, and where sources conflict

### Takeaway
The price, valuation and consensus tables are complete on one consistent source and date; the main holes are Korean short-selling data, 10-year forward-multiple history, filings-level checks of net cash and share counts, and valuations for four of the six unlisted companies.

### Cited Findings
Figures on which sources conflict (both shown; the one I trust more is named):
1. **Korean closing prices.** StockAnalysis and Yahoo: Samsung KRW 206,000, SK Hynix KRW 1,216,000. Naver: KRW 209,000 and KRW 1,233,000 at 20:20 KST. Trust the first pair: it is the KRX regular-session close; Naver's includes Nextrade after-market trades.
2. **Samsung market value.** KRW 1,324.45tn (StockAnalysis, common plus preferred) against KRW 1,221.87tn (Naver, common only, at KRW 209,000). Both are right for what they count; common-only at the KRX close is KRW 1,204.3tn [CALC].
3. **SMIC market value.** HKD 622.7bn (StockAnalysis, each class at its own price), HKD 415.4bn (all shares at the H price) and CNY 946.2bn (Tencent, all shares at the A price). The first is the most defensible single figure; the choice matters because the A-share trades 168% above the H-share.
4. **Japanese market values.** StockAnalysis excludes treasury shares, IR Bank includes them: Canon JPY 4.03tn against JPY 6.44tn. Trust the ex-treasury figure for valuation.
5. **Trailing P/E.** Samsung 6.35 (StockAnalysis), 9.38 (Naver), 11.5 (companiesmarketcap); SK Hynix 4.66, 5.50, 6.38; Tokyo Electron 30.46 (StockAnalysis) against 37.28 (IR Bank trailing); Disco 32.28 against 38.01; Hoya 28.48 against 31.44. Trust StockAnalysis: its trailing period includes the September-quarter results and it uses shares net of treasury stock; the others lag a quarter or more.
6. **Forward P/E definitions.** Next-twelve-months consensus (StockAnalysis), fiscal-year consensus (my FY1 and FY2 columns) and company-guidance basis (IR Bank) give, for example, Tokyo Electron 19.7, 25.2 and 24.8; Screen 10.1, 13.6 and 14.1; AGC 11.6, 14.0 and 26.7; Lasertec 25.4, 32.8 and 36.0. None is wrong; the fiscal year must be stated.
7. **Analyst targets and estimates for Korea.** Samsung average target KRW 475,629 (StockAnalysis, 36 analysts) against KRW 468,571 (Naver, 12 Nov); SK Hynix KRW 3,097,152 against KRW 3,062,000. 2026 EPS: Samsung KRW 47,640 against KRW 45,655; SK Hynix KRW 360,340 against KRW 355,258. Different panels; neither preferred.
8. **US short interest.** StockAnalysis shows ASML 1.33m shares and Entegris 8.57m; FINRA's 30 Oct 2026 figures are 1,143,226 and 8,541,510. Trust FINRA (primary, later date). Days to cover also differ because FINRA floors the ratio at 1.0 and uses its own volume window (Micron: FINRA 1.00, StockAnalysis 0.86, my ratio 0.92).
9. **52-week extremes.** Samsung high KRW 374,500 (Yahoo, StockAnalysis) against KRW 380,000 (Naver, which includes Nextrade sessions); Naura range 386.62–968.00 (Yahoo) against 385.86–967.24 (Tencent, different adjustment). Differences are under 1.5%.

These measure different things (capital paid in against voting rights), so they need not conflict; the voting terms were not re-verified.
