## 1. SMIC — results, economics, what teardowns confirm, listing structure

### Takeaway
SMIC's Q3 2026 results (published 12 Nov 2026) show a record quarter — revenue US$3.19bn, gross margin 29.9%, utilisation 97.3% — but the business still earns under 5% on equity, spends more on capex than it generates from operations, and roughly a quarter of 9M 2026 profit was non-recurring. Outside teardowns confirm 7nm-class (2023) and "N+3" (Dec 2025) production of Huawei phone chips; volumes, yields, cost and AI-chip capacity rest on estimates and press reports. The H-share (HK$48.52) trades about 61% below the A-share (RMB 110.50); US persons may buy neither; the H-share is on Hong Kong's short-selling list, while the practical position for the A-share was not verified.

## 2. SMEE (Shanghai Micro Electronics Equipment) — unlisted

### Takeaway
SMEE is China's only established maker of front-end lithography scanners, but nothing an outside party has confirmed goes beyond dry ArF/KrF/i-line tools and packaging/display steppers; every claim of a 28nm immersion tool in production traces to self-media, a Korean press report or the company, and two industry sources in autumn 2026 still describe it as not in production. The domestic immersion tool that outside media do report in low-volume production comes from a different, SiCarrier-linked company.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Position and ownership**
- Founded 2002 from an 863-Programme lithography project; founder He Rongming left in 2018; the state-assets regulator (SASAC) is shown as owner of 32% (citing Reuters, Mar 2023); prepared for a listing with CSC Financial in 2017 but has not listed [PRESS] — [Wikipedia: SMEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Micro_Electronics_Equipment)

## 6. Export controls on chip-making equipment as they stand in November 2026

### Takeaway
The US rules of October 2022, October 2023 and December 2024 are all in force. The September 2025 Affiliates (50%) Rule has been suspended since 10 Nov 2025; the suspension, due to end 9 Nov 2026, was extended to 10 Jan 2027, the same day to which China extended its rare-earth pause and to which the trade truce now runs. ## 7. "China is closing the gap" versus "China is stuck without EUV"

### Takeaway
Both statements are supported, on different axes. Independent teardowns show SMIC still shrinking without EUV (N+3 density above TSMC's 2020-vintage N6) and domestic tool share is rising fast. But each further step costs more and yields less, the measured density gap to TSMC's current node is about a factor of two, lithography is under 1% localised, and the best outside estimates put Huawei's AI-chip output at a few percent of Nvidia's through 2028.
