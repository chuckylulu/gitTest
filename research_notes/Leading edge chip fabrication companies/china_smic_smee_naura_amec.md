## 1. SMIC — results, economics, what teardowns confirm, listing structure

### Takeaway
SMIC's Q3 2026 results (published 12 Nov 2026) show a record quarter — revenue US$3.19bn, gross margin 29.9%, utilisation 97.3% — but the business still earns under 5% on equity, spends more on capex than it generates from operations, and roughly a quarter of 9M 2026 profit was non-recurring. Outside teardowns confirm 7nm-class (2023) and "N+3" (Dec 2025) production of Huawei phone chips; volumes, yields, cost and AI-chip capacity rest on estimates and press reports. The H-share (HK$48.52) trades about 61% below the A-share (RMB 110.50); US persons may buy neither; only the H-share is formally shortable.

## 2. SMEE (Shanghai Micro Electronics Equipment) — unlisted

### Takeaway
SMEE is China's only established maker of front-end lithography scanners, but nothing an outside party has confirmed goes beyond dry ArF/KrF/i-line tools and packaging/display steppers; every claim of a 28nm immersion tool in production traces to self-media, a Korean press report or the company, and two industry sources in autumn 2026 still describe it as not in production. The domestic immersion tool that outside media do report in low-volume production comes from a different, SiCarrier-linked company.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Position and ownership**
- Founded 2002 from an 863-Programme lithography project; founder He Rongming left in 2018; the state-assets regulator (SASAC) is shown as owner of 32% (citing Reuters, Mar 2023); prepared for a listing with CSC Financial in 2017 but has not listed [PRESS] — [Wikipedia: SMEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Micro_Electronics_Equipment)
- Products on its own website: "SSX600", "SSB800" and "SSC800" lithography series for IC, flat-panel and LED/MEMS/power applications; no immersion model is shown [COMPANY CLAIM] — [SMEE website](https://www.smee.com.cn/)

## 3. Naura and AMEC — results, products, sanctions, valuations

### Takeaway
Both grew revenue in 9M 2026 but in different shapes. Naura (broad platform, state-controlled, Entity-Listed) saw Q3 revenue growth stall at +1.3% with margin slipping and blamed component shortages.
