## 1. Claim test — "ASML ships about 65 EUV tools a year and plans about 30% more for 2027"

### Takeaway
Roughly right, but imprecise in three ways: "about 65" is ASML's 2026 output/capacity for low-NA (NXE) systems only and is a step-up from 40–53 EUV systems a year in 2022–2025, not a steady rate; the "30% more" is a 2027 capacity plan for low-NA EUV (about 85 units), not revenue or shipment guidance; and on 14 October 2026 ASML said even that plan will not meet demand and it is studying more than a further 30% for 2028. High-NA units (4–5 in 2026) come on top.

### Inferences
- 65 × 1.3 = about 85 low-NA in 2027; a further 30% gives about 110 in 2028 (my arithmetic, matching the analyst figures the CFO accepted).
- With 45 low-NA systems recognised in nine months, about 20 must be recognised in Q4 2026 to reach 65; Q4 guidance implies €10.5–11.5bn of system sales (guided sales less ~€3.7bn installed-base sales), so the number is plausible but not yet delivered.
- The CEO's "more than 50%" and the CFO's "nearly 50%" both describe 65 versus 44 (+48%); the CFO's wording is the accurate one.

### Gaps
- Total EUV units shipped (as opposed to recognised) for 2024 and 2025 were not found in the documents read; ASML has not published 2026 High-NA shipment counts.
- ASML gives no unit guidance for High-NA in 2027–2028.

## 2. ASML — business data sheet (a)–(d), with latest numbers

### Takeaway
ASML is the only supplier of EUV scanners, earns operating margins of 35–44%, funds itself entirely from cash flow, and has raised 2026 guidance at every report this year; the weak points in the record are forecasting (2025 targets cut in October 2024), a slower High-NA rollout than first planned, and dependence on one optics supplier, one dominant customer and a China business that policy can remove.

## 6. Export-control exposure

### Takeaway
EUV has never been licensed to China and newer immersion DUV models need Dutch licences; what remains — older DUV tools and servicing of the installed base, within China sales of €9.5bn in 2025 and about 20% of 2026 sales — is the target of the US MATCH Act proposal, which the Dutch government opposes and whose fate was not verifiable at 14 November 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Rules in force as ASML describes them: export licences are required under Dutch, US and other law "for EUV systems, specific DUV immersion systems, and some of our other products"; US measures add licence requirements for certain Chinese entities and "limitations on support by US persons for non-US origin items destined for advanced-node fabs in China"; from 15 Jan 2025 the Netherlands added certain metrology and inspection systems; in November 2025 the EU put Dutch national controls, including DUV immersion systems, on the EU control list; the US Affiliates Rule (1 Oct 2025) and China's rare-earth controls were each suspended until 10 Nov 2026 [FILING] — [ASML 2025 Annual Report](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/8ab959d4926657b/original/asml-2025-annual-report-strategic-report-section.pdf). EUV and "advanced DUV machines" are restricted for Chinese customers under Dutch measures dating from 6 September 2024, when the Dutch government took over licensing of the affected immersion models from Washington — [NL Times, 20 Aug 2026](https://nltimes.nl/2026/08/20/us-preparing-force-netherlands-ban-asml-selling-china); [The Register, ASML article index](https://www.theregister.com/Tag/ASML/).
- 11 Feb 2026: eight members of Congress asked for a countrywide ban on "chokepoint" chipmaking tools and subcomponents to China, matching controls by allies, and a crackdown on servicing — [The Register, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2026/02/11/lawmakers-want-tighter-chip-tool-curbs-on-china/4526584).
- April 2026: the MATCH Act (Multilateral Alignment of Technology Controls in Hardware, a Senate discussion draft) would require allies to match US curbs within 150 days, ban exports of immersion DUV lithography and cryogenic etch tools, restrict maintenance of installed tools and move to company-based criteria — [TrendForce, 9 Apr 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/04/09/news-u-s-proposes-bill-to-tighten-china-chip-tool-exports-targeting-duv-tools-to-slow-smic-and-peers-advanced-node-ambitions/).

- June 2026: Commerce Secretary Lutnick accused ASML of circumventing restrictions, saying one of its most sophisticated machines had reached China; ASML denied it (Bloomberg). - 20 Aug 2026: NU.nl reported the MATCH Act is on track to be added to the US defence budget, would ban the older DUV machines still allowed and bar ASML from maintaining machines already in China, with sanctions if the Netherlands refuses; Dutch chances of stopping it "appear slim" given bipartisan support [PRESS] — [NL Times, 20 Aug 2026](https://nltimes.nl/2026/08/20/us-preparing-force-netherlands-ban-asml-selling-china).
- Revenue at stake: China net sales €9,519.7m (2025), €10,195.1m (2024), €7,251.8m (2023) [FILING] — [20-F FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011378/R71.htm); about €27bn over three years including maintenance (NOS) — [NL Times, 24 Jun 2026](https://nltimes.nl/2026/06/24/dutch-government-irritated-us-plans-new-asml-export-restrictions). ### Inferences

## 7. Threats to the monopoly

### Takeaway
No rival EUV scanner, alternative light source or other lithography method has outside confirmation of production-capable performance; the two developments with real evidence are TSMC stretching low-NA EUV instead of buying High-NA (confirmed by TSMC) and Chinese plans to build immersion DUV tools at scale (a press report), which threaten High-NA timing and China DUV sales respectively, not the EUV monopoly itself.

### Cited Findings
- Chinese EUV: Reuters reported on 17 Dec 2025 that China had secretly completed a prototype EUV machine in Shenzhen with help from former ASML engineers, expected to produce working chips between 2028 and 2030 [PRESS] — [Wikipedia, ASML Holding, citing Reuters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASML_Holding); [Wikipedia, EUV lithography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_ultraviolet_lithography). ASML's CEO, 14 Oct 2026: "the fact this year are not different from the fact a year ago or even 5 years ago ... the progress is not very strong" [COMPANY CLAIM] — [Q3 2026 call](https://tickertrends.io/transcripts/ASML/Q3-earnings-transcript-2026).
- Chinese immersion DUV: The Information reported in July 2026 that China plans to start producing immersion DUV machines on a large scale for several Chinese chipmakers; its sources said the machines still lag in performance and reliability and need more testing [PRESS] — [NL Times, 28 Jul 2026](https://nltimes.nl/2026/07/28/asml-stocks-fall-reports-china-producing-advanced-chip-machines). NU.nl reported China has found that etching techniques combined with DUV can make chips that normally need EUV [PRESS] — [NL Times, 20 Aug 2026](https://nltimes.nl/2026/08/20/us-preparing-force-netherlands-ban-asml-selling-china).
- Alternative light source: xLight (Palo Alto, led by former Intel chief Pat Gelsinger) is developing a free-electron-laser EUV source; on 1–3 Dec 2025 the US Commerce Department's CHIPS R&D office signed a preliminary letter of intent for up to $150m in return for equity; the first system is being built at the Albany NanoTech complex; Gelsinger claims "tenfold improvements over today's technology"; no prototype date was given [COMPANY CLAIM; the letter of intent confirms government funding intent, not performance] — [The Register, 3 Dec 2025](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2025/12/03/pat-gelsingers-euv-lithography-gig-gets-150m-wink-from-us/2438804). ## 8. Carl Zeiss — Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology (SMT)

### Takeaway
Zeiss SMT is the sole source of ASML's optics, earned about €1.29bn of profit on about €5.0bn of revenue in the year to September 2025, and is the profit engine of a group whose other segments are shrinking; its expansion is partly financed by ASML, which holds 24.9% and had about €3.9bn of loans, prepayments and equity tied up in it at end-2025. It cannot be bought by outside investors.
