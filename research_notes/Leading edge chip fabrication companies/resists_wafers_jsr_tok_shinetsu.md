## Key question 1 — EUV photoresist market map

### Takeaway
Only one hard, dated, third-party share number was obtainable: TOK 29.3% of EUV resist shipments in 2025 (No.1; Fuji Chimera via TOK). Everything else on supplier shares is company wording or low-grade market-report copy; chemically amplified resist (CAR) remains the production standard, metal-oxide resist (MOR, JSR/Inpria) and Lam's dry resist have confirmed but narrow production footholds, and high-NA is where the resist choice is still open.

### Cited Findings
**Size and shares**
- Semiconductor photoresist market US$3,081m in 2025, +12.7% YoY (TOK calculation from SEMI data); US$2,689m in 2024, +14.1% [ESTIMATE] — [TOK Integrated Report 2026, p.10](https://www.tok.co.jp/application/files/7417/9299/7012/Integrated_report2026_all.pdf); [TOK prior-year report, p.9](https://www.tok.co.jp/application/files/6717/5465/4693/Integrated_report2024_all_a4.pdf)

Forecast volume CAGR 2025→2031: EUV 28.2%, ArF 7.4%, KrF 6.1%, g/i 3.8% [ESTIMATE; reliability: reputable Japanese research house, but quoted by the beneficiary, volume not value, and no other supplier's figure is shown] — same two TOK reports.
- JSR: "No.2 global share in photoresists" and "the only company able to offer both CAR and MOR for EUV" [COMPANY CLAIM, no measurer or period given] — [Mitsui Chemicals presentation, 9 Nov 2026, slide 15](https://jp.mitsuichemicals.com/content/dam/mitsuichemicals/sites/mci/documents/release/2026/event_261109.pdf)

**CAR vs MOR vs dry resist — who uses what**
- "For EUV, chipmakers tend to use traditional chemically amplified resists (CAR)"; MOR "have struggled to get a major foothold"; for high-NA, Intel, Samsung, SK hynix and TSMC "are evaluating several resist types, including CAR and metal oxide" — independent trade journalist Mark LaPedus, [Semiecosystem, 7 Jun 2026](https://marklapedus.substack.com/p/the-latest-news-in-lithography-2ef)

Customer is not named [COMPANY CLAIM] — [JSR release, 30 Aug 2024](https://www.jsr.co.jp/news/2024/20240830.html); [JSR FY3/2026 presentation, slide 11](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/4185/ir_material_for_fiscal_ym/204290/00.pdf)
- Lam Research: Aether dry resist "selected by a leading memory manufacturer as production tool of record for the most advanced DRAM processes" (customer unnamed) [COMPANY CLAIM] — [Lam release, 29 Jan 2025](https://newsroom.lamresearch.com/2025-01-29-Breakthrough-EUV-Dry-Photoresist-Technology-from-Lam-Research-Adopted-by-Leading-Memory-Manufacturer)

- ADEKA announced (Oct 2025) a ¥3.2bn plant for MOR metal compounds for high-NA EUV, start April 2028 [COMPANY CLAIM via aggregator] — [asset-alive](https://www.asset-alive.com/thema/?mode=show&tid=227)

**Switching costs / supply leverage precedent**
- Japan's 2019 measures covered "fluorinated polyimide, resist polymers and hydrogen fluoride" destined for Korea; Korea requested WTO consultations on 11 Sep 2019 and withdrew the complaint on 23 Mar 2023 [CONFIRMED by WTO record] — [WTO DS590](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds590_e.htm)
- JSR's then-CEO (27 Jun 2024): in lithography materials "we have top-class share and there are regulatory constraints; expanding share could become a problem" — i.e. resist-on-resist mergers face antitrust limits [COMPANY CLAIM] — [JSR/JICC press-conference Q&A](https://www.jsr.co.jp/news/assets/pdf/20240709_01.pdf)

### Inferences
- TOK moving from No.2 (28.0%) to No.1 (29.3%) means another supplier led EUV volumes in 2024 with more than 28%; with JSR calling itself No.2 in photoresists overall, EUV CAR is a three-horse race (TOK, JSR, Shin-Etsu) with each plausibly in the 20–30% band. This is inference, not a measured split.
- MOR revenue is still small: Inpria's US$60m compares with a ~US$3.1bn resist market and an EUV sub-market in the hundreds of millions of dollars.
- Because a chipmaker's resist is qualified layer by layer, the 2019 episode showed leverage without lasting share loss for the Japanese makers that built Korean plants (TOK, JSR) — inference from plant decisions above.

### Gaps
- No verifiable 2024/2025 EUV share for JSR, Shin-Etsu, Sumitomo Chemical, Fujifilm, DuPont/Qnity or Dongjin; no TECHCET or Fuji Keizai table obtained (search budget exhausted).
- Which chipmaker uses MOR or dry resist on which layer is not confirmed by any customer; qualification times and switching costs could not be sourced.
- Progress of Korean (Dongjin Semichem, SK Materials Performance, Samsung SDI, YC Chem) and Chinese resist makers: not researched in this session; do not rely on older recollection.

## Key question 2 — JSR (unlisted; JIC Capital funds → Mitsui Chemicals)

### Takeaway
Unusually for a delisted company, JSR is well documented: it still files securities reports (bond issuer, EDINET E39283) and Mitsui's and JIC's 9 Nov 2026 papers add three years of numbers. The semiconductor core is strong (electronic materials sales +24%, Digital Solutions core margin 25%), but FY3/2026 group profit was mostly disposal gains, the balance sheet is an LBO, and the "consolidator" thesis ended in a sale to a complementary chemical group rather than a resist merger.

TOK is a single-segment, net-cash resist and high-purity-chemical maker growing sales about 26% with operating margin above 21%, and it will hit its 2027 plan targets a year early.
