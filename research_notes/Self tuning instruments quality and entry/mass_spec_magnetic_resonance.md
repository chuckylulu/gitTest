## 2. What is already automated versus what is new (2025-2026)
### Takeaway
Agent-style control reached LC-MS in 2026, but via software layers rather than as built-in self-tuning: Waters AI Link (Model Context Protocol into waters_connect, launched 14-15 Oct 2026, GA planned November 2026) is the first major-vendor product found that lets third-party LLM agents create methods, run acquisitions and process results. All other majors' 2026 "AI" is data interpretation (Thermo, Bruker, SCIEX), help assistants (SCIEX), predictive maintenance / auto-diagnostics (Shimadzu, Agilent) or corporate AI partnerships (Thermo-OpenAI, Thermo-NVIDIA, Agilent-OpenAI-BCG).

### Cited Findings (new in 2025-2026)
- Waters AI Link: launched mid-October 2026; brings Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP) to waters_connect; customers can link waters_connect to Claude "and other large language models and AI agents"; agents can "create methods, run acquisitions, and process results without custom integration" and orchestrate connected devices such as sample preparation; early access at launch, general availability "in November" for waters_connect customers; when a molecule reaches QC, work moves into Empower (Waters' compliant system of record) and "the audit trail carries over"; no customers named; no pricing given. Waters also joined Anthropic's Model Hardware Standard (MHS) research program. Quote from Dave Leitham, VP Informatics: "AI can generate hypotheses faster than any lab can test them". Waters claims AI-driven lab-in-the-loop work is "already a new and fast-growing source of demand" for its instruments and consumables [company claim, not independently confirmed] — [BioPharma APAC, 15 Oct 2026](https://biopharmaapac.com/news/32/8561/waters-partners-with-anthropic-to-advance-ai-driven-autonomous-laboratory-workflows.html); [BioSpectrum Asia, 14 Oct 2026](https://biospectrumasia.com/news/106/28523/waters-launches-ai-link-for-advancing-ai-enabled-lc-ms-workflows-from-research-to-release.html). GA status on 12 Nov 2026: could not verify (only launch-period coverage found).
- Anthropic Model Hardware Standard (MHS) research preview, 27 Aug 2026: "a shared specification for AI agents to safely operate physical devices"; device reference files define capabilities and "safety limits"; accessible via MCP, CLI or APIs; partners named: AWS, Automata, Danaher, Doosan Robotics, MBF Bioscience, QIAGEN, Tecan, Universal Robots; research labs Genentech, UW, CMU, HHMI Janelia, QuEra, Tetsuwan. Devices named: liquid handlers, robotic arms, plate readers, qPCR, cameras, microscopes; mass spectrometers, NMR and chromatography NOT named at launch; only devices with a programmable interface supported; open-sourcing promised after preview, no date — [Anthropic, 27 Aug 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview). Danaher is "exploring MHS for smart instruments and autonomous laboratories"; Genentech case: Claude misread bubble-caused errors as software failures and made more foam until humans stepped in; Anthropic: "Claude's spatial and physical reasoning continues to require expert oversight"; no independent evaluation — [R&D World, 28 Aug 2026](https://www.rdworldonline.com/anthropic-wants-claude-to-run-life-sciences-rd-now-it-is-wiring-ai-agents-into-the-lab/) [secondary report].
