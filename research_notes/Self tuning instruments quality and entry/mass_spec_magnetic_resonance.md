## 2. What is already automated versus what is new (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Instrument self-tuning against a known standard is old and routine (MS autotune, automatic mass calibration, NMR auto-tune/match and shimming; JEOL claims an auto-tune NMR probe in 1985). What is new in 2025-2026 is (i) agent access through software layers (Waters AI Link with Model Context Protocol, Oct 2026; Anthropic's Model Hardware Standard research preview, Aug 2026, with Danaher and Waters involved), (ii) AI in data interpretation (Thermo, Bruker, SCIEX), and (iii) predictive maintenance and auto-diagnostics (Shimadzu, Agilent, Waters Alliance iS). No major maker was found shipping agent-run instrument set-up as a standard, validated feature, and no independent evaluation of agent-run set-up in this segment was found.

### Cited Findings - long-standing automation (baseline)
- JEOL: "introduced the world's first auto-tuning unit and auto-tune probe" in 1985, "enabling completely remote operation"; ECZ-series with Delta software plus auto sample changer and auto tuning automate routine measurements; Walkup NMR mode; AUTOMAS solid-state probe (July 2024) for serial samples [company claim] — [JEOL USA NMR pages](https://www.jeolusa.com/PRODUCTS/Nuclear-Magnetic-Resonance); [JEOL USA AUTOMAS release](https://jeolusa.com/NEWS-EVENTS/Press-Releases/high-throughput-solid-state-nmr-probe-royal-probe-automas-launched).
- Thermo Fisher Orbitrap Instrument API (IAPI) has allowed third-party real-time control on Tribrid instruments since 2016 (Instrument API 1.1.0.1, 30 Nov 2016); used by academic tools (e.g., Harvard Gygi lab Smart-TMT, which requires the user to "obtain an API license from Thermo") — [thermofisherlsms/iapi GitHub README](https://github.com/thermofisherlsms/iapi); [Gygi lab Smart-TMT page](https://gygi.hms.harvard.edu/smartTMTSoftware.html).

### Inferences
- "Self-tuning" in the narrow sense (tune/calibrate against a standard) is already a commodity feature in this segment; the 2026 frontier is one level up: method creation, acquisition scheduling and error recovery by agents, plus data interpretation. Waters is the only major found to expose method creation and acquisition to third-party agents as a product.
- The first agent products are deliberately confined to R&D software (waters_connect) with a hand-off to a validated CDS (Empower) for QC, which matches the regulatory picture in section 3.0.

### Gaps
- Agilent "SWARM autotune", Waters IntelliStart, Bruker TwinScape digital twin, TopShim and IconNMR, Shimadzu Analytical Intelligence details, Thermo Ardia and Exploris automatic calibration: no 2025-2026 primary documentation retrieved (could not verify current status).
- No peer-reviewed or preprint evaluation of LLM-agent method development or autonomous MS/NMR tuning was retrieved (search budget exhausted).
- Waters AI Link general availability on 12 Nov 2026: could not verify.

## 5. Where the evidence contradicts (or complicates) the thesis

### Takeaway
The thesis is partly supported (agents are starting to get control, starting with the maker that owns the software layer) but four pieces of evidence cut against it: tuning against a standard is already automated, regulated QC excludes LLM-driven control for now, makers are "opening" on their own terms, and 2026 growth comes from conventional drivers.

### Cited Findings
- The "expert set-up" step is already largely automated: NMR auto-tune since 1985 (JEOL claim), automatic MS calibration/autotune standard; 2026 AI features concentrate on data interpretation and maintenance, not tuning — [JEOL USA](https://www.jeolusa.com/PRODUCTS/Nuclear-Magnetic-Resonance); [LCGC, 5 Jun 2026](https://www.chromatographyonline.com/view/a-look-at-the-major-technical-themes-of-asms-2026).
- The judge is fast but not sufficient in regulated use: draft Annex 22 allows only static, deterministic models in GMP-critical applications and confines generative AI/LLMs to non-critical use with human oversight; USP <1058> revision adds lifecycle change control — [PharmaNow, 12 Aug 2026](https://www.pharmanow.live/pharma-it/annex-22-not-law-yet-what-to-invest); [Lachman, 25 Apr 2025](https://www.lachmanconsultants.com/2025/04/proposed-update-to-usp-on-analytical-instrument-qualification/). Waters' own design keeps agents in R&D software and hands off to Empower for QC — [BioPharma APAC](https://biopharmaapac.com/news/32/8561/waters-partners-with-anthropic-to-advance-ai-driven-autonomous-laboratory-workflows.html).
- Openness is vendor-gated: Thermo IAPI needs a licence agreement; Bruker plugins via its own software; Waters opens via its own waters_connect; MHS requires a programmable interface the maker provides — [IAPI README](https://github.com/thermofisherlsms/iapi); [Anthropic MHS](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview).
- Hardware-damage and physical-fault risk is real: Genentech MHS test where Claude misdiagnosed bubbles and made more foam — [R&D World](https://www.rdworldonline.com/anthropic-wants-claude-to-run-life-sciences-rd-now-it-is-wiring-ai-agents-into-the-lab/) (supports the thesis' obstacle).
- 2026 growth drivers are conventional: pharma LC/MS replacement (Waters legacy instruments +10% in Q3 2026; Agilent pharma +12%), China tenders (Shimadzu China orders +20-24%), semiconductors (Shimadzu TMP +56%, HORIBA, Bruker TOFWERK, Thermo EM) — filings and decks cited above.
- AI may raise instrument demand rather than replace labour: Waters and Thermo both frame AI as multiplying wet-lab experiments [company claims] — [BioPharma APAC](https://biopharmaapac.com/news/32/8561/waters-partners-with-anthropic-to-advance-ai-driven-autonomous-laboratory-workflows.html); [Thermo Q4 2025 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/01/29/thermo-fisher-tmo-q4-2025-earnings-transcript/).
- Service revenue is not visibly at risk yet: makers are raising service attach and prices (Waters 54% attach; Shimadzu recurring ratio 44% and service price revisions; Bruker aftermarket 38% of BSI) — sources above.

### Inferences
- Value capture so far points to the instrument maker that owns both the judge and the control software (Waters, Thermo), with agent platforms (Anthropic MHS/MCP, NVIDIA, OpenAI) as partners rather than displacers. A true shift of value to a neutral software layer would require standard, licence-free control interfaces (MHS open-sourced, LADS/SiLA adoption by majors); no evidence of that by 12 Nov 2026.
- In the three AI-spending scenarios: makers with semiconductor-linked lines (Shimadzu, HORIBA, JEOL, Bruker, Thermo EM) are more cyclical to AI capex; pure LC-MS makers (Waters, Agilent, SCIEX) are tied to pharma QC/replacement cycles and China, less to AI capex (inference from segment mix).

### Gaps
- No independent study measuring labour hours saved or service-revenue impact of agent-run set-up was found.
