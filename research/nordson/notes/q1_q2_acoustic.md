## 0. Does "SpinSAM" exist?

**Yes. SpinSAM is a real Nordson Test & Inspection product: the "SpinSAM™ Acoustic Micro Imaging (AMI) system", an automated 300 mm wafer acoustic inspection tool. A smaller "SpinSAM Lite" also exists [idx].**

- First public showing: Nordson's release dated 10 Jun 2024 says "the innovative SpinSAM™ AMI system will be unveiled in a video presentation for the first time at the show", i.e. **SEMICON West 2024, 9–11 Jul 2024, Moscone Center, San Francisco, booth 1233**. NORDSON CLAIM (mirror). Semiconductor Digest 10 Jun 2024 https://semiconductor-digest.com/?p=27025 ; Silicon Semiconductor 13 Jun 2024 https://siliconsemiconductor.net/article/119525/Nordson_Test_and_Inspection_to_showcase_Advanced_Semiconductor_Technologies [fetched]
- First listed booth showing: SEMICON Taiwan, 4–6 Sep 2024, TaiNEX, booth #L0516 ("the new SpinSAM Acoustic Micro Imaging (AMI) system"). NORDSON CLAIM (mirror), 3D InCites 4 Sep 2024 https://3dincites.com/?p=101056 [fetched]
- Spelling variants "Spin SAM", "SpinSam" and "SpenSam" all refer to the same product (podcast transcript). No other Nordson product named "Spin SAM" was found.
- In an SEC filing: the Nordson FY2025 Annual Report to Shareholders (ARS, filed 16 Jan 2026), CEO letter: "The new SpinSAM Acoustic Microimaging system sets a new benchmark in the industry with its high throughput and superior sensitivity, enabling precise defect detection in wafer-based assemblies. Using an innovative spin-scanning method, the system scans multiple wafers simultaneously, enhancing both productivity and accuracy in semiconductor inspection." NORDSON CLAIM. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/72331/000119312526014673/d69179dars.pdf [fetched via WebFetch binary + pdftotext].
