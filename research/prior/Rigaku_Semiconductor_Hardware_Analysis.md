| **1. Total Ionizing Dose (TID) Radiation Damage** | KeV X-rays generate electron-hole pairs in $\text{SiO}_2$/high-$\kappa$ gate dielectrics and shallow trench isolation, creating oxide trapped charge ($N_{ot}$) and interface traps ($D_{it}$) → MOSFET threshold voltage ($V_{th}$) shift, subthreshold leakage, and DRAM cell capacitor retention decay.<br>• **DRAM retention degradation threshold**: **Tens of rad to <1 krad(Si)**.<br>• **TLC NAND flash data corruption**: **~1 krad(Si)** (seconds of X-ray exposure).<br>• **FEOL $\text{SiO}_2$/Si interface damage**: **20–700 Gy (2–70 krad)**. | `XHENA AX-3000`, Comet `CA20`, Bruker `SKYSCAN`, `CD-3200T` / KLA `Axion` (post-transistor/BEOL & stacked HBM4 with logic base dies) | • **TSMC Packaging Metrology Director Zhihua Zou (SEMICON Taiwan, Sep 4, 2026)**: Publicly urged SK hynix, Samsung, and Micron to redesign 12-Hi/16-Hi HBM & HBM4 logic base dies with dedicated X-ray scan paths and radiation shielding because dense Cu/Si stacks force higher X-ray power that induces DRAM capacitor leakage, $V_{th}$ shifts, and permanent reliability loss during inline 3D laminography.<br>• **MDPI *Micromachines* (Nov 2026)** & **Nordson/IEEE TNS**: Confirm <1 krad(Si) DRAM vulnerability requires dose-management software (e.g., Comet `Dose Manager`) and sparse sampling. |

Periodic-Array Requirement & Brightness Wall in CD-SAXS** | Scattering intensity $I(\mathbf{Q}) = \left|\int_V \rho(\mathbf{r}) e^{-i\mathbf{Q}\cdot\mathbf{r}}\,d\mathbf{r}\right|^2$ concentrates signal into discrete Bragg diffraction rods $(h,k)$ only for spatially periodic lattices.<br>• Requires **40–100 μm** periodic test pads or dense memory cell arrays (statistically averages $10^5$–$10^6$ features per spot).<br>• **Mathematically blind to random isolated defects** (e.g., single missing via, stochastic EUV micro-bridge, isolated killer particle).<br>• **Logic Brightness Wall**: Sub-minute CD-SAXS on shallow logic structures (<100 μm spot, <1 mrad divergence) requires **>100× higher source brightness** than rotating anodes. | Rigaku `CD-3200T/3300T`, KLA `Axion T2000`, Bruker `Sirius-XCD` (Logic GAA/CFET & random-defect inspection) | • **IRDS 2024 Metrology Roadmap** & **MDPI *Crystals* 16, 265 (Apr 14, 2026)**: Explicitly state >100× brightness over rotating anodes is required for sub-minute logic CD-SAXS; commercial HVM CD-SAXS remains confined to strong-scattering high-aspect-ratio memory (3D NAND/DRAM).<br>• **Rigaku Journal 41(1) (2025, p. 2, 5)**: Confirms T-SAXS extracts ensemble-averaged periodic parameters over 10 min/site on 120 nm periodic square lattices.<br>• **Bruker SPIE (Apr 8, 2026)**: Demonstrates severe Poisson photon-count noise at short exposures requiring GAAE AI denoising (4 s vs 9 s/frame). |

* **Physics Ceiling on X-Ray TAM Expansion**: X-ray metrology holds structural dominance in non-destructive thin-film/superlattice (`MF-3400`, `XD-3300`) and HAR 3D NAND/DRAM CD-SAXS (`CD-3200T`), yet faces three hard physical constraints limiting substitution of optical/acoustic/e-beam tools: (i) rotating-anode flux walls (10 min/site full 3D scan in *Rigaku Journal* 41(1); >100× brightness deficit for sub-minute logic CD-SAXS per IRDS 2024), (ii) <1 krad(Si) DRAM/HBM ionizing-dose damage thresholds highlighted by TSMC in Sep 2026, and (iii) near-zero X-ray absorption contrast for sub-100 nm air gaps/delamination in <5 μm hybrid bonding relative to 200+ MHz SAM/PLA.
