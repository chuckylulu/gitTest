* **Acoustic Attenuation vs. Resolution Ceiling in HBM & Hybrid Bonding:** In conventional liquid-coupled SAM, ultrasonic attenuation scales quadratically with frequency ($\alpha \propto f^2$) in DI water and polymeric underfills/molding compounds, forcing an inverse depth–resolution tradeoff where 100–400 MHz HVM transducers penetrate full 775 µm bonded silicon wafers or multi-die stacks at multi-micron resolution, whereas GHz transducers (1–2 GHz) resolve ~0.8 µm voids only near surfaces; meanwhile, multi-die HBM stacks introduce overlapping interface echoes and acoustic shadowing below upper-layer delaminations. To bypass physical scan-time penalties at high resolution, PVA TePla, MCL, and ASE demonstrated in October 2026 that DCSCN deep-CNN super-resolution on low-resolution SAM C-scans raised ELK flip-chip "white-bump" recall from 5.2% to 100.0%, while TNO/imec (May 2026) demonstrated dry solid-contact 4 GHz HaWaCAM detecting 0.75 µm voids beneath 49 µm Si and projecting 2.5 µm void detection at 6 dB SNR beneath >800 µm Si.
* **Modality Orthogonality (SAM vs. PLA vs. 3D X-Ray Laminography):** Ultrasound reflects ~100% at air gaps as thin as ~200 Å, enabling full-wafer detection of closed planar delaminations and zero-volume bond-interface voids invisible to density-contrast X-ray CT/laminography (`CA20`, `XHENA AX-3000`), which instead provides <1 µm 3D volumetric imaging of dense Cu/Sn micro-bumps and TSVs across tall HBM stacks at minutes-per-ROI sampling speeds and under ionizing-dose limits. Dry Picosecond Laser Acoustics (`EchoScan`, demonstrated with Samsung on HBM hybrid bonding in April 2026) removes DI-water contamination risks and resolves 1 µm buried voids at shallow metal/dielectric interfaces, whereas automated SAM (`SAM Auto Wafer`, `SpinSAM`, `WAFER LINE`) handles full-wafer deep-interface screening via localized waterfall/degassed DI-water coupling and in-situ spin drying.
* **Qualification Bottleneck & Fab vs. OSAT Deployment:** Transitioning automated SAM from R&D/lab (`SAM 302 HD²`, `FineSAT 7`) into 24/7 Tier-1 HVM requires multi-stage evaluation (vendor demo -> customer R&D -> in-line pilot -> 24/7 regional field-service infrastructure), reflected in PVA TePla expanding from 1 to 5 qualified Tier-1 logic/memory customers between late 2023 and 9M 2026 while lifting automated SAM from 43% (2024) to 76% (9M 2026) of SAM revenue and booking existing Westhausen capacity through mid-2027. OSAT qualifications remain separate from PVA TePla's 5 Tier-1 fab count and face regional competition from Hitachi/Nordson across Asian OSATs and domestically subsidized SBT Ultrasonic (`688392.SH`) in Chinese memory/OSAT lines.
