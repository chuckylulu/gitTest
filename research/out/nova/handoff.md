## Extra cell — where Nova's tools compete with or complement Rigaku and Onto

| Where Nova competes with or complements Rigaku and Onto |
|---|
| **Compete with Onto.** Onto is a named main competitor [F] Q3-003. Nova meets Onto in integrated metrology (Nova's one key IM rival) [S] Q5-013, Q6-007 and in stand-alone optical CD (with KLA) [S] Q5-013. Onto reports its Atlas G6 OCD selected by a second GAA logic customer in Q1 2026 [C] XR-042. In packaging, Nova names Merck and Camtek, not Onto, as direct rivals [F] Q5-012, so the documented packaging overlap with Onto is limited [I] B-019. **Compete with Rigaku.** In HKMG and dipole film metrology, VeraFlex (XPS + low-energy XRF) [F] Q1-003 competes with Rigaku MF-3400 XRF/XRD and ONYX 3200 light-element XRF, which Rigaku markets against XPS [C] XR-028, XR-030. No non-vendor source names the socket holder [I] XR-044. **Complement.** X-ray diffraction (Bruker/Rigaku-type HR-XRD) is the reference for Nova's Raman and XPS on nanosheets [F] XR-035, XR-036. GlobalFoundries runs VeraFlex XPS as POR for SiGeB epi alongside pad-based XRF/XRD/SIMS methods [F] XR-033, XR-034. Nova's own X-ray + OCD hybrids use film signals, not X-ray CD [F] XR-011, TH-040. **What changed in 2026.** Onto now owns 27% of Rigaku [F] XR-019, XR-025. Their CD-SAXS + OCD Hybrid Engine targets memory production lines in 1Q 2027 [C] XR-026. Nova's main OCD rival is therefore tied to the X-ray vendor that competes with VeraFlex. The hybrid's first threat is to Nova's dimensional (OCD) sockets in HAR memory rather than to XPS [I] XR-046, XR-047. Nova has made no public statement naming the alliance [I] Q3-011. |
