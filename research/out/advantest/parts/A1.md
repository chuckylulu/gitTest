## Premise notes

- **Segments changed.** The run prompt lists "Test System / Mechatronics System / Services, Support and Others". From FY2025 Q1 Advantest reports only two segments: "Test System Business" and "Services and Others", and restated the comparatives [F] (A1-230). Test handlers and device interfaces (the former Mechatronics segment) now sit inside Test System Business as "Other Systems"/"Others", together with system-level test [F] (A1-339, A1-345). There is no separate Mechatronics figure for any period in this module.
- **The company defines FCF as operating CF + investing CF** [F] (A1-163), not as operating CF minus capex. From FY2026 Q1, investing CF includes large purchases of debt and equity instruments for strategic investments. Both definitions are shown below.
- **Filing dates.** The annual securities report (84th term) is dated June 26, 2026, and the semi-annual securities report (85th term) November 13, 2026, on their covers. Manifest and file names say 2026-06-25 and 2026-11-12. The `date` column uses the printed filing dates. No figures are affected.
- Run facts checked: FY2026 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2026) results were released 2026-10-28 in an IFRS, JPY release [F] (A1-094). Net income attributable to owners equals net income because there are no non-controlling interests [F] (A1-018, A1-101), so the slide "Net Income" line is used for single quarters.

## (e) Share of revenue tied to AI / HPC / HBM / advanced packaging, as the company states it

- **Not disclosed as a revenue share.** No document read gives a percentage of Advantest revenue for AI, HPC, HBM or advanced packaging (see gaps for the documents searched). In the FY2025 Q3 call (Jan 2026), asked for a GPU vs custom-ASIC split, the company said: "We do not disclose beyond 'Computing and Communications' categories," [C] (A1-302). It added that the category includes "HPC/AI and application processors. The majority are currently on [the HPC/AI side]" [C] (A1-303).
- **Closest company metric (application split of SoC tester sales only).** "Computing / Communications" was 95% of SoC Test Systems sales in FY2025 and 90% in FY2024 [F] (A1-295, A1-296). It is forecast at 90% for FY2026 [C] (A1-297). The company says these numbers are rounded to the nearest 5% [F] (A1-298). The same table appears in the Investors' Guide (Oct 2026) p.5 [F] (A1-300).
- **The company's definition** (Investors' Guide, Chapter 1 "Sales Composition - SoC Testers", p.5): "Computing" "includes leading-edge devices such as HPC (High Performance Computing) and AI, and 'Communications' include application processors (APs) for smartphones" [F] (A1-299). The category therefore mixes AI/HPC with smartphone application processors, and it covers SoC testers only. It excludes memory testers (including HBM), other systems and services.
- **Memory testers:** DRAM was 90% of FY2025 Memory Test Systems sales (rounded to 5%) [F] (A1-301). HBM is not split out of DRAM in any table read. The notes and tanshin only name HBM qualitatively, e.g. FY2025 Q2 tanshin "high-performance DRAM, such as HBM" [F] (A1-349).
- **Market, not revenue:** the company estimates "Computing and Communications" was about 80% of the CY2025 SoC tester TAM [C] (A1-304).
- **Implied by arithmetic, not stated by the company:** 95% x FY2025 SoC sales of 767,424 million gives about 729 bn of "Computing / Communications" SoC tester sales. That is about 64.6% of FY2025 net sales, with a range of 62.9-66.3% because of the rounding [I] (A1-469, Low: the category includes smartphone APs and the split is rounded). This is not an AI share.
- **Qualitative statement:** management says HPC/AI-related applications drove SoC Test Systems in FY2025 [C] (A1-305).
