## B2 Facts that most weaken each reading

Thesis tested: "AI accelerator and HBM test demand drives durable growth and margins at Advantest."

### Facts that most weaken the optimistic reading

1. **Concentration.** In FY2025 (Apr 2025-Mar 2026), NVIDIA International, Inc. alone took JPY 228,273m, 20.2% of net sales, and the top five customers took about 50% (about 48% in FY2024, Apr 2024-Mar 2025).
   - Tag: [F]. Ids: CL-069 (Annual Securities Report 84th, p.51), CL-070 (p.40-41). On the customer-group basis, the NVIDIA Group took JPY 243,735m (CL-068, p.134).
   - Why it weakens the reading: one customer group took more than a fifth of FY2025 sales [F] (CL-069, CL-068), and Teradyne says a merchant GPU customer is a "fast follower" expecting revenue in 2027 [C] (CL-046).
2. **HBM leg.** Memory test system sales were JPY 53.4 bn in FY2026 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2026), below the JPY 57.3 bn of FY2025 Q3 (Oct-Dec 2025), while Teradyne posted record memory test revenue of USD 301m in its Q3 2026 (quarter ended 27 Sep 2026).
   - Tag: [F]. Ids: CL-028, A1-262, CL-029, CL-055.
   - Why it weakens the reading: Advantest's memory line has not regained its FY2025 Q3 level while the main competitor's memory revenue sets records, so HBM test demand is not visibly flowing to Advantest; Teradyne attributes the record to HBM and DRAM test demand and a NAND resurgence [C] (TH-206). Management itself says its memory share may decline in CY2026 [C] (CL-052).
3. **Cycle.** In the last downturn, quarterly net sales fell 31.3% QoQ, from JPY 147.4 bn in FY2022 Q4 (Jan-Mar 2023) to 101.3 bn in FY2023 Q1 (Apr-Jun 2023), and operating margin fell from 26.2% to 14.1%, as printed by the company.
   - Tag: [F]. Ids: A6-326, A6-328, A6-170, A6-171.
   - One-off: management named about JPY 3 bn of inventory valuation losses in FY2022 Q4 (Jan-Mar 2023) [F] (A6-219). The margins with and without it are in the table above.
   - Why it weakens the reading: the previous record period ended in a single-quarter fall of that size, so record quarters alone do not show durability. Inventories now stand at JPY 352,426m (30 Sep 2026, FY2026 Q2 end) [F] (CL-088), which raises the write-down exposure if demand turns.

### Facts that most weaken the pessimistic reading

1. **The expected digestion did not come.** As of October 2025, Advantest expected a demand correction in 2H FY2025 (Oct 2025-Mar 2026) [C] (CL-131). Instead, single-quarter net sales rose in each of the next four quarters, from JPY 262.9 bn in FY2025 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2025) to 273.8 bn in FY2025 Q3 (Oct-Dec 2025), 328.1 bn in FY2025 Q4 (Jan-Mar 2026), 367.5 bn in FY2026 Q1 (Apr-Jun 2026) and 477.7 bn in FY2026 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2026).
   - Tag: [F] for the sales; the October 2025 expectation is [C]. Ids: CL-030, CL-024; printed FY2026 Q2 YoY +81.7% (A1-067).
   - Why it weakens the reading: management's own forecast gave the pull-in-then-digestion reading a dated test, and four quarters of reported sales moved the other way.
2. **SoC scale and margin.** SoC test system sales were JPY 353.3 bn in FY2026 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2026) against 173.7 bn in FY2025 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2025). The quarter's gross margin was 71.1% and its operating margin 55.9%.
   - Tag: [F]. Ids: CL-027, CL-029, CL-026, CL-025.
   - One-off: an inventory write-down reversal named for FY2026 Q1 [C] (A1-315) and 1H FY2026 [C] (CL-073); its amount and any Q2 part are NOT DISCLOSED (table above).
   - Why it weakens the reading: the AI-led SoC line went from JPY 173.7 bn to 353.3 bn in a year. The reported quarterly margin is far above the 26.2% of FY2022 Q4 (Jan-Mar 2023), the peak-sales quarter of the last cycle [F] (A6-170). A margin-reversion view therefore has to rest on future costs (DRAM [C] (CL-074)), not on reported results.
3. **Customers paying ahead.** The cash-flow statement shows an increase in advance receipts of JPY 44,215m in 1H FY2026 (Apr-Sep 2026), against 9,571m in 1H FY2025 (Apr-Sep 2025) and 9,748m in all of FY2025 (Apr 2025-Mar 2026).
   - Tag: [F]. Ids: A2-141, A2-139.
   - Why it weakens the reading: cash paid ahead of delivery is demand evidence that does not depend on company forecasts, and it runs against a reading in which buyers are about to pause. It does not show how far ahead orders run, because orders and backlog are not disclosed [F] (A2-001).
