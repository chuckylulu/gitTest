## A3 Guidance record - Advantest Corporation (TSE Prime 6857)

Fiscal year ends 31 March. Advantest names a fiscal year by its start year: FY2026 = Apr 2026-Mar 2027 [F] (A3-001). Figures are IFRS, JPY. Guidance and results are in JPY million in the tanshin and JPY billion in the slides. Classification rule used throughout: diff% = (actual - guidance) / guidance; **beat > +2%, in-line within +/-2%, miss < -2%**. A +/-5% band is shown as a sensitivity check. All arithmetic is in `calc/A3_*.py`.

**Method:**
- Each slot uses the guidance in force at the start of the quarter.
  - Q2 and Q4 slots: direct comparison with the printed Q2 or Q4 forecast.
  - Q3 slots: no Q3-only figure exists, so the 2H forecast is compared with the 2H actual (Q3+Q4).
  - Q1 slots: only the new full-year guidance exists, so it is compared with how the year ended.
- FY2026 Q1 is provisional because the year has not ended.
- Single-quarter actuals are the slide-printed values. 2H and FY2026 values are tanshin year-to-date differences, which agree with the slides to within 0.1 bn [I] (A3-296).
- Consequence of this method: two of the eight slots are half-year tests and two are full-year tests. The FY2025 Q1 slot repeats the annual comparison [I] (A3-292).
