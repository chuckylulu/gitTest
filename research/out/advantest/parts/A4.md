Fiscal year ends 31 March. FY2025 = Apr 2025-Mar 2026; FY2026 Q2 = Jul-Sep 2026. IFRS, JPY. Evidence rows: `parts/A4.csv` (A4-001 to A4-404). Calculations: `calc/A4_customers.py`, `calc/A4_region_shares.py`, `calc/A4_restatement.py`, `calc/A4_restatement_scan.py` (each with a `.out.txt`).

### Premise notes
- The run facts hold. The 84th Annual Securities Report covers the year ended 31 Mar 2026 and calls it FY2025 [F] (A4-049). The Q2 FY26 deck dated 2026-10-28 is the latest quarter [F] (A4-131 to A4-138).

The annual and semi-annual reports split by **customer location** [F] (A4-014). For FY2024 and FY2025 the two bases give the same numbers within rounding [I] (A4-383, High).

- Orders are not disclosed in the review window (see A2), so the orders restatement check cannot be run (gaps file).
- METI originals were not retrieved because the site refuses scripts. Japan's July 2023 list change appears here only as described by BIS [F] (A4-331).

### (c) Export rules that bind Advantest

**What the company says**
- The 84th report risk "Politics & Geopolitics" warns of "Restrictions or prohibitions ... on the provision of products, technologies, or maintenance services to specific countries or regions" [F] (A4-297).
- Its risk "Trade Compliance" says breaches could lead to "suspension or restriction of product shipments" [F] (A4-298). Advantest says it monitors export controls and sanctions and runs region-specific scenario analyses [F] (A4-299).
- The same text appears on the IR risk web page [F] (A4-300) and in the Integrated Annual Report 2026 [F] (A4-301). The 1H FY2026 report records no new risks [F] (A4-302).
- The 83rd report (FY2024) named "import and export restrictions and distorted licensing systems" tied to the US-China trade war [F] (A4-303).
- No specific rule is named in any of these documents: not the EAR, the Entity List, a foreign direct product rule or Japan's FEFTA/METI list [I] (A4-400, High).
- Management statements on impact:
  - Oct 2022: the US restrictions announced on 7 Oct 2022 would have "little direct impact" [C] (A4-305). Core products are developed in Japan and Europe, made in Japan or Asia, and "make little use of US technology" [C] (A4-306).
  - Apr 2024: its testers "do not represent equipment involved in manufacturing at advanced process nodes" [C] (A4-307). Its China exposure is "not that substantial" compared with peers [C] (A4-308).
  - Jan 2025: cited "drastic changes in US-China relations" [C] (A4-309).
  - Apr 2025: Chinese SoC testers are gaining at Chinese customers in low-end devices [C] (A4-310).
  - Apr 2026: in China it is "carefully navigating regulatory and market conditions" [C] (A4-311).
- The 84th report names Chinese competitors, among them Beijing Huafeng (Accotest) and Hangzhou Changchuan (CCTech) [F] (A4-304).
