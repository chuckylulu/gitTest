## A2 Leading signals - Advantest Corporation (TSE Prime 6857)

Fiscal year ends 31 March. Advantest names a fiscal year by its start year (FY2025 = Apr 2025-Mar 2026; FY2026 Q2 = Jul-Sep 2026). IFRS, JPY. Window: FY2025 Q1 (Apr-Jun 2025) to FY2026 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2026), with the 31 Mar 2025 balance sheet as the opening point. Evidence rows: `parts/A2.csv` (A2-001 to A2-177). Calculations: `calc/A2_*.py` with `.out.txt` outputs.

### Premise notes

1. **No orders, backlog or book-to-bill exist for the requested window.** Advantest said FY2021 Q4 (Jan-Mar 2022) was its last disclosure of orders and backlog [F] (A2-001). Its reason was that short-term order swings do not reflect mid/long-term growth [F] (A2-002). In Oct 2022 management said "We no longer disclose order data" [F] (A2-007). The FY2025 annual securities report also leaves out orders received [F] (A2-008). It uses the IFRS 15 practical expedient, so no remaining-performance-obligation amount is given [F] (A2-009). A book-to-bill ratio for FY2025-FY2026 therefore cannot be stated or computed. This section uses customer advances, inventories, receivables and management commentary instead.
2. **The segment names in the prompt are out of date.** From FY2025 there are two reportable segments: Test System Business and Services and Others [F] (A2-157). "Mechatronics" is no longer a reportable segment. Test handlers and device interfaces are discussed under "Other Systems" within the Test System business [F] (A2-173).
3. **Purchase commitments:** the annual securities report discloses only commitments for the acquisition of fixed assets (capex) [F] (A2-152, A2-153, A2-154). It gives no amount for material or component purchase obligations (see gaps).
4. **Quarterly granularity:** the inventory split and the contract-liability (advance receipts) balance appear only at fiscal year-ends. They are not in the quarterly financial results or the semi-annual reports (see gaps).
5. The prompt's period labels hold. The latest quarter is FY2026 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2026), released 28 Oct 2026 [F] (A2-038, document date).

### Key readings (inferences)

- **Inventory is rising faster than throughput.** At 30 Sep 2026 inventories were up 67.8% YoY [I] (A2-116; High). 1H cost of sales was up 30.5% [I] (A2-174; High). Inventory days rose from 193.0 to 233.2 between Sep 2025 and Sep 2026 [I] (A2-111, A2-115; Medium). Management describes the build as deliberate strategic stocking: DDR4 DRAM, JPY 70 bn of convertible-bond proceeds for capacity and inventory, and a "just-in-case" model [C] (A2-055, A2-056, A2-057). The Sep 2026 raw / WIP / finished split is not disclosed, so I cannot say whether the build is in components or in finished systems (gaps file).
- **Customer prepayments jumped in FY2026 Q1.** The advance-receipts cash inflow was JPY 36,984 m in Apr-Jun 2026 [F] (A2-140). That equals 92.1% of the 31 Mar 2026 contract-liability balance [I] (A2-175; Medium). The 1H FY2026 inflow was 4.54x the whole FY2025 inflow [I] (A2-176; High). The "Other current liabilities" line moved with these flows in every quarter, which is consistent with customers prepaying for capacity [I] (A2-150; Medium). The 30 Sep 2026 balance itself is not disclosed (gaps file).
- **Receivables collection looks normal, and the 31 Mar 2026 peak is consistent with quarter-end shipment timing.** DSO was 44.3-45.7 days in the last two quarters, against 63.6 days at 31 Mar 2026 [I] (A2-104, A2-105, A2-106; Medium).
- **Management describes demand as exceeding supply into CY2027** [C] (A2-025). It also says customers now share 18-month forecasts [C] (A2-023). None of this can be checked against order data, because orders are not disclosed [F] (A2-001, A2-008).
