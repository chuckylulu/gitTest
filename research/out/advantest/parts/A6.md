### Definition and screen
- **Downturn (definition used here):** T4Q net sales fall at least 5% below their running peak. The episode lasts until T4Q sales exceed that peak again. Inside each episode, the **peak quarter** is the highest single quarter among the four that make up the T4Q peak. The **trough quarter** is the lowest single quarter after it, before T4Q recovers. T4Q filters out Advantest's lumpy quarterly shipments.
- From FY2015 Q4 to FY2026 Q2 there are only three T4Q drawdowns of 1% or more: -6.55% (2015-16), -5.17% (2018-19) and -13.15% (2022-23) [I] (A6-272, A6-350). Any threshold up to 5.17% picks all three, so **the last two downturns are 2022-23 and 2018-19** [I] (A6-273). A threshold between 5.17% and 6.55% would make the last two 2022-23 and 2015-16. Above 6.55%, only 2022-23 qualifies [I] (A6-350). The selection of 2018-19 therefore rests on a 0.17 pp margin over a threshold that I chose. The T4Q screen cannot see quarters before FY2015 Q4, because the IFRS quarterly figures start in FY2015 Q1.

### Inventory valuation losses: what the ex-one-off figures depend on
Inventory valuation losses recognised as an expense were 6,324 (FY2018), 3,468 (FY2019), 3,307 (FY2020), 2,728 (FY2021), 6,344 (FY2022) and 7,347 (FY2023) [F] (A6-317 to A6-322). They recur every year. The amounts management named for a single quarter are about 2 bn in FY2018 Q4 (32% of that year's total) and about 3 bn in FY2022 Q4 (47%) [I] Medium (A6-353). The ex-one-off margins therefore move with what management chose to name. For 2018-19 the named trough-quarter loss narrows the margin drop from -8.4 pp to -5.2 pp. For 2022-23 the named peak-quarter loss widens it from -12.1 pp to -14.1 pp.

### Management's explanation of each downturn
- **2015-16:** "semiconductor manufacturers sought to adjust inventory and limit capital spending starting in the latter half of 2015" [C] (A6-238). Also: "net sales in the third quarter stagnated due to a downturn in orders received in the second quarter" [C] (A6-239). In 1H FY2016, "Profits were also negatively affected by yen appreciation" [C] (A6-240).
- **2018-19:** "major semiconductor manufacturers cutting back on their capital investment plans and moving towards substantial inventory adjustments since the second half of 2018" [C] (A6-241). On 25 April 2019, management said "the tester market is also entering a slowdown phase, but is expected to improve in the second half of FY19" [C] (A6-242), and "Memory tester demand is being impacted by memory" inventory adjustments [C] (A6-243). In FY2019 Q1, "the memory semiconductor market continued to deteriorate due to prolonged weak demand for data center servers and smartphones" [C] (A6-244), and SG&A rose with "reinforcement of customer" support [C] (A6-245).
- **2022-23:** In 9M FY2022, "the decline of the semiconductor market overall has been gradually accelerating" [C] (A6-246). On 26 April 2023, management said "semiconductor manufacturers are expected to continue their inventory and production adjustments for the time being" [C] (A6-247). At the trough (FY2023 Q1), it said "investment by customers which continued over the past three years has resulted in excess capacity in some of our customers' supply chains" [C] (A6-248). The full-year FY2023 release said "the weakening of the semiconductor market conditions led to excess capacity among many customers' supply chains" [C] (A6-250).
- **2007-09 (context):** customers "implemented production cuts and froze or postponed their capital expenditure programs starting from the second fiscal half" [C] (A6-251).

### Limits

- The downturn selection depends on my threshold: 2018-19 clears the 5% T4Q rule by 0.17 pp (A6-350).

- The inventory valuation losses named for FY2018 Q4 (~2 bn) and FY2022 Q4 (~3 bn) are approximate amounts stated orally in Q&A summaries. Such losses recur every year (A6-317 to A6-322), so the ex-one-off margins are shown both with and without them (A6-292 vs A6-351).
