### Definition and screen
- **Downturn (definition used here):** T4Q net sales fall at least 5% below their running peak. The episode lasts until T4Q sales exceed that peak again. Inside each episode, the **peak quarter** is the highest single quarter among the four that make up the T4Q peak. The **trough quarter** is the lowest single quarter after it, before T4Q recovers. T4Q filters out Advantest's lumpy quarterly shipments.
- From FY2015 Q4 to FY2026 Q2 there are only three T4Q drawdowns of 1% or more: -6.6% (2015-16), -5.2% (2018-19) and -13.2% (2022-23) [I] (A6-272). Any threshold from 1% to 5.2% picks the same three, so **the last two downturns are 2022-23 and 2018-19** [I] (A6-273). The T4Q screen cannot see quarters before FY2015 Q4, because the IFRS quarterly figures start in FY2015 Q1.

### Earlier episodes (context)
- **2015-16, IFRS quarters.** Net sales fell from 46,181 in FY2015 Q2 (Jul-Sep 2015) to 29,885 in FY2015 Q3 (Oct-Dec 2015), -35.3%. Sales regained the peak in FY2016 Q4, 6 quarters later [I] (A6-298). Operating income went from 4,202 to -219, and the margin from 9.1% to -0.7% (-9.8 pp). No one-off inside operating income was named, so the figures with and without one-offs are the same [I] (A6-299). A second low came in FY2016 Q3 (Oct-Dec 2016): sales 31,376 (-32.1% from the FY2015 Q2 peak) and a margin of 3.1% [I] (A6-300). T4Q fell 6.6%, a lower bound because earlier T4Q values cannot be computed [I] (A6-301).
- **2011-13, U.S. GAAP annual.** Net sales fell from 141,048 to 111,878 (-20.7%). The operating margin went from 0.6% to -32.5%, or -16.9% without the FY2013 Q3 inventory valuation losses of 4.0 bn and impairment of 13.5 bn [I] (A6-305); the two items are in [F] (A6-237).
- **2007-09, U.S. GAAP annual.** Net sales fell from 182,767 (FY2007) to 53,225 (FY2009), -70.9%. The operating margin went from 12.4% to -21.9% [I] (A6-306). FY2007 sales were already down 22.2% on FY2006 [F] (A6-173), so the decline from the true peak is larger, but FY2006 was not retrieved. In FY2008 the margin was -64.5% reported, or -28.5% without the named items: inventory valuation losses of 8.7 bn, restructuring of 5.1 bn and PP&E impairment of 13.8 bn [I] (A6-307, Low confidence: the release does not say which P&L line carries the restructuring cost); the items are in [F] (A6-234 to A6-236).

### Management's explanation of each downturn
- **2015-16:** "semiconductor manufacturers sought to adjust inventory and limit capital spending starting in the latter half of 2015" [C] (A6-238). Also: "net sales in the third quarter stagnated due to a downturn in orders received in the second quarter" [C] (A6-239). In 1H FY2016, "Profits were also negatively affected by yen appreciation" [C] (A6-240).
- **2018-19:** "major semiconductor manufacturers cutting back on their capital investment plans and moving towards substantial inventory adjustments since the second half of 2018" [C] (A6-241). In April 2019, management said "the tester market is also entering a slowdown phase, but is expected to improve in the second half of FY19" [C] (A6-242), and "Memory tester demand is being impacted by memory" inventory adjustments [C] (A6-243). In FY2019 Q1, "the memory semiconductor market continued to deteriorate due to prolonged weak demand for data center servers and smartphones" [C] (A6-244), and SG&A rose with "reinforcement of customer" support [C] (A6-245).
- **2022-23:** In 9M FY2022, "the decline of the semiconductor market overall has been gradually accelerating" [C] (A6-246). In April 2023, management said "semiconductor manufacturers are expected to continue their inventory and production adjustments for the time being" [C] (A6-247). At the trough (FY2023 Q1), it said "investment by customers which continued over the past three years has resulted in excess capacity in some of our customers' supply chains" [C] (A6-248). The full-year FY2023 release said "the weakening of the semiconductor market conditions led to excess capacity among many customers' supply chains" [C] (A6-250).
- **2007-09 (context):** customers "implemented production cuts and froze or postponed their capital expenditure programs starting from the second fiscal half" [C] (A6-251).
