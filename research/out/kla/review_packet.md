## 5 inferences the conclusions lean on most

1. **The AP figures use two definitions** [I] Q2-I01, Q2-I03.
   - Inputs: CY25 total AP systems revenue ~$950M (Q2 FY26 letter p4); CY25 AP Semi PC revenue ~$635M (Q3 FY26 letter p5); CY26 target ~$1.1B (SL-Q1FY27 p2).
   - 1,100 / 635 = +73.2%, which matches KLA's ">70%". 1,100 / 950 = +15.8%, which does not.
   - So "AP > $1.1B" refers to process control only.
2. **China share series** [I] A4-016..031.

   - Results: 44.6% peak (Apr-Jun 2024); 24.3% low (Jan-Mar 2026); 34.5% in Q1 FY27; 28.7% for Jan-Sep 2026.
