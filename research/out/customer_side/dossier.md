## Bottom line

- **On the six named steps, the methods named most often are electron-beam imaging and electrical test, mostly on test vehicles or in failure analysis.** Of the 46 Section 1 documents on the six named steps whose evidence is not press-only (34 concern HBM, TSV/micro-bump, bonding or wafer shape; 12 only memory film and CD metrology), e-beam (SEM/TEM/FIB) is named in 15, electrical test is named in 14, acoustic microscopy is named in 7, optical is named in 7, AFM is named in 6, infrared is named in 4, X-ray CT / laminography is named in 4, X-ray scatterometry / XRF / XRD is named in 2, laser-acoustic is named in 0 (count by `calc/method_counts.py`; one document can name several methods) [I] MAIN-013. Examples:

  - e-beam:
    - SK hynix FIB cross-section of TSV film [F] PAPM-221;
    - Samsung TEM of pre-bond SiCN [F] PAPM-058;
    - Intel FIB-SEM on chip-to-wafer hybrid-bond Kelvin structures [F] PAPL-036;
    - ASE: SEM/EDS of a defective micro-bump in failure analysis [F] PAPL-108; SEM/TEM of a Cu/Ag hybrid-bonded interface in an NTU-led paper with ASE co-authors [F] PAPL-115;
  - scanning acoustic microscopy (C-SAM / SAT / cSAM):
    - SK hynix HBM4 test vehicle [F] PAPM-136;
    - Micron wafer-to-wafer bonding [F] PAPM-016;
    - Intel chip-to-wafer hybrid bonding [F] PAPL-035;

## 5. Method by company: production, pilot or none ([I] rows only)

Each cell is an [I] row (SYN-001 to SYN-070 in `evidence.csv`), produced by `calc/method_matrix.py` from the evidence rows it cites.

How the cells are assigned:
- **PROD** = the company's own words say production, mass production, HVM, "currently in use" or in-line monitoring in a fab.
- **PILOT** = the authors report checks on production wafers or lines, or demonstration in products, without a deployment statement.
- **lab** = test vehicle, feasibility, development, failure analysis, or "examined".
- **-** = no statement found in the documents read.
- Press-only ([S]) and vendor-only items never set a cell; they are named in the basis notes.
- The label in brackets says what a PROD or PILOT cell rests on:
  - HBM = HBM stack, TSV or micro-bump line;
  - HB = hybrid or wafer bonding outside HBM;
  - FE = memory front end; fab-wide = front end, assembly and test;
  - 3D micro-bump = micro-bump sort of 3D-stacked logic;
  - offer = a service offering.
  - "[C]" means the status is the company's own untestable claim.
- Scope: HBM stacks, TSV/micro-bump, hybrid or wafer bonding, and memory front-end (FE) metrology set a cell. OSAT work outside HBM or bonding (wire bonding, substrate, flip-chip FA) and logic front end do not.
- For the three-way split asked for (production / pilot / none), **lab** and **-** both count as none: there is no production or pilot statement.
