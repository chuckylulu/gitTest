# Comet Holding AG (SIX: COTN) - Module DV: results and economics by division

Fiscal year = calendar year (ends 31 Dec). CHF. Divisions: PCT (Plasma Control Technologies), IXS (X-Ray Systems / Comet Yxlon),
IXM (Industrial X-Ray Modules), plus "Corporate" (unallocated Comet Holding AG costs) and elimination of intersegment sales.
Evidence ids refer to `research/out/comet/parts/DV.csv`. Arithmetic: `research/out/comet/calc/DV_halfyears.py` (half-years,
shares, one-offs, concentration ratio) and `research/out/comet/calc/DV_targets.py` (targets vs actual). The CSV is generated by
`research/out/comet/calc/DV_build_csv.py`, which copies table rows verbatim from the saved text files.

Definitions used throughout: EBITDA margin = segment EBITDA / total division net sales (incl. intersegment), as Comet computes it
in the segment note; "EBIT" = segment operating income. H2 = FY minus H1 [I]. Segment notes report sales, segment operating income
and EBITDA by division; they do **not** report orders or book-to-bill by division.

## Premise corrections
- "ebeam if still a segment in 2022": it was not; ebeam was divested in 2020 and Comet has reported three divisions since 2021.
- "Book-to-bill by division": Comet reports book-to-bill only for the group; divisional orders are not quantified (only qualitative, e.g. PCT above group average in H1 2026).
- "2024/2025 reallocation of shared costs": no segment re-definition or cost reallocation was found; the only change to prior-period segment figures is the FY2024/H1 2025 error restatement (inventory write-down and accrual errors), which affected PCT only and raised H1 2025 PCT EBITDA by CHF 2.27m.
- "Strategic options / sale / partnership for IXS": no such statement exists in the saved documents; management language is repositioning, restructuring and "downsizing low-margin businesses", with AR2025 stressing "focused actions, rather than large structural exits".
- Timing of the IXS restructuring is stated inconsistently by Comet (Q3 2025 in the FY2025 release vs Q4 2025 in AR2025 / "last quarter of 2025" in HY26).
- CMD 2025 took place on 13 Nov 2025 (saved deck file is dated 2026-01-09, its download date); CMD 2023 took place on 9 Nov 2023.
- IXS naming: segment notes use "X-Ray Systems (IXS)"; AR2025 narrative uses "Industrial X-Ray Systems"; brand Comet Yxlon.
