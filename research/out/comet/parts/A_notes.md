  of the ebeam lamp business to Tetra Pak effective 30 Nov 2020; the division was dissolved 1 Jan 2021.

  bond terms come from the Half-Year Report 2026, note 06.1.

  on a jury-instruction issue.

  derived or missing as marked.

  year; on a consistent basis H1 and H2 2025 margins are 10.1% and 10.2%.
