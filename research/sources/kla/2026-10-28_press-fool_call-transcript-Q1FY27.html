KLA (KLAC) Q1 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

AI-driven demand and record margins signal strong growth for semiconductor equipment.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Oct 29, 2026 at 1:24AM EST

+Fool.comon

Image source: The Motley Fool.

Wed, Oct. 28, 2026

  • Vice President of Investor Relations and Market Analytics - Kevin Kessel
  • Chief Executive Officer - Rick Wallace

TAKEAWAYS

  • Revenue -- $4.14 billion in the September quarter, representing 29% growth and exceeding the midpoint of the company's guidance range.
  • Non-GAAP Diluted EPS -- $1.22, driven by higher revenue and record operating margins.
  • Operating Margin -- 46.1%, reflecting 240 basis points of sequential expansion due to disciplined execution and top-line growth.
  • Gross Margin -- 62.7%, slightly above the midpoint of the guidance range despite headwinds from high memory costs.
  • Free Cash Flow -- $1.34 billion for the quarter, supported by $1.53 billion in cash flow from operations.
  • December Quarter Revenue Guidance -- $4.7 billion plus or minus $200 million, indicating a 25% revenue acceleration in the second half of calendar 2026 over the first half.
  • December Quarter EPS Guidance -- $1.44 plus or minus $0.11 on a non-GAAP basis.
  • 2026 Wafer Equipment Market Outlook -- Approximately the mid-$150 billion range, an upward revision from previous expectations as visibility improves.
  • 2027 Wafer Equipment Market Outlook -- Forecasted to exceed $200 billion, supported by new fab investments and leading-edge technology transitions.
  • Advanced Packaging Revenue -- On track to reach $1.1 billion or more for calendar 2026, representing over 70% growth.
  • Services Revenue -- $850 million, up 14% due to a growing installed base and high system utilization rates.
  • Semiconductor Process Control Mix -- Forecasted for the December quarter at 60% foundry and logic and 40% memory customers.
  • Memory Mix -- 84% DRAM and 16% NAND for semiconductor process control systems, reflecting increased intensity in high-performance computing.
  • Cash and Marketable Securities -- $4.7 billion as of Sept. 30, 2026, providing a flexible maturity profile.
  • Total Debt -- $5.95 billion, supported by investment-grade ratings from three major agencies.
  • Operating Expenses -- $688 million in the September quarter, with a forecast of $710 million for the December quarter to support infrastructure investments.
  • Lead Times -- Averaging approximately 12 months, with certain products like Gen4 and Gen5 lines extending further due to optical component constraints.
  • China Revenue Concentration -- Expected to stabilize between 27% to 29% of total revenue in the December quarter, down from a higher percentage in the September quarter.
  • Capital Returns -- $1.24 billion in the quarter, including $936 million in share repurchases and $305 million in dividends.
  • Research and Development Spending -- $395 million, focused on next-generation product development and scaling company infrastructure.
  • India Campus Investment -- The company made a significant land purchase in India to establish a new corporate campus for scaling global operations.
  • Share Count -- 1.31 billion diluted weighted average shares, reflecting retroactive adjustments for a 10-for-1 stock split effective June 11, 2026.

RISKS

  • Higgins stated, "This is being offset by persistently high memory costs for our system image processing requirements," noting that these costs currently impact gross margin leverage.
  • Higgins warned that although supply is increasing, the company is still "behind in supply" relative to strong customer delivery urgency.
  • Wallace noted that customer facility delays, including power or construction issues, can create bottlenecks for equipment utilization, noting that one customer could not take equipment because their clean room was delayed.

SUMMARY

KLA Corporation (KLAC -10.33%) reported record September quarter revenue and its first ever operating margin above 46%, signaling an acceleration in growth for the second half of calendar 2026. Management increased its outlook for the 2026 wafer equipment market and provided a preliminary 2027 forecast exceeding $200 billion. The company stated that visibility into calendar 2027 is unprecedented, supported by a growing backlog and numerous greenfield facility projects across foundry, logic, and DRAM segments. Demand is being driven by AI infrastructure investment and rising process control intensity across advanced technology nodes.

  • Management reported that advanced packaging is growing at nearly twice the rate of the broader advanced packaging market, driven by the move toward hybrid bonding and logic devices.
  • Higgins noted that the company is investing in every factory and plans to increase annual capital expenditures to between $600 million and $800 million to support multiyear growth.
  • Wallace indicated that DRAM intensity is approaching levels seen in leading-edge logic because high-performance computing requires higher yield and more stringent performance standards.
  • The company reported that 2-nanometer and 1.4-nanometer node investments are expected to be primary growth drivers for logic in the coming quarters.
  • Management stated that the supply of high-end optical components remains the primary bottleneck for system delivery, with lead times averaging approximately 12 months.
  • KLA is prioritizing internal systems engineering to utilize less memory in its tools to mitigate the impact of persistently high DRAM pricing.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • WFE (Wafer Fab Equipment): The machinery and tools used in the manufacturing of semiconductor wafers.
  • Process Control: The systems used to inspect and measure semiconductor wafers for defects and yield enhancement.
  • HBM (High Bandwidth Memory): A high-performance RAM interface used in 3D-stacked DRAM for high-performance computing.
  • DRAM (Dynamic Random Access Memory): A type of semiconductor memory used for data storage in computers and servers.
  • Foundry: A factory where semiconductor wafers are manufactured for other companies.
  • Logic: Semiconductor devices that process data to complete a task, such as CPUs and GPUs.
  • Advanced Packaging: Technologies that aggregate multiple semiconductor components into a single package to improve performance.
  • Gen4/Gen5: Successive generations of KLA's high-end optical inspection and metrology systems.
