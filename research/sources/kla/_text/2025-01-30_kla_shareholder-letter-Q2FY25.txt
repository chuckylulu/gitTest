Dear KLA Shareholders,

This letter reviews KLA’s overall performance for calendar 2024, the business and financial highlights
for the December quarter, and the March 2025 quarter outlook.

Calendar 2024 Recap
KLA delivered another year of relative growth outperformance, strong profitability, and healthy returns
to shareholders. In calendar 2024, revenue grew 12% to a record $10.85 billion, with our process
control systems business outpacing Wafer Fab Equipment (WFE) growth estimated to be in the mid-
single digits. KLA benefited from a return to growth at the leading edge, driven by increased
investments from our customers in AI and high-performance computing (HPC), continued momentum
in advanced packaging, and strong and sustainable Services performance.

KLA process control system revenue grew by over 12%, indicating that KLA increased its WFE market
share, as strength from both the leading edge in foundry and DRAM and legacy node Foundry and Logic
customers drove outperformance. In addition, KLA’s Services business grew 15% to $2.5 billion for the
year. KLA’s 2024 performance continues to reflect the increasing relevancy of process control and the
power of KLA’s product portfolio.

In the calendar year, KLA maintained industry-leading non-GAAP gross and operating margins at 61.4%
and 40.6% respectively. The company also grew free cash flow to $3.4 billion and returned $2.9 billion
in a combination of dividends and share buybacks. The strategic focus at the start of the year to
support customers, execute on product roadmaps, and prepare for growth at the leading edge
delivered the consistent outperformance that KLA stakeholders have come to expect.

December 2024 Quarter Results
To close the calendar year, KLA’s December quarter results were above the midpoint of non-GAAP
guidance ranges despite navigating through the business impact of new U.S. government export
controls released late in the quarter. December quarter revenue topped $3 billion for the first time,
finishing at the upper end of the guidance range at $3.08 billion, highlighted by strong customer
demand and solid company execution.

Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $8.20, finishing at the upper end of the guidance range for the quarter.
GAAP diluted EPS was $6.16 and was impacted by a $239.1 million impairment charge for goodwill and
purchased intangible assets in our PCB business or $1.76 per diluted share.

As expected, we are encouraged by the strong demand we are experiencing in leading-edge logic and with
specific memory customers supporting high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and advanced packaging. KLA’s
differentiated portfolio of solutions aligns exceptionally well in helping enable our customers to navigate
increasing complexity, growing design starts and larger semiconductor devices in an environment of rising
semiconductor demand.

We are confident that these factors position KLA for another year of growth and strong relative
performance compared with the overall WFE market in 2025.

Top 5 Highlights – December 2024 Quarter
KLA continued to deliver strong sequential and year-over-year revenue growth, demonstrating an
improving industry environment as the company is well-positioned to benefit from growth at the
leading edge. In Foundry/Logic, the resumption of scaling, incorporation of new technologies, larger die
sizes, a robust design environment, and rising capital intensity continue to be long-term secular
tailwinds for process control. In DRAM, investments supporting AI and HBM that require high
performance, reliability, have less redundancy, and more complex logic circuitry are drivers for our
business. This investment, in addition to an improving supply/demand environment, are positioning
the DRAM market for growth which should lead to an increase in WFE investment in calendar 2025.

Second, AI continues to be a crucial catalyst for KLA. We are aware of the recent revelations of
DeepSeek and the implications that it portends a diminished demand for advanced semiconductors in
support of the AI infrastructure build-out. As a company that has been developing AI models for use in
our own inspection systems for many years, our own experience supports the theory that increased
compute efficiency enables more adoption of AI in our platforms. The demand is clearly elastic. As it
pertains to the demand environment for advanced semiconductors, we see no reason to believe that
increased compute efficiency in AI will have an impact on the advanced demand environment in the
foreseeable future. AI is both an important driver and enabler of KLA’s business. The semiconductor
industry is experiencing higher value wafer volume, more complex designs, accelerating product cycles,
larger die sizes, and growing advanced packaging demand that can all be traced back to AI. These
trends support the increasing value of process control in assisting our customers in managing a
dynamic production environment as investments and complexity increase, which benefits KLA. From an
enablement perspective, we pioneered using and incorporating AI into our products beginning over a
decade ago and have re-designed our compute architecture to leverage GPUs for more robust
alogorithmic capability and cost leverage across most of our product portfolio. KLA’s future product
and service enhancements will leverage AI to improve the performance and customer cost of
ownership of our leading-edge systems in a way that directly benefits our customers’ time to results,
yield entitlement, and ROI on fab investments. KLA will also benefit as cost of compute scales more

Third, this quarter saw continued strong momentum for our advanced packaging portfolio, again driven
by AI and HPC investments. The growing demand for more powerful systems of chips is driving more

Fourth, the KLA Services business grew to $667 million in the December quarter, up 4% sequentially

some of our Services business due to access to certain customer fabs providing a growth headwind in
calendar 2025. However, over the long-term, given the incremental value opportunities in our Services
offerings and the industry investing in new production facilities to align with expected future demand,
we remain well-positioned to grow at the upper end of the annual Services revenue growth target of
12% to 14%.

Finally, the December quarter was another solid quarter from a cash flow and capital return
perspective. Quarterly free cash flow was $757 million. In calendar 2024, free cash flow was $3.4
billion, with a free cash flow margin (free cash flow as a percentage of revenue) of 31% over the same
period. This free cash flow margin ranks amongst the top companies in the S&P 500. Total capital
return in the December quarter was $877 million, comprised of $650 million in share repurchases and
$227 million in dividends. Total capital return over the past twelve months was $2.9 billion. In early
September, KLA announced its 15th consecutive annual dividend increase, up 17% to $1.70 per share
per quarter. KLA views explicit, consistent and healthy capital returns as fundamental to delivering
value for shareholders.

KLA’s December quarter results delivered strong sequential and year-over-year growth. The results also

global team, our commitment to assertive capital allocation, and maximizing long-term total
shareholder value.

Semiconductor Industry Demand Environment
The past decade has brought significant positive changes to the Semiconductor industry. Over the last
20 years, the industry heavily depended on one or two end-markets, first PCs and, later, mobile
phones. Today, the semiconductor industry is experiencing higher levels of through-cycle growth along
with more diversified demand. In addition, semiconductors are now strategic to a growing number of
industries and countries worldwide. Factors including the emergence of disruptive technologies such as
AI and the continuing advancement of innovation spurred by the power and performance benefits of
being at the leading edge are increasingly essential drivers in the ongoing buildout and upgrade of the
world’s data centers, which are currently shaping the semiconductor industry demand environment
more than any other factor. The commentary on incremental investment from the hyperscalers is
driving strong expected growth for HPC capability. Current projections of compounded annual growth
for this segment are expected to fundamentally change the composition of semiconductor revenue to
grow to be 40 to 50% or more of overall revenue over the next several years. For providers of leading-
edge semiconductor production capacity, this growth, coupled with rising and diverse design starts,
accelerating product cycles and larger die, increases the opportunity but also the risk in accelerating
time-to-market and ensuring chips function in line with higher performance specifications. These
trends, in the fastest-growing segment of semiconductor revenue, are encouraging and should lead to
increasing relevancy for KLA as measured by the share of the WFE market over the next several years as
our broad product portfolio plays a substantive role in enabling our customers' success. Finally, rising

Near-Term and Calendar Year 2025 Industry Outlook
The industry outlook continues to gain momentum in the near term, driven by increasing investment in
leading-edge logic, high-bandwidth memory, and advanced packaging.

We expect the WFE market to grow by a mid-single-digit percentage in 2025 from the high $90 billion
level for calendar 2024. Growth in calendar 2025 is expected to be fueled principally by increasing
investment in both leading-edge Foundry/Logic and Memory to support growing AI and premium
mobile demand offset by lower overall demand from China due to the digestion of elevated levels of
investment over the past couple of years. In an encouraging development, our top customer recently
said in an earnings call that they expect the number of new tape-outs for N2, or the 2 nanometer node,
in the first two years to be higher than both N3 and N5 in their first two years, fueled by both
smartphone and HPC applications. As communicated in early December, we continue to estimate the
impact on KLA’s revenue in calendar 2025 from recent export controls in China to be approximately
$500 million, plus or minus $100 million with roughly 70% of the impact affecting our systems business.
While we are hopeful, based on our interpretation of the regulations, that there should be licensing
opportunities that will mitigate some of this impact, we are taking a cautious view given the significant
delays in processing licensing requests by the U.S. government over the past few years. However, given
KLA’s business momentum, market share opportunities, and higher expected process control intensity
at the leading edge across all segments, we are confident we will continue to deliver growth
outperformance compared with the WFE market in 2025.

KLA’s unique product portfolio differentiation and value proposition are focused on enabling
technology transitions, accelerating process node capacity ramps, and ensuring yield entitlement in
high-volume production. We are encouraged by recent customer discussions and we are aligning
shipment slots with demand expectations. In this industry environment, KLA will continue to focus on
supporting customer requirements, executing on product roadmaps, and driving productivity.

About KLA Corporation
KLA Corporation (KLA) is the world's leading supplier of process control and yield management solutions
for the semiconductor and related microelectronics industries. The company's comprehensive portfolio
of products, software, analysis, services, and expertise is designed to help Integrated Circuit (IC)
manufacturers manage yield throughout the entire wafer fabrication process-from Research &
Development to final yield analysis. KLA offers a broad spectrum of products and services that are used
by every major semiconductor manufacturer in the world. We provide advanced process control and
process-enabling solutions for manufacturing wafers and reticles, integrated circuits, Packaging, and
printed circuit boards. In close collaboration with leading customers across the globe, our expert teams of
physicists, engineers, data scientists and problem-solvers design solutions that move the world forward.
Additional information may be found at: www.kla.com.
