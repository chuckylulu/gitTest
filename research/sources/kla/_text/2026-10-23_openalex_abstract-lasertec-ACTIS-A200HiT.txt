TITLE: High-throughput actinic patterned mask inspection and advanced defect management for efficient EUV mask qualification
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3117938
PUBLICATION DATE: 2026-10-23
VENUE: International Conference on Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography
AUTHORS: Michiteru Mizoguchi (Lasertec Corp. (Japan)) | Yuki Ushio (Lasertec Corp. (Japan)) | Mizuki Kobayashi (Lasertec Corp. (Japan)) | Hiroki Ohashi (Lasertec Corp. (Japan)) | Takao Saiki (Lasertec Corp. (Japan)) | Toshiyuki Todoroki (Lasertec Corp. (Japan)) | Ted Liang (Lasertec U.S.A., Inc. (United States)) | Hiroki Miyai (Lasertec Corp. (Japan))

ABSTRACT:
ACTIS, an actinic patterned mask inspection (APMI) system, has played a critical role in EUV mask qualification through its holistic inspection capability, detecting all printable defect types, including pattern defects, contamination, and multilayer phase defects, on masks with or without pellicles. As ACTIS has been widely adopted in high-volume manufacturing (HVM), industry needs have expanded beyond inspection sensitivity to include higher productivity and more effective use of APMI capabilities. Here we present enhancements to ACTIS to address these requirements. ACTIS A200HiT is a newly developed high-throughput model for wafer-fab applications. It delivers 3x higher inspection throughput than the conventional ACTIS A100 while maintaining the required detection sensitivity. In parallel, we have developed advanced defect management software solutions that fully leverage information obtained from ACTIS inspection. The solutions include defect filter, automatic defect classification (ADC) and defect printability prediction to reduce false and nuisance defects and characterize critical defects. Evaluation results with a programmed defect mask demonstrate that ACTIS A200HiT detects 20 nm defects in half-pitch (hp) 64 nm line-and-space (L/S) and 80 nm contact-hole (C/H) patterns. Full-mask inspection results further demonstrate that high-throughput APMI combined with defect classification and printability information enhances both inspection and disposition productivity for efficient EUV mask qualification and requalification.
