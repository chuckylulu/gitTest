1. Overview of Operating Results and Others
(1) Overview of operating results for the quarter
    During the first three months of this fiscal year, uncertainty in the economic outlook continued to prevail
    due to rising energy prices stemming from geopolitical risks, inflationary pressures, and concerns over
    the impact of monetary policies in major economies.

    In the semiconductor industry, our primary market, demand for cutting-edge semiconductors such as
    CPUs, GPUs, and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) has remained high due to robust investment in AI
    data centers, and device manufacturers have further expanded their capital investment plans. Under
    these circumstances, our business environment has remained favorable, and the outlook for orders in
    FY2027 has exceeded previous expectations.

    Our consolidated net sales for the three-month period ended September 30, 2026, progressed roughly
    in line with our expectations and totaled 40,369 million yen (down 25.5% year-on-year). By product
    segment, semiconductor-related products accounted for 23,849 million yen (down 42.9% year-on-
    year). Sales of other products accounted for 508 million yen (up 134.7% year-on-year) and services
    for 16,011 million yen (up 31.2% year-on-year).

    As for consolidated incomes during this period, we recorded an operating income of 15,769 million
    yen (down 41.0% year-on-year), an ordinary income of 14,711 million yen (down 45.8% year-on-year),
    and 10,734 million yen in net income attributable to owners of parent (down 43.7% year-on-year).

(2) Overview of financial position for the quarter
① Financial position
  Total assets at the end of the period amounted to 366,413 million yen, up 29,762 million yen from the
  end of the previous fiscal year. This was mainly due to the 19,265 million-yen increase in cash and
  deposits and the 15,600 million-yen increase in work in process, despite the 10,095 million-yen
  decrease in notes and accounts receivable-trade, and contract assets.

    Liabilities at the end of the period amounted to 126,903 million yen, up 37,103 million yen from the
    end of the previous fiscal year. This was mainly due to the 42,790 million-yen increase in advances
    received, despite the 7,002 million-yen decrease in income taxes payable.

    Total net assets, which consist of shareholders’ equity, accumulated other comprehensive income, and
    subscription rights to shares, amounted to 239,509 million yen. The capital-to-asset ratio was 65.4%.

② Cash flows
  Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period amounted to 110,768 million yen, up 19,265 million
  yen from the end of the previous fiscal year. Following is the status of cash flows and their contributing
  factors during the three-month period.

    Net cash provided by operating activities amounted to 39,102 million yen (up 558.6% year-on-year).
    This is mainly due to the fact that the sum of cash receipts, such as the 43,577 million-yen increase in
    advances received, the 14,711 million yen in income before income taxes, and the 9,878 million-yen
    decrease in notes and accounts receivable-trade, exceeded the sum of cash payments, such as the
    17,696 million yen increase in inventories and the 10,634 million yen spent for income taxes paid.

    Net cash used in investing activities amounted to 783 million yen (up 125.9% year-on-year). This is
    mainly due to the 569 million yen used in the purchase of property, plant and equipment and the 184
    million yen used in the purchase of intangible assets.

    Net cash used in financing activities amounted to 17,670 million yen (down 24.0% year-on-year). This
    is mainly due to the 17,657 million yen used for cash dividends.

(3) Information concerning forecast of consolidated financial results
    We have not revised our forecast for the fiscal year ending June 2027 announced on August 6,
    2026.
