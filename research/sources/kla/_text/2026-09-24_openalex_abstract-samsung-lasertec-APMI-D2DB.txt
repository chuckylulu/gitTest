ABSTRACT:
Advanced D2DB (Die-to-Database) modeling of complex patterns such as curvilinear SRAF (sub-resolution assist feature) and defect justification are challenging for EUV mask inspection. In the EUV mask back-end process, SEM and review tools are commonly used to justify defect classification and defect impact at the wafer level. However, it is a time-consuming process when the number of defects is high. In this paper, we propose various S/W application technologies for APMI (actinic patterned mask inspection). First, we introduce a novel D2DB modeling method that differentiates main patterns and SRAFs using design-data-assisted selection. Second, the auto defect screening (ADS) function using mask pattern and defect feature clustering is introduced to significantly reduce false defects. Third, the auto defect classification (ADC) is proposed by using deep learning. Finally, advanced S/W solutions are proposed, in which the illumination pupil and enlarged projection pupil of the actinic inspection tool are converted to those of an EUV scanner using a tailored optimization algorithm and optical model. By applying these methods, we developed an analysis capability for defects that affect the wafer process. This paper details these software techniques and their evaluation results.
