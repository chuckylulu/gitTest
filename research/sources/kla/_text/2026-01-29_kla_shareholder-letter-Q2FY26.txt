This letter reviews KLA’s overall performance for calendar 2025, the business and financial highlights
for the December 2025 quarter, guidance for the March 2026 quarter, and industry growth views for

Calendar 2025 Recap
KLA delivered another year of relative growth outperformance, strong profitability and free cash flow
(FCF) generation, and healthy returns to shareholders in calendar 2025. The record results
demonstrate KLA’s unique position for sustained outperformance in the Artificial Intelligence (AI) era,
guided by the KLA Operating Model. In calendar 2025, revenue grew 17% to a record $12.745 billion,
with our process control systems business outpacing forecasted low-double-digit growth from the
industry by several points. Non-GAAP earnings per share grew 29% year over year, demonstrating the
strong leverage in our model. KLA is also expected to grow process control market share in 2025. KLA’s
industry leadership in process control finds us playing a role in supporting all the major growth trends
in semiconductor manufacturing instrumental to AI, including advanced logic, high bandwidth memory,
and advanced packaging.

KLA process control system revenue grew by 19%, confirming the continuation of KLA’s multi-year
increase in share of the overall WFE market. In addition, KLA’s Services business grew 15% to $2.9
billion for the year. This result was slightly ahead of Services’ long-term growth target of 12-14%,
despite the ongoing negative impact of export controls limiting Services’ activity with certain
customers. It is clear from our results in calendar 2025 that the relevance of process control continues
to grow, and KLA’s comprehensive product portfolio is well positioned to capitalize on this trend.

For calendar 2025, KLA maintained industry-leading non-GAAP gross and operating margins of 62.8%
and 43.6%, respectively. The company also grew free cash flow 30% to $4.4 billion and returned $3.0
billion in a combination of dividends and share buybacks. KLA’s consistent cash flow generation ranks
the company in the top tier of all public companies, pointing to KLA’s financial strength to invest
consistently in new innovation, enabling growth opportunities across our technology portfolio, along
with making the investments in capacity required to support our growth while still maintaining a
consistent return of capital to shareholders.

December 2025 Quarter Results
Turning now to the December quarter results, KLA delivered strong results across the board. Revenue
was $3.3 billion, with non-GAAP diluted EPS at $8.85 and GAAP diluted EPS of $8.68.

KLA’s leadership in process control uniquely positions the company in the critical path of the infrastructure
build-out required to support AI. Demand for leading-edge logic, DRAM led by high-bandwidth memory
(HBM) and advanced packaging capabilities were again key drivers of KLA’s performance in the quarter.
Add to this our Services business growing mid single digits sequentially, and KLA is benefiting from multiple
growth vectors. Investment in scaling AI infrastructure and capabilities has accelerated growth at the
leading-edge of the semiconductor industry, driving higher levels of new designs, increased complexity,
shorter product cycles, higher-value wafers, and rising demand for advanced packaging. In DRAM, AI-driven
investments have mostly centered on HBM, which demands superior performance, improved reliability,
reduced redundancy, and advanced logic circuitry integrated into the base die that controls the HBM stack.

The growing importance of process control in the semiconductor and advanced packaging
manufacturing process is driven by ongoing semiconductor scaling, the integration of new architectures
and materials, and the increasing diversity and complexity of advanced device designs. Specifically,
process control accelerates time-to-results by resolving process integration challenges during the
research and development (R&D) and early fab ramp phase, while also optimizing and monitoring yield
in high-volume manufacturing. The rapid growth of high-value designs at the leading edge presents
new challenges for our customers as they optimize yield, limit process variability, drive capital
efficiency, and manage delivery timing in a high-volume production environment. These dynamics
serve as a tailwind for higher levels of investment. Beyond traditional challenges in the front-end of the
wafer manufacturing process, the evolution and growing complexity of advanced packaging are
generating new demand and opportunities for KLA’s process control and process solutions. Finally, as
KLA systems become more technologically advanced and have longer service lives in fabs, our Service
business continues to gain strategic importance, driven by rising customer expectations for tool
performance and availability across all customer segments, creating a strong, predictable long-term
tailwind for overall KLA revenue growth.

Top 5 Highlights December 2025 Quarter
First, KLA delivered record quarterly revenue of $3.3 billion, a 7% year-over-year increase for the
quarter, and capping a year of 17% year-over-year growth. This was fueled by continued investment in
leading-edge foundry/logic and high bandwidth memory (HBM) in DRAM. In foundry/logic, the
continuation of scaling with an increasing pipeline of design starts, adoption of larger die sizes in
advanced architectures, and increasing capital intensity continue to serve as long-term secular drivers
that uniquely benefit process control. In memory, increasing performance specs, reduced redundancy,
and increasing logic content in devices supporting high performance compute (HPC) applications are
driving structurally higher process control intensity across all customers.

Second, AI remains a core driver of KLA’s performance and a key factor in our growing industry
leadership. KLA’s advanced systems apply AI-driven analytics to deliver actionable insights that
streamline chip manufacturing and accelerate innovation and time-to-yield for next-generation AI
applications. Roughly ten years ago, KLA began integrating AI into our systems and rearchitecting our
compute architecture to a GPU-centric design, enabling simultaneous workloads and improved
algorithmic performance at a lower cost compared to CPU-based computing alternatives. We believe
that our experience reflects the compelling capabilities, cost, and power consumption available with
GPU-based compute architectures and is fueling the ongoing HPC infrastructure buildout. Over the
past five years, we have enabled our entire product portfolio with these capabilities. KLA’s innovative
architecture enhances computational throughput, reduces latency, and delivers superior cost efficiency
and time-to-results. Future KLA innovations with AI will produce actionable data for our customers that
accelerate system performance, reduce the cost of ownership, and improve their return on investment
in KLA systems.

Third, our advanced packaging portfolio continues to grow rapidly. The rising demand for more
powerful systems of chips is driving advanced packaging. This trend is increasing the value of process
control in the chip package and fueling significant growth in this expanding market for KLA. Customer
adoption of KLA’s advanced packaging portfolio demonstrates the success of our strategic market
diversification and validates the strength of our current product capabilities and our long-term
technology roadmap. KLA’s advanced packaging systems revenue continues to gain momentum
through a combination of market share gains across all customer segments and intensity gains. KLA
continues to see strong momentum in advanced packaging revenue growth and market share with
calendar 2025 total systems revenue of approximately $950 million, representing over 70% year-over-
year growth, above our forecast from last quarter driven by our process control portfolio. For calendar
2026, we expect this momentum to continue with year-over-year percentage growth expectations in
the mid-teens driven by faster than market growth from our process control products.

Fourth, the KLA Services business grew to $786 million in the December quarter, up 6% sequentially,
and 18% year-over-year. This was the 16th consecutive year of annual service revenue growth with a
compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 12.4% over that timeframe. The consistency and resiliency of
our service contract penetration remain key aspects of this differentiated business in the industry.

Semiconductor Industry Demand Environment
Over the past ten years, the semiconductor industry has experienced substantial growth and
diversification. Semiconductors are a significant enabler of the global economy, and as semiconductor
end markets continue to diversify, we see ongoing innovations in semiconductor designs and
manufacturing processes. Today’s industry growth is driven by expanding demand for HPC and data
center investment, fueled by the rapid and widespread adoption of AI. Combined with the growing use
of semiconductor-based technologies and diversified end-market requirements, these factors are
fostering stronger, more resilient long-term growth across the industry.

AI is driving a pivotal technology inflection point, accelerating innovation, and compelling leading-edge
demand for both logic and memory while simultaneously igniting the need for advanced packaging.
KLA’s comprehensive portfolio of solutions is uniquely positioned to support this growth, enabling
advanced requirements and powering the global buildout and modernization of data centers serving
both commercial and sovereign HPC requirements.

For providers of leading-edge semiconductor production capacity, rapid growth combined with
increasing design diversity, shorter product cycles, and larger die sizes creates significant opportunities
but also heightens the challenge of accelerating time-to-market while ensuring that stringent
performance requirements are met in high-value devices. These dynamics reinforce KLA’s growing
relevance, as reflected in our expectation of continued growth in our share of the WFE market over the

Additionally, the emerging and fast-growing advanced packaging market further strengthens KLA’s
relative position. Finally, rising semiconductor content across diverse end markets, coupled with
strategic investments in legacy nodes, remains a key driver of dependable, long-term growth for KLA
and the semiconductor equipment industry.


Industry Outlook
The industry outlook continues to be driven by increasing investment in leading-edge logic, leading-
edge memory via HBM, and the increasing adoption and complexity of advanced packaging.

The growth of advanced packaging supporting heterogeneous chip integration has opened a significant
new market opportunity for KLA. What was once captured as a rounding error in WFE forecasts has
now grown into an approximately $11 billion plus market experiencing rapid growth and adoption,
according to KLA’s internal estimates. This is particularly true as chip densities increase and the
processing required for packaging creates risk for our customers in integrating high-value devices in a
strong demand environment. For KLA, this creates a new served available market (SAM) that is poised
to augment the company’s revenue growth over the next several years. Given this, we plan to include
this market in our forecasts going forward. The market and technology roadmap for leading-edge WFE
supporting HPC is driving relative inflections for process control. These drivers, coupled with the
evolving complexity of advanced packaging, support an even broader market opportunity for the
company.


Calendar 2026 Market Outlook
The industry outlook for calendar 2026 has strengthened over the past few months. We expect the
core WFE market to grow in the high-single to low-double digits, reaching the low $120 billion range,
up from approximately $110 billion in calendar 2025. In addition, we expect the advanced packaging
component of the market to grow at a similar rate to approximately $12 billion for a total market
forecast in the mid $130 billion range, an increase in the low double digits versus our forecast for 2025.
The customer spending profile is expected to broaden across all major end markets.. Given KLA’s strong
business momentum, expanding market share, and higher process control intensity at the leading-edge
across all segments, we begin calendar 2026 well-positioned to outperform and increase our share of
the overall market. We are experiencing strong customer momentum that has accelerated over the
past three months and is reflected in our system backlog and sales funnel. Our view today is that the
first half of calendar 2026 revenue will grow mid-single digits compared to the second half of calendar
2025, with accelerating growth in the second half of calendar year 2026. Customer lead times for our
products are increasing due to supply constraints, limiting first-half growth potential across many of
our products.

KLA’s unique product portfolio differentiation and value proposition are focused on enabling
technology transitions, accelerating process node capacity ramps, and ensuring yield entitlement in
high-volume manufacturing. The market environment and the complexity of our customers’ technology
roadmaps are compelling, presenting both challenges and opportunities for KLA to maintain its relative
outperformance. In this industry environment, we remain focused on supporting customers, investing
for the future, executing product roadmaps, and driving productivity across the enterprise.

Outlook for the March 2026 Quarter
KLA’s March quarter guidance is for revenue of $3.35 billion, plus or minus $150 million. Foundry/Logic
revenue from semiconductor customers is forecasted to be consistent with the December quarter at
approximately 60%, and memory is expected to be approximately 40% of Semi Process Control systems
revenue to semiconductor customers. Within memory, DRAM is expected to be roughly 85% and NAND
the remaining 15%. As always, these business mix approximations pertain solely to our semiconductor
customers and do not fully reflect our total semiconductor process control systems revenue.




Non-GAAP gross margin for the quarter is forecasted to be 61.75% plus or minus 1 percentage point.
Although volume levels are relatively consistent quarter to quarter, product mix is modestly weaker
versus the December quarter. This guidance also includes the incremental impact of the rapidly
escalating cost of DRAM chips used in the company’s image processing computers that ship with our
systems, creating a headwind to our gross margin expectations. This pricing environment has changed
profoundly over the past two to three months and has been highlighted regularly in industry press and
analyst reports. While we expect this pricing environment to be transitory, current modeling suggests
that this situation will persist through calendar 2026 and have a roughly 75bps to 100bps negative
impact on gross margins for the calendar year. As DRAM capacity additions accelerate over the next
several quarters, we expect this cost dynamic to improve as we exit the calendar year, and we are
modeling a return to a normalized pricing environment for our memory requirements in our longer-
term business forecasts. Considering this impact, coupled with product mix and volume expectations,
we expect gross margins to be approximately 62% plus or minus 50 basis points in calendar 2026.

Non-GAAP operating expenses are forecasted to be approximately $645 million in the March quarter.
For calendar 2026, we will continue to prioritize next-generation product development and company
infrastructure investments to support expected revenue growth over the next several years, and we
anticipate these expenses to grow roughly $15 million sequentially throughout the calendar year. Our
business model is designed to deliver 40% to 50% incremental non-GAAP operating margin leverage on
revenue growth over the long run.

Other model assumptions include: Non-GAAP other income and expense, net, of approximately $25
million for the March quarter and we expect it to remain at this quarterly level for the calendar year.

In Conclusion
Our near-term revenue guidance reflects consistent growth and relative strength. We expect to
outperform the WFE market in calendar 2026 driven by multiple tailwinds driving rising process control
intensity and growth in advanced packaging. KLA continues to focus on delivering a differentiated
product portfolio that addresses customers' technology roadmap requirements and production
efficiency objectives which are driving our longer-term relevance and growth expectations. The KLA
Operating Model guides our best-in-class execution Our focus on customer success, innovative
solutions, and operational excellence drives industry-leading financial performance, enabling us to
return capital consistently and predictably.

KLA’s business is uniquely positioned to capitalize on today’s technology inflections and growth drivers.
We are encouraged by strengthening customer confidence and engagement, which informs our
business forecast. The long-term secular trends driving semiconductor industry demand and
investments in WFE and advanced packaging are compelling and represent a relative performance
opportunity for KLA over the next several years.

KLA’s business has gone from being primarily indexed to leading-edge R&D investment and fab capacity
ramp, to now addressing all growth phases in WFE and advanced packaging, enabling leading-edge
process development, time to results in fab capacity ramps, and optimizing yield in a high-volume
manufacturing environment. In addition, the growing investment in custom silicon, particularly among
hyperscalers developing their own custom chips, has led to a proliferation of new higher-value design
starts and increased demand on our customers to deliver performance, volume, and time-to-market.
As design mix and complexity grows, so does the need for advanced process control. As a result, KLA is
seeing consistent growth in process control intensity, as each new chip design requires rigorous
inspection, metrology, and yield optimization solutions. KLA is uniquely positioned to benefit from
these trends as we expand our market leadership and deliver differentiated value to our customers.

About KLA Corporation
KLA Corporation (KLA) is the world's leading supplier of process control and yield management solutions
for the semiconductor and related microelectronics industries. The company's comprehensive portfolio
of products, software, analysis, services, and expertise is designed to help Integrated Circuit (IC)
manufacturers manage yield throughout the entire wafer fabrication process-from Research &
Development to final yield analysis. KLA offers a broad spectrum of products and services that are used
by every major semiconductor manufacturer in the world. We provide advanced process control and
process-enabling solutions for manufacturing wafers and reticles, integrated circuits, Packaging, and
printed circuit boards. In close collaboration with leading customers across the globe, our expert teams of
physicists, engineers, data scientists and problem-solvers design solutions that move the world forward.
