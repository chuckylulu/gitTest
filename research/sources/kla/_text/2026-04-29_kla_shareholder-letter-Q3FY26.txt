Dear KLA Shareholders,

This letter covers KLA’s business and financial results for the March 2026 quarter, our outlook for the
June 2026 quarter, and some highlights from our recent March 12, 2026, Investor Day, including the
2030 financial targets.

March 2026 Quarter Results
KLA delivered strong results across the board for the March quarter. Revenue was $3.415 billion, with
non-GAAP diluted EPS at $9.40 and GAAP diluted EPS of $9.12.

KLA’s leadership in process control continues to gain momentum. We are coming off an Investor Day where
we detailed how KLA’s portfolio approach to solving customer process challenges and enhancing yield
learning cycles within process control is unique and requires not only a significant and compounding R&D
investment, but also precise execution across the supply chain, manufacturing operations, and service.
KLA’s strength lies in leveraging capabilities across our comprehensive portfolio, rather than relying on a
single product to solve our customers' biggest challenges. Our customers always optimize for capability,
cost, and efficiency, aiming for the best total cost of ownership for inspection and measurement. KLA helps
drive down the total cost of ownership by allowing customers to more effectively optimize through a
portfolio of systems and technologies that are designed to work together to accelerate learning cycles and
improve yield. This drives our customers' success as well as our own.

What is clear in our quarterly results is that many of the same trends driving our revenue growth are
improving, and our expectations for equipment industry growth for calendar 2026 and 2027 are building.
KLA remains uniquely positioned on the critical path of AI infrastructure expansion. Process control
relevance is increasing as the volume of design starts at the leading edge continues to expand at a rapid
pace. This isn’t limited to just the foundry/logic market, as the memory market has significantly increased
its adoption of process control due to the rise of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) in DRAM. The importance
of KLA’s products and services in memory has increased because of HBM, as requirements have increased
for performance and reliability. In addition, die sizes are larger, redundancy is lower and the complexity of
the device is substantially higher, including the need for advanced logic integrated into the base die to
control the HBM stack.

It is important to distinguish that design activity and rising memory complexity are not the only catalysts
driving benefits for KLA and process control. Faster product cycles, higher-value wafers and masks, rising
design complexity and variability, and the growing demand and complexity of advanced packaging all
require significantly more process-control solutions. These solutions shorten time-to-results by addressing
process integration challenges in the R&D and early fab ramp phase, while continuing to manage yield with
strong design mix in high-volume manufacturing. The rapid expansion of high-value, leading-edge
manufacturing and advanced packaging is creating new challenges for customers as they balance yield
optimization, process variability, capital efficiency, and delivery schedules in high-volume manufacturing.
KLA is well-positioned to address these opportunities.

Finally, as KLA systems become more technologically advanced and have longer service lives in fabs,
our Service business continues to gain strategic importance, driven by rising customer expectations for
tool performance and availability across all customer segments, creating a strong, predictable long-
term tailwind for overall KLA revenue growth.

Calendar 2030 Long-Term Financial Targets
At our March 12, 2026, Investor Day (click here for links to Investor Day materials), KLA introduced new
long-term revenue growth targets along with a calendar 2030 financial model, and increased our
capital allocation target to over 90% of free cash flow. We also announced the 17th consecutive
increase in our quarterly dividend level (+21% to $2.30/share) and a $7 billion share repurchase
authorization to supplement the $3.3 billion remaining under the previous authorization. KLA’s revised
higher 13-17% revenue CAGR objective through calendar 2030 reflects strong growth across our key
business segments and includes an increased long-term Services revenue growth CAGR model of 13-
15%. Our long-term model assumes a baseline semiconductor industry growth CAGR of ~11% from
calendar 2025 to calendar 2030, with the Wafer Equipment market growing ~1 percentage point faster
than the semiconductor industry to $215 billion, plus or minus $20 billion, by calendar 2030. Given the
growing relevance of process control across all customer segments, we expect KLA to continue to
outperform the equipment market on the top line, drive operating leverage, and continue to deliver
our best-in-class financial model.




CY25 Market Share Update: Another Year of Share Growth for KLA
Recently published industry research reports showed KLA increased its global share of both overall
Wafer Equipment and the Process Control market in calendar 2025. This growing market leadership
was highlighted by significant gains in Advanced Wafer Level Packaging, where KLA assumed the #1
position in this market, increased market share by 14 percentage points and achieved approximately
70% year-over-year revenue growth. Market share also improved across mask inspection, optical
patterned wafer inspection, and electron beam inspection. Since 2021, KLA’s share of Process Control
has grown by 360 basis points and is approximately 7x greater than the nearest competitor.

Top 5 Highlights March 2026 Quarter
First, KLA delivered record quarterly revenue of $3.415 billion, up 4% sequentially and 11% year-over-
year. This growth was driven by sustained strength in investment for both leading-edge foundry/logic
and high bandwidth memory (HBM). In foundry/logic, the continued pace of scaling, supported by a
growing pipeline of design starts, increasingly driven by the hyperscalers shaping the leading-edge
roadmap, and broader adoption of larger die sizes in advanced architectures remain as long-term
secular drivers that uniquely benefit process control. In memory, higher performance specs, reduced
redundancy, and increasing logic content in devices designed for high-performance computing (HPC)
applications are spurring structurally higher process control intensity across the customer base.

Second, the strength in our business can be directly correlated to AI. AI is both a core driver of KLA’s
performance and an enabler of our expanding industry leadership. KLA’s industry-leading systems apply
AI-driven analytics, noise suppression and simulation capabilities to deliver actionable insights that
streamline chip manufacturing and accelerate innovation and time-to-yield for next-generation
semiconductor AI applications. Over the past five years, we have enabled our entire product portfolio
with increasing AI capabilities. KLA’s innovative architecture enhances computational throughput,
reduces latency, delivers superior cost efficiency and time-to-results. Continued R&D investments are
designed to create new KLA innovations with AI that produce actionable data for our customers,
accelerating system performance, reducing the cost of ownership, and improving our customers’ return
on investment.

Third, KLA achieved the #1 position in process control for Advanced Wafer Level Packaging in calendar
2025, driven by strong customer adoption of the KLA packaging portfolio, which is unmatched in
breadth and depth in the marketplace. The rising demand for more powerful systems of chips is
increasing the importance of process control in the chip package and fueling significant growth in this
expanding market. Rapid customer adoption of KLA’s advanced packaging portfolio validates the
strength of our current product capabilities and our long-term technology roadmap which leverages
common R&D across process control products and brings highly capable existing wafer front-end
solutions into this increasingly relevant market. Advanced Packaging market growth in calendar 2026 is
further accelerating. KLA’s process control systems revenue continues to gain momentum in this

March 2026 Quarter Financial Highlights
KLA’s March quarter results reflect strong year-over-year growth with an industry-leading margin
profile, highlighting our market leadership, consistent execution, and the dedication of our global
teams in meeting customer commitments.

Revenue was $3.415 billion, above the guidance midpoint of $3.35 billion. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was
$9.40, and GAAP diluted EPS was $9.12, each above the midpoint of the respective guidance ranges.

Non-GAAP gross margin was 62.2%, 45 basis points above the midpoint of guidance, driven by better than
modeled services business mix and manufacturing scale due to higher business volume. Non-GAAP
operating expenses were $670 million and included $389 million in R&D and $281 million in SG&A.
Operating expenses were higher than expected principally due to prototype materials timing and other
reserve adjustments. Non-GAAP operating margin was 42.6%. Non-GAAP other income/expense, net, was
$9 million in income. The variance relative to guidance was due to a significant mark-to-market gain of a
strategic supply investment. The quarterly non-GAAP effective tax rate was 15.4%. At the guided tax rate of
14.5%, Non-GAAP earnings per share would have been $0.10 higher or $9.50.

Semiconductor Industry Demand Environment
Over the past ten years, the semiconductor industry has experienced substantial growth and
diversification. Semiconductors are a significant enabler of the global economy, and as semiconductor
end markets continue to broaden, we see ongoing innovations in semiconductor designs and
manufacturing processes. Industry growth is driven by expanding high performance compute (HPC)
investment, fueled by the rapid adoption of AI. Together with the growing use of semiconductor
technologies and increasingly diversified end-market demand, these trends are supporting stronger
and more diversified long-term industry growth.

AI is driving a major technology inflection, accelerating innovation and increasing demand for leading-
edge solutions across logic, memory, and advanced packaging. KLA’s comprehensive product portfolio
is uniquely positioned to support this growth, enabling the compute roadmap and powering the
buildout and modernization of the world’s data centers.


Calendar 2026 and 2027 Market Outlook
The industry outlook continues to strengthen across all segments. We expect the Wafer Equipment
market, which includes Advanced Packaging, to exceed $140 billion in calendar 2026.

The strength of demand and customer engagement in insuring KLA has the capacity to support
numerous new fabs projects currently under construction has led to unprecedented demand visibility
from our customers. While normally we would not comment on 2027 growth rates in April of 2026, this
demand environment gives us confidence in calendar 2027 visibility for the Wafer Equipment market.
Today, we expect the calendar 2027 year-over-year growth rate to be higher than our growth rate
expectations for calendar 2026. KLA has strong business momentum, expanding market share, and
higher process control intensity at the leading-edge across all segments. Given all of this, we are well-
positioned to continue to increase our share of the overall market in calendar 2026 and 2027. The
customer engagement that we are experiencing is reflected in our growing systems backlog and sales
funnel. We continue to expect quarter-to-quarter revenue growth throughout calendar 2026 and
strong business momentum leading into calendar 2027. For calendar 2026, we expect sequential
revenue growth for the company to accelerate leading to high teen revenue growth year-over-year and
the Semiconductor Process Control systems business growing over 20%.

KLA’s product portfolio differentiation and value proposition focus on enabling technology transitions,
accelerating process-node capacity ramps, and ensuring yield entitlement in high-volume
manufacturing. The market environment and the complexity of our customers’ technology roadmaps
are compelling, presenting both challenges and opportunities for KLA to maintain its relative
outperformance. In this industry environment, we remain focused on supporting customers, investing
in the future, executing product roadmaps, and driving productivity across the company.

relative performance opportunity for KLA over the next several years.

KLA’s business has gone from being primarily indexed to leading-edge R&D investment and fab capacity
ramps, to now addressing all growth phases in Wafer Equipment, enabling leading-edge process
development, time to results in fab capacity ramps, and optimizing yield in a high-volume
manufacturing environment. In addition, the growing investment in custom silicon, particularly among
hyperscalers developing their own custom chips, has led to a proliferation of new higher-value design
starts and increased demand on our customers to deliver performance, volume, and time-to-market.
As design mix and complexity grows, so does the need for process control. As a result, KLA is seeing
consistent growth in process control intensity, as each new chip design requires rigorous inspection,
metrology, and yield optimization solutions. KLA is uniquely positioned to benefit from these trends as
we expand our market leadership and deliver differentiated value to our customers.

About KLA Corporation
KLA Corporation (KLA) is the world's leading supplier of process control and yield management solutions
for the semiconductor and related microelectronics industries. The company's comprehensive portfolio
of products, software, analysis, services, and expertise is designed to help Integrated Circuit (IC)
manufacturers manage yield throughout the entire wafer fabrication process, from Research &
Development to final yield analysis. KLA offers a broad spectrum of products and services used by every
major semiconductor manufacturer worldwide. We provide advanced process control and process-
enabling solutions for manufacturing wafers and reticles, integrated circuits, Packaging, and printed
circuit boards. In close collaboration with leading customers across the globe, our expert teams of
physicists, engineers, data scientists and problem-solvers design solutions that move the world forward.
Additional information may be found at: www.kla.com.

relations website (ir.kla.com), including SEC filings, press releases, public earnings calls, and conference
webcasts. These channels are used to communicate with the public about the company, products,
services, and other matters.
