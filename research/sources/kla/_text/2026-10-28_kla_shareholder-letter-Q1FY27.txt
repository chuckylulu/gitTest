CEO Update - September 2026 Quarter Results
KLA's September quarter beat the midpoint of our guidance ranges across all key metrics, and when
combined with our December quarter guidance, demonstrates the growth acceleration we expected for
the second half of calendar 2026. Revenue was $4.14 billion supported by continued strength in leading-
edge foundry/logic tied to AI infrastructure investments, and increasing process control intensity across
leading-edge devices and advanced packaging.

The September quarter reflected continued strength in KLA’s leading market position in process control as
growing semiconductor complexity drives more stringent requirements. Industry investment is
accelerating, driven by AI infrastructure, a strong design environment, larger, more sophisticated, and more
valuable device architectures, increasing High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM) adoption, and expanding
advanced packaging requirements. Across these trends, customers rely on KLA’s portfolio of systems,
services, and expertise to accelerate yield learning, scale productivity, and introduce new technologies into
high-volume manufacturing.

Since the start of calendar 2026, the AI demand environment has continued to strengthen, driven by
increased data center investments, growing compute requirements, and broader adoption of advanced
nodes. All of this has led our customers to continue raising their wafer equipment investment guidance for
both calendar 2026 and 2027. Customer engagement is exceptionally strong, with meaningfully improving
visibility supporting a more favorable outlook for wafer equipment investment. This is reflected in our
growing backlog, as well as in our sales funnel forecasts. We expect momentum across our business to
continue into the December quarter, and we are positioned to deliver significant growth in calendar 2027.
As a result, we have been proactively working with our strategic supply chain partners over the past year to
increase their capacity to keep up with our demand.

Advanced packaging has become increasingly critical to AI infrastructure expansion, creating additional
process control requirements. These increasing requirements are expanding the opportunity for KLA’s
advanced packaging process control portfolio. Advanced packaging roadmaps continue to incorporate
more sophisticated wafer front-end processes, increasing the need for advanced process control
capabilities. KLA’s broad process control portfolio enables the company to address these requirements
across a growing range of applications. For calendar 2026, Advanced Packaging process control systems
revenue remains on track to hit approximately $1.1 billion, or more than 70% year-over-year growth, and
nearly twice the growth rate of the advanced packaging market.

The growing complexity of KLA’s installed base and longer tool lifecycles continue to increase demand for
services. KLA Services revenue was $850 million in the quarter, up 14% year-over-year, supported by a
growing installed base and continued customer focus on tool performance, productivity, and availability in
this tight system utilization environment. Underscoring its predictability and visibility, the Services business
remains on track for its 17th consecutive year of annual revenue growth. This recurring, subscription-like
business strengthens our operating model, enhances free cash flow generation, and reinforces our long-
term capital allocation strategy. KLA services growth for the calendar year is expected to be at the top end
of the Services revenue growth rate model of 13 to 15%.

September 2026 Quarter Financial Highlights
KLA delivered strong sequential and year-over-year growth in the September quarter. For the first time
in our company history, KLA delivered non-GAAP operating margins over 46%. These results reflect our
market leadership and disciplined execution, supported by our global teams' dedication and focus on
customer success.

Revenue of $4.14 billion was above the midpoint of guidance and rose 13% sequentially and 29% year-
over-year. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.22, and GAAP diluted EPS was $1.18, each above the midpoint of
the respective guidance ranges.

Non-GAAP gross margin was 62.7%, slightly above the midpoint of the guidance range. Non-GAAP
operating expenses were $688 million, including $395 million in R&D and $293 million in SG&A. Non-GAAP
operating margin rose 240 bps sequentially to 46.1%.

Non-GAAP other income/expense, net, was $38 million in expense. The quarterly non-GAAP effective tax
rate was 14.1%.




Non-GAAP net income was $1.60 billion, GAAP net income was $1.55 billion, cash flow from operations
was $1.53 billion, and free cash flow was $1.34 billion. The company had 1.311 billion diluted weighted
average shares outstanding for the quarter.

Semiconductor Industry Demand Environment
Semiconductors continue to play an increasingly important role in the global economy, with industry
growth driven by investment in AI infrastructure and expanding demand across a broader range of
applications and end markets. Together, these trends are supporting stronger, more durable, and more
diversified long-term growth for the industry.

At the same time, the growing complexity of AI-enabled semiconductors is increasing process-control
intensity throughout the manufacturing flow, reinforcing KLA's long-term growth opportunity. KLA's
differentiated portfolio is uniquely positioned to address these requirements, while sustained AI data
center investment and broader adoption of advanced packaging architectures continue to expand the
company's addressable market.

Calendar 2026 and 2027 Market Outlook
The industry outlook continues to strengthen, with visibility improving as customers increase long-term
investment plans. Despite some fab space limitations, customers have been very resourceful and are
accelerating delivery expectations across all segments. We are therefore raising our expectation for the
wafer equipment market, including advanced packaging, to approximately the mid-$150 billion range in
calendar 2026. KLA backlog continues to grow, and our sales funnel visibility is unprecedented,
supported by new fab investments by a broad base of customers. We continue to plan for significant
growth in calendar 2027. While we will have more to say about market and customer segment
expectations for next year in our earnings call in late January, we currently expect the wafer equipment
market to exceed $200 billion in calendar 2027.

Customer engagement remains robust with numerous new fab projects and greenfield facilities
scheduled to come online in the quarters ahead. The primary area of focus for our customers is on
delivery urgency aligned with new project plans. Against this backdrop, KLA's business momentum and
critical role in leading-edge process control position us to deliver accelerating growth for the remainder
of calendar 2026 and continued strong growth in 2027. Our expectations of revenue growth
acceleration in the second half of 2026 are playing out as more capacity comes online in our long-lead-
time supply chain areas. This is resulting in growth in the second half of calendar year 2026 over the
first half of approximately 25% and positions the company for continued sequential growth into
calendar 2027.


Outlook for the December 2026 Quarter
KLA’s December quarter guidance is for revenue of $4.7 billion, plus or minus $200 million.
Foundry/Logic revenue from semiconductor customers is forecast to be approximately 60%, and
memory is expected to be about 40% of Semiconductor Process Control systems revenue from
semiconductor customers.

Within memory, DRAM is expected to account for approximately 84%, with NAND representing the
remaining 16%. As a reminder, these business mix approximations pertain solely to our semiconductor
customers and do not fully reflect our total Semiconductor Process Control systems revenue.

Non-GAAP gross margin for the December quarter is forecasted to be 62.5%, plus or minus one
percentage point. While the company is demonstrating expected operating leverage across the
business as it grows, this is being offset by persistently high memory costs for our system image
processing requirements as we have discussed in prior quarters. We are actively engaged across the
portfolio to optimize memory efficiency in our systems along with other product cost-reduction
initiatives. These efforts, coupled with driving our usual returns-based value sharing with our
customers, should offset this headwind over time.

Conclusion
In conclusion, process control, and KLA in particular, remains critical to the AI ecosystem build-out and
benefits from numerous ongoing relevance inflections, including increases in leading-edge designs
across foundry and logic, device size and value, rising complexity and performance requirements, and
the growing importance of advanced packaging.

KLA is experiencing strengthening momentum and a broader set of growth drivers across the
semiconductor ecosystem. The rate and pace of our backlog growth is a key signal of our accelerating
momentum and increased confidence in our visibility leading into calendar 2027. Our September
quarter results demonstrate the strength of KLA’s market position and operating model execution and
support continued confidence in our performance moving forward. These recent results also illustrate
the strength of KLA’s market-leading portfolio, disciplined operating model execution, and continued
confidence in improving our performance moving forward.

About KLA Corporation
KLA Corporation (KLA) is the world's leading supplier of process control and yield management solutions
for the semiconductor and related microelectronics industries. The company's comprehensive portfolio
of products, software, analysis, services, and expertise is designed to help Integrated Circuit (IC)
manufacturers manage yield throughout the entire wafer fabrication process, from Research &
Development to final yield analysis. KLA offers a broad spectrum of products and services used by every
major semiconductor manufacturer worldwide. We provide advanced process control and process-
enabling solutions for manufacturing wafers and reticles, integrated circuits, Packaging, and printed
circuit boards. In close collaboration with leading customers across the globe, our expert teams of
physicists, engineers, data scientists and problem-solvers design solutions that move the world forward.
Additional information may be found at: www.kla.com.

Investors and others should note that KLA announces material financial information on its investor
relations website (ir.kla.com), including SEC filings, press releases, public earnings calls, and conference
webcasts. These channels are used to communicate with the public about the company, products,
services, and other matters.
