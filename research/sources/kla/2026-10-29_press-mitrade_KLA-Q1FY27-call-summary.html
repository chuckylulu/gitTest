    KLA (KLAC) Fiscal Q1 2027 Earnings Call: Record Revenue and Stronger 2027 Outlook

    Source Tradingkey

    Key Takeaways

    • KLA Corporation reported record fiscal Q1 2027 revenue of $4.14 billion, up 13% sequentially and 29% year over year. Revenue exceeded the midpoint of the company’s guidance range.
    • Non-GAAP operating margin reached 46.1%, exceeding 46% for the first time in KLA’s history. Gross margin was 62.7%.
    • Management guided fiscal Q2 2027 revenue to $4.7 billion, plus or minus $200 million, indicating continued sequential growth.
    • KLA raised its calendar 2026 wafer equipment market estimate, including advanced packaging, to the mid-$150 billion range. Management currently expects the market to exceed $200 billion in calendar 2027.
    • Advanced packaging process-control systems revenue remains on track to reach approximately $1.1 billion in calendar 2026, representing growth of more than 70% year over year.
    • Management said backlog and sales-funnel visibility support significant growth in calendar 2027, with KLA expecting to outgrow the wafer equipment market.
