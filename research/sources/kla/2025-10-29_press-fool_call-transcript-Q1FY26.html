SUMMARY

KLA Corp. (KLAC -10.33%) reported double-digit year-over-year growth in both revenue and profitability (non-GAAP) for the September quarter, highlighted by robust non-GAAP earnings per share and a gross margin of 62.5% that surpassed prior guidance for the September 2025 quarter. Strategic commentary emphasized acceleration in advanced packaging revenue and record service business expansion, as well as new revenue segment mix disclosures for December, and noted improvements in manufacturing efficiencies. Management announced a 12% annual dividend increase on April 30, 2025, and $5 billion share repurchase program, while guidance projects modest sequential growth, continued momentum in both DRAM and advanced packaging, and positions the firm to outperform mid to high single-digit WFE market growth rates for the calendar year. The call addressed export control risks with quantified revenue impacts, acknowledged ongoing normalization in China business, and provided specific margin discussion tied to product mix, tariffs, and evolving service contributions.

  • The CFO highlighted that changes to backlog disclosures now align with industry peers, and current normalized lead times are between seven and nine months.
  • KLA’s advanced packaging process control share has risen from around 1% to nearly 6% as of calendar year 2025, with management stating, "We're approaching 6%" (non-GAAP, CY2025). based on internal 2025 market projections.
  • Management confirmed rising process control intensity and share gains in both DRAM and leading-edge logic, attributing new demand drivers to "Accelerating investment in scaling AI infrastructure."
  • Service revenue (non-GAAP) is expected to remain in the 12%-14% growth range in CY2025, with a similar outlook for CY2026, driven by utilization, contractual pricing, and complexity of installed base systems.
  • Gross margins in calendar year 2026 are expected to be primarily driven by product mix, with tariffs representing a "structural" headwind but mitigated by company actions over time, according to Bren D. Higgins.
  • The outlook anticipates flat to modestly higher first-half 2026 revenues versus 2025 (calendar year, non-GAAP basis), with accelerated growth in the second half of CY2026 as new fab space and broader investments advance.
  • KLA’s management noted increased customer urgency for DRAM and advanced process control driven by both AI adoption and high bandwidth memory requirements, leading to stronger demand than previously anticipated.
  • Export controls are expected to reduce China revenue mix steadily into the mid-20s percent by calendar year 2026.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • WFE (Wafer Fab Equipment): Capital equipment used in semiconductor wafer fabrication processes, including inspection, etch, deposition, and metrology systems.
  • HBM (High Bandwidth Memory): Advanced DRAM technology enhancing bandwidth, primarily used in AI and high-performance computing applications.
  • Advanced Packaging: Techniques enabling heterogeneous integration of multiple chips or dies, improving performance, density, and power efficiency in semiconductor devices.
  • Process Control Intensity: A measure of the proportion of wafer fab equipment investment allocated to inspection and metrology tools within semiconductor manufacturing.
  • SAM (Served Available Market): The portion of the total market that a company’s products and services can actively target with its offerings.
