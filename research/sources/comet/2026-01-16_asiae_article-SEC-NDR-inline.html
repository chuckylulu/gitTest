Sec Plans NDR for Institutions... To Present HBM Inline Inspection Equipment Development Status

by Jang Hyowon

Published 16 Jan.2026 10:58(KST)

Share Language Text Size Print
View original image

Sec, a specialized company in electronic beam-based materials, parts, and equipment, announced on January 16 that it will hold a Non-Deal Roadshow (NDR) for major domestic investment institutions on February 4 and 5. The event is sponsored by Hyundai Motor Securities.


A representative from Sec stated, "Due to the growing interest in our inline X-ray inspection equipment for HBM, which we introduced with the goal of completing development in February, there has been an increase in IR requests from securities firms, leading us to decide to hold the NDR. We plan to conduct one-on-one meetings with major securities firms over the two days."
