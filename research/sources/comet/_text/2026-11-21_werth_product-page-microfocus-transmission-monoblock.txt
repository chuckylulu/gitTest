Microfocus transmission in monoblock design

High-resolution X-ray source for economical measurement with high resolution

Benefits

Fast measurement with high resolution in a compact design

These X-ray sources in monoblock design, which are unique on the market, enable measurements with high resolution and extremely short measurement times. By using transmission targets, these tubes offer focal spot sizes of just a few micrometres, even at high power levels. This achieves a measuring speed up to five times faster than sources with reflection targets. The unique monoblock design in open construction, in which the tube head, the high-voltage generator and the vacuum pump form a single unit, enables very low maintenance costs with an unlimited service life.

Other benefits

The maintenance interval of the monoblock tubes is 12 months, usually the service is carried out as part of the annual maintenance of the machine

The open design means that wearing parts of the source can be replaced if necessary

The compact design enables use in all machine types, even those that require very little space

The acceleration voltage can be increased from 130 kV to 160 kV at a later date without having to replace the source

With an acceleration voltage of 200 kV or 230 kV, workpieces with a higher density and longer radiographic lengths can also be measured

Long-life components guarantee high availability even in multi-shift operation

Technical Data

Technical Data

Acceleration voltages

up to 160 kV, 200 kV or 230 kV

Tube power

up to 80 W or 140 W
