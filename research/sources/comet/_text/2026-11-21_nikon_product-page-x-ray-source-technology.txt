Product Highlights
Unique 225kV Rotating.Target 2.0

Rotating.Target 2.0 allows continuous operation up to 450W with up to 3 times higher resolution for the same power, or 3 times faster data collection for a given resolution.

Unique 450kV Microfocus Source

Nikon’s 450kV/450W microfocus X-ray source is the only one in the world to offer high energy combined with a high-resolution focal spot size of 80µm, allowing inspection of dense components.

Superior X-Ray Source

Nikon’s market-leading Xi Nanotech X-ray microfocus source is unique due to its exclusive integral generator design and unparalleled 160kV maximum energy and 20W true-target power.

Core Features

Low Cost of Ownership
All Nikon's X-ray sources are open-tube design, which allows an unlimited lifecycle, with user-replaceable filament cups, offering a low cost of ownership.

One Source, 5 Target Options
Select the right target for your application: from the transmission target with sub-micron feature recognition, to the world's only Rotating.Target 2.0 for outstanding performance or the 320kV target for denser samples

Unique X-ray Target Optimization
The unique multi-metal target allows users to change the X-ray emission profile for analysis of lower density materials. The target can be changed between tungsten, silver, molybdenum, and copper in seconds without breaking the vacuum.
