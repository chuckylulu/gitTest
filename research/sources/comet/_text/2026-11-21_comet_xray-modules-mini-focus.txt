Speed and accuracy

Quality control of parts for aircraft and space vehicles

iXRS HP 15 series

Robust inline CT
The iXRS XP modules are ideal choice when uptime and high throughput are of utmost importance.
The modules are dedicated to the most demanding inline CT applications optimizing power performance, focal spot size, and field of view. The X-ray tubes include two identical and thermally separated focal spots.

Typical applications
Inline inspection for Automotive and 
Aerospace parts
100% inspection of battery packs and other critical components where reliability is of utmost importance
