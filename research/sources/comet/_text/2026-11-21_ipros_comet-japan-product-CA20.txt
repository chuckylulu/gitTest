[For Semiconductors] CA20: High-Speed X-ray CT Inspection

Non-destructive high-speed inspection of micron-sized details!

In the semiconductor industry, defect detection in the manufacturing process is essential to ensure product quality and reliability. Particularly as miniaturization progresses, even slight abnormalities in internal structures can significantly impact product performance. The CA20 addresses the challenges of 3D ICs in semiconductor packaging and enables rapid defect detection. This contributes to improved yield and shortened development periods.

【 Application Scenarios 】
■ Internal structure inspection of semiconductor packages
■ Defect detection in solder joints
■ Inspection of wafer-level packaging (WLP)

【 Benefits of Implementation 】
■ Quality assurance of products through non-destructive testing
■ Cost reduction through early detection of defective products
■ Shortening of development cycles

Inspecting the Future.
Comet Yxlon carries on the legacy of the industrial X-ray business originally developed by Philips, through the X-ray division of Yxlon International, founded in 1998.
Headquartered in Hamburg, Germany, Comet Yxlon specializes in the development and manufacturing of X-ray inspection systems, serving customers worldwide.
In 2007, the company became part of the Swiss technology group Comet, and in 2022, rebranded from YXLON to Comet Yxlon.

Comet Yxlon provides industrial X-ray CT inspection systems and related services for non-destructive testing (NDT) and failure analysis.
Our product lineup ranges from high-resolution nano-focus inspection systems to high-power, large-scale CT systems, enabling us to deliver customized solutions that meet a wide range of customer needs.
Our systems are widely used across industries such as automotive, semiconductors and electronics, aerospace, and defense.
We support our customers with innovative products and services that empower them to shape the markets of the future.
