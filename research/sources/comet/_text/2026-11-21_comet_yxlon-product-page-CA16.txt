CA16 | 2D and 2.5D X-ray inspection for flip chips and wafer bumping

CA16

Submicron 2D and 2.5D X-ray inspection
for flip chips 
and wafer bumping.

The CA16 addresses key challenges in the bump inspection of flip chips and wafers. Through 2D and 2.5D X-ray imaging combined with AI-powered automatic image analysis and metrology, the system identifies critical defects with submicron precision. With inline capability and direct process feedback, the CA16 increases yield, and accelerates time-to-market

From lab to fab. 
Whether you need the highest quality images to inspect your parts in a Lab/R&D environment, or you require a fully-automated solution that easily fits into your existing production process, the CA16 is available in a variety of configurations to suit your needs. 

MXI applications

The CA16 MXI/Lab configuration is designed to provide fast, high resolution images of single layer bumps for manual inspection, for example C4 bumps and BGA's. For the fastest scan time, 2D X-ray provides rapid results, while 2.5D X-ray captures images at a tilted angle to provide additional depth information. 

2.5D X-ray imaging clearly shows defects like bridged bumps

2D X-ray allows for rapid evaluation of wafer bumps for critical defects such as voids

AXI applications

With the addition of an  EFEM loader for 300mm wafers, and our AI-powered software packages for automatic image analysis and metrology, the CA16 in AXI/Fab configuration automaitcally identifies and measures defects in both bumps and TSVs and feeds these insights back directly into your processes for improvement. 

AI-powered metrology clearly identifies and measures voids in a 2D X-ray image of a wafer with solder bumps (50 μm – 30 μm single layer)*.
*Test parts designed for Comet by Fraunhofer IZM-ASSID, they contain defects for illustrative purposes

User friendly recipe set-up, including simple configuration of production limits, easy test and validation and import/export between systems

Software Solutions

From software tools that rapidly detect and analyze the size and severity of defects, such as voids and bridges, to solutions that ensure delicate components remain shielded from harmful X-rays, the CA16 is equipped with a range of advanced software solutions across our three pillars of Clarity, Efficiency and Insights. Explore just a few of these below.  

1. Clarity
Image acquisition
Gain highest image data quality: See more details of your inspection samples by capturing brilliant 2D and 2.5D images. 

Clarity Packages

Our Clarity Packages for 2D and 2.5D  image aquisition are tailor-made for inspection with the CA16 system. Due to the precise manipulation of the CA16, its dedicated semi X-ray imaging chain and the powerful 2D volume reconstruction software, they allow for excellent resolution and capture of the finest details.
Explore our entire software suite

2. Efficency - Connectivity & management
Increase your productivity: Streamline workflows between systems, software and people through seamless connectivity and automation.    

Dose Manager

The Dose Manager package continuously monitors and tracks the X-ray dose that the sample is subjected to, taking into account the tube power and the distance to the sample. To avoid damage to sensitive components such as flash and DRAM memories, it switches off the X-ray automatically once the dosage limit is reached. The package includes a Dose Reduction Kit with a collimator and 4 filters, shielding parts outside the region of interest from radiation. 
Explore our entire software suite

Batch Manager / Wafer Manager

Loading multiple parts at the same time by using a multi-part holder can significantly increase your throughput. The Batch Manager package allows you to automatically inspect parts in a grid by applying the same inspection sequence in a grid of a multi-part holder. Deviations of the sample positioning are corrected without user interaction. 
For wafer inspection applications, our newly released Wafer Manager package automatically aligns wafers for inspection in seconds. Users can define the wafer map manually, or use our intuitive wizard. With the wafe rmap, you can quickly select the relevant dies for your inspection and the system will perform the same inspection task on each to standardize your inspection process.
Explore our entire software suite

Improve product and process quality: Extract critical information from image data to stay ahead of your market.    

CoW Insights X 2D*

CoW Insights X 2D is our specialised software for the CA16 equipped with a fully-automated inspection package specifically designed for wafer level packaging. It provides fast, sufficient quality 2D image acquisition, and ensures early defect detection and process control for fine-pitch micro bumps down to 20μm on wafers
