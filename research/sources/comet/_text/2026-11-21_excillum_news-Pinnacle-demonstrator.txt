Excillum and Pinnacle establish demonstrator for high-speed X-ray inspection

Together with US-based Pinnacle X-Ray Solutions LLC., Excillum continues to push the boundaries with unmatched high-speed CT inspection for non-destructive testing and manufacturing process improvements. The demonstration system in Atlanta, Georgia, is available for customers in industries such as automotive and aerospace to explore manufacturing quality improvements and cost reduction potentials. The capability of this groundbreaking technology enables it to be used for at-line, in-line and end-of-line quality inspection, and to support ramp-up of production volumes in e.g. battery cell manufacturing.

Unmatched throughput and resolution computed tomography (CT) X-ray system made available for the North American NDT (non-destructive testing) and electric vehicle (EV) battery manufacturing market.

The tailored system using the Excillum MetalJet X-ray source promises high-speed quality control to reduce recalls and improve yield.

Made for at-line, in-line and end-of-line inspection, and for ramp-up of production volumes.

Excillum and 
Pinnacle
 have established a demonstrator CT system for inspection of batteries and other components using 
the Excillum MetalJet X-ray technology
. The demonstrator, located in Atlanta, GA, will be used to commercialize high-speed CT inspection in collaboration with Pinnacle’s customers.

“We are impressed with Pinnacle’s professionalism and efficiency in setting up the CT demonstrator in the US and attracting users to the site. We look forward to supporting American battery cell manufacturers,” says Fredrik von Hofsten, Business Development Manager at Excillum.

Pinnacle’s Chief Product Officer, Dirk Steiner, adds: “We are very excited to be able to have access to this unique technology, and the first results show very impressive image quality. We believe that the high X-ray flux and stability of the focal spot will open many doors in our industry. Our customers in North America now have the opportunity to see what this technology can do for their applications even outside the battery industry. “

Fredrik von Hofsten, Excillum

About Pinnacle X-Ray Solutions LLC

Pinnacle X-Ray Solutions LLC, is a leader in design, manufacturing, fabrication, and integration of industrial digital X-Ray (2D) and computed tomography (3D) equipment. Equipment is used to inspect the worlds most advanced hardware in aerospace, space, defence, automotive, and numerous other industries. Our headquarters in Duluth, Georgia, USA has been integrating advanced custom, in-line, at-line, and standard system solutions since 2014. Together with our portfolio of companies, Willick Engineering, and PiXL (Pinnacle industrial X-Ray laboratory) we have set the new benchmark in this industry.
