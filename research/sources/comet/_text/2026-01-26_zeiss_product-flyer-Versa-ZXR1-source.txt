When it comes to non-destructive 3D imaging, ZEISS Versa X-ray microscopes (XRM) provide you with unique, advanced capabilities
from resolution to reconstruction. As the pioneer and global leader in X-ray microscopy, ZEISS serves the world’s largest XRM
installed base, trusted by researchers in over 7,000 scientific publications*.


The VersaXRM 730 flagship X-ray microscope offers the highest resolution performance with ultimate spatial resolution <450 nm,
while maintaining better than 500 nm resolution across the entire operating range from 30 – 160 kV. Enjoy sub-one-minute
tomographies with continuous motion scanning.
