Excillum and Baker Hughes’ Waygate Technologies announce technology collaboration for high-resolution X-ray computed tomography

By

Ingrid Aksnes
May 6, 2025
November 3rd, 2025
No Comments

Excillum, the global leader in the field of advanced microfocus and nanofocus X-ray sources, and 
Waygate Technologies
, a Baker Hughes business and global leader in nondestructive testing (NDT) solutions for industrial inspection, today 
announced a technology collaboration
. At Control Messe 2025 in Stuttgart, Germany, Waygate Technologies unveiled its new extremely high-resolution computed tomography (CT) system, the Phoenix Nanotom® HR, which incorporates the 
Excillum NanoTube N3 X-ray source
.

Excillum NanoTube N3 is incorporated into Waygate Technologies’ Phoenix Nanotom
®
 HR.

NanoTube N3, the nanofocus X-ray tube, enables higher resolution based on 300 nm focal spot technology across the full voltage range of 40-160 kV.

Based on the collaboration, Waygate Technologies and Excillum plan to continue to deliver new CT technology in the future to transform how industries approach inspection and quality assurance.

“We are excited to present our Phoenix Nanotom® HR here at Control 2025, and disclose our strategic collaboration with Excillum,” said Ludovic Milosevic, General Manager 
Radiography Systems
 at Waygate Technologies. “In part thanks to Excillum’s nanofocus source, the new HR version delivers up to five times better resolution than our state-of-the-art Nanotom M. That puts it on par with advanced optical magnification scanners – but with a simpler system, faster learning curve, greater flexibility, and at a better price point than comparable solutions”.

“At Excillum, we are dedicated to advancing X-ray technology to enable new possibilities for our partners and customers,” adds David Lindblom, CEO of Excillum. “Our collaboration with Waygate Technologies is a testament to our shared commitment to innovation and ability to push the boundaries of what is possible in industrial CT and NDT.”

David Lindblom, CEO Excillum

About Waygate Technologies:

Waygate Technologies 
is part of the Inspection portfolio within the Baker Hughes (NASDAQ: BKR) Industrial & Energy Technology business segment. As an industrial inspection solutions provider and world leader in nondestructive testing (NDT), we ensure safety, quality and productivity. We combine more than 125 years of experience and a collection of heritage product brands including Krautkrämer, Phoenix, Seifert, Everest and Agfa NDT. Today, hundreds of brands in the electronics, energy, battery, automotive, aviation, space exploration, and additive industries trust our technologies. We drive digital transformation through a broad portfolio of award-winning solutions in industrial radiography and computed tomography (CT), remote visual inspection (RVI), ultrasound (UT), and robotic inspection.
