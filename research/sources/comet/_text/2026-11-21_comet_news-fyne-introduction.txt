FYNE – the X-ray source for high-tech industries

April 2, 2025

Comet is proud to introduce the FYNE 160 NF4, our high-end open X-ray source designed for demanding high-resolution applications such as those of the semiconductor and electronics industries. Developed by our X-ray Modules team, FYNE is a breakthrough in precision inspection, offering nanoscale imaging capabilities for the growing volume of smaller, more powerful components. Read on to discover how FYNE is setting a new standard for high-resolution inspection.

Why FYNE, why now?
Increased technological requirements are reshaping numerous industries towards advanced manufacturing processes requiring ever-higher levels of precision. The rise of AI, smart manufacturing and miniaturization demands inspection tools that go beyond conventional techniques.
FYNE is the response to these evolving industry needs – delivering next-generation quality testing for high-resolution applications such as advanced packaging and microelectronics . With its nanoscale imaging capabilities, superior stability, and extended lifespan, FYNE redefines what’s possible for X-ray inspection, empowering manufacturers to achieve higher yields and faster time-to-market.

FYNE in a nutshell
Fast nanoscale inspection
: 400 nm resolution, optimized dose settings.
Stable imaging
: Reliable imaging and exceptional focal spot stability.
Maximized uptime
:10× longer lifespan than previous open tube technology.

Innovation through collaboration

FYNE’s development was a joint effort across R&D, Production and Market facing teams, ensuring that the module meets both the highest industry standards and the cutting-edge requirements of the market. These diverse teams worked together closely  throughout the development process – testing, refining, and optimizing FYNE to ensure it delivers the necessary reliability and performance for demanding production environments.
Collaboration is a core value at Comet, and this project exemplifies our commitment to working alongside our partners to push the limits of technology. By aligning our expertise with industry needs, we create solutions that truly drive progress.

Strengthening our presence in the semiconductor industry

With the launch of FYNE, Comet reaffirms its dedication to advancing semiconductor inspection and supporting our partners in the industry. This milestone underscores our strategic focus on high-growth markets and our commitment to delivering the cutting-edge tools that enable the next generation of technology.
As we continue to push the boundaries of what’s possible in X-ray inspection and beyond, FYNE represents not just a product, but a step forward in precision, efficiency, and reliability – helping our customers build the future with confidence.
