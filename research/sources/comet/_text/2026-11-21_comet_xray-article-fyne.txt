Introducing FYNE: the high-end solution

February 3, 2025

Meet FYNE – the newest innovation in open X-ray tube technology, designed specifically for high-resolution applications. As AI and smart manufacturing reshape the tech landscape, manufacturers face increasing pressure to deliver better, faster solutions while optimizing yield and time-to-market. FYNE is our response to these challenges, redefining what’s possible for X-ray inspection. Discover how it can transform your manufacturing processes:

Advanced technology for challenging inspection
As technology reshapes the industrial world, the demand for smaller and higher-performing components is growing at an unprecedented rate. But with increased complexity manufacturers face new challenges: managing thermal behavior, improving production yields, and ensuring reliability in designs. These demands require inspection tools that go beyond traditional methods.
X-ray technology provides unmatched precision in detecting hidden defects, allowing manufacturers to achieve higher yields and faster time-to-market That’s why we are proud to launch FYNE – the next generation in quality testing for high-resolution applications.

What makes FYNE so fine?

Fast nanoscale inspection 
Reliably delivering 400 nm resolution balanced with optimized dose settings, FYNE provides precise inspection of the most advanced samples without sacrificing efficiency.

Superior focal spot stability
Reliable nano-scale imaging goes together with great source stability. The FYNE module ensures exceptional focal spot stability during extended imaging sessions, meaning you can operate with peace of mind.

Maximized uptime 
Advanced emitter technology extends the lifespan by 10 times compared to our previous open tube technology, enabling thousands of hours of uninterrupted inspection and maximizing operational uptime.

Focus topic. Semiconductor segment insights

November 10, 2026

The semiconductor industry has mastered the art of precision. Nanometer-scale processes, extensive testing, and sophisticated control systems create an environment that appears highly predictable. But while process control continues to improve, many of today's most critical defects are becoming harder to see as they disappear inside complex, multi-layer structures. As advanced packaging reshapes the industry, visibility itself is emerging as a decisive factor in manufacturing success.
