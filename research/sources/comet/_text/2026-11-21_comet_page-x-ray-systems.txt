X-ray Systems

X-ray Solutions

Under the Comet Yxlon brand, we develop X-ray and CT solutions that enable non-destructive testing for a variety of industries, with a focus on the semiconductor, electronics and automotive sectors.

               To Comet Yxlon's Website

At a glance
Our X-ray systems division designs and produces radioscopic and CT inspection solutions for a broad variety of industrial applications and fields. Whether in the semiconductor, electronics, automotive or aerospace industry, our customers are among the largest manufacturers in the world – major enterprises that place their confidence in our outstanding quality. Our roots go back to the discovery of the X-rays by Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen in 1895 and the construction of the first X-ray tube by Carl Heinrich Florenz Müller in Hamburg in 1896.
