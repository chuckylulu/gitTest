Xenocs integrates Excillum’s MetalJet F X-ray source into its product portfolio

By

Ingrid Aksnes
August 10, 2026
No Comments

Excillum, a global leader in extreme brightness microfocus and nanofocus X-ray sources, and Xenocs, a global leader in nanoscale characterization solutions for materials science, are proud to announce the integration of Excillum’s new 
MetalJet F
 liquid-metal-jet source into Xenocs’ product portfolio.

Excillum’s MetalJet F12 integrated as X-ray source in Xenocs’ Xeuss Pro SAXS instrument.

MetalJet F12 complements the range of X-ray sources already supported by Xenocs, providing an option for applications that require high X-ray brightness and long-term stability such as advanced scattering, X-ray imaging, and SAXS/GISAXS/WAXS.

A Xenocs Xeuss Pro with MetalJet F12 will be available at Xenocs’ demonstration laboratory in Grenoble, France to customers and academic partners for evaluation of the source’s performance with on-site support from Xenocs’ application team.

“We are pleased to add the MetalJet F12 to the portfolio of sources we support,” said Frederic Bossan, Managing Director of Xenocs. “Our goal is to offer our customers a broad and balanced choice of X-ray technologies, so that they can select the solution that best fits their own applications. We look forward to welcoming visitors to our demo lab in Grenoble to experience the capabilities of the MetalJet F12 alongside our other offerings.”

“We are proud to see the MetalJet F12 integrated into Xenocs’ portfolio,” commented David Lindblom, CEO of Excillum. “Xenocs has a strong reputation in X-ray characterization, a well-established customer base, and is a key partner since more than 10 years. Our MetalJet provides the brightest laboratory X-ray source available for SAXS, maximizing sample throughput while delivering superior signal-to-noise ratio.”

David Lindblom, CEO Excillum

Martin Norrefeldt, Business Development Manager

About Xenocs

Xenocs headquartered in Grenoble, France, designs, develops, and manufactures X-ray characterization instruments — including SAXS/WAXS, GISAXS and Imaging technology — serving academic institutions and industrial customers worldwide.
