Semicon West 2024

Our takeaways

Preparing for the future. That was Semicon West 2024.

July 16, 2024

Reflecting on Semicon West 2024, we are thrilled by the incredible experiences and connections made over the past few days. Our team was on-site to meet with valued partners and discuss how cutting-edge technology can support future front-end and back-end semiconductor manufacturing processes. We believe that preparing for the future also means investing in people. That's why we conducted many activities in parallel to prepare the workforce of tomorrow and demonstrated our commitment to the future in a broader sense. 

Read on for our key takeaways.

Nurturing new talent to close the industry's workforce gap
As the semiconductor industry continues to advance at an unprecedented pace, addressing the talent gap is crucial for sustaining its competitive edge. This year, we took proactive steps on three fronts:

20 Under 30 Award
At the Semicon West 
20 Under 30
 Award Breakfast, we recognized young leaders who not only exemplify excellence and innovation but also inspire future generations to explore semiconductor careers. We are proud of them. Read the special message we addressed to them 
in a social media post.

Workforce Development Pavilion
Our HR team was present on-site and was impressed by the bright, ambitious talents who are eager to start their careers. As sponsor of the WFD Pavilion, we are glad to contribute to building an inclusive and equitable talent pipeline in the semiconductor industry.

Keynote “Fostering a Dynamic Workforce"
Echoing Semicon West's theme of "Stronger Together," our keynote by Isabella Drolz, VP Marketing & Product Strategy at our X-ray Systems Division, sparked insightful discussions on addressing the talent gap.

Advanced Packaging quality testing with cutting-edge X-ray technology
As Moore's law drives the miniaturization and complexity of microchip structures, new inspection technologies are essential. Our 
CA20
 X-ray solution, designed for the semiconductor industry, enables non-destructive testing of Advanced Packaging structures. At our booth, the interactive Discovery Desk demonstrated the advantages of X-ray inspection, capturing the interest of many chip manufacturers. We proudly showcased 
Comet Yxlon
’s revolutionary contribution to the semiconductor market, alongside high-resolution X-ray tubes from 
Comet X-ray
.

Comet Plasma Control Technologies
’ solutions enhance production efficiency. 
Synertia

We were delighted by the interest in our products and look forward to strengthening relationships with partners.

Final thoughts
As we conclude our journey at Semicon West 2024, we are filled with inspiration and a renewed commitment to innovation and growth in the semiconductor industry. While addressing the talent gap remains a challenge, we are optimistic about the bright minds ready to make their mark and contribute to 
make the future, better

From EPFL to semiconductor innovation in action: Naïla’s 6-month R&D internship at Comet

October 22, 2026

Finding an industrial internship that directly applies advanced physics can be a challenge for university students. When Naïla, a Master’s student in Applied Physics at EPFL (Swiss Federal Technology Institute of Lausanne), joined Comet for her internship, she stepped straight into real-world R&D. Over six months with the Design Verification Testing (DVT) team for RF-Generators, she characterized plasma chambers, debugged complex RF matchboxes, and discovered an unexpected scientific result in plasma dynamics. Here is her journey from campus theory to cutting-edge semiconductor engineering.
