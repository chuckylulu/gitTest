Our history

FYNE – The Future of Nano Focus X-ray Technology

2025                

In 2025, Comet X-ray unveils FYNE, its latest nano focus X-ray module, setting a new benchmark for ultra-high-resolution imaging. Designed for applications demanding extreme precision, FYNE enables the detection of the smallest defects in semiconductors, electronics, and advanced materials.
Its cutting-edge nano focus technology ensures unparalleled clarity, making it an essential tool for quality assurance and research. By introducing FYNE, Comet X-ray once again pushes the limits of non-destructive testing, offering customers the most refined imaging capabilities available. This milestone underscores our ongoing mission to provide the most advanced X-ray solutions for the industry's most critical challenges.

Xplorer Sealed Micro Focus – A New Standard in Precision

2024                

Engineered for superior stability and maintenance-free operation, Xplorer delivers consistently high image resolution with minimal downtime. Its sealed tube technology enhances longevity while eliminating the need for frequent recalibration, ensuring uninterrupted workflow and cost savings for customers.
Ideal for electronics and other inspection jobs requiring high resolution the Xplorer series empowers industries to perform highly detailed inspections with greater efficiency. This innovation strengthens Comet X-ray's position as a leading supplier in high-resolution non-destructive testing solutions.

ECO portable X-ray system wins Red Dot Design Award

2024                

With the introduction of the design awarded ECO low-weight portable X-ray series in 2023, Comet X-ray addressed the growing demand for mobile, high-performance inspection solutions. Designed for field applications, this series combines lightweight construction with robust performance, allowing for easy transport and operation in challenging environments.
The ECO series ensures fast, reliable imaging without compromising on quality, making it ideal for industries such as oil and gas, shipbuilding, and infrastructure maintenance. By enhancing mobility and reducing operator strain, this milestone reinforces our dedication to providing user-friendly, high-precision solutions that meet the evolving needs of on-site inspections.

Revolutionizing X-ray Inspection with MesoFocus

2022                

In 2022, Comet X-ray introduced the revolutionary MesoFocus X-ray tubes and modules, bridging the gap between traditional micro and mini focus technologies. This breakthrough delivers enhanced resolution and power, optimizing the inspection of dense and complex materials.
MesoFocus technology enables customers to achieve superior image clarity while maintaining efficiency, making it ideal for applications in battery inspection, additive manufacturing, and aerospace. By pioneering this innovation, Comet X-ray redefined non-destructive testing for traditional and new non-destructive testing application such as the inspection of EV batteries.

Expansion with FXE Open Micro Focus Series

2021                

Comet X-ray expanded its portfolio in 2021 with the FXE open micro focus series, reinforcing the commitment to precision and innovation. Designed for applications requiring high-resolution imaging and detailed defect detection, the FXE series delivers superior image quality with exceptional reliability.
By broadening our product range, we provide customers with more tailored solutions, empowering industries such as electronics, aerospace, and automotive to achieve higher inspection accuracy and efficiency. This milestone marks a significant step in our mission to advance non-destructive testing capabilities and the mission to the number one one-stop-shop for industrial X-ray modules.

Comet X-ray launches the PXS EVO 300D/1200

2020                

Now Comet X-ray delivers more power for the most challenging field inspection jobs - in the PXS EVO design, you know and trust.
The new PXS EVO 300D/1200 features a superior combination of 300 kV and 1200 W constant potential X-ray power for extreme performance, making it ideally suited for all high-performance field inspection jobs.

YXLON Copenhagen becomes Comet X-ray

2020                

The Copenhagen location has been a part of COMET since 2007 and is now fully integrated into the COMET X-ray Modules Division.
The name-change cements COMET X-ray's dominance as the leading X-ray tubes and modules provider in the industrial X-ray market.

Comet launches the ION series

2020                

The ION integrated X-ray series is tailored for X-ray scanning applications and ideal for industries such as food processing, sorting, thickness gauging, and security in particular.
The ION series X-ray system consists of a tube head module with integrated cooling and the high voltage generator and an industrialized control unit with power supply, safety, and control modules. It's a flexible and scalable technology platform customized to specific customer needs.

Comet launches the high-voltage iVario generator

2017                

Comet launched the high-voltage iVario generator in 2017, further strengthening its position as the leading one-stop shop for industrial X-ray modules.

SMART EVO wins top prize at the Red Dot award

2015                

SMART EVO from YXLON Copenhagen is now an award-winning top achievement after being presented with the prestigious Red Dot Award; Best of the Best in Product Design 2015.
Best of the Best is the highest individual achievement in the Red Dot Award. 2014                

YXLON Copenhagen heralds a new era of smarter workflows for your portable X-ray applications with the global launch of the SMART EVO series.
YXLON Copenhagen has developed the new SMART EVO industrial X-ray system. Built in Denmark to stringent specifications with the highest quality components, the products are built to last and operate accurately even in the harshest environments.

A milestone in metal-ceramic x-ray technology

2008                

Production of 600 kV metal-ceramic X-ray tube modules began in 2008. It is a game-changing milestone in metal-ceramic X-ray technology. The high-power 450 kV Compactus series was launched soon after, and VarioFocus modules were launched in 2009.

Innovative and stable during the world economic crisis

2008                

With the presentation of its new high-power product line at the 17th World Congress on Non-destructive Testing in Shanghai in 2008, COMET underscored its leading position in industrial X-ray technology.
E-beam technology was recognized with the Swiss Technology Award in 2009. With its compact, low-voltage electron emitter, COMET developed a top-quality product suitable for sterilizing packages in in-line processes.
In 2010, COMET emerged even stronger from one of the most severe global financial crises ever, thanks to its systematic implementation of the Group strategy, improved productivity, and cost optimization efforts.

Comet turnover reaches chf 1 million

1955                

Turnover reached CHF 1 million in 1955, and the company posted its first net profit. After some delays, the company began producing rotary anode X-ray tubes under a license from its partner in 1956.
More than 90% of production is exported, and the company accounts for 1.5% of the world market in medical X-ray tubes for medical applications.
ETH Zurich University is involved in the company's Panoramix project, and several patent applications result from this work.

ANDREX aps was founded in Copenhagen

1954                

In 1954, Holger Andreasen began trading with X-ray units. The units were named ANDREX, which referred to his last name ANDREasen and to X-ray. Aage Andreasen won a large order for 25 units of 160 kV for the United States Air Force and opened a small factory at Islands Brygge in Copenhagen. In 1955, the first real ANDREX portable X-ray system was released.
Over the following years, the factory in Copenhagen was enlarged, and more employees were recruited as production and sales grew at a steady pace. More than 30 types of tubes from 80 kV to 300 kV and over 20 different control units were developed.
In 1970 the first line of mobile X-ray products was manufactured in Copenhagen.

Comet industrial x-ray is founded

1948                

Comet AG is founded by Gerhard Steck in Berne-Liebefeld, Switzerland in 1948. It will produce fixed anode X-ray tubes for medical applications.
The company leases several floors in a brand-new factory building ideally located in a well-developed area.
Switzerland lacks the highly qualified specialists that the new company needs, so it hires skilled glassblowers and vacuum specialists from East Germany and Italy and hires a young X-ray engineer from France.
The company aims to ensure it develops a highly skilled workforce, hiring only the very best professionals from the very beginning.

The beginning of X-ray technology

1895                

On November 8th, 1895, Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen discovered the rays he called "X-rays." Later on the rays were renamed to "Röntgenstrahlen" in German in his honor, though the original designation has lasted to this day in English.
Based on Röntgen's discovery, Carl Heinrich Florenz Müller, a native Thuringian glass-blower and factory owner developed the first X-ray tube in Hamburg in 1896.
