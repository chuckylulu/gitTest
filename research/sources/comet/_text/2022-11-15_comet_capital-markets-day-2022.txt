Delivering the energy for plasma processes




•   Important steps during semiconductor manufacturing like adding, removing or modifying material are done using plasma.
•   Comet PCT’s products are generating and controlling high frequency process energy.
•   The amount of energy, the envelope and the way it is controlled has a very strong influence on the plasma process.
