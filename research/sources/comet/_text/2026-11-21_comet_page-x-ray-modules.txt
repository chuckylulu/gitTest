X-ray Modules

Our Comet X-ray Modules division develops and manufactures highly compact x-ray source and portable x-ray modules for non-destructive testing, metrology and airport security inspection.

               To Comet X-Ray Website

At a glance
Comet Industrial X-Ray Modules develops and manufactures metal/ceramic X-ray sources, and the related components, for the non-destructive examination of materials in the electronics, automotive, aerospace, pipeline and steel industries, and also for the stationary and mobile inspection of baggage and cargo at airports and borders.
