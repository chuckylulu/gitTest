Important information regarding
AGM on April 23, 2020
As a result of the exceptional situation around the coronavirus outbreak,
and in reliance on section 6a (1) (b) of Ordinance 2 on Measures to Fight
the Coronavirus (as amended March 16, 2020), Comet has decided to hold
the Annual Shareholder Meeting of April 23, 2020 without the physical
presence of shareholders.


All shareholders are asked to issue a written or electronic proxy to the
independent proxy with their voting instructions. Further details will follow
with the meeting notice.
