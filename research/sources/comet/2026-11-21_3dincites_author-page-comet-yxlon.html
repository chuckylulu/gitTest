About Comet

Comet Yxlon is a global leader in the development and manufacture of high-end X-ray-based inspection systems across a range of industries, with special focus on semiconductors. With solutions designed for easy operation and high throughput, they are ideal for both lab and production environments, and can be operated manually, semi-automatically, or adapted to a fully automated setup. Comet Yxlon’s collaborative approach to product development is driven by a passion for technology and a curiosity that leads to asking the right questions for their customers, resulting in inspection solutions that go beyond cutting-edge hardware, with dedicated software that serves three specific areas of interest: "Clarity" for detail-rich image acquisition, "Efficiency" for smart, connected solutions that boost productivity, and "Insights" for powerful analysis solutions. All three ensure that customers see better, faster and more.

Comet Yxlon's CA20 inspection solution is specially designed to tackle the challenges of Complex 3D IC production. It offers incredible resolution for the inspection of micron-sized details and harnesses AI-driven image analysis to detect even the smallest critical defects, like voids, bump shift, or head-in-pillow, with reliable precision to drive yield. The CA20 reveals complex structures and provides data-driven insights to accelerate time-to-market and time-to-volume at a scale and speed that were previously unattainable.

Comet Yxlon’s Life Cycle Service offers comprehensive system support with scalable service packages. From user training to maintenance, repair and spare parts, or upgrades and updates for a future-proof investment, the company's modular system design and upgrade concept allows users to adapt their inspection system to their changing requirements. With sales and service locations in North America, China, Korea, Japan, and Taiwan, a partner network in around 50 countries, and a completely digitized service organization, Comet Yxlon is directly on-site with its customers everywhere.
