The VLSI Workshop sessions are key elements of the 2025 Symposium on VLSI Technology & Circuits program. The Workshop sessions will be held during the VLSI Symposium in person. The topics and scope of the Workshops are merging research and application, which has not been covered in detail in the technical program of the symposium and could be the future new areas of the symposium.

*Details will be announced later.

VLSI Symposium Workshops

Anyone who register for the main symposium or Short Course can participate in the VLSI Symposium Workshops (Fee is included/no separate registration required)
