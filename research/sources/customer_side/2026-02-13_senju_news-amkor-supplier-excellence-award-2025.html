Senju Metal Industry Co., Ltd. receives Supplier Excellence Award 2025 from Amkor Technology, Inc.

February 13, 2026

On December 9, 2025, Senju Metal Industry Co., Ltd. has received the Supplier Excellence Award 2025 from Amkor Technology, Inc.
Selected from among more than 3,000 suppliers worldwide, this award recognizes outstanding performance and commitment to quality.
 
Amkor Technology, Inc. is a semiconductor manufacturing company headquartered in the United States with production facilities in South Korea, the Philippines, the United States, China, Taiwan, Malaysia, Portugal, Japan, and Vietnam, and is one of the world's leading OSAT (outsource assembly and test) companies.
