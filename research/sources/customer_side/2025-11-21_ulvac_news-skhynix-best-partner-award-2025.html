ULVAC Receives "Best Partner Award" from SK hynix ―Recognized for Continuous Commitment to Technical Excellence and Quality Improvement

ULVAC, Inc. and ULVAC KOREA, Ltd. (hereafter collectively referred to as "ULVAC") received the "Best Partner Award" from SK hynix Inc. (referred to as SK hynix), a leading semiconductor manufacturer in Korea, at the "2025 Partners Day" event held on September 29, 2025.

This award recognizes ULVAC's advanced technological capabilities and continuous efforts to improve quality, as demonstrated by its ENTRON sputtering system, which was qualified as POR* within SK hynix's semiconductor manufacturing process.

ULVAC has earned strong trust from its customers through close partnerships, with a consistent focus on both technological innovation and quality assurance.

In this award, the following three aspects were expecially recognized:

  • Ensuring Technical Reliability: The sputtering system "ENTRON" demonstrated process reliability and uniform quality, earning POR qualification.
  • Enhancing Technological Capabilities through Group Collaboration: The ULVAC Group strengthened its collaborative framework, promoting unified efforts across product development, performance verification, and customer support.
  • Establishing a Pre-evaluation System: Evaluation systems and verification processes were implemented at the Technology Center PYEONGTAEK in Korea to ensure stable quality from the early stages of development.

"The path to developing new markets and technologies is never easy. However, I believe that maintaining the determination to keep challenging ourselves―no matter the difficulties―is what truly matters. This achievement is the result of the dedication and passion of our entire team working together. For ULVAC, which has faced many challenges and setbacks over the years, delivering a project for SK hynix represents a major milestone. It proves that continuous effort ultimately leads to success. We will continue to move forward with the spirit of challenge and collaboration, supporting each other's growth and striving for even greater achievements," said Seung-Su Choi, President of ULVAC Korea, Ltd.

ULVAC will take this award as an opportunity to further strengthen collaborative development with customers and strive to establish a stable technological foundation in the semiconductor market.

Building on this achievement, ULVAC will continue to embrace new challenges and pursue sustainable growth as an organization that creates value together with customers.

* POR (Process of Record): The process by which specific equipment or materials are officially qualified and adopted within semiconductor manufacturing.
