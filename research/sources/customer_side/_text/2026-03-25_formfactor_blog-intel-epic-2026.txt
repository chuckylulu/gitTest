FormFactor Named a 2026 Intel® EPIC Supplier Award Winner

March 25, 2026
This recognition reflects FormFactor’s ongoing focus on quality, operational excellence, and collaboration. Our teams remain dedicated to maintaining high standards and continuously improving processes to meet demanding performance expectations.

FormFactor has been recognized as a 
2026 Intel® EPIC Supplier Award winner
, one of Intel’s highest honors for supplier excellence. The Intel EPIC Supplier Award recognizes a select group of suppliers across Intel’s global supply chain for outstanding quality, performance, and commitment to continuous improvement.

About the Intel EPIC Supplier Award

The Intel EPIC Supplier Award is Intel’s top supplier recognition, awarded annually to companies that demonstrate exceptional performance, high quality standards, and a strong focus on continuous improvement. Each year, only a limited number of suppliers worldwide receive this distinction.

FormFactor was named among the 2026 award recipients as part of Intel’s official announcement in the Intel Newsroom.

Commitment to Quality and Continuous Improvement

This recognition reflects FormFactor’s ongoing focus on quality, operational excellence, and collaboration. Our teams remain dedicated to maintaining high standards and continuously improving processes to meet demanding performance expectations.

Learn More

Intel announced the full list of 2026 Intel EPIC Supplier Award recipients in its official Newsroom release.

Read Intel’s official press release: 
https://newsroom.intel.com/corporate/intel-announces-2026-epic-supplier-award-recipients
