These techniques utilize reduced sampling plans, thus managing to achieve faster run rate. When used for C2W, a subset of selected measurement points often results in sparse and limited alignment information. The implementation of 100% overlay measurement is needed as a crucial tool to advance varying manufacturing aspects of heterogeneous integration. However, this typically comes at the cost of throughput. Departing from the traditional stepwise procedure of \"move-acquire-measure,\" a new metrology concept is introduced to accomplish image acquisition on the move, using predefined alignment key positions, exact image capture timing, and high precision stage control, to achieve high overlay metrology throughput with 100% chiplet inspection. This paper will highlight the development of C2W overlay metrology at Intel, with an emphasis on its roadmap towards different hybrid bonding scaling generations.
