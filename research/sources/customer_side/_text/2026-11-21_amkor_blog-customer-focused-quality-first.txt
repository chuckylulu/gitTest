Putting the Customer at the Center of Quality

November 17, 2026 in 
 by 
Marcom

			Share:

Amkor’s evolving 
Quality Policy
 strengthens our focus on customer needs, continuous improvement and accountability. In an industry of increasing complexity and evolving requirements, quality is fundamental to trust.

At Amkor, that responsibility is reflected in the evolution of our Quality Policy from 
Quality First
 to 
Customer-Focused Quality First
. The change reinforces a simple principle: quality begins with the customer and is delivered through the actions of every employee.

Building Quality Into Every Decision

Customer-Focused Quality First builds on Amkor’s longstanding commitment to quality by placing greater emphasis on understanding and anticipating customer needs. Quality extends beyond meeting product requirements. It means identifying potential challenges early, addressing issues effectively and continually improving the way we work.

We demonstrate this commitment through three customer-focused pillars:

Proactive Customer Approach
: Understanding and meeting customer needs, anticipating risks before they become issues, and applying creative thinking to challenge norms.

Clear Communication
: Keeping communication transparent and open with our customers and stakeholders.

Commitment to Excellence
: Solving problems at their source through disciplined analysis, driving customer satisfaction with speed of execution, and taking ownership until results are verified and sustained.

These actions strengthen prevention, reduce the potential for recurrence and support reliable execution that consistently meets our customers’ expectations.

A Commitment Shared Across Amkor

Delivering consistent quality requires the involvement of people across our global organization.

Employees contributed their perspectives as the Quality Policy evolved, helping shape a shared understanding of customer-focused quality. That engagement continued through World Quality Month, bringing employees together around customer focus, accountability and continuous improvement.

While responsibilities differ across sites and functions, each employee has a role in the quality of what we deliver.

Strengthening Customer Trust

As semiconductor technologies advance, maintaining a strong quality culture requires us to continually improve alongside them.

Customer-Focused Quality First provides a common direction for that effort. It guides how we identify risks, solve problems and take responsibility for delivering results our customers can depend on.

By keeping the customer at the center of our decisions and continually improving how we work, we strengthen the trust that supports lasting customer relationships and Amkor’s long-term success.
