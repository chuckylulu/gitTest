                                Summary
• The rapid expansion of AI technology introduces several challenges
  in testing, particularly in thermal management.

• To effectively manage thermal challenges, integrated solutions
  combining the tester, probe card, and chuck are essential.

• With successful AI chip testing, AI in turn makes us more capable
  and efficient.
