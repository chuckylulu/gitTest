One-Team Spirit: SK hynix’s Journey to HBM Leadership

June 24, 2025

SK hynix did not become the HBM market leader overnight. From developing TSV technology, which laid the foundations for HBM, in 2009 through to supplying samples of HBM4 in 2025, the company has embarked on a long journey to the top. Find out how SK hynix’s unique “one-team” culture helped them get there.

To read the full story, visit the SK hynix Newsroom:
