Unlocking transformative possibilities with our supply chain partners

Ben Tessone

Published: 10/31/2025

Last week we proudly celebrated Micron’s annual 
Supplier Awards
, recognizing the top-performing partners who delivered above and beyond to help make our fiscal 2025 a record year.

This year we celebrated outstanding performance in new categories and were delighted to recognize several multi-year winners. We raised a toast to 14 award recipients, an exceptional group of partners that have set the bar for cost, quality, delivery, partnership, and innovation.  Their contributions were critical in fueling Micron’s technology leadership, driving significant growth in AI-driven product revenue, and supporting our expanding global footprint.

Our partnerships are pivotal in driving resilience, earning industry recognition, and reinforcing our position as a customer of choice. By pairing exceptional supplier performance with a commitment to deeper collaboration, we’ve delivered measurable improvements in business outcomes and strengthened our leadership within the semiconductor ecosystem. As AI accelerates at an unprecedented pace, these alliances are crucial to unlocking more transformative possibilities. Together, we’re capturing this momentum and shaping the future of memory and storage.

A heartfelt thank you and congratulations to our award winners, and to every supplier helping power Micron’s journey forward.

SVP Global Procurement & Supply Chain, Chief Procurement Officer

Ben Tessone

Ben Tessone is the SVP of Global Procurement & Supply Chain, and Chief Procurement Officer at Micron Technology, responsible for enabling supply, maximizing value, and building trust to strengthen Micron’s competitive advantage. He has over 20 years of procurement and supply chain experience in the semiconductor industry. Under Ben’s leadership, the team is on a transformational AI-First journey to build critical capabilities in end-to-end supply chain excellence, strategic cost management, sustainability, and risk resilience.
