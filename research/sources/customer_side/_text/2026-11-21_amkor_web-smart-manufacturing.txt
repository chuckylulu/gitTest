 Smart Manufacturing

Intelligent Manufacturing. Operational Excellence. Customer Success.

Amkor’s Industry 4.0 strategy is focused on creating a smart, connected manufacturing environment that drives innovation, enhances productivity, and delivers exceptional value to both Amkor and our customers.

Our vision
 is to be the industry-leading OSAT provider, leveraging automation, data-driven intelligence, and advanced manufacturing technologies to set the standard for innovation, efficiency, and operational excellence.

Our mission
 is to transform manufacturing by integrating Industry 4.0 principles and smart factory technologies, enabling greater efficiency, agility, quality, and productivity across our global operations.

Why Industry 4.0? The Five Pillars of Our Transformation

Our commitment to Industry 4.0 is built on five core pillars that enable smarter, more connected manufacturing across Amkor’s global operations. These pillars guide how we leverage automation, data, and digital technologies to enhance quality, improve efficiency, and increase operational agility. By embedding these capabilities throughout our factories, we deliver greater value to our customers through improved performance, faster decision-making, and a more resilient supply chain.

Quality

We ensure sustainability, stability, and reliability through process optimization and superior defect detection and management, resulting in higher yields and greater customer confidence.

Productivity

We relentlessly improve yield, cycle time, and equipment utilization through strategic automation, comprehensive workforce training, and a culture of continuous innovation.

ESG

We are committed to reducing our carbon footprint through energy efficiency, waste reduction, and a strong ethical framework that values diversity and community engagement.

Data-Driven Decisions

We leverage real-time data and advanced analytics (FDC, Big Data) to enable faster, more accurate decisions, improving process control and risk management.

Cost Competitiveness

We achieve superior cost structures through optimized processes, intelligent supply chain management, and the use of digital twins for simulation and efficiency gains.

Industry 4.0 Capabilities & Solutions

Amkor deploys a sophisticated suite of technologies to drive efficiency, quality, and innovation.

Advanced Automation & Robotics

Automatic Material Handling Systems (AMHS):
 Includes Overhead Hoist Transport (OHT), Automated Guided Vehicles (AGV), and Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMR) for hands-free, 24/7 material transfer.

Warehouse Automation (AS/RS):
 Automatic storage and retrieval systems for accurate, real-time inventory management and 24/7 operation.

Mobile Solutions:
 An all-in-one mobile platform for real-time transactions, data entry, and collaboration, improving work efficiency by 30%.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Automated Defect Classification (ADC):
 AI-powered vision systems for inspection, reducing AOI operators by 30% and significantly improving yield and cycle time.

Predictive Maintenance (PdM):
 Real-time sensor monitoring and anomaly detection using AI/ML to predict failures, reduce downtime, and lower maintenance costs.

Data Analytics & Big Data:
 A scalable platform (Google BigQuery, TIBCO Spotfire) for real-time visibility and analytics to improve OEE, yield, and decision-making.

AI 365 Copilot:
 An AI-powered tool integrated into Microsoft 365 to enhance office productivity and automate tasks.

Operational Technology & Cybersecurity

CIM-Controlled Equipment:
 Centralized management for recipe/program loading, machine monitoring, and process control.

End-to-End Traceability (ULT):
 Provides complete visibility from wafer to unit, including process steps, materials, and equipment.

FDC & SPC:
 Real-time Fault Detection and Classification (FDC) combined with Statistical Process Control (SPC) for immediate quality control and root cause analysis.

OT/IoT Cybersecurity:
 A robust, multi-layered security framework including asset visibility, network segmentation, vulnerability management, and threat detection to ensure operational resilience.

Amkor’s Automation Strategy: A Deeper Dive

At Amkor, every Industry 4.0 and automation initiative is strategically aligned with our business objectives to deliver measurable value for our customers. We apply a disciplined governance framework that ensures projects are scalable, sustainable, and capable of driving long-term operational excellence. By establishing clear performance metrics and accountability throughout implementation, we continuously measure success against defined KPIs.

Our approach to technology selection is equally deliberate; we identify the right solution for each challenge, balancing innovation, cost-effectiveness, and maintainability to create reliable, high-performance systems that enhance quality, efficiency, and operational uptime across our global manufacturing network.

To maximize business impact, we prioritize automation investments based on proven ROI and their ability to improve key business outcomes. Each project is evaluated against both tangible benefits, such as labor productivity, energy efficiency, and material optimization, and intangible benefits, including product quality, customer satisfaction, and workplace safety. A critical component of our Smart Factory journey is the development of an automation-driven culture.

Through continuous workforce training and skill development, we empower employees to leverage advanced technologies, accelerate innovation, and drive successful digital transformation. This combination of technology, governance, and people enables Amkor to consistently deliver smarter, more agile manufacturing solutions for our customers.

Amkor’s Automation Roadmap

Smart Factory Automation: A Multi-Approach Strategy

Amkor’s Smart Factory strategy combines automation, connectivity, and advanced analytics to create a more intelligent and efficient manufacturing environment. By integrating processes, data, logistics, and shop-floor automation, we are driving greater visibility, productivity, quality, and operational agility across our global operations. 

These capabilities are further strengthened by emerging technologies such as AI, machine learning, digital twins, and advanced robotics, enabling the next generation of semiconductor manufacturing..

Process Automation

Auto EQ & Online Connection:
 Equipping all equipment for seamless connectivity.

Storage Control:
 Intelligent storage management on the factory floor.

Validation & Process Control:
 Automating validation steps and process control for consistent quality.

User Interface:
 Digitalization of all process control and transaction interfaces.

Data Automation

Traceability:
 Full lot and unit-level traceability (ULT) from start to finish.

Paperless Operations:
 Replacing physical documents with online lot travelers and log sheets.

Auto SPC & Data Collection:
 Automatic collection of operational and equipment log data.

Carrier Management:
 Intelligent management of material carriers.

Logistic Automation

Digitalization:
 End-to-end digital management of logistics.

Automated Warehousing (AS/RS):
 Automated storage, retrieval, and inventory control.

Material Transport:
 Automated transport from the warehouse to the manufacturing floor.

Shop Floor Automation

Data Collection & Analytics:
 Automatic data gathering and centralized storage for reporting.

AMHS (OHT, AGV, AMR):
 Advanced Automated Material Handling Systems for process-to-process transportation.

Planning & Simulation:
 Advanced systems for full automation, including Auto Packing and Inventory Management.

Advanced Technologies

AI / Machine Learning:
 Expanding the scope of AI/ML applications, including advanced Predictive Maintenance (PdM) and Automated Defect Classification (ADC).

Humanoid Robotics:
 Piloting humanoid robots for logistics and manufacturing tasks to address labor challenges.

Digital Twins:
 Creating virtual replicas of our factories for simulation, optimization, and predictive analysis.
