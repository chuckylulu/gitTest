Amkor Recognizes Henkel Adhesive Technologies with Supplier Excellence Award

April 17, 2025 in 
Company News
 by 
Ramon Lizarraga

			Share:

Amkor’s Robert Lanzone, Corporate VP of System in Package (SiP) BU, presented Henkel Adhesive Technologies with the prestigious Amkor Supplier Award. This recognition celebrates Henkel’s outstanding support and significant contributions to helping us deliver exceptional solutions to our SiP customers. Their technical expertise, responsive service, and innovative approaches have been instrumental to our mutual success.

Amkor deeply appreciates Henkel’s unwavering dedication, commitment to excellence, and alignment with our mission. Strong partnerships are vital to our ongoing leadership in advanced semiconductor packaging and testing services.

Congratulations to the entire Henkel team on this well-deserved recognition. We look forward to continuing our valuable collaboration and achieving greater success together.

Learn more about Amkor’s SiP Packaging solutions at 
https://amkor.com/sip
