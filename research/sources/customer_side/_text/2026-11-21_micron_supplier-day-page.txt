Micron Supplier Day

This year’s Supplier Day provided an incredible opportunity for representatives from over 130 of our top suppliers to come to Boise, Idaho, and see our expanding headquarters up close. Their contributions and close partnership are critical to our ability to scale AI, as we jointly innovate, invest in critical supply chains, and meet the increasing demand for memory and storage. 

                Scaling the Future of AI Together

As AI accelerates, Micron and its suppliers are scaling together to build the future of memory, storage, and innovation.
