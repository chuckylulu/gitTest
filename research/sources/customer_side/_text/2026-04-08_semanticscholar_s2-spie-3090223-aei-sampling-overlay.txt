As technology nodes continue to shrink, stress differences at the Peri\u2013Array boundary become more pronounced, leading to localized wafer deformation and nonlinear overlay registrations. Since ADI marks are absent in these regions, only AEI measurements can capture this device-level effect. Therefore, it is critical for MTD to account for these differences. However, because MTD accuracy depends on both AEI and ADI sampling, random AEI sampling risks missing these boundary regions, and results to that MTD failing to capture overlay variations caused by these boundary regions. To address this, we proposed a robust AEI sampling strategy that prioritized patterns exhibiting significant AEI\u2013ADI deltas. Firstly, candidate intra-field sampling sets were generated with symmetric coverage, including more than 800 device patterns that differed from scribe-line marks. For each candidate AEI sampling, we computed the model accuracy of AEI and selected the AEI sampling with the best model accuracy. Secondly, to mitigate suboptimal production MTD, we simulated ideal conditions by applying AEI = ADI + (optimal MTD - used MTD). The AEI sampling based on this intrafield sampling method has been demonstrated to improve overlay accuracy and better capture the device-level behaviors, providing a more representative and robust AEI control methodology."}
