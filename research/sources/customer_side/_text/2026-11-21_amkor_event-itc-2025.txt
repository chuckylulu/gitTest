ITC 2025

Amkor Technology is pleased to invite you to the 
International Test Conference (ITC) 2025
, taking place from September 21 to 26 in San Diego, California, at the Hilton San Diego Bayfront.

Vineet Pancholi, Sr. Director, Manufacturing Test Technologist, will present “
Best Practices for Testing 2.5D Chiplet Package Designs.
” His presentation will explore innovative testing approaches for advanced 2.5D chiplet architectures, highlighting methodologies that ensure reliability and performance while addressing the unique challenges of heterogeneous integration and multi-chip configurations.

When: 
September 21, 2025 - September 26, 2025

Where: 
Hilton San Diego Bayfront

Location: 
San Diego, California
