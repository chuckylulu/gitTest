산업용 원자현미경

Advanced Packaging

As semiconductor packaging architectures evolve — from CoWoS and SoIC to hybrid bonding, chiplet integration, and panel-level packaging — nanoscale precision is essential throughout the manufacturing process. Park Systems’ automated AFM platforms provide high-resolution characterization of bonding surfaces, redistribution layers (RDL), TSV structures, thin films, and overlay alignment features with exceptional accuracy and repeatability.
