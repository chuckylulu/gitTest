Bruker ProteoScape™

Bruker ProteoScape™ (BPS) aims to be a comprehensive proteomics data analysis platform utilizing the concept of real-time data streaming monitoring of system suitability. It provides analysis of dda-PASEF (including de novo sequencing with BPS Novor) and dia-PASEF workflows with TIMS DIA-NN and TIMScore to increase the accuracy and confidence in the determining FDR.  BPS is a combined hardware and software solution for existing and anew timsTOF customers.
