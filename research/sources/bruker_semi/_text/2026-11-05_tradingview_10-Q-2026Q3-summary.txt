Bruker 3Q 2026: Revenue $874M, EPS ($0.81) — 10-Q Summary
Less than 1 min read
BRKR
Bruker’s third-quarter revenue rose 1.6% year over year, while its net loss widened. Revenue growth came mainly from BSI BIOS and BEST; higher gross profit and cost savings were offset by goodwill impairment charges and increased minority-investment impairment.

Business Highlights
Revenue by segment
: BSI BIOS revenue grew 1.5% and BEST revenue rose 11.2%; BSI NANO revenue declined 1.3%.
Gross profit
: Rose to $429.3M from $379.4M; cost savings, lower restructuring costs and favorable revenue mix contributed.
Goodwill impairment
: A $174.5M charge followed lower expectations for the BSB&CA reporting unit amid competitive changes and results below expectations.
Segment reorganization
: Effective July 1, Bruker reorganized its reportable segments to streamline its structure and support decision-making and customer focus.
