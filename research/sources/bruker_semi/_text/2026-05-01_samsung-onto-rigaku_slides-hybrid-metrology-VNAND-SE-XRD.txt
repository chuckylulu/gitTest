Conclusions and Future Work

 Summary

 • X-ray diffraction enables structural monitoring beyond optical SE for deep VNAND.

 • MGML improves agreement with reference measurements toward production targets.

 • Continuous scanning stabilizes signals and supports shorter, production-ready recipes.



 Future Work

 • Further reduce scan time while maintaining robustness.

 • Extend the approach to more complex 3D architectures.

 • Integrate more advanced model-guided analysis.
