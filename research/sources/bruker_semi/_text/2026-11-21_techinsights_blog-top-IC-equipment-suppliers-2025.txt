Top IC Manufacturing Equipment Suppliers in 2025: Market Share, Rankings, and Growth Drivers

July 17, 2026

The global IC manufacturing equipment market reached $136.2 billion in 2025
, growing 13% year-over-year as continued AI investment and expanding semiconductor manufacturing capacity supported equipment spending. While overall growth remained strong, regional policy developments, AI-driven investment, and currency movements continued to shape competitive conditions across the industry.

The latest TechInsights market analysis examines the suppliers leading the industry, the forces driving market growth, and the competitive trends shaping the global IC manufacturing equipment market.

Figure 1 – The global IC manufacturing equipment market reached $136.2 billion in 2025, growing 13% year-over-year (Source: 
TechInsights
)

Who Were the Top IC Manufacturing Equipment Suppliers in 2025?

ASML extended its position as the market leader, generating $27.5 billion in revenue, extending its leadership as the world's only supplier of extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography systems. Applied Materials, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron, and KLA completed the top five, underscoring the continued dominance of wafer fab equipment suppliers within the broader market.

While the top five IC manufacturing equipment suppliers remained unchanged between 2024 and 2025, the market forces influencing competitive performance continue to evolve. AI-driven investment, public-sector investment, trade restrictions, and broader geopolitical developments continue to shape the competitive landscape.

What's Driving Growth in the IC Equipment Market?

The largest share of market growth came from wafer fab equipment, which 
contributed $11.8 billion of the market's $15.6 billion annual increase
. Although smaller than the wafer fab equipment segment, the test equipment segment grew at a higher percentage.

Investment remains closely tied to the rapid expansion of AI infrastructure and the manufacturing capacity required to support advanced semiconductor production. Public-sector investment programs have also contributed to continued spending on new fabrication facilities.

Regional dynamics also influenced supplier performance. Chinese equipment vendors benefited from opportunities created as foreign suppliers reduced their presence in the Chinese market following US-led export restrictions.

Why These Trends Matter for Strategy Teams

Annual supplier rankings are only the starting point. Strategy and finance teams also need to understand which equipment segments are growing, how regional market dynamics are influencing competition, and the factors driving investment across the semiconductor manufacturing ecosystem.

This overview highlights only a portion of the trends shaping the IC manufacturing equipment market.
