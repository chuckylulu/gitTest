Park Systems Named as Global Leader in AFM Market by MarketsandMarkets

News provided by

                  Park Systems Corp.

Aug 30, 2025, 03:52 ET

Share this article

Share this article

SUWON, 
South Korea
, 
Aug. 30, 2025
 /PRNewswire/ -- According to the recent 
"Atomic Force Microscopy Market – Global Forecast to 2030"
 report published by 
MarketsandMarkets™, 
Park Systems holds the highest global revenue share in the atomic force microscopy (AFM) market. The report projects that the global AFM market will grow from 
USD 513.6 million
 in 2024 to 
USD 762.2 million
 by 2030, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 7.1%.

Continue Reading

Introducing Park Systems

The report identifies Park Systems as a "
Star Player
," citing its combined leadership in both revenue and product footprint. The company's proprietary True Non-Contact™ Mode technology enables high-resolution imaging without damaging delicate samples, which is particularly effective for applications in semiconductors, materials science, and life sciences. Its automated AFM solutions cater well to both industrial quality control and academic research, according to the report.

The 
2024 Company Evaluation Matrix
, a quadrant analysis of key industry players in the report, highlights Park Systems' leadership. Positioned in the upper-right quadrant labeled "Star Players," Park Systems stands alone as the only company to lead in both revenue and product footprint. The matrix evaluates players based on several parameters such as R&D efforts, product offerings, consumer perception index, partnership networks, geographic presence, annual revenues, and recent strategic activities—areas where the report recognized as outperforming its competitors.

The company has expanded its technological breadth through strategic acquisitions. Most notably, the integration of Lyncée Tec SA, a Swiss innovator in Digital Holographic Microscopy (DHM), strengthens Park Systems' position as a one-stop provider of multimodal metrology solutions.

This merger reflects Park Systems' long-term vision of building an interconnected ecosystem for hybrid nanoscale analysis, where AFM, DHM, and imaging spectroscopic ellipsometry (ISE) technologies work together to provide more comprehensive insights.

MarketsandMarkets, in its latest analysis, recognizes Park Systems not only as the current leader in AFM revenue but also as a company with a broad product footprint and recognized technological strengths in nanoscale metrology. With a strong global presence and an expanding portfolio through strategic acquisitions, the company is well positioned to drive broader adoption and applications of atomic force microscopy across a wide range of industries, the report notes.

About Park Systems Corp. (KOSDAQ: 140860)

Park Systems is a global leader in atomic force microscopy (AFM) and advanced nano-metrology systems, providing high-resolution, non-destructive metrology solutions for semiconductors, displays, materials, life science, and data storage. Known for its True Non-Contact™ technology and automation capabilities, the company delivers superior accuracy and ease of use. With recent acquisitions of Accurion (ISE, AVI) and Lyncée Tec (DHM), Park Systems is expanding into next-generation optical metrology. Headquartered in 
South Korea
, it operates a global network across 
Asia
, 
Europe
, and the Americas. Learn more at 
www.parksystems.com
.
