                                Why X-Ray?
Ø X-ray photons penetrate through materials and enable to investigate inner
  structure from the size of sub-nanometer (atomic scale) to micrometer of the
  materials, non-distractively.

 ü X-ray Diffraction/Scattering
                     1. HR-XRD : Composition and thickness of epitaxial layers
                     2. X-ray reflectivity : Thickness and stacking structure of multilayers
                     3. Topography : Crystal defects, dislocations
                     4. GI-SAXS : Nanoimprint mold, EUV resist structures, FinFET, GAA, etc.
                     5. T-SAXS : 3DNAND, DRAM structures


 ü X-ray Imaging
                     1. µ m Structure : TSV, Packaging, etc.
                     2. nm Structure : Device scale imaging
