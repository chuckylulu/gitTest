Item 8.01.	Other Events.

As previously announced, effective July 1, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), Bruker Corporation (the “Company”) completed a reorganization of its reportable segments to streamline its organizational structure and further facilitate decision making, accountability, customer focus, and innovation. Prior to the Effective Date, the Company had the following four reportable segments: (i) Bruker Scientific Instruments (“BSI”) BioSpin, (ii) BSI CALID (Chemicals, Applied Markets, Life Science, In Vitro Diagnostics, Detection), (iii) BSI NANO, and (iv) Bruker Energy & Supercon Technologies (“BEST”).

As a result of this reorganization, as of the Effective Date, the Company’s reportable segments consist of the following four reportable segments:

•

 BSI BIOS
, consisting of:

o
The BioSpin division, which combines the former Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy, Preclinical Imaging, Biopharma and Applied Services, and Services and Lifecycle Support divisions, all of which were previously part of the former BSI BioSpin segment;

o
The SciY (formerly Integrated Data Solutions) division, previously part of the former BSI BioSpin segment; 

o
The Chemspeed Technologies (“CST”) (formerly Automation) division, previously part of the former BSI BioSpin segment;

o
The Daltonics division (formerly Bruker Life Sciences Mass Spectrometry and Bruker Applied Mass Spectrometry), previously part of the former BSI CALID (Chemicals, Applied Markets, Life Science, In Vitro Diagnostics, Detection) segment; 

o
The Optics division, previously part of the former BSI CALID segment; and

o

The Mimetas division, a new division formed during the third quarter of 2026 following the acquisition of a majority interest in Mimetas B.V
.

•

BSI Microbiology and Infection Diagnostics (
“
BMID
”
)
, consisting of the Microbiology, Molecular Diagnostics, and Biomedical Systems divisions, which were previously part of the former BSI CALID segment.

•

BSI NANO
,

•

BEST, 
for which no changes were made to the reportable segment structure in connection with the reorganization.

For information purposes and to provide investors with historical information on a basis consistent with its new reporting structure, the Company has provided recast reportable segment information that conforms to the new reporting structure. This reportable operating segment realignment did not impact the Company’s historical consolidated results of operations, financial position or cash flows.

Attached as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated by reference in this Item 8.01 are schedules containing recast reportable segment information for the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, the three months ended March 31, 2025 and 2026, the three and six months ended June 30, 2025 and 2026, and the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025. The recast information has been derived from, and should be read in conjunction with, the Company's previously filed Annual Reports on Form 10-K for the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, and Quarterly Reports on Form 10-Q for the periods ended March 31, 2025 and 2026, June 30, 2025 and 2026, and September 30, 2025, in which the corresponding financial information was originally reported.

As noted above, the segment changes and supplemental disclosures discussed above have no impact on the Company’s historical consolidated results of operations, financial position or cash flows. The recast financial information contained in Exhibit 99.1 does not represent a restatement of the Company’s previously issued financial statements.
