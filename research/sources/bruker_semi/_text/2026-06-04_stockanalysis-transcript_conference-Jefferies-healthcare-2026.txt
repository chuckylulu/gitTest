 Summary

Strong order growth in Q1 was led by semiconductor and international academic/government markets, while U.S. funding delays persist. Biopharma and security segments are robust outside the U.S., and cost-saving actions are driving margin improvements, with double-digit EPS growth expected through 2027.

We feel like we're kind of moving to pivoting to another period of growth for Bruker.

Very encouraged to see what we see in Europe, and in particular in China, on the biotech side.
