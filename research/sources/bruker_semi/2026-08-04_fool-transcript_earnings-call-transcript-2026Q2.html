Bruker (BRKR) Q2 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Semiconductor metrology and energy research orders surge over 50% organically.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Aug 11, 2026 at 12:40PM EST

+Fool.comon

Image source: The Motley Fool.

DATE

Tuesday, Aug. 4, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. ET

CALL PARTICIPANTS

  • Director of Investor Relations - Joe Kostka
  • President and Chief Executive Officer - Frank Laukien
  • Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer - Gerald Herman

TAKEAWAYS

  • Revenue -- $838.5 million for Bruker Corporation (BRKR -9.08%), growing 5.2% year over year and reflecting a return to organic growth during the second quarter.
  • Organic Revenue Growth -- 2.8%, which reaches 3.4% when excluding the impact of U.S. tariff refunds.
  • Scientific Instruments (BSI) Bookings -- 10% organic growth year over year, representing the fourth consecutive quarter with a book-to-bill ratio above 1.0.
  • Non-GAAP Gross Margin -- 52.1%, an expansion of 350 basis points compared to 48.6% in the second quarter of 2025.
  • Non-GAAP Operating Margin -- 14.1%, a 510-basis point increase from 9.0% in the prior year, driven by cost-saving actions and a favorable volume mix.
  • Non-GAAP EPS -- $0.49, a 53% increase from $0.32 in the second quarter of 2025, supported by operational efficiency and tariff refund benefits.
  • GAAP Results -- Diluted loss per share of $0.41, including a $134.9 million noncash goodwill impairment charge related to automation and spatial biology businesses.
  • Biopharma Bookings -- Growing more than 20% organically in the second quarter, reflecting demand for NMR, X-ray, and mass spectrometry solutions.
  • Semiconductor Metrology -- Greater than 50% organic order growth in the second quarter, driven by demand for high-bandwidth memory and advanced packaging for AI scaling.
  • Deep Tech Performance -- Energy research organic orders rose over 50% in the second quarter and over 100% in the first half of 2026, including multiyear orders for fusion energy and high-energy physics.
  • Cost Savings -- $30 million delivered in the second quarter toward an annualized goal of $140 million in cost reductions for fiscal year 2026.
  • Fiscal Year 2026 Revenue Guidance -- $3.54 billion to $3.57 billion, representing reported growth of 3% to 4% and organic growth of 1% to 2%.
  • Fiscal Year 2026 Non-GAAP EPS Guidance -- $2.10 to $2.15, an increase of 15% to 17% over fiscal year 2025.
  • Fiscal Year 2026 Operating Margin Outlook -- Expansion of 250 to 300 basis points year over year, primarily driven by cumulative cost-saving actions.
  • Currency Impact -- Projected foreign exchange tailwind for the full year was lowered from 1.5% to 0.5% due to a stronger U.S. dollar.
  • Q3 2026 Outlook -- Organic revenue expected to be flat to slightly up, reflecting a $20 million shift in semiconductor revenue from the third to the fourth quarter.
  • Q4 2026 Expectations -- Implied revenue of $1.02 billion to $1.04 billion, with meaningful increases in organic growth and margins driven by higher volume and ultra-high field NMR installations.
  • Regional Performance -- Organic revenue grew approximately 10% in both the Americas and Europe, while Asia Pacific revenue declined in the low double-digit percentage.
  • China Market Dynamics -- Revenue declined in the low double-digit percentage, but academic and government orders in the region grew more than 20%.
  • Semiconductor Metrology Profitability -- This business unit reported an EBIT margin of approximately 30% with organic revenue growth greater than 15% in the first half of 2026.
  • New Operating Structure -- Adoption of the Bruker Biosystems Group is expected to drive an additional $20 million in cost reductions for fiscal year 2027.
  • Net Leverage -- 2.8x as of June 30, 2026, based on improved EBITDA and profitability in the second quarter.

RISKS

  • Herman stated, "Our top line results in the second quarter of '26 were unfavorably impacted by U.S. tariff refunds and a stronger U.S. dollar, which translated into less tailwind on revenue in total, approximately 110 basis points," noting the headwind to reported growth.
  • Laukien stated, "U.S. academic orders still remained weak in Q2," identifying a continued soft demand environment in domestic research funding.
  • Herman stated, "$135 million related to our automation and spatial biology businesses, which continued to experience operating losses," explaining the primary driver of the noncash goodwill impairment charge.

SUMMARY

Management reported that Bruker Corporation returned to organic revenue growth in the second quarter, establishing a new organizational structure to integrate biosystems and diagnostics workflows. The company prioritized investments in deep tech sectors like semiconductor metrology and energy research, where demand for artificial intelligence scaling and fusion development drove significant order activity. Despite persistent weakness in certain academic funding environments, management focused on multiyear cost reductions and facility rationalization to enhance operational profitability. The strategic shift toward connected life science workflows is intended to align with customer needs in post-genomic drug discovery, multiomics, and specialty diagnostics.

  • CEO Laukien noted that deep tech tools, including semiconductor metrology and energy research, now account for approximately 15% of the company's portfolio.
  • Management integrated the BioSpin, Daltonics, and Optics divisions into a new Biosystems Group to address the "growing need for scientific integration in the post-genomic era."
  • CEO Laukien stated that visibility in the semiconductor sector for the next six quarters "seems excellent" due to AI-driven orders for memory and advanced packaging.
  • CFO Herman indicated that for the fourth quarter, the company expects "meaningful sequential and year-over-year increases in organic revenue growth" due to significantly higher volume and favorable mix.
  • CEO Laukien observed that deep tech orders typically have longer delivery times of 9 to 24 months, resulting in revenue lagging order growth by several quarters.
  • Management established the Bruker Microbiology and Infection Diagnostics group to focus on molecular diagnostics, hospital hygiene, and antimicrobial resistance testing.
  • CFO Herman confirmed the company remains on track to deliver $140 million in annualized cost savings in 2026, with $30 million realized during the second quarter alone.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • BEST: Bruker Energy & Supercon Technologies, the segment focused on superconducting materials and advanced tools for energy research.
  • BSI: Bruker Scientific Instruments, the segment comprising life science, microbiology, and nanoanalysis tools.
  • FTIR: Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy, an analytical technique used to obtain an infrared spectrum of absorption or emission of a solid, liquid, or gas.
  • MALDI Biotyper: A mass spectrometry-based platform used for the rapid identification of microorganisms like bacteria, yeasts, and fungi.
  • Multiomics: A biological analysis approach that combines data sets from various "omics" fields, such as proteomics and metabolomics, to understand complex biological systems.
  • NMR: Nuclear Magnetic Resonance, a high-resolution imaging and analytical technique used to determine the structure and dynamics of molecules.
  • Sepsis Reflex Testing: A diagnostic process where a positive initial test automatically triggers a subsequent, more detailed test to identify specific pathogens or resistances.
