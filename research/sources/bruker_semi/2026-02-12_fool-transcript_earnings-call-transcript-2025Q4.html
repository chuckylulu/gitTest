Bruker (BRKR) Q4 2025 Earnings Call Transcript

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Updated Feb 12, 2026 at 11:01AM EST

+Fool.comon

Image source: The Motley Fool.

DATE

Thursday, February 12, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. ET

CALL PARTICIPANTS

  • President and Chief Executive Officer — Frank Laukien
  • Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer — Gerald N. Herman
  • Director of Investor Relations — Joe Kostka

TAKEAWAYS

  • Q4 Revenue -- $977.2 million, flat year over year, with a 4.1% currency tailwind, 0.8% M&A growth, and 5.1% organic revenue decline.
  • Organic Revenue Decline Q4 -- 5.1% in both Bruker Scientific Instruments (BSI) and BEST segments, net of intercompany eliminations.
  • Q4 Non-GAAP Operating Margin -- 15.7%, down 240 basis points year over year, driven by lower volume, unfavorable mix, tariffs, and currency headwinds, partially offset by $25 million in cost savings from 2025 initiatives.
  • Q4 Non-GAAP Diluted EPS -- $0.59, down from $0.76, with a reported tax rate of 29.9% versus 32.5%.
  • Q4 Free Cash Flow -- $207.3 million, up $54 million compared to Q4 2024, with operating cash flow of $230 million, the highest in company history.
  • Weighted Average Diluted Shares Q4 -- 171.7 million, up 13% due to the September 2025 mandatory convertible preferred stock offering.
  • 2025 Full-Year Revenue -- $3.44 billion, up 2.1%, with a 2.3% currency tailwind, 3.5% contribution from acquisitions, and 3.7% organic revenue decline.
  • 2025 Full-Year Non-GAAP Operating Margin -- 12.6%, down 280 basis points year over year, with a combined 280 basis points of headwind from M&A, tariffs, foreign exchange, and lower volume impact.
  • Aftermarket Revenue -- Rose to 38% of BSI revenues in 2025 from 35% in 2024, growing organically.
  • Segment Performance 2025 -- BioSpin declined mid-single digits (offset by Chemspeed lab automation growth and NMR weakness); CALID constant exchange rate revenue grew at a high-single-digit percentage (led by ELITech and Optics, offset by softness in mass spectrometry); Bruker Nano declined low-single digits, as spatial biology and biopharma growth were offset by academic and industrial market declines; BEST revenue down mid-single digits, with semiconductor metrology revenue flat.
  • Order Book and Backlog -- BSI book-to-bill remained above 1.0 for two consecutive quarters; 2026 begins with approximately seven months of revenue in backlog.
  • BEST Multiyear Orders -- Over $500 million in superconducting wire orders from MRI manufacturers received at the end of Q4 2025 and early 2026 for multi-year delivery, plus more than $40 million in Extreme Light Infrastructure orders expected to convert to revenue mostly late in 2026.
  • Geographic Revenue Trends Q4 2025 -- Americas down low-teens percentage; Europe down high-single digits; Asia-Pacific up high-single digits, with China achieving double-digit growth in Q4 and now representing under 14% of total revenue (formerly 16%-17%).
  • Guidance 2026 -- Reported revenue of $3.57 billion to $3.60 billion, representing 4%-5% reported growth; organic revenue growth of 1%-2%; M&A contribution of 1.5%; estimated 1.5% currency tailwind; non-GAAP operating margin expansion of 250-300 basis points; non-GAAP EPS guidance of $2.10 to $2.15, indicating 15%-17% growth (8% FX headwind implies 23%-25% constant exchange rate EPS growth).
  • Q1 2026 Outlook -- Anticipates mid-single-digit percentage decline in organic revenue, with meaningfully lower operating margin and EPS compared to Q1 2025 due to tough comparisons and lagging tariff/exchange headwinds.
  • Cost-Saving Initiatives -- 2025 actions expected to exceed $120 million, with updated annualized cost reductions for 2026 closer to $140 million, fully effective by Q3.
  • Backlog Normalization Target -- Management references a five-and-a-half month normal backlog for BSI longer term, suggesting gradual normalization may take place in 2026.
  • Ultra-High-Field NMR Systems -- Only one gigahertz-class NMR installation expected to contribute revenue in 2026, down from two in 2025 and four in 2024; potential for two to three annual installations beginning 2027.
  • Aftermarket Revenue Mix -- BSI aftermarket component revenue at 38% for 2025, up from 35% prior year, and expected to be an ongoing growth area.

RISKS

  • 2025 saw organic revenue decline of 3.7% due to headwinds from academic funding, tariffs, and currency, which management called "a difficult year 2025 with headwinds from academic funding, tariffs, and currencies."
  • Non-GAAP operating margin in Q4 2025 came in 100 basis points below expectations due to unfavorable mix, delayed tariff offsets, and stronger-than-expected foreign exchange impact.
  • Management expects a mid-single-digit organic revenue decline and "operating margin and EPS to be down meaningfully compared to Q1 2025" in the first quarter of 2026 due to a challenging prior-year comparison and slow recovery in academic/government funding.
  • U.S. academic and government markets remain weak, with bookings down "in the high teens," and revenues from this segment expected to decline organically in 2026.

SUMMARY

Bruker Corporation (BRKR -9.08%) reported flat revenue performance in the fourth quarter, with organic declines across all operating segments, while currency and acquisitions provided modest offsets. Management introduced 2026 guidance with low-single-digit organic sales growth, benefiting from significant cost reductions and major multiyear orders in superconducting solutions, while continued operating and EPS gains hinge on execution of structural margin improvements. Near-term caution remains for Q1, as comp-driven declines in organic revenue and margin are forecast before a sequential recovery, with order momentum seen as a key driver for the rest of the year.

  • President and CEO Laukien highlighted, "we have gone even stronger or even further on the cost initiatives. We now expect these to yield on an annualized basis closer to $140 million or even higher than that."
  • CFO Herman noted that aftermarket revenue now comprises 38% of BSI sales and "saw growth in the low-single-digit range year over year."
  • The fourth quarter saw record operating cash flow and a $145 million debt repayment, reducing year-end leverage to approximately 3.1.
  • Management expects molecular diagnostics (ELITech) and spatial biology platforms to drive strength, citing 30% more platform placements in 2025, with "very good growth" anticipated from molecular diagnostics in 2026.
  • Management reiterated, "there is still some lingering uncertainty in the U.S. academic and government market," and does not anticipate a significant snapback in any end market except gradual improvements in biopharma, semi, and diagnostics during 2026.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • Book-to-bill: A measure indicating the ratio of orders received to product shipped and billed in a period, used to assess demand momentum.
  • Aftermarket revenue: Revenue derived from sales of services, consumables, and parts for instruments already installed at customer sites.
  • Gigahertz-class NMR: Nuclear magnetic resonance systems with magnetic field strengths at or above 1 gigahertz, typically sold for advanced research applications.
  • CLIA laboratories: U.S. clinical labs certified under the Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments, permitting lab-developed diagnostic tests.
  • LDT: Laboratory Developed Test, custom diagnostic assays created and used within a single CLIA-certified lab, often leveraging emerging research tools.
  • Project Accelerate 3.0: Bruker's current portfolio expansion strategy emphasizing innovation in post-genomic research, proteomics, multi-omics, and diagnostics.
