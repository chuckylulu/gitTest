 Group CEO Message
  Building on Success
  I am pleased to say that FY2025 was another successful year for Advantest. We achieved record highs in sales,
  operating profit, and net income, supported by continued strong demand for AI-related semiconductors and
  high-performance computing (HPC) applications. To me, these results demonstrate a strong foundation built over
  many years of supporting our customers with superior test solutions. Careful planning and strategic investment
  in key technologies allowed us to seize significant opportunities this past year, offering competitive solutions that
  cater to the complexity of modern high-performance devices. We also invested in expanding capacity and new
  partnerships, so we can serve customers as they expand production to meet rising demand.

  As we expect the market for AI to continue to grow throughout FY2026 and beyond, Advantest remains uniquely
  positioned to navigate the converging pressures of today and enable our customers to bring new innovations to
  market with speed and velocity. With this in mind, I see FY2026 as a year full of opportunity.


  Innovating Across the Test Flow
  The path I see ahead of us continues to be shaped by demand for AI. AI-related investment is not limited to one
  application or generation of devices. Rather, we’re seeing its influence across the industry and beyond. AI chips
  are mosaics of innovation, pulling the most advanced technologies from every sector—combining high-speed
  processors with high-bandwidth memory devices, and now even optical interconnects and photonics. Moreover,
  next-generation advanced packaging technology is expected to become even more complex.

  reliability, performance—as these complexities converge, the test requirements for high-performance devices

 Group COO Message
  Clear Accountability and Collaboration Supporting Global Operations
  Against the backdrop of growth in the semiconductor market and continued advancements in technological
  innovation, Advantest has expanded its business globally. In this environment, the key challenge is how we
  build a management and operational foundation that enables us to respond swiftly and effectively to diversifying
  customer needs and rapidly changing markets. I believe this foundation is an important element that underpins
  Advantest’s competitiveness.

  In the semiconductor industry, the pace of technological innovation and market changes is accelerating, and the
  value chain, spanning from R&D, production, sales, and support, is expanding globally. To respond effectively
  to this environment, Advantest has established a structure that empowers the CxOs by granting them authority
  and accountability within their respective domains, rather than centralizing decision-making. For a global
  company like Advantest, we believe that enhancing the quality and speed of decision-making and implementing
  management practices that allow the entire Group to achieve shared success is the most effective management
  approach. Under this structure, we maintain accountability in each management domain while enabling high-
  quality decision-making based on expertise and agile collaboration across functions and regions. As a result, even
  as our business continues to expand globally, we are able to maintain operations that combine speed, scale, and
  consistency, and build a management foundation that is resilient to change.

  Execution Capability Supported by Collaboration Among RU, BU, and FU
  Advantest’s global operations are based on close collaboration among Regional Units, Business Units, and
  Functional Units, each of which plays a distinct role. Regional Units are closest to customers and markets,
  and are responsible for regional operations, including sales, support and services, and supply chain activities.
  They make prompt decisions and take appropriate action based on the specific circumstances in each region.            Koichi Tsukui

 Advantest’s business segments are broadly divided into the Test System Business Segment and the Services and Others Segment. The test solutions,
 which integrate our core products, test systems, with peripheral devices and other equipment, meet the needs of our industry as semiconductor devices

 Our R&D
 Anticipating next-generation test challenges through global collaboration
 Advantest’s advanced measurement technology is supported by a global R&D structure that
 combines deep technical expertise with close alignment to customer roadmaps. As semiconductor
 devices become more complex, test requirements continue to expand in accuracy, performance,
 coverage, speed, and efficiency. Advantest’s role is to anticipate these evolving requirements and
 develop technologies that help customers address increasingly complex challenges across the
 semiconductor value chain.




                                                                               An integrated approach to innovation

                                                                               As semiconductor architectures evolve, test challenges are no longer limited to a single device, process, or test insertion. Technologies such as chiplets,
                                                                               advanced packaging, high-bandwidth memory, and silicon photonics are creating new requirements that span thermal behavior, signal integrity, power
                                                                               management, data volume, and system-level performance. Addressing these challenges requires an integrated approach to solution development,
                                                                               combining multiple technical perspectives to respond to increasingly complex customer requirements.

                                                                               At the same time, the pace of change in the semiconductor industry continues to accelerate, making shorter and more agile development cycles
                                                                               increasingly important. Advantest is strengthening its ability to respond to market windows through initiatives such as advanced development tools, parallel
                                                                               development, and technology pre-development.



                                                                               Delivering value through customer-driven R&D

                                                                               Through R&D that is closely connected to customer needs and future technology trends, Advantest continues to strengthen the measurement technologies
                                                                               and integrated test capabilities that form the foundation of its business. This commitment enables Advantest to support the development and production of
                                                                               leading-edge semiconductor devices and contribute to innovation across the semiconductor ecosystem.

 Our Customer Relations
 Building trust through close engagement with customers
 Trusted customer relationships are central to Advantest’s business. In a semiconductor industry
 where technology, market conditions, and customer requirements are changing rapidly, customer
 relations are not limited to introducing products or responding to immediate demand—they are a
 critical means of understanding customers’ technology roadmaps, production priorities, business
 models, and strategic challenges at an early stage.



                                                                              Understanding customers’ current and future needs
                                                                              Advantest works closely with leading semiconductor companies around the world to understand the issues they face as devices become more complex. Our customer
                                                                              base spans the semiconductor value chain, including fabless companies, IDMs, foundries, and OSATs. This broad customer reach enables Advantest to understand
                                                                              diverse requirements across design, manufacturing, assembly, packaging, and test, and to provide solutions that address challenges at multiple points in the
                                                                              semiconductor ecosystem.

                                                                              In particular, Advantest has built strong relationships with leading customers driving AI and HPC-related semiconductor innovation. As AI devices become more
                                                                              complex and performance requirements continue to rise, these customers require solutions that go beyond individual test systems. They seek comprehensive expertise
                                                                              that spans test content, device handling, thermal and power challenges, production ramp-up, supply capabilities, field support, and data-driven improvements.

                                                                              By maintaining close and continuous dialogue with customers, Advantest seeks to understand not only what customers need today, but also what they will need tomorrow.
                                                                              This customer engagement allows us to identify emerging requirements early and align our solutions with customers’ long-term technology and business priorities.


                                                                              Deepening trust as a long-term partner
                                                                              Long-term trust enables Advantest to engage with customers not only on current projects, but also on future opportunities. By maintaining close relationships with
                                                                              industry leaders and monitoring changes in technology and market direction, Advantest can identify emerging needs early and support customers as a strategic partner.

                                                                              Through trusted customer relationships, Advantest strengthens its ability to solve customer challenges, support semiconductor innovation, and continue expanding its
                                                                              role across the semiconductor value chain.

 To further promote value sharing with shareholders, the Company has established stock ownership guidelines
 recommending that executive officers hold the Company’s shares, including restricted stock (RS) and restricted
 stock units (RSUs), above prescribed levels. Both value-based and share-count criteria are established based on
 the amount of base compensation and the share price at the start of the Mid-Term Management Plan, and executive
 officers are expected to meet either criterion within five years.

 • Group CEO: Equivalent to four years of base compensation
 • Executive officers other than the Group CEO: Equivalent to two years of base compensation

 The Company has also established malus and clawback provisions. If certain events occur, including violations of
 applicable laws, regulations or internal rules, or the discovery of errors requiring a material retrospective restatement
 of the consolidated financial statements, the Company may, by resolution of the Board of Directors, reduce future
 compensation or require the repayment of compensation previously paid.

                                                          Singapore | Singapore Polytechnic

     Since 2021, Advantest Singapore has collaborated with Singapore Polytechnic (SP) on talent development and workforce development. In 2022, the two organizations
     established the Test Engineering Center, a co-location test and training facility with a fully integrated V93000 and M4841 test cell, located on the SP campus.

     The center uses Advantest’s V93000 SoC test system to provide a specialized curriculum for students majoring in electrical and electronics engineering for IC testing and
     semiconductor manufacturing learning journey. It also offers collaboration opportunities through internships, continuing education programs, and industry events to prepare
     the talent pipeline for the semiconductor ecosystem in Singapore.

     As part of this collaboration, 20 SP students and three faculty members visited the Gunma R&D Center in September 2025. They learned about Advantest’s business and the
     manufacturing process for test systems and observed memory test systems and test handlers on the laboratory floor. At the museum, they traced the evolution of Advantest’s
     test solutions, from single-function measuring instruments developed in the 1950s to today’s advanced semiconductor test systems.
