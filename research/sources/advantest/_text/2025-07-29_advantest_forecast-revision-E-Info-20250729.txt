<Reasons for Revisions>
   In the semiconductor tester market, while the recovery in demand for non-AI applications is still
expected to take time, the elevated demand for AI-related applications is increasing at a pace exceeding
initial expectations, driven by factors such as growing complexity and expanding demand. Furthermore, the
increasing number of companies entering the AI semiconductor market is also expected to further drive
demand. Based on this outlook and the progress made for the three-month period ended June
30, 2025, Advantest has revised its full-year consolidated earnings forecast for the current
fiscal year as shown above.
