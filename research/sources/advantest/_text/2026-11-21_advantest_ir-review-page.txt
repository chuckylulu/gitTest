During the six-month  period ended September 30, 2026, the global economy as a whole maintained a  steady growth trajectory, supported by increased investment related to AI,  particularly in the United States. On the other hand, uncertainties persisted  amid geopolitical risks, including the situation in the Middle East, and  inflation concerns.

Under  such global economic conditions, the semiconductor market continued to grow  robustly, driven by accelerating competition in AI technology and broader  adoption across society. AI-related high-performance semiconductors such as SoC  semiconductors and memory semiconductors for data centers drove market growth.

In Advantest’s business, as production volumes of AI-related  high-performance semiconductors increased and semiconductor complexity grew  with the adoption of advanced packaging and other technologies, customers  continued to invest actively in our testers. Advantest worked to expand  production capacity throughout the supply chain to meet customers’ strong  tester demand in a timely manner.

As a result of the above, net sales were (Y) 845.2 billion (60.5%  increase in comparison to the corresponding period of the previous fiscal  year), and operating income was (Y) 456.8 billion (96.5% increase in comparison  to the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year), due to factors  including an increase in net sales and an improved sales mix. In addition,  Advantest recorded financial income of approximately (Y) 57.0 billion,  primarily attributable to valuation gains on investment securities. As a result,  income before income taxes was (Y) 510.2 billion (121.3% increase in comparison  to the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year) and net income was (Y) 368.1 billion (116.8% increase  in comparison to the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year). The  depreciation of the yen against the U.S. dollar also contributed to our  financial performance, with net sales, operating income, income before income  taxes, and net income all reaching record highs in a six-month period. Average  currency exchange rates in the current period were 1 USD to 160 JPY (146 JPY in  the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year), and 1 EUR to 185 JPY  (166 JPY in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year). The  percentage of net sales to overseas customers was 99.4% (98.3% in the corresponding  period of the previous fiscal year).
