2. Reasons for Determination of Interim Dividends
  The shareholder return policy of Advantest has set a target to achieve a cumulative total return ratio(*) of
50% or more over the three years of the third mid-term management plan with a minimum dividend of 30.00
yen per share for annual. Based on this policy and the business results for the first half of fiscal year ending
March 31, 2027, we have decided to pay an interim dividend at 49.00 yen per share.
(*) Total return ratio: (Dividend + Share repurchase)/Consolidated net income
