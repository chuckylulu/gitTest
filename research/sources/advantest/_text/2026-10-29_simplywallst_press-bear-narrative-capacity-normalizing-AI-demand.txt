6857: Stronger Guidance And Buybacks Leave Little Room For Error
Analysts have raised their price target for Advantest to around ¥31,300 from about ¥29,300, citing revised assumptions for revenue growth, profit margins and future P/E that support a higher fair value estimate.

What's in the News for Advantest

Advantest Corporation's Board of Directors authorized a new equity buyback program on October 28, 2026, allowing repurchases of up to 11,000,000 shares, about 1.52% of outstanding shares, with a stated aim of providing shareholder returns and improving capital efficiency. Source: company announcement.

The buyback program is valid until March 24, 2027, with a total planned amount of ¥280,000 million and is in addition to prior repurchase activity that included 5,759,000 shares already bought back under a 2025 program. Source: company announcement.

Advantest revised its consolidated earnings guidance for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, now guiding to net sales of ¥1,863,000 million, operating income of ¥967,000 million, and net income of ¥741,500 million, with basic earnings per share of ¥1,027.08. This compares with prior guidance of net sales of ¥1,714,000 million, operating income of ¥846,000 million, and net income of ¥660,000 million. Source: company guidance update.

The company announced a second quarter dividend for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027 of ¥49.00 per share, payable on December 1, 2026, compared with ¥29.00 per share a year earlier. Source: company dividend announcement.

Asian technology stocks, including Advantest, recently saw share price declines during a wider semiconductor sector sell off that was linked to concerns about potential further US Federal Reserve interest rate hikes and the cost of funding AI infrastructure projects. Source: regional market news coverage.

Valuation Changes for Advantest

Discount Rate: Assumed discount rate is 9.57%, indicating no meaningful change in the risk input.

Future P/E: Future P/E assumption has risen from 37.14x to 39.32x, implying a slightly higher valuation multiple applied to Advantest in the updated model.

Key Takeaways

Bearish analysts expect Advantest to face downside risk if AI driven SoC tester demand normalises, while its rapidly expanded capacity and input cost pressures weigh on margins.

The main factor that needs to go right is that Advantest converts its high SoC tester market share and capacity build out into sustainable, profitable volumes, without being materially affected by memory tester share losses or higher DRAM costs.

The numbers imply the share price already assigns a premium to Advantest for continued AI related tester growth and high profitability, which may leave limited room for disappointment if these expectations are not maintained.

What Advantest Does

Advantest manufactures and sells semiconductor test systems, metrology tools, mechatronics related products, software and cloud based test solutions, and related services for chip makers and electronics customers. The company also generates revenue from maintenance, consumables, used product sales, equipment leasing, consulting, support, and training.

Catalysts

What are the underlying business or industry changes driving this perspective?

Although Advantest ties a much larger SoC tester market to sustained AI and high performance computing demand, any moderation in orders for CPUs, GPUs and custom accelerators would expose how much recent revenue depends on a single application wave. This could slow top line growth if tester utilization normalizes.

While the V93000 platform currently supports a high share of SoC tester demand, customer efforts to qualify alternative vendors across CPUs, GPUs and networking raise the risk that this de facto standard status is diluted over time. This could lead to slower order intake and weaker earnings than recent market share levels suggest.

The accelerated build out toward roughly 10,000 V93000 systems per year, with management already evaluating capacity beyond that level, heightens the chance that production outstrips sustainable demand. This would leave fixed costs underutilized and put pressure on gross margin and operating profit.

Advantest points to early wins in silicon photonics and optical I/O testing as a future demand driver. Yet if these applications scale more slowly or customers delay high volume production, the company could carry added R&D and ecosystem costs without matching revenue, which would weigh on earnings quality.

Management acknowledges a dip in memory tester market share and rising DRAM prices that are only partly passed through. If product transitions fail to regain share while component costs stay elevated, the combination of weaker memory tester revenue and squeezed margins could drag on overall profitability.

Assumptions
How have these above catalysts been quantified?
This narrative explores a more pessimistic perspective on Advantest compared to the consensus, based on a Fair Value that aligns with the bearish cohort of analysts.
The bearish analysts are assuming Advantest's revenue will grow by 22.2% annually over the next 3 years.
The bearish analysts assume that profit margins will shrink from 37.3% today to 33.3% in 3 years time.
The bearish analysts expect earnings to reach ¥747.7 billion (and earnings per share of ¥1097.84) by about October 2029, up from ¥460.0 billion today. However, there is some disagreement amongst the analysts with the more bullish ones expecting earnings as high as ¥2040.1 billion.
In order for the above numbers to justify the price target of the more bearish analyst cohort, the company would need to trade at a PE ratio of 39.3x on those 2029 earnings, down from 58.2x today. This future PE is greater than the current PE for the JP Semiconductor industry at 20.1x.
The bearish analysts expect the number of shares outstanding to decline by 0.47% per year for the next 3 years.
To value all of this in today's terms, we will use a discount rate of 9.57%, as per the Simply Wall St company report.

Risks

What could happen that would invalidate this narrative?

AI related SoC tester demand could prove structurally stronger than the bearish view assumes, supported by Advantest’s estimate that the SoC tester market reached about US$6.9b in 2025 and its raised 2026 market forecast. This would help sustain revenue and earnings rather than lead to a material slowdown.

Advantest’s position as a de facto standard in SoC testers, with an estimated 66% market share in 2025 and management’s stated path toward higher share, could make it harder for rivals to displace the V93000 platform and could support higher than expected revenue and more resilient gross margin.

Early traction in silicon photonics and optical I/O testing, including a large ATE order and ecosystem work such as integrating additional software flows into the V93000, could open a new test category that adds an incremental revenue stream and supports long term earnings if adoption of these technologies broadens.

Valuation
How have all the factors above been brought together to estimate a fair value?
The assumed bearish price target for Advantest is ¥31309.07, which represents up to two standard deviations below the consensus price target of ¥44938.1. This valuation is based on what can be assumed as the expectations of Advantest's future earnings growth, profit margins and other risk factors from analysts on the more bearish end of the spectrum.
However, there is a degree of disagreement amongst analysts, with the most bullish reporting a price target of ¥55000.0, and the most bearish reporting a price target of just ¥23000.0.
In order for you to agree with the more bearish analyst cohort, you'd need to believe that by 2029, revenues will be ¥2246.5 billion, earnings will come to ¥747.7 billion, and it would be trading on a PE ratio of 39.3x, assuming you use a discount rate of 9.6%.
Given the current share price of ¥37070.0, the analyst price target of ¥31309.07 is 18.4% lower. Despite analysts expecting the underlying business to improve, they seem to believe the market's expectations are too high.
