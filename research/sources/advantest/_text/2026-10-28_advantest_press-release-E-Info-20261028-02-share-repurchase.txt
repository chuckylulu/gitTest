              Notice Concerning the Decision on Repurchase of Treasury Shares
  (Under the provisions of its Articles of Incorporation pursuant to Article 459, paragraph 1 of
                                        the Companies Act)

Tokyo – October 28, 2026 – Advantest Corporation (Advantest) hereby announces that its Board of Directors
today resolved to repurchase its own shares pursuant to Article 459, paragraph 1 of the Companies Act, as follows.

1. Reason for repurchasing own shares
The shareholder return policy of Advantest has set a target to achieve a cumulative total return ratio(*) of 50% or
more over the three years of the third mid-term management plan. In consideration of the FY2026 earnings
forecast announced today and the situation of cash on hand, Advantest will repurchase treasury shares for the
purpose of shareholder returns and improving capital efficiency.
          (*) Total return ratio: (Dividend + Share repurchase)/Consolidated net income

2. Details of repurchase
(1) Type of shares to be repurchased:            Common shares
(2) Total number of shares to be repurchased: Up to 11 million shares
   (Equivalent to 1.5% of outstanding shares excluding treasury shares as of September 30, 2026)
(3) Total purchase price for repurchase:         Up to 280 billion yen
(4) Period of repurchase:                        From October 29, 2026 to March 24, 2027
(5) Method of repurchase:                        Market purchases on the Tokyo Stock Exchange

(Reference)
Number of treasury shares as of September 30, 2026
Number of outstanding shares (Excluding treasury shares): 721,952,905 shares
Number of treasury shares:                                 10,047,095 shares
