   Sample preparation remains one of the most consequential
bottlenecks identified in the survey. The listed concerns include
preparing ultra-thin lamella without introducing artifacts,
achieving accurate delayering in advanced BEOL & low k
structures, accessing regions of interest such as BSPDNs,
TSVs, TGVs, & hybrid bonded interfaces, & consistently
preparing large or non-standard geometries affected by bow or
warpage. These are not minor procedural inconveniences. In
many advanced packages, the act of exposing the structure can
change fracture paths, redistribute stress, smear interfaces, or
remove evidence needed for reliable root cause identification.
