Summary


     •   There is a growing sense of uncertainty in the semiconductor market and the semiconductor
         production equipment market, due to the trade dispute between the United States and China,
         geopolitical risks, and fluctuations in the semiconductor supply chain

     •   Supported by increases in SoC tester investment, 1Q results exceeded initial expectations

     •   Although we expect a recovery in 2H, any revision to our forecast will be contingent on
         confirmation of order trends in 2Q and 3Q

     •   The tester market has leveled off, but we will strive to achieve our mid-term management
         targets by capturing demand related to advanced technology and increasing our market
         share
