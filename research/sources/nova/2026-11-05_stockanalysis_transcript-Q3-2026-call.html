Earnings Call: Q3 2026

Nov 5, 2026

  • Earnings release

Summary

Q3 revenue reached a record $287.8M, up 28% YoY, with record profitability and strong memory and advanced-packaging demand. Q4 guidance points to a record FY26 revenue year above $1B and continued momentum into 2027.

Operator

Good day and welcome to the Nova Limited third quarter 2026 results conference call. All participants will be in listen-only mode. Should you need assistance, please signal a conference specialist by pressing the star key followed by zero. After today's presentation, there will be an opportunity to ask questions. To ask a question, you may press star then one on your telephone keypad. To withdraw your question, please press star and then two. Please note this event is being recorded. I would now like to turn the conference over to Miri Segal, CEO of MS-IR. Please go ahead.

Miri Segal
CEO, MS-IR

Thanks, operator, and good day, everyone. Welcome to Nova's third quarter 2026 financial results conference call. With us on the line today are Gaby Waisman, President and CEO, and Guy Kizner, CFO. Before we begin, I would like to remind our listeners that certain information provided on this call may contain forward-looking statements, and the safe harbor statement outlined in today's earnings release also pertains to this call. If you have not received a copy of the release, please view it in the Investor Relations section of the company's website. Gaby will begin the call with a business update, followed by Guy with a financial overview. We will then open the call for the question-and-answer session. I will now turn the call over to Gaby Waisman, Nova's President and CEO. Gaby, please go ahead.

Gaby Waisman
President and CEO, Nova

Thank you, Miri, and thank you all for joining us today. I will start the call by summarizing our third-quarter performance highlights. Following my commentary, Guy will review the quarterly financial results in detail. We delivered another record quarter, exceeding the top end of our guidance, with revenue reaching approximately $288 million and growing 28% year-over-year, as well as record profitability. This performance was driven in part by record revenue from memory and advanced packaging segments. The strength of the quarter was reflected in outstanding performance in multiple product lines. We achieved record results across integrated metrology, standalone dimensional metrology, and chemical metrology solutions, coupled with record service revenue. These results manifest the continued industry momentum as well as the proliferation of our portfolio across regions, customers, market segments, and critical applications. Our healthy pipeline continues to provide solid visibility into customer demand.

Furthermore, we now guide the second half of 2026 to grow more than 33% compared to the second half of 2025, demonstrating an accelerating momentum of our business into 2027. The semiconductor industry's investment momentum is substantial. The mounting requirements for computing power, memory, and connectivity are driving increased investments in advanced logic, memory, and packaging nodes. We see this reflected in robust demand across our customer base as our industry continues to expand capacity and transitions to more advanced manufacturing technologies. These trends reinforce our confidence in demand durability. For Nova, the opportunity is shaped not only by the overall level of industry investment, but also by the split to specific nodes and architectures. As spending shifts towards more complex designs and applications, including gate-all-around, the need for advanced process control and the intensity of metrology keep climbing.

While we grow at a fast pace, our growth relative to WFE may vary from time to time, depending on the investment mix. Our objective remains to outperform the market over time while continuing to leverage opportunities created by capacity expansions and unique value offerings. Memory was an important growth engine this quarter, reaching record levels of both revenue and bookings, primarily driven by strong DRAM demand. This performance was supported equally by our dimensional and materials metrology solutions. Our integrated metrology products benefited from capacity expansions in advanced DRAM manufacturing. In materials metrology, demand for our XPS solution continued to grow as customers increased sampling rates and expanded the platform's use into new applications, increasing tool utilization. This translates into growing number of tools per fab at leading memory customers, including a significant order from a major customer that further solidified the demand profile in 2027.

Advanced packaging was another significant contributor during the quarter, with record performance of our standalone dimensional metrology solutions and continued strong momentum across the portfolio. Our sales to OSAT customers picked up significantly, and we see multiple opportunities to expand our presence in this market. We are particularly pleased with the continued adoption of the Nova WMC platform across both logic and HBM applications and across a broad geographic footprint, with more than 10 customers having placed orders or completing acceptance of the platform, and additional evaluations underway. Alongside growing application content in dimensional metrology, we see adoption of our materials metrology solutions in HBM-related applications, complemented by strong performance from our chemical metrology portfolio. Together, these developments are expanding Nova's participation across the evolving advanced packaging ecosystem. Advanced logic sustained its robust momentum during the quarter, driven by healthy demand.

Looking ahead, as investment mix increasingly shifts towards gate-all-around devices, we expect a step function in the number of tools needed to support process complexity and metrology requirements, and in the value provided by our Nova PRISM platform. The logic segment also represents an opportunity for our front-end chemical metrology. During the quarter, the AncoScene platform successfully completed acceptance and release for high-volume manufacturing by a leading foundry, and it continues to grow in both market share and customer base. These developments broaden our opportunity in advanced logic and reinforce the increasing relevance of our portfolio. Nova's growing installed base continued to drive record service revenue, with strong performance in every region. Customers remain focused on maximizing production capacity, which increases the importance of tool availability, productivity, and robustness. These dynamics drove approximately 50% year-over-year growth in value-added services revenue, led by strong demand for upgrades supporting advanced node production.

As our installed base expands, we see further opportunities to deliver value throughout the life cycle of our solutions. As our business continues to scale alongside industry demand, we remain focused on ensuring that our operational infrastructure and supply chain can support our growth and meet our customer commitments. We are working closely with our suppliers to align capacity and planning with our needs while continuing to strengthen the resilience and breadth of our supply base. At the same time, we're expanding our own manufacturing capacity, including the new clean room in Asia, which is expected to become operational by year-end. Our outstanding execution this quarter, combined with a robust outlook for the fourth quarter, puts us on track to exceed $1 billion in annual revenue, reaching this important milestone a year ahead of our current five-year strategic plan.

With a strong pipeline, extended customer visibility, and an increasingly favorable industry investment mix, we believe Nova is well-positioned to carry this momentum into 2027. We look forward to discussing the next chapter of Nova's journey at our Investor Day on March 9th, 2027, in New York City, where we will present our future strategy and provide greater insight into the market and technology transitions ahead, the opportunities they create, and how we are aligning our portfolio and investments to drive Nova's next phase of growth. Now, for some more details on our financials, let me hand over the call to Guy.

Guy Kizner
CFO, Nova

Thanks, Gaby. Good day, everyone. I will begin by reviewing our third-quarter financial achievements and then provide guidance for the fourth quarter. We delivered another quarter of record results, generating $287.8 million in revenue, our third consecutive quarter of all-time highs. Revenue exceeded our guidance and increased 13% quarter-over-quarter and 28% year-over-year. This achievement is a testament to the strength of our core offerings, as well as our ability to capture and respond to the continued demand in the market. Product revenue distribution was approximately 61% from logic and foundry and 39% from memory and others. Product revenues included four customers and four territories, which contributed each 10% or more to product revenues. In the third quarter, gross margin was 57.7% on a GAAP basis and 59.1% on a non-GAAP basis. These strong margin levels reflect the high value of our product delivered to customers.

Combined with record revenue, they enable us to generate another quarter of strong profitability. As expected, operating expense increased to $73.3 million on a GAAP basis and $68 million on a non-GAAP basis, as we continue to invest in innovation and future growth opportunities. The increase was driven primarily by higher R&D spending to support our product roadmap and technology leadership, as well as costs related to ongoing strategic evaluations. Operating margins in the third quarter reached 32% on a GAAP basis and 35.5% on a non-GAAP basis, surpassing the upper range of our target model of 28%-33%. These results highlight our ability to translate revenue growth into profitable expansion. The effective tax rate in the third quarter was approximately 18%.

Earnings per diluted share in the third quarter on a GAAP basis were $2.64 per diluted share, and earnings per share on a non-GAAP basis were $2.86 per diluted share, exceeding the high end of our third quarter guidance. Turning to the balance sheet, we ended the third quarter with $1.8 billion in cash equivalent, bank deposits, and marketable securities. Our free cash flow for the third quarter reached $132 million, bringing total positive free cash flow for the first three quarters of 2026 to approximately $206 million. This performance highlights the strong cash-generating capability of the business and the quality of earnings. In addition, consistent with our balanced capital allocation strategy, we repurchased approximately $38.5 million of shares during the quarter, following the $200 million share repurchase authorization announced in September.

Next, I would like to outline our guidance for the fourth quarter of 2026. We currently expect revenues for the quarter to be between $305 million and $315 million. GAAP earnings per diluted share to range from $2.78- $2.94. Non-GAAP earnings per diluted share to range from $3.03- $3.18. At the midpoint of our fourth quarter 2026 estimates, we anticipate the following: gross margins of approximately 57% on a GAAP basis and approximately 59% on a non-GAAP basis. Operating expense on a GAAP basis to increase to approximately $78 million. Operating expense on a non-GAAP basis to increase to approximately $72 million. Financial income on a non-GAAP basis is expected to increase to approximately $18.7 million in the fourth quarter, reflecting the higher cash balance on hand.

Effective tax rate is expected to be approximately 17%. Before I conclude my remarks, I would like to highlight that based on the midpoint of our fourth quarter guidance, we expect to finish 2026 with another year of record revenues. Moreover, in the third quarter, we surpassed our long-term milestone of $1 billion in revenue on the last 12-month basis and delivered profitability at the high end of our target range. Both achieved a full year ahead of our original plan. Reaching these milestones ahead of schedule is a testament to the strength of our business model, the disciplined execution of our strategy, and the significant value our process control portfolio creates for customers. Most importantly, it reflects our ability to consistently generate attractive returns and long-term value for our shareholders. With that, we will be pleased to take your questions. Operator?

Operator

We will now begin the question-and-answer session. To ask a question, you may press star then one on your telephone keypad. If you are using a speakerphone, please pick up your handset before pressing the keys. If at any time your question has been addressed and you would like to withdraw your question, please press star and then two. Our first question comes from Atif Malik with Citi. Please go ahead.

Atif Malik
Analyst, Citi

Hi. Thank you for taking my questions. Gaby, you talked about objective to outperform WFE, which is a function of spending mix. So when you look at DRAM versus foundry logic mix next year with higher gate-all-around mix and new logic project, do you think that spending mix closes the gap from this year and helps Nova outperform?

Gaby Waisman
President and CEO, Nova

Thank you, Atif, for the question. Let me perhaps give some color to what makes the 2027 investment mix more favorable for us, for Nova. I'll start with gate-all-around. First, we see some shift towards incremental investments in gate-all-around, where intensity of metrology increases by about 30% compared to FinFET. Our Nova share in gate-all-around across all players is higher as well, so that also supports the increase. In memory, we do expect continued strong double-digit growth in DRAM investments and moving to some more advanced DRAM nodes, moving to such as 1C, 1D, whereas we will see some additional intensity, including in materials metrology, where we see increased sampling, new applications, and higher tool utilization, which drives an increase in numbers of tools per fab, which reach up to nine.

Obviously, process control and saying that in memory is lower than logic as a whole, but we do see some positive trend in that vector. In advanced packaging, and specifically in HBM, we continue to see the capacity expansion, the process complexity driving our business, and we see some, as I mentioned during the transcript, I do see some adoption in OSATs of some of our tools as a result of those players capitalizing on leading manufacturers' advanced packaging tool selections that were already made. Last but not least, we do see some new customers and entrances into the market, and those participants create additional opportunities for us to broaden our customer base and footprint. Overall, what I see is that 2027 combines both the WFE growth with an investment mix favorable for Nova, and some opportunities for increased application penetration and expansion of our market share.

I do anticipate this momentum to drive into next year. I would also say that we do see some linearity in the business in the quarter-over-quarter growth next year in that respect.

Atif Malik
Analyst, Citi

Very helpful. Guy, on the topic of supply chain, there's some concerns in the market that shortages of components like gas delivery and optical could limit the ability to ship more. Are you seeing any issues with Nova or your ability to ship more products, or the bottlenecks elsewhere?

Gaby Waisman
President and CEO, Nova

We are seeing, we're definitely seeing this rapid industry ramp stretching our supply chain, and we're taking steps to ensure that we keep delivering and maintaining our commitments to customers in spite of it. Supply chain resilience remains an area that requires active management, attention, and mitigation. We're working very closely with both existing suppliers on long-term capacity initiatives while also striving to broaden our supply chain and investing in Nova's own manufacturing infrastructure. I did mention our new clean room in Asia, and that supports our operational agility, provides us more capacity as part of our long-term plan. It's closer to customers, by the way, and definitely broadens the local supply chain ecosystem for us.

Atif Malik
Analyst, Citi

Thank you.

We definitely see some initial pilots next year with the transition to 4F² that will further increase the process control intensity for those customers. The opportunity in general is diverse and comes from our entire product portfolio. For the dimensional metrology, it's both the capacity expansion and an increasing intensity in HBM.

In the materials metrology, we see increased sampling rates, new applications, and all of that is driving additional tools per fab. I mentioned reaching the bar of nine with leading customers, and chemical metrology is definitely the growing adoption of our front-end solutions.

Shane Brett
Analyst, Morgan Stanley

Got it. That's great to hear. For my follow-up, I'd love to hear about the competitive environment in material metrology. You pioneered this market, have driven adoption with customers. If we do see competition emerge, just how do you think this market will change for you? Thank you.

Gaby Waisman
President and CEO, Nova

We anticipated the competition in material metrology. I indicated that a few times in the past, and it's a testament to the fact that this is becoming a meaningful process control area for both customers and obviously for peers and competitors. We see new entrants that will come and compete probably towards the second half of next year, and we are investing considerably in order to maintain a very significant competitive gap to make it very challenging for new entrants. But we do welcome the competition, and again, it indicates the importance of this market and the fact that it will continue to grow and be very attractive in terms of the importance it possesses with our customers.

Shane Brett
Analyst, Morgan Stanley

Thank you.

Hi, thanks so much for taking my question. To start, could you give us a sense of where foundry and logic and memory end up for Q4?
