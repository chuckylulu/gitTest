                        (Progress of Disclosure) Notification Regarding Changes in
                  the Largest and Principal Shareholder and Other Affiliated Company

  Rigaku Holdings Corporation (the “Company”) announced in its disclosures titled “Notice of Capital and Business
Alliance Agreement with Onto Innovation Inc., and Changes in the Largest and Principal Shareholder and Other Affiliated
Company” dated April 21, 2026 and “Notification Regarding Secondary Offering of Shares and Change in Principal
Shareholder” dated May 22, 2026 that, in connection with the transfer of shares of common stock of the Company by
Atom Investment, L.P. (“Atom”) to Onto Innovation Inc. (“Onto Innovation”) (the “Share Transfer”) and the secondary
offering of shares of common stock of the Company by Atom as the selling shareholder (the “Secondary Offering”), Onto
Innovation was expected to become the Company’s largest and principal shareholder and other affiliated company, and
Atom was expected to cease to be either the Company’s largest and principal shareholder or a principal shareholder. The
Company hereby announces that the Share Transfer was implemented on August 10, 2026, and that, as a result, these
changes (the “Changes”) will occur, as set forth below.


 1. Scheduled Date of the Changes

   August 12, 2026 (scheduled date of completion of share transfer procedures)



 2. Circumstances Leading to the Changes

   Settlement of the Secondary Offering was completed on June 8, 2026 and the number of shares of common stock of
 the Company held by Atom became 61,774,100 shares, following the exercise of the green shoe option in connection
 with the Secondary Offering.

    In addition, the Company received notification on August 10, 2026 from Atom and Onto Innovation that the Share
 Transfer of 61,123,436 shares was implemented. As a result, Onto Innovation will become the Company’s largest and
 principal shareholder and other affiliated company, and Atom will cease to be either the Company’s largest and
 principal shareholder or a principal shareholder.
