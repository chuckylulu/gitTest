Why Nova T550
®
?

Part of Nova’s fleet of solutions, Nova T550
®
 is built with unique features to keep the cost of ownership down for semiconductor manufacturers. With full commonali­­ty and the same optic design as the Nova i550
®
 integrated metrology platform, Nova T550
®
 completes Nova’s unique, highly efficient CMP and etch metrology and process control offering.

The high productivity and excellent metrology performance of the Nova T550
®
 enables increased sampling rates and high-performance film thickness and optical CD metrology capabilities for advanced nodes. The platform’s robust architecture delivers extremely low variability and superior tool-to-tool matching.

When supported by Nova’s central management, control and connectivity suite, Nova T550
®
 enhances operational efficiency and advanced metrology control functionality.
