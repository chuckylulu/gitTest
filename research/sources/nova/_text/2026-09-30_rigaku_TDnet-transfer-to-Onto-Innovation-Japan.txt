                                    Notification Regarding Changes in
                  the Largest and Principal Shareholder and Other Affiliated Company

  Rigaku Holdings Corporation (the “Company”) hereby announces that changes in its largest and principal shareholder
and other affiliated company (the “Changes”) are expected to occur on October 1, 2026, as set forth below.


 1. Scheduled Date of the Changes

   October 1, 2026 (scheduled date of completion of share transfer procedures)



 2. Circumstances Leading to the Changes

    The Company has received notification from Onto Innovation Inc. (“Onto Innovation”), the Company’s largest and
 principal shareholder and other affiliated company, that on September 29, 2026, it agreed to transfer all of the shares
 (61,123,436 shares) of common stock of the Company that it owns to its wholly-owned subsidiary, Onto Innovation
 Japan Co., Ltd. (“Onto Japan”) (the “Share Transfer”). As a result of the Share Transfer, Onto Japan will become the
 Company’s largest and principal shareholder and other affiliated company, and Onto Innovation will cease to be the
 Company’s largest and principal shareholder or a principal shareholder of the Company.

5. Outlook

  The impact of the Changes on the Company’s business results is expected to be minor.


6. Change in Non-listed Parent Company, etc. Subject to Disclosure
  While Onto Japan, a non-listed company, will become an other affiliated company of the Company as a result of the
Changes, it is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Onto Innovation and we consider that Onto Innovation is the entity that
has the greatest influence on the Company. Because shares of Onto Innovation are listed on the New York Stock
Exchange, it does not fall within the scope of non-listed parent companies, etc. subject to disclosure.


7. Agreements on Governance between the Company and Shareholders
   To maintain the existing arrangements following the Share Transfer, an agreement has been entered into pursuant
to which Onto Japan will become a party to the capital and business alliance agreement dated April 21, 2026 between
the Company and Onto Innovation. The Company has accepted this agreement.
    For the terms and purposes of the capital and business alliance agreement, please refer to the Company’s disclosure
titled “Notice of Capital and Business Alliance Agreement with Onto Innovation Inc., and Changes in the Largest and
Principal Shareholder and Other Affiliated Company” dated April 21, 2026.
