   Future work will focus on extending the proposed frame-
work across multiple XPS tool types, product lines, and pro-
cess chemistries. In parallel, standardized operating procedures
(SOPs) are being developed to support consistent deployment
across tools and fabs. The proposed framework is structured
to support applicability across a broad range of operational
conditions while maintaining stable and predictable perfor-
mance. Consistent evaluation and validation practices are
employed to ensure reliable behavior in diverse measurement
contexts. Empirical studies demonstrate stable performance
under representative conditions, enabling dependable use for
comparative analysis and ongoing monitoring. To support
long-term robustness, the framework accommodates periodic
incorporation of newly available data as part of routine opera-
tion. In addition, monitoring of measurement consistency over
time is supported to ensure sustained performance as operating
conditions evolve. Collectively, these characteristics provide a
scalable and maintainable foundation for applying data-driven
methods to advanced semiconductor metrology.
