Nova T500®

High-productivity metrology platform

Nova T500
®
 is a high-productivity metrology platform designed to address the unique challenges of the semiconductor manufacturing industry. The powerful platform delivers increased sampling rates and advanced metrology capabilities.

Industry Standard, Field Proven Platform

Outstanding Performance

Engineering Station Supporting IM Product Line
Used for recipe creation and as a redundancy tool that enables Pre/ Post measurement for additional sites and data collection

Why Nova T500
®
?

Nova T500
®
 helps manufacturers meet the growing need for high productivity in advanced semiconductor manufacturing. Featuring the same optical design and having full commonality with the Nova i500
®
 integrated metrology platform, Nova T500
®
 completes the company’s unique, highly efficient CMP metrology and process control offering.

As part of Nova’s fleet solution for semiconductor manufacturers, Nova T500
®
 is qualified to support a broad range of tools, including Nova i500
®
, and is available in multiple configurations.

When supported by Nova’s central management, control and connectivity suite, Nova T500
®
 platform enhances operational efficiency and advanced metrology control functionality.
