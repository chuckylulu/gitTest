 Summary

WFE spending is set for modest growth through 2026, driven by AI, HPC, and capacity additions across multiple regions. Key technology transitions, especially gate-all-around, and advanced packaging are fueling market share gains and product adoption. China remains significant but flat, while ongoing R&D and M&A are central to future growth.
