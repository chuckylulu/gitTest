Business and Operating
Performance

Memory was a major growth driver. Revenue and bookings reached record
levels, primarily due to strong DRAM demand. Integrated metrology
benefited from advanced DRAM capacity expansion, while XPS demand
increased as customers raised sampling rates and added applications.
Management said the number of tools per fab has reached as many as nine
at leading customers.

Advanced packaging also reached record levels for stand-alone
dimensional metrology. Nova reported stronger sales to outsourced
semiconductor assembly and test customers and continued adoption of Nova
WMC across logic and high-bandwidth memory applications. Materials and
chemical metrology are also gaining exposure to HBM-related
processes.

Advanced logic demand remained healthy. Management said
gate-all-around production requires approximately 30% greater metrology
intensity than FinFET and that Nova’s share is higher in gate-all-around
applications across customers. The company remains on track for
cumulative gate-all-around revenue of $500 million from 2024 through
2026.

In front-end chemical metrology, AncoScene completed acceptance and
was released for high-volume manufacturing by a leading foundry. Nova
said the platform is progressing through additional customer penetration
stages across several geographies.

Service revenue set another record as the installed base expanded.
Value-added services revenue increased approximately 50% year over year,
led by upgrades supporting advanced-node manufacturing.

Nova is expanding manufacturing capacity to support demand. A new
cleanroom in Asia is expected to become operational by year-end,
increasing capacity and placing production closer to customers and local
suppliers.
