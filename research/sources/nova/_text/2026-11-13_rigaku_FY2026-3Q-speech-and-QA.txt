programs for successor products starting in 2027.

Let me explain each one individually, starting from the top. First, for logic, we have GI-SAXS, a product
designed to meet the needs of GAA/CFET 3D structural metrology. We are actively advancing its development.
We expect to deliver the first unit to our first customer in the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, and we anticipate
that the market as a whole will begin to take off starting in fiscal year 2027. In this area, we aim to establish
this as the de facto standard, by integrating software through collaboration with Onto.

Below that, in the area common to both logic and memory, we have XD, which features High-Resolution XRD
technology to meet the measurement needs for Si/SiGe superlattice analysis. We did not have a product for
this before, but we launched it last year, and it has been performing well.




                                                                                                                   9
More than half of our major global customers have already adopted it as their POR, and we are now on track
to achieve our projected revenue for FY2030 by FY2028. With an eye on the future generation, we are also
starting a development program for the successor model.

Next, for the measurement of high-k metal gate-related ultra-thin film, we have launched the MF-3400, a

While this year still many of GEN3—previous generation models—are being adopted for mass production, we

production lines for memory, and, equipped with new software developed in collaboration with Onto, we
have exceeded our projections. We are now on track to achieve the FY2030 revenue target as early as FY2028.

This memory technology will continue to advance. As deep trenches become even deeper and technologies

with these trends in mind.

Below is the XRTmicron, which we added from Q2. Previously, we had lab tools and near fab tools, but we

At the very bottom is hybrid metrology, which is a joint development with Onto. This project has become
