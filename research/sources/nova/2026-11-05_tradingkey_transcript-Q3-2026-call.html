Nova (NVMI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call: Record Revenue and Q4 Guidance

Nov 5, 2026 9:36 PM
View all comments（0）


Nova Limited reported record Q3 2026 revenue of $287.8 million, up 28% year over year, driven by strong DRAM and advanced packaging demand. Non-GAAP diluted EPS reached $2.86, and the non-GAAP operating margin rose to 35.5%. Management expects Q4 revenue between $305 million and $315 million, keeping the company on track to surpass $1 billion in annual revenue in 2026. Growth into 2027 is expected to be supported by gate-all-around transitions and HBM capacity expansion. Risks include potential supply chain stretching and upcoming XPS competition, which the company aims to mitigate through product enhancements and manufacturing expansion.

  • Non-GAAP operating margin reached 35.5%, above the company’s 28%-33% target model. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $2.86.
  • Memory revenue and bookings reached record levels, supported primarily by DRAM capacity expansion, higher materials-metrology sampling rates and broader application adoption.
  • Advanced packaging delivered record stand-alone dimensional metrology results. More than 10 customers have ordered or completed acceptance of the Nova WMC platform, with additional evaluations underway.
  • Management expects Q4 revenue of $305 million to $315 million and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $3.03 to $3.18. The company remains on track to exceed $1 billion in annual revenue in 2026.
  • Management expects the second half of 2026 to grow more than 33% from the second half of 2025 and sees a more favorable investment mix entering 2027, including gate-all-around, advanced DRAM and HBM-related spending.

Business and Operating Performance

Memory was a major growth driver. Revenue and bookings reached record levels, primarily due to strong DRAM demand. Integrated metrology benefited from advanced DRAM capacity expansion, while XPS demand increased as customers raised sampling rates and added applications. Management said the number of tools per fab has reached as many as nine at leading customers.

Advanced packaging also reached record levels for stand-alone dimensional metrology. Nova reported stronger sales to outsourced semiconductor assembly and test customers and continued adoption of Nova WMC across logic and high-bandwidth memory applications. Materials and chemical metrology are also gaining exposure to HBM-related processes.

Advanced logic demand remained healthy. Management said gate-all-around production requires approximately 30% greater metrology intensity than FinFET and that Nova’s share is higher in gate-all-around applications across customers. The company remains on track for cumulative gate-all-around revenue of $500 million from 2024 through 2026.

In front-end chemical metrology, AncoScene completed acceptance and was released for high-volume manufacturing by a leading foundry. Nova said the platform is progressing through additional customer penetration stages across several geographies.

Service revenue set another record as the installed base expanded. Value-added services revenue increased approximately 50% year over year, led by upgrades supporting advanced-node manufacturing.

Nova is expanding manufacturing capacity to support demand. A new cleanroom in Asia is expected to become operational by year-end, increasing capacity and placing production closer to customers and local suppliers.

The company had already surpassed $1 billion in trailing-12-month revenue during Q3.

For 2027, management expects momentum from gate-all-around investment, continued double-digit DRAM spending growth, advanced DRAM transitions including 1c and 1d, HBM capacity expansion and broader customer adoption. Management also expects sequential growth through the year, but did not provide formal 2027 financial guidance.

Risks and Areas to Watch

  • Rapid industry expansion is stretching the supply chain. Nova is working with existing suppliers on long-term capacity, broadening its supplier base and investing in internal manufacturing infrastructure.
  • Management said Nova’s growth relative to wafer fabrication equipment spending can vary depending on the mix of investments across nodes and architectures.
  • New competitors may enter the XPS materials-metrology market in the second half of 2027. Nova is investing in higher-performance products and lower cost of ownership to maintain its competitive position.
  • Metrion has longer lead times, although four of five major customers are already using the platform in high-volume manufacturing, proliferation programs or evaluations.
  • Management expects cost increases beyond Q4, but believes higher volumes, operating efficiencies, product enhancements and pricing adjustments can offset them.

Analyst Q&A Highlights

Management described the 2027 spending mix as increasingly favorable for Nova. Gate-all-around metrology intensity is approximately 30% higher than FinFET, while advanced DRAM, HBM and new market entrants could expand application penetration and customer reach.

On memory, management attributed strong growth to capacity expansion and broader use of Nova’s dimensional, materials and chemical metrology portfolio. Initial pilots related to the transition to 4F-Square are expected in 2027 and could further increase process-control intensity.

Nova expects potential XPS competition to emerge around the second half of 2027, but not across its entire materials-metrology portfolio. The company is developing new XPS products with higher performance and improved cost of ownership.

Management said four of five major target customers have adopted Metrion through high-volume manufacturing, broader deployment or evaluations. Nova aims to add the fifth customer in 2027.

On profitability, management maintained its Q4 non-GAAP gross-margin outlook at approximately 59% and said it currently sees no specific gross-margin headwind. Quarterly margins may fluctuate by approximately one percentage point.
