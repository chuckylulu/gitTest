Citi’s 2026 Global TMT Conference

Sep 8, 2026

Summary

WFE growth for 2026 is being revised upward, driven by AI and advanced logic, with advanced packaging and new products fueling business growth. Gross margins are strong, operational leverage is increasing, and a $200 million buyback was announced. Expansion in manufacturing and supply chain agility supports future demand.
