# Camtek Ltd. (CAMT) – research folder

- `Camtek report.md` – the report: six company questions, Module A, Module B ledger and handoff table.
- `notes/` – the evidence base: one notes file per topic, one ledger entry per claim, and a gap-fill and consistency file.

## Rules used (inferred – please check)

`research/CLAUDE.md` still holds a placeholder where the rules block belongs, and nothing in the repository defines "Module A" or "Module B". This research used the stand-ins below. They follow the conventions of the earlier prompt files in this repository's history. If your rules differ, the notes can be re-cut to match.

1. Data and information only. No rankings, recommendations or investment opinions. The ledger verdicts judge whether the evidence supports a claim; they do not judge the stock.
2. Every figure has a value, a period, a source date and a source.
3. Sources are tagged **[CO]** if Camtek controls them (filings, releases, calls, decks, executives quoted elsewhere), **[IND]** if they are independent, and **[CO-relayed]** if a third party repeats management.
4. Company statements are marked QUANTIFIED or QUALITATIVE.
5. Derived numbers are labelled DERIVED, with the arithmetic shown. Estimates are labelled ESTIMATE, with the method shown.
6. Gaps are stated as gaps, and contradictions are recorded.
7. A claim backed only by company statements can be rated at most "partly supported".

**Module A (stand-in): financial and corporate fact base.** It covers revenue, margins, cash, debt, customers, geography, ownership, people, legal and regulatory matters, guidance track record and capacity. It also covers the additions you asked for: China share by quarter and export rules, customer concentration, convertible notes, and gross margin by quarter with mix effects.

**Module B (stand-in): claims ledger.** For each claim it records who makes the claim, a verdict (supported / partly supported / not supported / unresolved) with a confidence level, evidence for and against, how much of that evidence is independent, what would change the verdict, and dated checkpoints. A handoff table closes the report and adds your requested cell: the share of revenue that depends on micro-bump inspection.
