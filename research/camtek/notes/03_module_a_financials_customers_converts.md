# Camtek Ltd. (CAMT): Module A fact base (financials FY2021 to 9M 2026, customers, convertible notes, ownership, people, legal, guidance record, facilities)

**Scope:** Module A items (a) to (h). (a) Annual financials FY2021 to FY2025 and 9M 2026, cash flow, cash, headcount, R&D. (b) Customer concentration. (c) Convertible notes and financing. (d) Ownership. (e) Key people. (f) Legal and regulatory matters, including war risk. (g) Guidance track record since Q1 2024. (h) Facilities and capacity.

Other files cover the rest of Module A. Quarterly gross margin with mix effects, application mix and China share by quarter are in `02_quarterly_revenue_mix_margin_china.md`. Export rules are covered briefly in section (f) here.

Fiscal year = calendar year. The latest reported quarter is **Q3 2026**: results released 4 Nov 2026 on 6-K, and the call was held the same day. Notes compiled 21 Nov 2026. Data only: no recommendations, price targets or opinions.

**Conventions**
- [CO] = company-controlled. This covers filings, releases, call transcripts (even when a third party hosts them), decks, Camtek's website, and Camtek executives quoted anywhere.
- [IND] = independent.
- [CO-relayed] = a third party repeating management.
- QUANTIFIED / QUALITATIVE marks each company statement.
- DERIVED = my arithmetic, with the working shown.
- ESTIMATE = my estimate, with the method shown.
- INFERRED = my identification from indirect evidence, not stated by any source.
- "$M" = US$ millions. Figures are GAAP unless marked non-GAAP.
