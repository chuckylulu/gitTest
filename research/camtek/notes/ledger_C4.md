# Claims ledger, C4: "Camtek's 2026 orders convert to revenue on the schedule Camtek stated"

**Scope:** An adversarial review of claim C4 for Camtek Ltd. (Nasdaq/TASE: CAMT), Migdal HaEmek, Israel. Fiscal year = calendar year. Research date: 2026-11-21. The latest reported quarter is **Q3 2026** (release and call on 2026-11-04; 6-K). The latest company event before that was the pre-announcement and Investor & Analyst Day on 2026-10-14.

**Method:**
- Started from `notes/08_orders_backlog_capacity.md`, with cross-checks against `02_`, `03_`, `04_` and `07_`.
- Re-checked the key Camtek statements at source on 2026-11-21:
  - Q3 2026 release PDF, re-downloaded from cdn.camtek.com;
  - 14 Oct 2026 pre-announcement, read on PR Newswire;
  - 2 Jun 2026 order release, read on PR Newswire;
  - 30 Mar 2026 and 10 Feb 2026 order releases, read on camtek.com;
  - FY2025 20-F risk factors, local text copy;
  - Q2 2026 call, Q3 2026 call and Analyst Day transcripts, local copies, the relevant passages re-read.
- Ran new searches for disconfirming evidence:
  - competitor backlog and lead-time statements (Onto Q3 2026);
  - industry equipment and component lead times (TrendForce, ETNews, ijiwei, Digitimes);
  - customer capex and capacity plans (ASE, SK hynix, TSMC CoWoS, Amkor);
  - analyst and press commentary;
  - export-control legislation.

**Tags:**
- [CO] = company-controlled.
- [IND] = independent.
- [CO-relayed] = a third party repeating Camtek.
- QUANTIFIED / QUALITATIVE marks each Camtek statement.
- DERIVED = arithmetic shown.
- ESTIMATE = method and inputs shown.
- INFERRED = my reading of facts; not stated by any source.

Data only. No recommendation, price target or investment opinion.

## 6. Ruling

- **Refutation attempt:** I looked for any report, by Camtek or anyone else, of a cancelled, pushed-out or late-delivered Camtek order in 2026. **None was found.** Camtek has also not missed a revenue guide since at least Q1 2024. On the evidence available today, the claim is **not refuted**.
- **Survival test:**
  - The claim's 2026 leg survives at the aggregate level through Q3 2026. That evidence is entirely [CO]: reported revenue against Camtek's own backlog-based guidance.
  - The 2027 leg has not yet been tested at all.
  - The "schedule" itself is broad, and partly built on forecasts and slot requests rather than POs (A1, A2).
  - Camtek's own 20-F denies long-term commitments (A3).
  - Independent evidence adds real delivery-side risk: component and equipment lead-time stretch [20][21][22]. Camtek's supply-chain wording reversed within one quarter [12][14].
  - Customer-side timing risk is tied to cleanroom completion [13][14][18][23].
- **Rule applied:** a claim supported only by company statements cannot be rated above "partly supported". All the independent items support the demand environment, not Camtek-specific conversion.
- **Verdict: partly supported, medium confidence.**
  - Strongest form that survives: "Camtek's 2026-delivery orders have converted on schedule at the aggregate level through Q3 2026."
  - Weakest element: the 2027-delivery portion, including the $105M June orders and most H2 2026 bookings, together with the unquantified PO share of the $260M HBM figure and of the 2027 outlook.
