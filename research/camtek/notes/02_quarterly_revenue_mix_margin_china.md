## 1. Revenue by application, exactly as Camtek states it (Question 2)

### 1.1 Camtek's category labels and definitions, and how they changed (quotes)

Camtek has never published a revenue-by-application table in a release or a 20-F. Every application split comes from the CEO's prepared remarks or from Q&A on the earnings calls, and the figures are almost always rounded ("approximately", "around", or a range). The labels changed four times:

| Period | Label used | Camtek's definition (quote) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | "Advanced interconnect packaging" (AIP), with HBM and heterogeneous integration (HI) inside it | "I would like to clarify that under the term advanced interconnect packaging, we include chiplet modules, HBM, heterogeneous integration, WLP." (Rafi Amit, CEO). QUALITATIVE definition. | [24] |
| 2023 | Remainder | "The remaining 40% is divided between compound semiconductor for power device, CIS, and process control applications." | [24], [25] |

The 20-F business sections describe the markets but give **no revenue split**:
- "Camtek's systems inspect wafers for the most demanding semiconductor market segments, including Advanced Packaging, Chiplets, HBM, Compound Semiconductors, Memory, CMOS Image Sensors, Power, RF and MEMS" [22].
- 20-F FY2025 risk factor: "Approximately 50% of our revenues are generated from products supporting the production of artificial intelligence ('AI') applications" (QUANTIFIED, FY2025) [22].

**Definition-change summary:** "AIP" (2023, covering HBM, chiplets, HI and WLP) → "HPC" (2024–Q3 2025: HBM + chiplets [+ silicon substrates], with conventional AP and fan-out reported separately) → "AI-related products" (Q4 2025–Q1 2026) → "Advanced Packaging" with an AI majority, and "high-end AP" (Q2–Q3 2026). From Q1 2025 the HPC figure has been a range, because OSAT tools cannot be attributed to HPC with certainty. Camtek has said several times that it does **not** split HBM from chiplet/CoWoS revenue, apart from Q1 2024 and the H2 2023 HBM comment [27], [32], [34], [37].

### 1.3 Management's stated reasons for movements in the mix (quotes)

- **Q1 2023 (HBM >10%):** "customers in the HBM segment account for over 10% of our revenue in Q1, which represent a significant increase over Q1 last year." Also: "The HBM, I'm not sure, it can be a lot more than 10% by itself" (Ramy Langer). QUANTIFIED. [23]
- **Q2–Q4 2023 (AI-driven HBM/chiplet orders):**
  - "We expect that chiplet modules and HBM will account for over 30% of our business in 2024." QUANTIFIED. [3], [24]
  - "The high demand for high performance computing (HPC) was the main driver behind our record fourth quarter." QUALITATIVE. [5]
- **Q1 2024 (HBM 40%):** "Until the first quarter of last year, HBM accounted for a single-digit percentage of our revenue. Starting from the second half of last year ... they accounted for approximately 20%. This quarter, the revenue for HBM applications was 40% of our total revenue." QUANTIFIED. [27]
- **Q2 2024 (AP down from 80% to ~70%):** "This is the result of the other businesses that are growing ... These numbers also vary from quarter to quarter ... related to shipments and when customers are planning their clean room." QUALITATIVE. [28]
- **2024 HPC landing at ~50% rather than 50–60%:** "when we looked at the beginning of the year ... we said we'll do 50% to 60% in revenues for the HPC. At that time, the revenues we assumed for this year were lower ... from the numbers we anticipated that we will do for the HPC, we are very close to the target." QUALITATIVE. [29]
- **2025 (CoWoS moves to OSATs, HPC given as a range):** "We continue to see a shift of CoWoS like production toward OSATs, a trend that is favorable for our business." QUALITATIVE. [32], [33]
- **2026 (AP and AI share rising):**
  - "we expect that at the end of this year, we will probably 80% of our revenues will go toward Advanced Packaging ... the Advanced Packaging revenues in the first quarter compared to the fourth quarter, we expect growth of 70%." QUANTIFIED. [36]
  - Release: "AP revenue to grow approximately 70% between the first and fourth quarters of 2026." QUANTIFIED. [15]
  - Slide: "45% growth in AP sales is expected in H2 2026 compared with H1." QUANTIFIED. [17]
  - Reason given: "The transition to HBM4 and the associated capacity expansion have driven large orders from multiple HBM customers ... Rapid 2.5D/3DIC capacity expansion." QUALITATIVE. [17]
  - "the segment of the advanced packaging ... tend to lag behind the front end by ... 1 or 2 quarters." QUALITATIVE. [35]
- Consistency check, DERIVED: Q1 2026 AP ≈ 70% × $121.7M = $85.2M; × 1.7 = $144.8M. Q4 2026 guidance $180M × ~80% AP = $144M. The two are consistent.
- **Non-AP business:** "the non-AP ... I would say the business is stable ... We have some orders for CMOS Image Sensors that are significant" (Q2 2026). QUALITATIVE. [36]
- **Photonics:** 5% of orders; "'27 will be more than the 5%." QUANTIFIED. [36]

## 4. Contradictions

1. **China share for 2025: forecast vs actual.**
   - Q4 2024 call: "anywhere between 30% and 35% coming from China in 2025" [30].
   - Q2 2025 call: "a little bit higher than [~30%]" [32].
   - FY2025 20-F: China = **49%** of revenue ($243.9M, up 84% DERIVED) [22].
   - The Q3 2025 and Q4 2025 calls gave no updated FY2025 China percentage [33], [34].
2. **Korea collapse vs HBM-leadership messaging.**
   - Korea revenue fell from $117.1M (27.3%) in 2024 to $36.9M (7.4%) in 2025 (DERIVED from [21], [22]).
   - Over the same period management said "Our market position ... is very strong at all the HBM manufacturers" and that Camtek is "tool of record for all the 3D metrology for HBM4 at all the HBM manufacturers" (Q3 2025) [33].
   - This is not a direct contradiction, but the claims sit in tension, and Camtek gave no explanation for the Korea decline in the material reviewed.
3. **Gross-margin improvement promised and repeatedly deferred.**
   - Q4 2024: Hawk/G5 GM benefit "only in 2026" [30].
   - Q3 2025: "should gradually improve over the next few quarters" [33].
   - Q4 2025 and Q1 2026: improvement in H2 2026 [34], [35].
   - Q2 2026: exit-2026 target 52.5–53% [36].
   - Q3 2026 actual: 51.1%, "a bit lower than our expectations"; improvement now "starting from the first quarter of 2027" [37].
   - Non-GAAP GM has stayed in a 51.0–52.1% band for seven quarters (Q1 2025–Q3 2026), while G5/Hawk rose from ~30% of FY2025 revenue to ~57% of Q3 2026 systems revenue [16], [37].
4. **HBM ceiling.**
   - Q1 2023: "The HBM, I'm not sure, it can be a lot more than 10% by itself" [23].
   - Q1 2024: HBM 40% of revenue [27].
5. **HBM history restated.**
   - Q1 2023 call: HBM ">over 10%" of Q1 2023 revenue [23].
