# Camtek Ltd. (CAMT): orders, backlog, visibility, contract terms, capacity and lead times (Question 6)

**Scope:** Question 6 of the Camtek brief. It covers:
- every order, booking, backlog or "visibility" figure that relates to 2026 (order announcements, year-to-date order totals, HBM order-and-forecast figures, the 2027 outlook and the longer-term target);
- what Camtek counts as an order;
- Camtek's backlog disclosure policy;
- cancellation and rescheduling terms;
- revenue recognition;
- contract liabilities and customer advances as a cross-check;
- capacity expansion and lead times;
- a historical test of order conversion (2022 to 2026 announcements against reported revenue).

Fiscal year = calendar year. The latest reported quarter is **Q3 2026** (release and call on 4 Nov 2026, 6-K). These notes were compiled on 21 Nov 2026. Data only: no recommendations, price targets or opinions.

**Conventions**
- [CO] = company-controlled. This includes call transcripts hosted by third parties.
- [IND] = independent.
- [CO-relayed] = a third party repeating management.
- QUANTIFIED / QUALITATIVE marks each Camtek statement.
- DERIVED = my arithmetic, with the working shown.
- ESTIMATE = my estimate, with the method and inputs shown.
- "$M" = US$ millions.
- Third-party transcripts are machine or LLM transcriptions and can contain errors. Where a transcript and a release differ, the release governs.

Conversion arithmetic for 2024 and 2025 (DERIVED):
- **H2 2024:** orders publicly tied to H2 2024 delivery were at most ~$25M + $20M + part of >$25M + part of $20M (G5), i.e. ≤ ~$90M. H2 2024 revenue was $229.6M (= 429.234 − 199.601) [11], [13]. The announced orders are therefore at most ~40% of the period's revenue, and their shipment cannot be checked one by one.
- **2025:** G5 + Hawk ~30% of $496.1M = ~$149M of 2025 revenue (DERIVED, from management's "approximately 30%" [51], [52] and FY2025 revenue [17]). That is more than the ~$70M in G5/Hawk orders announced for 2025 delivery ($50M Hawk + $20M G5) [32], [33]. So most new-product revenue in 2025 came from orders that were never announced separately.
- **No order press release was issued between 27 Nov 2024 and 10 Feb 2026** [37]. Orders in that period were mentioned only on calls, and mostly without figures.

## 3. What counts as an "order"? (definition)

Camtek has **not published a definition** of "order", "backlog" or "pipeline" in any 20-F, release or deck reviewed [1]–[3], [8]–[21], [38]–[41]. Management's spoken descriptions are:

1. **Backlog = purchase orders (POs) in hand.**
   - "we already have P- you know, POs on hand. We have it in the backlog" (Q4 2025 call) [53]. QUALITATIVE.
2. **Pipeline is separate from backlog.** At Analyst Day (14 Oct 2026) an analyst asked Camtek to sort the 2027 number into "commitments … forecast, orders, and prepays". The COO answered [56]:
   - "it is backlog. The backlog is significant. We did not say the numbers".
   - "When we talk about pipeline … This means that we know that a customer has the budget, and he intends to place the order. Most of the cases, very high percentage, when we refer to pipeline, those orders are actually received on time."
   - "Many cases, it is a case that a customer already asked us to slot already, to give him a slot, and committed to give the purchase order at a later date. These are the cases of the pipeline".
   - QUALITATIVE.
   - Implication (INFERRED): a "slot" reservation without a PO is pipeline, not backlog. Camtek did not give the share of the 2027 outlook covered by backlog vs pipeline.
3. **The $260M HBM figure is explicitly not orders only.** It is described three ways:
   - "order and forecast" (Q1 2026 call) [54];
   - "Orders in the pipeline" (Q1 2026 slide) [38];
   - "$260 million of POs and forecast" (Q2 2026 call) [55].
   - The PO portion is not disclosed.
4. **The YTD order totals ($600M, $800M)** are described as "orders received" / "incoming orders" [19], [21], [56]. **Not disclosed:**
   - whether they include service contracts, spare parts or upgrades (service revenue is "around $40 million this year" [57]);
   - whether they are net of cancellations or rescheduling;
   - whether framework agreements or letters of intent are counted.
5. **Stand-alone order releases** use the words "order", "orders", "multi-system order" and "initial orders" [22]–[36]. None of them uses the words framework agreement, letter of intent or forecast. The one exception is the Feb 2026 IDM release, which aggregates "a series of smaller repeating orders" into the $45M [34].

## 4. Backlog disclosure policy

- **The 20-F has no backlog figure and no backlog definition** for FY2023, FY2024 or FY2025.
  - The word "backlog" appears only in the risk factor: "we cannot assure you that we will be able to maintain improving trends and convert our backlog into sales" and "lack of visibility/low levels of backlog from the preceding quarter" [1], [2], [3]. QUALITATIVE.
- **No remaining-performance-obligation (RPO) disclosure** was found in the FY2023 or FY2024 financial-statement notes. The FY2024 note says "revenues are recognized over a period of less than a year" [2].
  - The FY2025 F-pages were checked only through the XBRL R pages. No RPO schedule appears in the FilingSummary list of reports [7].
- **Calls:** in 2023 management said "we don't answer to specific question about the backlog or about the book-to-bill" [45]. In Oct and Nov 2026: "We did not say the numbers" / "this is as far as we can go" [56], [57].
- **Practice:** Camtek gives cumulative "orders received since…" figures in units (2022–2023) or dollars (2026). It gives qualitative backlog adjectives ("healthy", "strong", "record", "significant"). It gives forward revenue guidance "based on backlog and pipeline".

## 6. Revenue recognition

- **Point in time, at transfer of control, generally on shipment.**
  - FY2023 and FY2024 20-F: "The Company recognizes revenue from contracts for sales of products when the Company transfers control of the product to the customer. This generally occurs upon shipment whereas previously it was generally upon installation at the customer's premises." [1], [2]
  - FY2024 notes: "The transfer in control event is generally determined based on shipping terms." [2]
- The same paragraph adds that revenue is recognised "once the product is operating in accordance with its specifications and signed documentation of the arrangement, such as a signed contract or purchase order, has been received" [1], [2], [3].
- **Installation and extended warranty are separate performance obligations.**
  - "a fixed amount is deferred from revenue in respect of the installation services yet to be performed".
  - Non-standard (longer than 12-month) warranty income is deferred and recognised ratably [2], [3].
- **FY2025 wording change:** the FY2025 20-F MD&A paragraph omits the sentence "This generally occurs upon shipment whereas previously it was generally upon installation" [3]. The FY2025 financial-statement note (F-pages) was not read in full, so I could not verify whether the policy itself changed. See Contradictions / open items.
- Standard warranty is 12 months; service contracts start after it [3].

### 10.4 Implied backlog arithmetic for 2026–2027 (ESTIMATE)

Camtek does not disclose backlog. The bounds below use only Camtek's own figures, and they rest on stated assumptions.

- **Inputs:**
  - orders received 1 Jan–14 Oct 2026 >$800M [21], [56];
  - revenue 1 Jan–30 Sep 2026 = $416.2M [20];
  - Q4 2026 revenue guidance ~$180M [20];
  - 2027 outlook >$800M [20].
- **Step 1 (DERIVED):** 800 − 416.2 = **>$383.8M** of 2026 orders not yet recognised as revenue by ~mid-Oct 2026, **if** all 9M 2026 revenue came from orders placed in 2026.
  - Some 9M 2026 revenue came from orders placed in 2025 (e.g. ~$20M of the $45M IDM Hawk orders [34]; Hawk repeat orders "for shipment in 2026 and 2027" received by Nov 2025 [52]).
  - Some came from service revenue that may not be counted as "orders".
  - So the true unshipped 2026-order balance is likely **higher** than $383.8M. This is a lower-bound ESTIMATE.
- **Step 2 (ESTIMATE):** if Q4 2026 revenue (~$180M) is drawn entirely from those 2026 orders, then ≥ 383.8 − 180 = **~$204M** of 2026 orders would remain for 2027 delivery.
  - That is ≈ 25% of the >$800M 2027 outlook (204 / 800).
  - Not included: orders received after 14 Oct 2026 ("momentum has continued" [57]), pre-2026 orders scheduled for 2027 (e.g. the Hawk repeat orders "for shipment in 2026 and 2027" [52]), and the PO portion of the $260M HBM figure.
- **Cross-check:**
  - The $105M of June orders is explicitly all for 2027 [36].
  - Management said that from about Aug 2026 most new orders "will be for '27" [55].
  - At least $200M was added between Aug and Oct [56].
  - These items alone point to a 2027 backlog of at least ~$200–300M by mid-Oct 2026. ESTIMATE, low precision; Camtek has not confirmed any number.
- **Book-to-bill proxy (DERIVED; mismatched periods):**
  - orders 1 Jan–~10 Aug (>$600M) / revenue H1 2026 ($254.9M) ≈ 2.4;
  - orders 1 Jan–14 Oct (>$800M) / revenue 9M 2026 ($416.2M) ≈ 1.9.
  - Both overstate a same-period ratio, because the order windows run past the revenue windows.
  - Analyst framing [IND]: "you guys are booking almost $300 million per quarter in orders" (Cantor, Analyst Day [56]). This is the analyst's arithmetic, not a Camtek figure.

## Contradictions

1. **$260M HBM figure: orders or pipeline?** It is called "already received order and forecast" on the Q1 2026 call [54], "Orders in the pipeline" on the Q1 2026 slide [38], and "$260 million of POs and forecast" on the Q2 2026 call [55]. Camtek's own Analyst Day definition treats "pipeline" as orders not yet received [56]. The PO share is not disclosed.
2. **Stated manufacturing capacity moves inconsistently.**
   - Feb 2024: "at least $500 million" [46].
   - Nov 2024: "over $600 million", rising to ~$660M with Europe [48].
   - May 2025: "over $500 million" [50].
   - Feb 2026: "well over $700 million" [53].
   - Oct 2026: "$1.2 billion and beyond" [56].
   - The May 2025 figure is below the Nov 2024 figure. No unit or definition is given.
3. **Timing of the European capacity slipped.**
   - Nov 2024: new capacity in Europe "will start operating in 2025" / "will happen next year" [48].
   - Nov 2025: "We are also going to add some capacity in Europe" (future tense) [52].
   - Feb 2026: "late 2026" [53].
   - May 2026: "ongoing" [54].
   - German manufacturing floor space is unchanged in the FY2025 20-F (7,000 ft²) [3].
4. **Visibility language reversed within seven months.**
   - FY2025 20-F (Mar 2026): "We typically operate with limited visibility and do not have long-term purchase commitments with many customers" [3].
   - Oct–Nov 2026: "For the first time in Camtek's history, order visibility extends approximately one year ahead" [40], [56], [57].
   - This is a change in stated circumstances rather than a factual conflict, but the 20-F risk wording still applies to contract terms: no long-term commitments are disclosed.
5. **Backlog sufficiency vs outcome (2023–2024).** Nov 2023: "The backlog today that we have in hand still does not support the record revenues" [45]. FY2024 nonetheless reached a record $429.2M [13]. Camtek's annual outlooks have relied on pipeline conversion, not only on backlog.
6. **FY2024 capex: two figures in the same 20-F.**
   - The capex table total is $21.838M [2], [3].
   - The MD&A text says "approximately $10.4 million" [2].
   - The cash-flow "purchase of fixed assets" is $10.1M (file 03).
   - The table includes right-of-use assets ($7.0M) and inventory transferred to fixed assets ($4.5M). By contrast, the FY2025 text figure ($22.5M) matches the table total, so the two years' text figures are not on the same basis.
7. **G5 initial orders: 2025 vs Q4 2024 delivery.** The 24 Sep 2024 launch release says orders were "for 2025 deliveries" [31]. The 12 Nov 2024 call says the $20M of G5 orders had "delivery starting in Q4 this year" [48]. These may be different orders; Camtek did not reconcile them.
8. **Revenue recognition wording (open item).** The FY2023 and FY2024 20-Fs say product revenue recognition "generally occurs upon shipment whereas previously it was generally upon installation" [1], [2]. The FY2025 20-F MD&A omits that sentence [3]. All three keep the phrase "once the product is operating in accordance with its specifications". The FY2025 F-page note was not checked, so it is unresolved whether this is a policy change or an editing change.
9. **Prior-year cash figure in the Q4 2025 release.** It states cash and investments of "$851.1 million compared to $448.6 million as of December 31, 2024" [17]. $448.6M is the 31 Dec **2023** figure. The 31 Dec 2024 figure was $501.2M [13]. This is a typo in the release; it is not relevant to orders but is recorded here.
10. **Pre-2026 order release frequency vs order volume.** Camtek issued no order press releases from 27 Nov 2024 to 10 Feb 2026 [37]. Yet the Q3 2025 call cites "significant orders" from two Tier-1 OSATs, multiple G5 orders at a major IDM, and Hawk repeat orders [52]. Order announcements are therefore selective and cannot be summed into an order book.
