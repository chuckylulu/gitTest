# Camtek Ltd. (CAMT): competition at the same accounts against Onto, KLA, and Chinese and other local vendors (Question 5)

**Scope:** Question 5. This file covers wins, losses, dual-sourcing and share statements at SK hynix, Samsung, Micron, TSMC and the large OSATs (ASE/SPIL, Amkor, JCET, Tongfu, Powertech), with Intel added because both Camtek and its rivals cite an "IDM" account. The evidence comes mainly from **non-Camtek sources**: customers, Onto Innovation, KLA, procurement filings, Korean, Taiwanese and Chinese trade press, and analysts. Camtek's own share claims are kept in a separate section and tagged [CO]. Onto's and KLA's disclosed advanced-packaging figures are included for comparison. Local Korean, Taiwanese and Chinese inspection vendors are also covered.

Fiscal year = calendar year. Latest Camtek quarter reported: **Q3 2026** (release and call on 2026-11-04). Latest Onto quarter: Q3 2026 (call on 2026-11-09). Latest KLA quarter: September 2026 (call on 2026-10-28). Notes compiled 2026-11-21. Data only: no recommendations.

**Notation**
- [CO] = Camtek-controlled. [IND] = independent. [CO-relayed] = a third party repeating Camtek management.
- Competitor statements (Onto, KLA) are [IND] in the sense of "not Camtek". They are still **interested parties' marketing claims** and are marked as such.
- Each statement is labelled **FIRSTHAND** (the customer, the vendor about its own order, or a filing), **SECONDHAND** (press or analyst reporting with a named basis), or **RUMOUR/UNSOURCED** (no basis given).
- Camtek statements are marked QUANTIFIED or QUALITATIVE.
- DERIVED = arithmetic shown. ESTIMATE = method and inputs shown. INFERENCE = a logical deduction, with its premises shown.

## Contradictions

1. **Camtek "have not lost any market share" vs Onto "displacing the previously established tool of record".**
   - Camtek said it had not lost share on 2025-11-10 [9], 2026-02-18 [10] and 2026-05-12 [11].
   - Onto, 2026-03-16 [19]: a leading HBM maker moved "the majority of their 2D inspection needs" to G5, plus "a similar number" of 3Di (3D bump metrology) orders, "displacing the previously established tool of record".
   - Onto, 2026-02-19 [22][23]: a >$240M VPA including >$60M of 3D bump metrology at an HBM maker.
   - Onto names neither the customer nor the incumbent; the incumbent could be KLA or a local vendor. The two statements are therefore **not provably in conflict**, but they are in direct tension for the HBM 2D market.
   - Unresolved.
2. **Camtek "tool of record for all the 3D metrology for HBM4 at all the HBM manufacturers" (2025-11-10 [9]) vs Onto 3Di.**
   - Onto 3Di "completed full qualification at… two high bandwidth memory customers" (2025-11-06 [24]).
   - Onto's March 2026 3Di orders were placed in the same deal that displaced a tool of record [19].
   - Onto says 3Di addresses a **pre-reflow** step that "previous technologies couldn't reliably measure" [24]. That may be a new step, which would make the two claims compatible.
   - Unresolved.
3. **Camtek "very strong position in all three" HBM makers (Feb 2025 [6]) vs Korea revenue −68.5% in 2025** (DERIVED from [15]). Camtek gave no explanation (see also notes file 02).
4. **Camtek on local competitors changed tone within three weeks.**
   - 2026-10-14 [13]: local players "in Korea, in Taiwan, in China… are doing an excellent work and takes a market share"; China has "a couple… of good companies… no doubt that the local players will play a significant role".
   - 2026-11-04 [14]: "We do not see any competitive threat from those small competitors in China".
   - 2026-05-12 [11]: in China "the main pressure is coming from the local players".
5. **Camtek vs KLA on who leads packaging process control.**
   - KLA: "#1 position in process control for advanced wafer level packaging for 2025", +14 pts share [32].
   - Camtek: "most of the business is between us and Onto, and… a little bit with KLA" (2024-11-12 [5]); "in the specific market that we play, we are significantly larger" (2026-11-04 [14]); "not… apples to apples" (2026-02-18 [10]).
