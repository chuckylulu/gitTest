**Tags:**

## 3. What the independent evidence does and does not establish

**Established (FIRSTHAND, independent of Camtek):**
1. In 2026, at least **one leading HBM maker** placed double-digit Dragonfly G5 orders for "the majority of their 2D inspection needs", plus a similar number of 3Di units, after a competitive evaluation. Onto calls this a displacement of the incumbent tool of record [9].
2. Onto signed a **>$240M HBM VPA** (2D plus >$60M of 3D bump metrology), weighted about two-thirds to 2027 [10].
3. Onto's 3Di is **qualified at two HBM makers** [11]. Its 3Di revenue more than doubled in 2026, which Onto attributes to share gain [12].
4. **SK hynix publicly honoured Onto** for HBM defect prevention with G5, at the 2026 Partners Day (as reported by Onto) [13].
5. Onto holds a **Samsung JDP** for non-destructive HBM/HB inspection, with a tool on a mass-production line [15].

**Not established by any source found:**
- The identity of the HBM maker in [9]/[10] (SK hynix is the most plausible given [13], but this is INFERENCE).
- That the displaced tool of record was **Camtek's** (it could be KLA or a local vendor for some steps).
- Any HBM-specific market share for Camtek, Onto or KLA, by any party.
- Any SK hynix, Samsung or Micron statement naming Camtek in 2025–26.
- Whether Camtek's >$50M 2027 Hawk order [5] comes from the same maker as Onto's G5 win (dual-sourcing) or from a different one.

**Net:**
- The independent record shows Onto **taking new HBM positions in 2026, including at least one explicit incumbent displacement in 2D and 3D**.
- Camtek's counter-evidence shows a still-large, growing HBM order book, but only from its own disclosures.
- "Holds share" might survive for 3D bump metrology at some makers (MS 2026-11-02 [19], secondhand).
- "Holds or gains share against Onto at the HBM makers", taken as a whole, is not supported.

## 4. Contradictions

1. **Camtek "not lost any market share" vs Onto's "displacing the previously established tool of record".**
   - Camtek said it had not lost share on 2025-11-10 [1], 2026-02-18 [2] and 2026-05-12 [3].
   - Onto announced the >$240M HBM VPA on 2026-02-19 [10] and the G5/3Di displacement at a leading HBM maker on 2026-03-16 [9].
   - Camtek's later statements do not address the displacement. Onto does not name the incumbent. **Unresolved.**
2. **Camtek, earlier vs later, on HBM breadth.**
   - "very strong position in all three" (2025-02-12, notes 07 §1.2) and tool of record "at all the HBM manufacturers" (2025-11-10 [1]).
   - Then orders plus forecast from "2 HBM manufacturers" (2026-05-12 [3]).
   - Not a strict contradiction, because forecast coverage need not equal tool-of-record status, but the scope narrowed.
3. **Camtek's HBM leadership messaging vs its Korea revenue.** Korea revenue fell −68.5% in 2025 [8], while Onto's Korea revenue held at about −1.7% (DERIVED) [14]. Camtek has given no explanation (notes 02).
4. **Morgan Stanley, July vs November 2026 (both secondhand).**
   - 2026-07-12: "HBM share risks relative to Onto Innovation and KLA"; "HBM metrology share is diverging" [18].
   - 2026-11-02: Camtek "appears to hold" 3D bump metrology; the competitive pressure named is local vendors in 2D [19].
   - These may be reconcilable (2D vs 3D, HBM vs all AP), but the emphasis shifted.
5. **Onto "the only company recognized for technology achievement" [13] vs SK hynix's own page.** The SK hynix page captions plural winners in the technology-innovation category and lists 11 awardees without names (notes 07, contradiction 7). Not resolved.
6. **Onto on 3Di: "new step" vs "share gain".**
   - 2025-11-06 [11]: 3Di measures a pre-reflow step "previous technologies couldn't reliably measure", which suggests new demand.
   - 2026-11-09 [12]: "generally, it's share gain".
   - The two framings bear differently on whether Onto's 3Di growth came at Camtek's expense.
7. **Camtek on 3D demand vs analyst hearsay.** On 2026-10-14 the COO said "I do not see any hesitancy from ordering… orders for HBM and specifically for 3D bump metrology" [6], against Needham's report of HBM customer "hesitation… to buy the dedicated 3D bump metrology" [24]. Neither side gives data.
