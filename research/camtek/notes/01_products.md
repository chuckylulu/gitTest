## 1. Portfolio overview: what Camtek says it sells now

- The FY2025 20-F (filed 2026-03-19) [51] says: "As of today, we produce and sell the Eagle platform, the Hawk platform and the MicroProf platform, and have phased out all other models (Falcon, Condor, Gannet)." QUALITATIVE.
- The October 2026 investor deck's portfolio slide [50] groups products into three families. QUALITATIVE:
  - **Hawk family**: "Cutting edge solutions for Chiplets, HBM and Hybrid Bonding"
  - **Eagle family**: "Inspection and Metrology for Advanced Packaging", led by Eagle G5
  - **Metrology family**: NanoProf® ("Elevating Nanoscale Metrology"), covering advanced packaging (die and wafer warp, RDL, TSV), hybrid bonding (pre-bonding metrology) and wafer fabrication (post-CMP metrology)
  - **360°SCAN**: an add-on module for Eagle and Hawk
- The FRT acquisition from FormFactor was agreed 2023-09-18 for $100M cash, subject to adjustments, and closed 2023-11-01 [29][30]. QUANTIFIED. FRT is headquartered in Bergisch Gladbach, Germany. It brought "hybrid multi-sensor SurfaceSens™ technology" and the MicroProf line. Camtek expected FRT to "contribute over $30 million to the annual revenues in 2024" [29] (QUANTIFIED). No actual FRT revenue figure was found.
- The Visual Layer acquisition (Tel Aviv visual-AI company) was announced 2026-04-13 [31]. Financial terms were "not disclosed" [31][54]. Closing was reported with the Q1 2026 results ("whose acquisition we recently closed") [36]. Globes put Visual Layer's total funding at $7M [54] [IND]. That is a funding total, not a deal value.
  - DERIVED: intangible assets plus goodwill rose from $84M (2025-12-31) to $129M (2026-06-30) [48], an increase of $45M (129 − 84). Camtek has not attributed this increase. The Visual Layer purchase price is **not disclosed by Camtek**.
- Manufacturing sites are Migdal HaEmek (Israel) and Bergisch Gladbach (Germany). Final integration takes 6–12 weeks; tool assembly and power-up are done by subcontractors [51 FY2025]. QUANTIFIED.

## Contradictions

1. **Hawk minimum defect size: 100 nm vs. 150 nm.**
   - 100 nm: the website says "Down to 0.1um defect sensitivity" [1]. The Q4 2024 call (2025-02-12) said "detecting 100 nanometer defects" [40]. The Q4 2025 call (2026-02-18) said customers "looking to go to 100 nanometers" will go to Hawk [43].
   - 150 nm: the FY2024 and FY2025 20-Fs [51] say "150 nanometer". So do the Q2 2025 call (2025-08-05: "down to 150 nanometers … Already, we're demonstrating it at customers") [41], Asia Economy [53] and the Oct 2026 deck ("Defects down to 150nm") [50].
   - The deck also lists "Sub 150nm defects" and "sensitivity down to 150nm" for hybrid bonding as a **2027–28 roadmap** item [50]. That implies sub-150 nm is not yet available, which conflicts with the website's 0.1 µm claim.
2. **Hawk micro-bump pitch: <10 µm vs. <12 µm.** The website says "sub 10 um bumps pitch" [1]. The Q4 2024 call said "pitch lower than 10 microns" [40], and Asia Economy said "below 10 μm" [53]. The FY2024 and FY2025 20-Fs say "pitches less than 12 micron" [51].
3. **Installed base.**
   - The 20-F figure was identical ("over 3,000") at 2024-12-31 and at 2025-12-31 [51], despite 2025 revenue of $496.1M.
   - By Oct 2026 Camtek cited "~4,000" [50][46], roughly 1,000 more within about 10 months.
   - No unit-shipment data is disclosed to reconcile these figures.
4. **MicroProf MHU throughput.** "Up to 220 wafers per hour" (FY2023 20-F [51]) vs. "up to 180 wafers per hour" (current website [14]).
5. **AI software timing.**
   - FY2023 20-F: Camtek "intend[s] to offer" ADC [51].
   - FY2025 20-F (Mar 2026): "During 2026 we will offer our AI technology that includes inspection and automatic defects classification" [51].
   - The website already lists ADC, AiDC, EDC and 2D AI-Based Defect Detection, with EDC "included as a standard capability" [19][20].
   - Yet the CFO said on 2026-11-04: "At this point, we have pretty much 0 software offerings. This will start in next year in 2027" [46].
   - The gap may be that the earlier items are bundled features rather than separately sold products; Camtek has not stated this explicitly.
6. **New-platform share, basis of measurement.**
   - Q2 2026: the call said "~50% of our systems revenue" [45]; the script said "50% of the revenues" [48].
   - Q3 2026: the CEO said "~57% of … systems revenue"; the script said ">50%"; an analyst and the CFO said "more than 55% of sales/revenues" [46][49].
   - These figures mix total-revenue and systems-revenue bases.
7. **Eagle G5 2D sensitivity vs. older Eagle models.**
   - G5 is said to offer "higher resolution" [2][23], yet its stated BF sensitivity (0.5 µm) is coarser than Eagle-i Plus (BF 0.42 µm) [6] and Eagle-i ("down to 0.2μm") [5]. DF is 0.3 µm on both G5 and i Plus.
   - Measurement conditions (magnification, defect type) are not given, so the figures may not be comparable.
8. **Eagle-AP bump range.** Eagle-AP Plus: "Bump range: 2-250μm" [4]. Bump & Copper Pillar market page: sizes "from 2µm to 500µm" [22]. Bumps per wafer: 50 million (Eagle-AP) [3] vs. over 60 million (AP Plus) [4] vs. "hundreds of million of bumps per wafer" (market page, no model named) [22].
9. **Hawk "enhanced version".** The Q3 2025 call promised "an enhanced version of the Hawk early next year" [42]. No dated launch announcement for it was found. The Q2 2026 call refers only to "The Hawk, combined with its enhanced optical capabilities" [45].
10. **Press-release dates.** Body dates differ from the newsroom list dates:
    - Eagle G5 launch: Sept 24 vs. Sep 29, 2024 [23]
    - Hawk IDM order: Feb 10 vs. Feb 11, 2026 [26]
    - $105M orders: June 2 vs. Jun 05, 2026 [27]
    - Visual Layer: Apr 13 vs. Apr 14, 2026 [31]
11. **Q3 2026 release internal inconsistency (non-product, recorded for the record).** The highlights bullets say "GAAP operating income of $8 million" and "GAAP net income of $8 million". The body of the same release says GAAP operating income was $39.8M and GAAP net income $42.8M [38].
12. **Hawk throughput claims.** Website: "x2 higher TPT compared to Eagle G5" [1]. Eagle G5: "2X Higher Throughput" vs. earlier Eagles [2]. Q4 2024 call: some customers may take the Hawk "to get a double throughput for the same space of the machine" [40]. These are consistent with each other. None states the measurement condition (recipe, magnification, sensitivity), except the IR mode figure of 10 WPH at 1 µm [1]. DERIVED: if both ratios refer to the same task, Hawk ≈ 4× prior-generation Eagle (2 × 2). Camtek has not made that claim.
