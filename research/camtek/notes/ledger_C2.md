**Who makes the claim:** Camtek management only (COO Ramy Langer, CEO Rafi Amit, marketing director Eyal Segev), in calls from 2023-05-10 to 2026-11-04 and in the 2026-10-14 deck.
- I found no independent analyst or customer making the Camtek-specific claim.
- An analyst (Barclays, 2025-02-12) relayed it back as "You guys have talked about…" [4]. That is [CO-relayed].

**What the claim contains.** Camtek never says "HB will not shrink our bump business" in those words. Its position has two parts:
- (a) HB adds new steps that Camtek can address.
- (b) Micro-bumps keep growing and HBM5 stays on bumps, so the existing bump-metrology base is not displaced in the forecast window.

Camtek has never stated the **net** effect as a number (see §4).

## 4. Net effect: what can and cannot be quantified

- **Exposure (handoff from `06_hybrid_bonding.md` §6, ESTIMATE, not re-derived here):** share of LTM revenue to Q3 2026 ($544.3M, DERIVED in `06`) tied to die-to-die micro-bumps that HB would remove, mainly HBM core-die stacking bumps plus logic 3D stacking.

  | Case | Share of LTM revenue | $ |
  |---|---|---|
  | Low | 5.4% | ~$29M |

## 5. Verdict

**Verdict: PARTLY SUPPORTED. Confidence: medium.**

**What survives:**
1. HB adds new inspection and metrology steps with tighter specs. This is supported by independent sources: competitors Onto and KLA, SemiEngineering, and Besi/Samsung/SK hynix adoption data [10]–[21].
2. HB is not removing micro-bumps on a scale that would shrink Camtek's market in 2026–2027. This is independently supported: SK hynix stays on MR-MUF through HBM4E; TCB stays larger than HB through 2030 in Yole's forecast; hybrid bump + HB schemes are expected [10][11][17][20].

**What does not survive, or is unverified:**
1. The Camtek-specific part. No independent source shows Camtek winning HB steps, and its HB revenue is "low magnitude" while HB bonder demand is in the hundreds of systems [3][16].
2. Camtek lacks products today for post-bond voids and Cu dishing, and its 150 nm pre-bond sensitivity is coarser than the 50–100 nm levels cited by SemiEngineering, Nextin and KLA [1][18][21][22].
3. Camtek's claim that HBM5 stays on bumps contradicts every independent forecast found [10][24].
4. The net effect has never been quantified by Camtek or any independent party.

Under the ledger rule, a claim supported only by company statements cannot rate above "partly supported". Here the industry-level half has independent support, but the Camtek-specific "expands instead of shrinking" conclusion rests on Camtek's statements and its unreleased roadmap.

**Independent evidence share:**
- Of the 12 "for" items, 9 are [IND] (F1–F10 except F9 partly) but 2 are [CO] (F11, F12). The [IND] items support the industry premise, not Camtek's capture of it.
- Of the 10 "against" items, 6 rest wholly or partly on [IND] evidence (A1–A4, A6, A9).
